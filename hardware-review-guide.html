How to review this layer

Each article should teach one physical constraint before it names a systems or hardware technique. The paper anchors should then prove that the constraint is real: what was scarce, what moved, what was saved, what broke, and where the claim stops.

Failure signs

Local speedup only

The paper speeds up one kernel but does not show that the system bottleneck moved.

No scarce boundary

The prose never says which memory, link, area, power, queue, or trust boundary is protected.

Weak evidence

The metric omits workload, baseline, machine, scale, or failure case.

Layer mismatch

The method optimizes software while the real constraint sits in memory, interconnect, circuit, or operations.
