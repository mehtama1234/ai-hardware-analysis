# Extending RAJA Parallel Programming Abstractions with Just-In-Time Optimization

**Venue:** SC · **Subtheme:** Performance Modeling

## What It Does

Integration of Proteus JIT framework (LLVM-based) with RAJA portability layer; introduces dimension specialization optimization

JIT compilation for portable parallel loop bodies; dimension specialization replaces runtime queries with constants

## The Key Result

- **Speedup Amd:** 1.2x-23x
- **Speedup Nvidia:** 1.1x-15x

## Why This Approach

Extends RAJA to support JIT-deferred compilation. Introduces dimension specialization optimization. Demonstrates speedups on both AMD and NVIDIA GPUs

This work addresses the fundamental problem: AOT parallel programming models cannot perform context-specific optimizations due to lack of runtime information

## What It Leaves Open

- Evaluation limited to RAJAPerf benchmarks; impact on real applications unclear.
