# isscc-2025-102: DMT Wireline Receiver with Time-Based ADC

## Summary
353mW 112Gb/s discrete multitone wireline receiver datapath in 5nm FinFET using time-based ADC and per-tone MLSE to handle complex channels with ripples/notches.

## Key Contributions
- Discrete multitone (DMT) modulation
- Time-based ADC design
- Per-tone equalization and MLSE
- 353mW power at 112Gb/s

## Hardware Target
ASIC

## Technique Category
Circuit-design, Equalization, Interconnect

## Workloads
LLM-inference, LLM-training

## Baselines
