# QQ: Is 2-bit Enough? Exploiting Quantization to Enhance Computation and Memory Efficiency in Quantum Simulation

**Authors:** Hyoju Seo, Seokhyeon Lee, Yongtae Kim 0001

**Venue:** ICCAD

## Summary
QQ proposes integer-based quantization for quantum simulations by exploiting gate structure symmetry, achieving dramatic reductions in computation and memory overhead.

## Key Contributions
- Integer-based quantization scheme for quantum simulations
- Separation of real and imaginary components eliminating zero operations
- Up to 50% reduction in matrix-vector multiplications
- Up to 66.7% reduction in additions, 400,834× energy savings
