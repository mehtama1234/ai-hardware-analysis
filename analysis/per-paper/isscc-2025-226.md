# isscc-2025-226: Auto-Balanced Multi-Phase Power Converter

**Problem:** Multi-phase buck/hybrid converters require per-phase current sensing and closed-loop duty-cycle adjustment to maintain balanced inductor currents, adding complexity. Load transients create large output undershoot.

**Approach:** Segmented-interlacing multi-phase hybrid converter with inherent duty-cycle auto-balancing and simultaneous multi-inductor energization during transients.

**Key novelty:** Inherent inductor current auto-balancing without per-phase sensing.

**Applications:** Datacenter point-of-load (PoL) power delivery.

**Tags:** power-delivery, multi-phase, converter, datacenters, pocl, balancing
