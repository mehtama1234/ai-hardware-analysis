# isscc-2025-229: Integrated-Buffer Rail-to-Rail SAR ADC

**Problem:** High-resolution SAR ADCs require large capacitive DACs to meet kT/C noise, heavily loading the input buffer. Buffer power dominates overall ADC power consumption.

**Approach:** Rail-to-rail 3rd-order noise-shaping SAR ADC with continuous-time correlated level shifting in integrated input buffer, avoiding need for separate higher supply voltage.

**Key novelty:** Continuous-time level shifting integrating buffer into SAR noise-shaping loop.

**Metrics:** 105.4dB SFDR, rail-to-rail swing, >13-bit resolution.

**Tags:** sar-adc, high-resolution, noise-shaping, input-buffer, linearity, energy-efficient
