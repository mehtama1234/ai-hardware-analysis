# isscc-2025-067

**Title:** 32.4 A Dual-LC-Resonant Isolated DC-DC Converter Achieving 65.4% Peak Efficiency and Inherent Backscattering

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Problem:** Integrated isolated DC-DC converters (with on-chip transformers) suffer low efficiency (~52%) due to TX LC-oscillator and RX full-bridge rectifier losses; feedback requires digital isolators.

**Key Contribution:** Dual-LC resonant topology with inherent backscatter feedback achieves 65.4% peak efficiency (+6–13% over state-of-the-art), eliminating digital isolators.

**Method:** Optimized dual-LC resonators (independent TX/RX tuning), resonant rectification, modulated load impedance for backscatter feedback.

**Hardware:** ASIC

**Metrics:** 65.4% peak efficiency

**Techniques:** Power delivery, circuit design

**Primary Theme:** Dual-LC resonant isolated DC-DC with backscatter
