# A Cross-model Fusion-aware Framework for Optimizing (gather-matmul-scatter)s Workload

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only analysis)

## Problem
Sparse neural networks (RGCN, SpConv, MoE) use gather-matmul-scatter primitives, but optimizations are model-specific, require expensive search, and fail on dynamic inputs.

## Method
Efficient-GMS proposes **dataflow analysis** to identify shared patterns and four fusion-based dataflow variants. A **performance model** reduces search space, and a **lightweight heuristic** dynamically selects optimal dataflow.

## Key Novelty
Cross-model fusion patterns that transfer optimization strategies across diverse sparse neural network architectures without retuning.

## Results
- **1.46× speedup** (RGCN)
- **1.32× speedup** (SpConv-based)
- **1.15× speedup** (MoE)
