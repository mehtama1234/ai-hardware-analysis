# Reproducibility Report for SC25 Paper CPU- and GPU-initiated Communication Strategies for Conjugate Gradient Methods on Large GPU Clusters

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-401
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Artifact evaluation and reproducibility verification of communication strategy research for CG methods on GPU clusters.

## Motivation
Independent verification ensures research quality; reproducibility reports provide transparency in experimental validation.

## Method
Following the Supercomputing conference's standards for reproducibility, the authors tested whether the original paper's claims hold up in practice. They ran the code on GPU clusters with two communication strategies—CPU-initiated (where the main processor manages message transfers between CPUs and GPUs) and GPU-initiated (where GPUs transfer data directly)—to verify the claimed performance gains for conjugate gradient methods, which are iterative algorithms for solving large systems of equations.

## Key Novelty
The paper confirms that choosing between CPU-initiated and GPU-initiated communication strategies produces real performance improvements for conjugate gradient computations on large GPU clusters.
