# 50Gb/s Burst-Mode Optical Receiver

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**ID:** isscc-2025-243

## Problem
50G PON receivers must handle severe inter-symbol interference from chromatic dispersion while locking rapidly in burst-mode operation. Existing designs trade fast lock for equalization depth.

## Solution
Burst-mode NRZ receiver in 28nm CMOS with 5-tap FFE (3 pre, 1 post) and 7-tap DFE (4 floating, up to 16th tap) using edge equalization for 15ns lock time and deep ISI cancellation.

## Key Innovation
Deep equalization (5-tap FFE + 7-tap DFE) with ultra-fast 15ns burst-mode lock time.

## Results
- **Data rate:** 50 Gb/s
- **Lock time:** 15 ns
- **Equalization:** 5-tap FFE + 7-tap DFE
- **Photodiode:** APD support per ITU-T standard

## Hardware Target
ASIC

## Techniques
Circuit design, signal processing, equalization
