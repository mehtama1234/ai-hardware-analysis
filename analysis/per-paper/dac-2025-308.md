# HiSpTRSV: Tile-Level Parallelism for Sparse Triangular Solve on FPGAs

**Venue:** DAC 2025  
**Confidence:** abstract-only

## Summary

HiSpTRSV is an FPGA accelerator for sparse triangular solve (SpTRSV) that exploits inter-tile parallelism beyond prior intra-tile designs. Achieves 3.58x speedup vs. GPU, 9.59x energy efficiency, and 34.3% improvement over SOTA FPGA accelerators.

## Key Contributions

- **Inter-Tile Parallelism**: First to exploit parallelism across tiles while respecting sparse dependencies
- **Dependency Graph Parsing**: Fine-grained construction enabling effective parallelization
- **Communication Efficiency**: Bidirectional matching with modular indexing minimizing overhead
- **Strong Performance**: 3.58x vs. GPU, 9.59x energy efficiency vs. GPU, 34.3% vs. SOTA

## Technical Focus

Addresses data dependency challenge in triangular solves through careful dependency graph analysis and tile-based scheduling. Filtering mechanisms and bidirectional matching optimize communication in multi-tile execution.
