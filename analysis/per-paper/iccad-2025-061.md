# Quantum State Preparation Based on LimTDD

**Venue:** ICCAD

## Problem
Efficient quantum state preparation for complex quantum states requires better representations and circuit implementations.

## Motivation
Rapid advancement in quantum technologies demands efficient state preparation methods to improve quantum computing practicality.

## Method
Local Invertible Map Tensor Decision Diagram (LimTDD) combines tensor networks with decision diagrams to enable efficient representation and manipulation of quantum states through optimized data structure design.

## Key Novelty
LimTDD representation combining tensor networks and decision diagrams for efficient quantum state representation.

## Contributions
- Exponential efficiency gains over existing methods in best-case scenarios
- Reduced quantum circuit complexity
- Efficient representation of complex quantum states
- Theoretical and practical foundation for quantum computing
