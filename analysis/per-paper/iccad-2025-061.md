# Quantum State Preparation Based on LimTDD

**Venue:** ICCAD

## Problem
Efficient quantum state preparation for complex quantum states requires better representations and circuit implementations.

## Motivation
Rapid advancement in quantum technologies demands efficient state preparation methods to improve quantum computing practicality.

## Method
LimTDD works by splitting quantum states into smaller, connected pieces using tensor networks—a mathematical way to break large states into factors. These pieces are then stored using decision diagrams, a tree-like structure that compactly represents them by skipping redundant branches. Because quantum states grow exponentially in size, this compact factorization allows the method to represent and work with states that would otherwise be too large to fit in memory.

## Key Novelty
Combining tensor networks with decision diagrams creates a more compact representation for quantum states.
