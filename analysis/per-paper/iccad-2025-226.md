# P2P-Chiplet: Partition and Placement Co-Optimization for Multi-Chiplet Architecture

**Paper ID:** iccad-2025-226  
**Venue:** ICCAD

## Problem

Multi-chiplet designs require optimizing partition (block-to-die assignment) and placement sequentially, but this dependency creates partition-placement inconsistency where solutions optimal in one phase may yield inferior overall results. Existing frameworks fail to harness co-optimization benefits.

## Motivation

Modern cost pressures prevent large monolithic SoCs from following Moore's Law, requiring multi-chiplet designs where partition and placement decisions must be jointly optimized to realize the full benefit of chiplet disaggregation.

## Method

Introduces HeteroACG, an optimized Abstract Constraint Graph structure that aggregates topological partition and physical placement optimization spaces. Decomposes sequential partition-placement flow into interleaved fine-grained epochs and employs alternating progressive optimization strategy to maintain partition-placement consistency. Uses progressive epoch updates to refine solutions iteratively.

## Key Novelty

First partition-placement co-optimization framework addressing the partition-placement inconsistency problem through interleaved fine-grained epochs and HeteroACG structure.

## Contributions

- HeteroACG structure enabling unified optimization of partition and placement decisions
- Alternating progressive optimization strategy preserving partition-placement consistency
- Demonstrates cost reduction and performance improvement over sequential approaches
