# BITLUME: Precision-Flexible Photonic Computing for Ultra-Fast and Energy-Efficient DNN Acceleration

**Paper ID:** iccad-2025-230  
**Venue:** ICCAD

## Problem

Silicon photonics for deep learning acceleration faces precision challenges due to noise and non-idealities, limiting practical deployment for high-precision computations. Electronic accelerators are reaching performance and energy limits for emerging applications.

## Motivation

Photonics offers superior bandwidth and energy efficiency for neural network acceleration, but precision limitations prevent adoption for workloads requiring >8-bit precision.

## Method

BITLUME is a photonic computing unit (a processor that uses light instead of electricity) that can perform multiplications with more than 8-bit precision, overcoming a key limitation of typical light-based systems. To achieve this, it uses a precision-flexible design—meaning you can adjust how precise each calculation needs to be—combined with an optimized round-truncation algorithm that simplifies numbers while keeping them accurate. The system also employs a data-mapping strategy that reduces conversions between light and electrical signals and maximizes data reuse. It integrates photonic components with programmable electronics (FPGA) and RF components into a hybrid architecture.

## Key Novelty

BITLUME is the first photonic processor that adjusts precision for neural network operations, achieving 3.7× faster dot-product calculations (the fundamental operation in neural networks) than an A100 GPU.

## Contributions

- Precision-flexible photonic computing unit supporting variable-precision multiplications
- Round-truncation algorithm optimizing optoelectronic conversions and data reuse
- Hybrid optoelectronic architecture with up to 3.35× speedup and 1.53× energy savings vs. photonic accelerators, and 10.78× speedup, 4.12× energy savings vs. A100 GPU
