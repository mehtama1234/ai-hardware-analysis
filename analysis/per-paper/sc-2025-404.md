# Kilometer-Scale AI-Powered and Performance-Portable Earth System Model (AP3ESM) to Achieve Year-Scale Simulation Speed on Heterogeneous Supercomputers

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-404
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Ultra-high-resolution Earth system models (ESMs) at kilometer scale require exascale resources; achieving performance portability across heterogeneous architectures is challenging.

## Motivation
Climate and weather simulation at breakthrough resolution enables realistic forecasting; AI-enhanced parameterizations and mixed-precision boost efficiency.

## Method
AP3ESM: Kokkos/OpenMP performance-portable implementation with AI-enhanced physical parameterizations, adaptive algorithms, and mixed-precision on Sunway and GPU systems.

## Key Novelty
First kilometer-scale ESM delivering year-scale simulation speed (0.85-0.54 SYPD); captures Super Typhoon Doksuri with extreme rainfall prediction.

## Contributions
1. Breakthrough kilometer resolution Earth system model
2. Year-scale simulation speed on 37.2M Sunway cores (0.54 SYPD holistic)
3. AI-enhanced physical parameterizations
4. Successful extreme weather event prediction (Super Typhoon Doksuri)
