# PARO: Hardware-Software Co-design with Pattern-aware Reorder-based Attention Quantization in Video Generation Models

**Venue:** DAC
**ID:** dac-2025-386

## Problem
3D full attention in video generation models creates massive attention maps (56.50 GB for CogVideoX-5B) consuming excessive computation time (1 min per 49-frame video on A100).

## Motivation
Video generation with transformers dominates inference cost; attention quantization requires careful pattern handling to preserve quality.

## Method
PARO investigates diverse 3D full attention patterns and proposes reorder technique unifying them into block-diagonal structure. Block-wise mixed-precision quantization achieves lossless compression at 4.80-bit average. Output-bitwidth aware PE array co-design optimizes mixed-precision QK multiplication.

## Key Novelty
Pattern-aware reorder technique unifying diverse attention patterns into block-diagonal structure enabling efficient mixed-precision quantization.

## Contributions
1. Analysis of diverse 3D full attention patterns in video generation
2. Reorder technique unifying patterns into block-diagonal structure
3. Block-wise mixed-precision quantization at 4.80-bit average for lossless compression
4. Output-bitwidth aware PE array enabling full mixed-precision utilization
5. 2.71x speedup vs NVIDIA A100, 6.38-7.05x over SOTA ASIC accelerators
