# ArbiterQ: Improving QNN Convergency and Accuracy by Applying Personalized Model on Heterogeneous Quantum Devices

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Distributed QNN training on heterogeneous NISQ devices suffers from device noise, limited qubits, and online/offline transitions; naïve approaches degrade accuracy.

## Key Idea
Personalize QNN models per QPU using behavioral vectors (hardware features) and share gradients among similar-behavior devices with fine-grained shot scheduling.

## Method
- Model vector: logical-level parameters
- Behavioral vector: hardware-level features
- Gradient sharing among similar behavioral QPUs
- Shot-oriented inference scheduling

## Contributions
1. Dual representation (model + behavioral vector) for device personalization
2. Gradient sharing for heterogeneous parallelism
3. Fine-grained shot-oriented scheduling
4. 4.03x speedup with 7.87% loss reduction vs. EQC

## Technique
Scheduling, parallelism
