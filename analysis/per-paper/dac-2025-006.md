# Late Breaking Results: Encoder-Decoder Generative Diffusion Transformer Towards Push-Button Analog IC Sizing

**Problem:** Time-consuming optimization methods for analog IC sizing lack generalization to unseen performance targets.

**Method:** The system uses an encoder-decoder diffusion transformer—an AI model with attention mechanisms that generates solutions by iteratively refining random noise. During training, it learns the relationship between circuit performance targets (like desired power and speed) and the device sizes needed to achieve them. Given new performance targets, the model generates IC sizing solutions directly, without expensive optimization loops. This produces accurate results in seconds and generalizes to designs not seen during training.

**Key Contribution:** Generative model approach enables fast analog sizing with superior generalization beyond training targets.

**Results:** Outperforms recent deep learning-based sizing techniques.
