# ISSCC 2025-198: Cryo-BiCMOS Controller for Trapped-Ion Quantum Computers

## Core Contribution
Integrated cryo-BiCMOS (bipolar + CMOS) controller operating at 4K for quantum-charge-coupled-device (QCCD) trapped-ion quantum computers, enabling scalable multi-qubit systems with high fidelity.

## Technical Approach
- **Technology**: BiCMOS (combines bipolar's superior cryo performance with CMOS integration)
- **Operating Temp**: 4K (moderate cryogenic, simpler than mK systems)
- **Architecture**: QCCD-compatible control for ion trapping, shuttling, and two-qubit entanglement
- **Qubit Platform**: 9Be+ ions with long coherence times and high gate fidelities

## Performance Metrics
- **Single-Qubit Error**: ~10^-6 (state-of-art for trapped ions)
- **Two-Qubit Error**: ~10^-4 (approaching fault-tolerance threshold of 10^-4)
- **Coherence Time T2**: Up to hours
- **Excited-State Lifetime T1**: Nearly infinite
- **State Prep & Measurement Fidelity**: >99.5%

## Baselines
- Room-temperature control electronics (high latency, qubit coherence loss)
- Previous cryo controllers without full QCCD support or integration

## Hardware Impact
Enables practical scaling of trapped-ion quantum computers from tens to hundreds of qubits via QCCD architecture, with on-chip control reducing system complexity and improving coherence maintenance.
