# dac-2025-075: High-Performance Computing Architecture Exploration with Stage-Enhanced Bayesian Optimization

**Problem:** Automated design space exploration for complex HPC processor architectures faces combinatorial explosion and time-consuming simulation bottlenecks.

**Method:** Bayesian optimization learns from each processor design test to predict which untested configurations look promising—avoiding wasteful random exploration. This work improves three stages of that process: initial sampling (strategically selecting starting configurations), surrogate model construction (building a fast approximate model of expensive simulation), and acquisition function design (deciding which designs to test next). By improving all three stages, the method navigates the massive design space efficiently and identifies Pareto-optimal Arm Neoverse V1 configurations that balance power, performance, and physical area.

**Key Novelty:** Stage-enhanced Bayesian optimization that systematically improves all three optimization stages.

**Hardware Target:** CPU  
**Technique Category:** compiler, scheduling  
**Workloads:** HPC  

**Metrics:** Pareto-optimal design quality and diversity improvement

**Confidence:** low (abstract only)
