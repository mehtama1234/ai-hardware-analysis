# SiST: Token Similarity and Sparsity Aware Optimization for Transformers on FPGA

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**Paper ID:** iccad-2025-260

## Problem Statement
Transformer inference on resource-constrained FPGAs is expensive due to high computational cost in QKV generation, attention, and FFN modules.

## Motivation
Prior FPGA work focuses on attention computation, but QKV generation and FFN typically dominate overall cost; sparsity and token patterns offer optimization opportunities.

## Method
SiST combines cross-stage sparsity prediction before QKV generation with token similarity-based clustering in FFN and weight sparsity exploitation, supported by hardware architecture handling unstructured sparsity.

## Key Novelty
Holistic algorithm-architecture co-design exploiting both token similarity and sparsity across all three transformer modules on FPGA.

## Contributions

1. 45.9–52.2% computational overhead reduction
2. 2.35–8.44× speedup
3. 1.50–14.09× throughput improvement
4. 2.43–3.44× energy efficiency gain
