# ISSCC 2025-203: Cryo-CMOS Ultra-Low-Capacitance SAR ADC for Quantum Readout

## Core Contribution
7-bit charge-injection SAR ADC with record 4fF input capacitance operating at 6.5K, drastically reducing quantum qubit readout driver requirements and overall system power.

## Technical Approach
- **DAC**: Charge-injection (CI) topology maximally exploiting cryo-CMOS low kT/C noise environment
- **Input Network**: 4fF total capacitance (vs. typical 50–100fF SAR designs)
- **Sampling Rate**: 800MS/s
- **Resolution**: 7-bit
- **Temperature**: 6.5K (dilution-refrigerator compatible)

## Performance Metrics
- **Input Capacitance**: 4fF (record low, ~10x reduction vs. conventional SAR)
- **SFDR**: 64.1dB (excellent linearity for dispersive qubit readout)
- **SNDR**: 40.7dB
- **Power**: 2.89mW at 800MS/s (ADC proper; doesn't include driver power but enables major driver reduction)
- **Technology**: 40nm bulk CMOS

## Baselines
- Prior cryo-CMOS SAR ADCs (1.8–10mW with higher input impedance)
- Room-temperature SAR ADCs (kT/C noise prevents ultra-low capacitance)
- Cryo-CMOS LNA + mixer + SAR systems (currently dominate readout power budget)

## Hardware Impact
Addresses critical bottleneck in quantum computing: readout circuit power per qubit. By minimizing ADC input capacitance, associated LNA/mixer driver power drops significantly—essential for scaling cryogenic quantum processors to 1000+ qubits.
