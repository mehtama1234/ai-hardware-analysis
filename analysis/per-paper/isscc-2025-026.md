# isscc-2025-026: Linear Optical Driver with Integrated 5-Tap FFE

**A 100Gbaud 4Vppd Distributed Linear Driver with Cross-Folded Transmission Lines and Cross-Coupled Gm Cells**

## Summary
100 Gbaud linear pluggable optics (LPO) driver providing 4 Vppd output swing with integrated 5-tap feed-forward equalization using cross-folded transmission-line (TL) distributed architecture.

## Technical Approach
- **Distributed topology**: Cross-folded TLs and cross-coupled Gm cells for high gain and large swing
- **FFE integration**: TL delay-lines implementing 5 programmable equalization taps
- **Performance**: Simultaneous high gain (low loss) and large output swing
- **Technology**: 0.13 µm SiGe BiCMOS

## Key Innovation
Cross-folded TL-based distributed driver architecture achieving both high gain and large swing with integrated FFE—previously infeasible due to fundamental tradeoffs.

## Contributions
- Cross-folded TL distributed topology
- Cross-coupled Gm cells optimizing gain-swing tradeoff
- Integrated 5-tap FFE without external DSP
- 100 Gbaud LPO demonstration

## Target Workloads
Linear optical communications, short-reach fiber optics, LPO interconnects
