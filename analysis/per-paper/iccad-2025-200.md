# VeriSAT: the Hardware Design of Modern SAT Solver

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-200

## Problem
Traditional SAT solvers execute in software; hardware-accelerated approaches lack optimization for concurrent operations and efficient data structures.

## Motivation


## Method
First modern SAT solver in synthesizable SystemVerilog with hardware-optimized data structures: linked-list literal-watching, concurrent propagation tree, and pipelined clause learning.

## Key Novelty
First fully-synthesizable SystemVerilog SAT solver with custom hardware-optimized data structures and parallelized processes.

## Contributions
- Hardware-optimized linked-list literal-watching with cached literals
- Concurrent propagation tree for faster conflict detection
- Pipelined clause learning for high throughput
- 1044x speedup over SAT-Hard, 18x over SAT-Accel, 30x over MiniSat
