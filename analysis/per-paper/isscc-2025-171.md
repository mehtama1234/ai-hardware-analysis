# isscc-2025-171: Injection-Locked Quadrature-Clock Generator for HBM

**Title:** 22.2: An 850µW 2-to-5GHz Jitter-Filtering and Instant-Toggling Injection-Locked Quadrature-Clock Generator for Low-Power Clock Distribution in HBM Interfaces

**Problem:** HBM clock distribution requires parallel quadrature-clock routing with high power, quadrature error, and power-supply-induced jitter.

**Approach:** Injection-locked quadrature-clock generator with jitter filtering and instant-toggling, distributed locally across DQ groups.

**Key Result:** 850µW total power, eliminates separate QE-correctors, supports HBM4 scalability.

**Hardware:** ASIC (HBM clock circuit).

**Tags:** hbm, clock-distribution, injection-locked, quadrature-clock, jitter-filtering, power-efficient
