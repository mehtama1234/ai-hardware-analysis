# Harrow: Synthesis of Optical Logic Circuits via Harmonic Mean and Integer Partition

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only analysis)

## Problem
Optical signal attenuation in photonic integrated circuits causes energy loss and requires unnecessary optical-electrical conversions during logic synthesis.

## Method
Harrow designs optical logic circuits starting with a binary decision diagram (a tree-like representation of logic operations) and then optimizes optical combiners—devices that merge light signals. It improves how efficiently these combiners preserve signal strength using harmonic mean optimization (a mathematical method that finds the best balance between multiple objectives). It also removes redundant combiners using integer partition (a technique for systematically grouping components to identify and eliminate duplicates). Finally, it arranges these optimizations in a sequence that produces the best overall circuit performance.

## Key Novelty
Harrow combines harmonic mean optimization of DC combiners (devices that merge light signals) with integer partition-based redundancy elimination to improve signal efficiency in optical logic circuits.
