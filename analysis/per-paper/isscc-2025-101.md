# isscc-2025-101: Configurable Gamma Spectrometer for Surgery

## Summary
3x3.3mm 180nm CMOS configurable gamma photon spectrometer with per-pixel adaptation and energy-resolving capability for precision radioguided cancer resection.

## Key Contributions
- mm-scale pixelated single-gamma detector
- Per-pixel configurability (flux/PVT)
- Energy-resolving scheme
- Depth encoding via gamma energy profiles

## Hardware Target
ASIC

## Technique Category
Circuit-design, Sensor

## Workloads
None (medical)

## Baselines
State-of-the-art gamma probes, X-ray dosimetry systems
