# isscc-2025-106: DustNet—Ultrasonic Implant Network with TDM and Backscatter

**Problem:** Multi-implant neural recording networks require 200-400kb/s aggregate data rate from 4-8 implants at cm-scale depths with minimal infection risk.

**Method:** DustNet uses ultrasound—sound frequencies around 1-9 MHz that pass through body tissue without getting absorbed—to send data to and from tiny implants placed several centimeters deep. Each implant takes its turn transmitting (time-division multiplexing) so they don't interfere with each other. When receiving, implants send data back by reflecting the ultrasound at one of 16 different signal strengths (16-level ASK backscatter modulation), allowing each implant to transmit at 50kb/s.

**Key Novelty:** TDM ultrasonic architecture with multi-level backscatter achieving clinically relevant depths and aggregate data rates.

**Main Results:** 50kb/s per implant, 200-400kb/s aggregate, several cm implant depth.

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only analysis)
