# DAO-Stabilized Always-On MEMS Gyroscope

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**ID:** isscc-2025-253

## Problem
MEMS gyroscopes for event-driven wearables must minimize always-on current for battery life while achieving fast wake-up times. Burst-mode PLL drive loops suffer parasitic-relaxation-oscillation (PRO) instability from temperature variations.

## Solution
Duty-cycle-automatic-optimization (DAO) technique adaptively adjusts drive-loop duty cycle based on temperature to suppress parasitic-relaxation-oscillation. Enables 1.5µW always-on power with stable 2.8ms wake-up time across -40 to +85°C.

## Key Innovation
DAO technique suppressing parasitic-relaxation-oscillation for reliable, efficient always-on MEMS drive loops.

## Results
- **Wake-up time:** 2.8 ms (1.9× faster than prior 5.45ms)
- **Always-on power:** 1.5 µW
- **Temperature stability:** ±5% from -40 to +85°C
- **Improvement:** Eliminates PRO instability

## Hardware Target
ASIC, SoC

## Techniques
Circuit design, power efficiency, thermal compensation
