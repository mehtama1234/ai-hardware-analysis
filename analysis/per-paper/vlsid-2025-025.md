# K Band High Power Broadband AlGaN/GaN HEMT Balanced Power Amplifier for Satellite Transponder

**Venue:** VLSID

## Problem
K-band satellite transponders require high-power, high-efficiency broadband power amplifiers

## Motivation
Satellite communication demands integrate power/efficiency/bandwidth; GaN HEMT enables superior performance

## Method
The amplifier splits the input signal into two parallel paths and amplifies each using AlGaN/GaN HEMT transistors (gallium nitride devices that efficiently handle high power). When the amplified signals are recombined, this balanced configuration cancels out distortion that would otherwise occur, resulting in cleaner output and higher efficiency. To manage the heat from high-power operation and support high-frequency performance, all components are integrated on a single silicon carbide (SiC) substrate using UMS's 0.15-micrometer manufacturing process, which creates transistor gates small enough to operate at the required frequencies.

## Key Novelty
This balanced power amplifier design delivers 38dBm (about 6 watts) of output power with 32% power-added efficiency (the portion of input power converted to useful output) and 23.6dB gain (amplification factor) across the 18-22 GHz K-band frequency range.
