# isscc-2025-235: Doherty-Modulated mm-Wave LNA for Blocker Tolerance

**Problem:** Emerging mm-wave applications (massive MIMO, automotive radar, SATCOM, AR/VR) require high-linearity receivers handling multiple concurrent signals or weak signals amid strong interferers. Conventional LNAs optimize noise but fail under blocker linearity stress.

**Approach:** mm-Wave LNA using Doherty active load modulation to enhance linearity while maintaining low noise, dynamically adjusting gain-vs-linearity trade-off under strong blockers.

**Key novelty:** Doherty active load modulation applied to mm-wave LNA enabling simultaneous high linearity and low noise.

**Applications:** Massive MIMO, automotive radar, SATCOM-on-the-Move, AR/VR.

**Tags:** lna, mmwave, linearity, doherty, blocker-tolerant, receiver
