# Energon: Unveiling Transformers from GPU Power and Thermal Side-Channels

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-015

## Problem
GPU power and thermal fluctuations leak architectural details of transformer models deployed in MLaaS environments, enabling extraction of layer counts and attention head information without physical access.

## Motivation
Security of proprietary transformer models in shared GPU deployments is critical as side-channel attacks remain largely unexplored.

## Method
The attack monitors how much electrical power the GPU draws and how hot it gets while running the transformer model—these fluctuate naturally as different layers execute. Without needing special admin permissions, the attacker analyzes the statistical patterns in these power and temperature readings to map out the model's structure. Different transformer configurations (with varying numbers of layers and attention heads) produce distinct power signatures, so the architectural details can be reverse-engineered from these leaked power patterns.

## Key Novelty
This demonstrates that GPU power and temperature readings can reveal a transformer's architecture details without needing special access privileges.

## Contributions
- Novel power/thermal side-channel analysis against transformers
- Extraction of encoder/decoder layer and attention head information
- Black-box adversarial attack achieving >93% success rate using extracted architecture
- Evaluation across multiple public language and vision transformer models
