# Energon: Unveiling Transformers from GPU Power and Thermal Side-Channels

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-015

## Problem
GPU power and thermal fluctuations leak architectural details of transformer models deployed in MLaaS environments, enabling extraction of layer counts and attention head information without physical access.

## Motivation
Security of proprietary transformer models in shared GPU deployments is critical as side-channel attacks remain largely unexplored.

## Method
Exploits temporal variations in GPU power consumption and thermal output via unprivileged access to extract architectural parameters; analyzes encoder/decoder layers and attention head counts through statistical power signatures.

## Key Novelty
First demonstration of GPU power and thermal side-channels to reverse-engineer transformer architecture details at user privilege level.

## Contributions
- Novel power/thermal side-channel analysis against transformers
- Extraction of encoder/decoder layer and attention head information
- Black-box adversarial attack achieving >93% success rate using extracted architecture
- Evaluation across multiple public language and vision transformer models
