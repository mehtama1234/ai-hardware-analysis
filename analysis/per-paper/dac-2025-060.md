# ChipAlign: Instruction Alignment in Large Language Models for Chip Design via Geodesic Interpolation

**Venue:** DAC | **Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Chip-specific LLMs like ChipNeMo lack instruction alignment capability, limiting their ability to follow explicit human directives and serve as practical assistant chatbots for hardware design engineers.

## Motivation
Combining general instruction-aligned LLMs with domain-specific chip expertise addresses a critical practical bottleneck in deploying LLMs for hardware design automation.

## Method
ChipAlign uses geodesic interpolation in weight space to merge instruction-aligned and chip-specific LLMs without training, fusing weights along the manifold to produce a model that inherits both strong instruction following and chip domain knowledge.

## Key Novelty
Training-free model merging via geodesic interpolation on weight manifolds to simultaneously achieve instruction alignment and domain expertise.

## Contributions
- Novel geodesic interpolation strategy for weight-space model merging without training
- 26.6% improvement on IFEval instruction-following benchmark for chip LLMs
- 3.9% improvement on OpenROAD QA and 8.25% on production-level chip QA benchmarks

## Metrics
- Instruction following: 26.6% IFEval improvement
- QA performance: 3.9% OpenROAD, 8.25% production-level

## Baselines
- ChipNeMo, OpenROAD QA baseline, state-of-the-art chip LLMs

## Limitations
Evaluation limited to QA and instruction-following benchmarks without full design flow integration.
