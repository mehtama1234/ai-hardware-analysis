# RSizing: Robust Bayesian Optimization for Analog Circuit Sizing Under Process Variations

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-211  
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Analog circuit sizing must account for process variations that affect device performance and reliability.

## Motivation
Robust analog circuit design accounting for process variations is essential for yield and reliability in advanced technology nodes.

## Method
The approach uses a statistical model—heteroscedastic Gaussian processes, which learns when predictions are more or less reliable depending on operating conditions—to capture how manufacturing variations affect circuit behavior. Bayesian optimization then iteratively searches for the best circuit design by testing candidates and learning what to try next across three refinement phases. The method incorporates the learned uncertainty directly into the optimization goal, ensuring final designs work reliably even when manufacturing causes component values to vary.

## Key Novelty
This is the first application of heteroscedastic Gaussian processes (a statistical technique that learns varying levels of prediction confidence) within Bayesian optimization to design analog circuits that stay robust to manufacturing variation.

## Contributions

1. Heteroscedastic GP model for process variation uncertainty
2. Three-phase Bayesian optimization strategy
3. Robust sizing framework accounting for process variations
