# Late Breaking Results: Practical Electromagnetic Fault Injection on Intel Neural Compute Stick 2

**Venue**: DATE  
**Primary Theme**: physical fault injection vulnerability in edge ML accelerators  

## Problem
Edge ML devices deployed in critical IoT infrastructure lack empirical evaluation of physical fault injection vulnerability; security posture of real edge accelerators undefined.

## Method
The researchers used low-cost pulse generator hardware to send electromagnetic pulses (bursts of electromagnetic energy) at an Intel Neural Compute Stick 2, a small AI chip used in edge devices. By systematically testing different pulse configurations—varying things like timing, location, and intensity—they found ways to make the AI classifier output wrong answers, and demonstrated these attacks could be repeated reliably.

## Key Novelty
They demonstrated the first practical, repeatable electromagnetic pulse attacks that make a real Intel Neural Compute Stick 2 AI chip misclassify data.

## Contributions
- Practical EMFI attack methodology on commercial edge ML device (NCS2)
- Low-cost attack setup achieving repeatable misclassifications
- Empirical characterization of fault parameter space for edge ML inference
