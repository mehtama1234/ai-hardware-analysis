# Optimized Trajectory Quantum Simulation

## Problem
Classical simulation of noisy quantum systems requires exponential overhead (2^n x 2^n density matrices). Trajectory methods reduce this but waste compute through redundant operations.

## Approach
Pre-Trajectory Sampling combines:
1. Error type tailoring to match experimental conditions
2. Batch sampling without redundant computation
3. Efficient error information collection

## Results
- **35-qubit QEC**: up to 106x speedup + 1 trillion shot dataset
- **85-qubit**: 16x speedup + 1 million shot dataset

## Significance
Enables large-scale noisy quantum simulation for validating quantum error correction codes. Massive generated datasets useful for training classical models of quantum behavior.

## Impact
Demonstrates that algorithmic optimization can yield orders-of-magnitude improvements in quantum simulation efficiency. Critical for near-term NISQ device validation.
