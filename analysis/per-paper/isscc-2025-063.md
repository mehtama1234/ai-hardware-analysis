# isscc-2025-063

**Title:** 23.5 MAE: A 3nm 0.168mm2 576MAC Mini AutoEncoder with Line-Based Depth-First Scheduling for Generative AI in Vision on Edge Devices

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Problem:** Diffusion-based generative models on edge devices face DRAM bandwidth bottlenecks (40–80% energy cost) and temporal under-utilization of limited MACs.

**Key Contribution:** Line-based depth-first fusion + H-reuse caching reduces DRAM access; global scheduling achieves 84.5% utilization in 0.168 mm².

**Method:** Line-based depth-first fusion, local H-reuse caching, direct-link bypass, scheduling optimization framework.

**Hardware:** NPU, ASIC

**Metrics:** 576 MACs, 12.38 TOPS/W, 84.5% utilization, 0.168 mm²

**Workloads:** Diffusion, vision (autoencoders)

**Techniques:** Dataflow, memory-system, kernel-fusion, scheduling

**Primary Theme:** Domain-specific NPU for edge generative vision
