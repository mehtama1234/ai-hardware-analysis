# asplos-2025-099: The ASPLOS 2025 / EuroSys 2025 Contest on Intra-Operator Parallelism for Distributed Deep Learning

**Venue:** ASPLOS  
**Confidence:** low (abstract only)

## Problem
Selecting the optimal intra-operator parallelism strategy — how to partition and distribute each neural network operation across multiple accelerators — is a hard combinatorial optimization problem that significantly impacts distributed training and inference performance.

## Motivation
As models grow larger and clusters more heterogeneous, automatic parallelism strategy search becomes critical to unlocking maximum hardware utilization; no single prior method dominates across diverse architectures.

## Method
The contest provided a benchmark suite of neural network operations and asked participants to submit model partitioning algorithms to minimize execution time. The paper retrospectively analyzes the problem formulation, benchmark design, key algorithmic challenges (combinatorial explosion of partitioning strategies), and the quality of submissions received over a multi-month competition.

## Key Novelty
A structured community-wide competition and new benchmark suite that exposes the state-of-the-art gap in intra-operator parallelism strategy search.

## Contributions
- Formal problem formulation for intra-operator parallelism strategy selection.
- A new benchmark suite covering diverse neural network operator types and cluster configurations.
- Retrospective analysis of competition submissions revealing current state-of-the-art and open challenges.
- Identification of key algorithmic opportunities for improving model partitioning.
