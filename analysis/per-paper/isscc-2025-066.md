# isscc-2025-066

**Title:** 21.9 A 20MHz & 1MHz Dual-Loop Non-Uniform-Multi-Inductor Hybrid DC-DC Converter with Specified Inductor Current Allocation and Fast Transient Response

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Problem:** Series-capacitor buck converters achieve high efficiency but suffer slow transient response due to low loop bandwidth limiting inductor slew rates; load steps show poor voltage regulation.

**Key Contribution:** Dual-loop control (20 MHz fast + 1 MHz slow) + specified current allocation achieves 2x load step slew rate improvement vs. prior designs.

**Method:** Dual-loop architecture, non-uniform multi-inductor specification, optimized current allocation strategy.

**Hardware:** ASIC power converter

**Techniques:** Power delivery, circuit design, control

**Primary Theme:** Fast transient multi-inductor buck hybrid DC-DC
