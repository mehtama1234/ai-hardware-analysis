# Designing and Training Neural Networks for Analog In-Sensor Deployment: A Hardware-Aware Analysis

**Venue:** ICCAD

## Problem
Deploying CNNs on analog in-sensor hardware faces power, area, and latency constraints alongside noise, quantization errors, and lack of digital ISP preprocessing.

## Motivation
Edge AI and IoT require ultra-low latency and energy efficiency by processing directly in analog domain to minimize data movement.

## Method
Hardware-aware analysis deriving design recommendations for AIS-optimized CNNs including network topology guidelines, training recipes for noise/quantization robustness, and strategies for processing raw unrefined sensor data.

## Key Novelty
Hardware-aware design guidance specifically optimized for analog in-sensor CNN deployment addressing noise and raw-data challenges.

## Contributions
- Design recommendations for AIS-friendly network topologies
- Training recipes improving noise and quantization robustness
- Strategies for learning from raw sensor data without digital ISP
- Complementary software-based techniques to circuit and architecture approaches
