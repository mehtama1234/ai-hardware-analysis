# isscc-2025-098: PAM-4 VCSEL Direct-Drive Optical Engine

## Summary
0.9pJ/b 108Gb/s PAM-4 VCSEL-based direct-drive optical engine for co-packaged optics with complex-zero equalizer and high-linearity TIA, eliminating DSP re-timing.

## Key Contributions
- Complex-zero CTIE equalization for VCSEL
- High-linearity transimpedance amplifier
- Direct-drive without re-timer
- 0.9pJ/b energy efficiency
