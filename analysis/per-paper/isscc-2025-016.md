# isscc-2025-016: Low Phase-Noise Quad-Core Class-F₂₃ Oscillator

**An 18.5-to-23.6GHz Quad-Core Class-F23 Oscillator Without 2nd/3rd Harmonic Tuning**

## Summary
First quad-core Class-F₂₃ oscillator eliminating harmonic tuning penalty, achieving -193 dBc/Hz peak FoM and 1/f³ PN corner that remains constant (140–250 kHz) across 5.1 GHz tuning range.

## Technical Approach
- **Topology**: Circular-connected quad-core VCO with transformer-based resonant tank
- **Harmonic alignment**: Automatic via F-class intrinsic harmonic properties, no switched-capacitor arrays
- **Q-factor**: Circular inductors achieve higher Q than star-connected inductor sets at mm-wave

## Key Innovation
Elimination of explicit 2nd/3rd harmonic tuning structures by leveraging Class-F₂₃ intrinsic harmonic shaping, reducing Q-factor degradation across full tuning range.

## Contributions
- 10 dB FoM improvement over prior work
- Wide tuning without harmonic-alignment penalty
- Stable 1/f³ PN corner across frequency range

## Target Workloads
5G/6G frequency synthesis, high-speed transceivers, communications

## Limitations
- Absolute phase noise at various offsets not specified
- Area and power consumption not detailed
