# isscc-2025-004: 16-QAM Intradyne Coherent Optical Receiver

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)

## Problem
16-QAM coherent detection doubles data rate vs. QPSK but requires high-linearity analog signal processing and carrier phase recovery (CPR) while maintaining low power—existing DSP-heavy approaches consume too much power for short-reach links.

## Approach
28nm CMOS **intradyne coherent receiver** with:
- Novel analog **phase detector** for robust 16-QAM carrier phase recovery
- Low-complexity design avoiding high-power digital DSP
- High-linearity analog front-end

## Key Contribution
Novel analog CPR phase detector enables efficient 16-QAM reception (1.54pJ/b) without DSP overhead.

## Performance
- Energy: 1.54 pJ/b
- Data rate: 64 Gb/s
- Modulation: 16-QAM (2× QPSK capacity)
- Technology: 28nm CMOS
