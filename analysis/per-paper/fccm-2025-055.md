# HP-FFT: A General High-Performance FFT Generator Using High-Level Synthesis

**Problem:** Existing FFT HLS IP libraries suffer from low throughput and poor usability due to inadequate parallelism exploitation, while high-throughput RTL FFT designs lack portability and flexibility.

**Method:** The paper analyzes FFT algorithm loop structure to uncover hierarchical parallelism opportunities. HP-FFT is developed as a general HLS generator supporting multiple functionalities with customizable parallelism settings, enabling users to configure architectures balancing resource efficiency and throughput.

**Novelty:** Systematic exploitation of hierarchical parallelism in FFT loop structures through HLS-based generation to achieve both high performance and hardware portability.
