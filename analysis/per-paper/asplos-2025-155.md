# asplos-2025-155: Optimizing Quantum Circuits, Fast and Slow

**Venue:** ASPLOS  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Quantum circuit optimization separates fast rewrite-rule passes (local, low quality) from slow unitary synthesis (global, high quality, exponential cost) without a unified framework to exploit their synergies.

## Motivation
Minimizing gate count is critical for NISQ execution; existing optimizers either sacrifice quality or scalability, leaving a large optimization gap.

## Method
The guoq algorithm models rewriting and resynthesis as abstract circuit transformation operators. Fast rewrite passes simplify local patterns and identify high-gain subcircuits for targeted unitary synthesis; synthesis achieves global optima on bounded-size subcircuits that local rules cannot reach. Fast-slow interleaving exploits each approach's strengths.

## Key Novelty
Unified abstract circuit transformation framework composing fast rewrite rules and slow unitary synthesis as first-class operators, enabling synergistic optimization via the guoq algorithm.

## Contributions
- Unifying framework: rewriting and resynthesis as abstract circuit transformation operators
- guoq algorithm interleaving fast rewrite passes and synthesis on high-gain subcircuits
- Strong outperformance of existing quantum circuit optimizers across wide benchmarks
- Radically simple algorithm achieving superior optimization through fast-slow synergy
