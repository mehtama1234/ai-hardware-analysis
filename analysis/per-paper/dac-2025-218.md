# GTN-Path: Efficient Path Timing Prediction through Waveform Propagation with Graph Transformer

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
LUT-based STA loses fidelity vs. SPICE at advanced nodes; balancing accuracy/speed for sign-off timing is challenging.

## Key Insight
Graph transformer networks can model standard cells/interconnects and propagate waveforms more accurately than LUT-based STA while remaining fast.

## Method
The approach treats a circuit as a graph (network of connected logic components and wires) and uses a graph transformer network—an AI model trained to learn structural relationships—to predict how electrical signals propagate through it. It learns these predictions from accurate but expensive SPICE simulations, capturing waveforms (signal shapes over time) and timing delays. The model achieves ~3% error compared to SPICE while running 3510× faster.

## Contributions
- GTN for waveform-based timing prediction
- Graph-based cell/interconnect modeling
- 2.96–2.98% error vs. SPICE, 3510× speedup
- 12× speedup vs. commercial sign-off STA
