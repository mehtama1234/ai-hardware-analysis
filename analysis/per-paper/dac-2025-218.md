# GTN-Path: Efficient Path Timing Prediction through Waveform Propagation with Graph Transformer

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
LUT-based STA loses fidelity vs. SPICE at advanced nodes; balancing accuracy/speed for sign-off timing is challenging.

## Key Insight
Graph transformer networks can model standard cells/interconnects and propagate waveforms more accurately than LUT-based STA while remaining fast.

## Method
Graph Transformer Network predicts post-layout timing via waveform propagation; models cells/interconnects as graphs with transformer attention.

## Contributions
- GTN for waveform-based timing prediction
- Graph-based cell/interconnect modeling
- 2.96–2.98% error vs. SPICE, 3510× speedup
- 12× speedup vs. commercial sign-off STA

Graph transformer waveform propagation for accurate fast timing prediction.
