# NN-AdderNet: Nonnegative and Sparse Weight Optimization for Ultra-Low-Bitwidth AdderNet

**Problem:** AdderNet lacks effective quantization and compression strategies, limiting bitwidth reduction while maintaining accuracy.

**Method:** NN-AdderNet reformulates the sum-of-absolute-difference (SAD, a multiplication-free calculation) to make weights nonnegative, eliminating the sign bit per weight and saving 1 bit per weight. It identifies two complementary sparsity patterns in the quantized weights—two different arrangements of mostly-zero values—and uses lossless compression (reducing file size without data loss) with both patterns simultaneously. These changes together drastically reduce the bitwidth needed for ultra-low-bitwidth quantization while keeping the model accurate.

**Key Result:** Compressed weight bitwidth down to 4-bits or lower; negligible accuracy loss; demonstrates hardware energy and latency improvements.

**Hardware Target:** ASIC, FPGA  
**Workloads:** CNN, vision, LLM-inference
