# PICBench: Benchmarking LLMs for Photonic Integrated Circuits Design

**Venue**: DATE  
**Primary Theme**: LLM benchmarking for photonic circuit design  

## Problem
LLMs show promise for digital chip design automation but photonic integrated circuit design remains unexplored; PIC design is time-consuming and repetitive.

## Method
PICBench creates a test suite that measures how well AI language models can generate netlists—detailed specifications describing photonic integrated circuits (optical chips that process information using light). It validates the AI-generated designs by simulating them and comparing the results to expert-written solutions, checking both the correctness of the instructions and whether the circuits work as intended. The framework systematically tests different AI models and different ways of instructing them (prompt engineering strategies) to determine which approaches are most effective.

## Key Novelty
PICBench is the first systematic test and evaluation framework specifically designed for measuring how well AI language models can design photonic integrated circuits.

## Contributions
- PICBench benchmark with dozens of PIC design problems from device to circuit level
- Automatic evaluation via simulation comparison to expert solutions
- Comparative analysis of multiple LLMs for PIC design
- Evaluation of prompt engineering techniques for circuit generation
