# VLSID 2025-030: Wide Dynamic Range Differential Drive CMOS Rectifier

## Summary
Presents a fully self-biased differential drive rectifier for RF energy harvesting with a wide dynamic range. Achieves 60.5% peak PCE at -28.9 dBm with 14 dB DR (74% improvement over cross-coupled designs).

## Problem
RF energy harvesting requires rectifiers maintaining high power conversion efficiency across variable input power levels. Reverse conduction losses degrade efficiency at low input powers.

## Method
It uses a differential drive rectifier (comparing opposite-phase inputs) built in 65-nanometer CMOS technology. The circuit self-biases itself—automatically adjusting its internal voltage levels without needing external bias circuits—to minimize wasted power when current tries to flow backward (reverse conduction) while keeping normal power losses low. This automatic adaptation lets the rectifier maintain efficiency across a wide range of input power levels.

## Key Results
- **Peak PCE**: 60.5% at -28.9 dBm input power
- **Dynamic Range**: ~14 dB
- **Improvement**: 74% DR improvement vs conventional cross-coupled rectifier
- **Frequency**: 953 MHz

## Applications
- IoT and wearable devices
- Ultra-low power RF energy harvesting systems
- Battery-less wireless systems

## Limitations
- Simulation results only (post-layout)
- Single frequency evaluation (953 MHz)
- Fixed load impedance (10kΩ)
- No comparison with other wide-DR rectifier topologies
