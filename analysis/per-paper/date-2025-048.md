# Multi-Partner Project: A Model-Driven Engineering Framework for Federated Digital Twins of Industrial Systems (MATISSE)

**Venue**: DATE  
**Primary Theme**: model-driven framework for federated digital twins  

## Problem
Federation of multiple digital twins introduces complex challenges in design, development, analysis, deployment, execution and maintenance including identity management, metadata sharing, cross-DT communication/synchronization, and governance that existing single-DT approaches cannot address.

## Method
Proposes model-driven engineering framework leveraging traceability as core infrastructural service. Provides conceptual framework for continuous engineering of federated digital twins addressing DT identification, metadata management, cross-DT communication protocols, and federation governance. Demonstrates two concrete industrial scenarios.

## Key Novelty
Federated digital twin framework with integrated traceability and model-driven continuous engineering for coordination across multiple digital twin systems.

## Contributions
- MATISSE conceptual framework for federated digital twins with emphasis on traceability
- Research roadmap for continuous engineering, verification, validation, and monitoring services
- Two concrete industrial scenarios demonstrating practical application framework
