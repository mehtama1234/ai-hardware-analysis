# Multi-Partner Project: A Model-Driven Engineering Framework for Federated Digital Twins of Industrial Systems (MATISSE)

**Venue**: DATE  
**Primary Theme**: model-driven framework for federated digital twins  

## Problem
Federation of multiple digital twins introduces complex challenges in design, development, analysis, deployment, execution and maintenance including identity management, metadata sharing, cross-DT communication/synchronization, and governance that existing single-DT approaches cannot address.

## Method
The approach uses model-driven engineering (designing systems through machine-readable specifications) with traceability (tracking how information and changes flow) embedded as the core infrastructure. It creates a framework for coordinating multiple industrial digital twins by solving their main challenges: identifying which twin is which, managing shared information about the data (metadata), enabling direct communication between twins, and establishing rules for how they coordinate. The framework was validated on two real industrial scenarios.

## Key Novelty
The core innovation is using traceability—automatic tracking of information and changes—as the central mechanism for coordinating multiple industrial digital twins as a federation.

## Contributions
- MATISSE conceptual framework for federated digital twins with emphasis on traceability
- Research roadmap for continuous engineering, verification, validation, and monitoring services
- Two concrete industrial scenarios demonstrating practical application framework
