# isscc-2025-167: Physical Intelligence in Robotic Systems

**Title:** 1.2 From Chips to Thoughts: Building Physical Intelligence into Robotic Systems

**Problem:** Generative AI requires immense energy for training and deployment, limiting real-time autonomous systems despite revolutionary accessibility.

**Approach:** Integrate physical intelligence (perception + reasoning + control) through energy-efficient chip and system design for robotic platforms.

**Key Result:** Framework for deploying generative AI on edge robots with energy awareness.

**Hardware:** ASIC, SoC, FPGA (embedded robotic compute).

**Tags:** physical-intelligence, robotics, generative-ai, edge-computing, energy-efficiency, embodied-ai
