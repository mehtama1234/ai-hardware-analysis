# Molten Chloride Small Modular Reactor Performance Characteristics for Data Center Operation

## Overview
MOOSE-based simulation of molten chloride SMR behavior at partial load (control rods 60% open) to assess data center power delivery feasibility.

## Problem & Motivation
- **Challenge**: Data centers need high-reliability power; conventional large reactors impractical (footprint, cost). Molten chloride SMRs promoted for data center deployment but operational behavior at partial load unknown.
- **Opportunity**: Understand steady-state thermal-hydraulic dynamics below maximum capacity
- **Strategic Context**: Energy-intensive data centers seeking carbon-neutral power sources

## Architecture & Method
**Simulation Framework**
- MOOSE multiphysics simulation environment
- Molten chloride reactor model
- Coupled thermal, hydraulic, and neutronics physics

**Operating Condition**
- Control rods at 60% open (below-maximum capacity)
- Steady-state analysis
- Data center power matching scenario

## Key Contributions
1. **MOOSE-based simulation** of molten chloride SMR
2. **Steady-state characterization** at partial load
3. **Data center operational feasibility** insights

## Simulation Insights
- Molten chloride + uranium serves as both fuel and coolant (dual-purpose)
- Partial-load operation typical for data center matching scenarios
- Steady-state stability critical for reliable power supply

## Limitations
- **No performance metrics** reported (simulation approach paper, not results)
- **No comparison** with conventional reactors or other SMR designs
- **Data center load profile** details missing
- **Control dynamics** (transient response, ramp rates) not discussed
- **Economic/practical feasibility** not addressed
- Abstract-only prevents technical evaluation

## Relevance to Hardware/AI Systems
**Low-medium relevance**: Addresses data center power infrastructure rather than computing hardware or systems optimization. Relevant to understanding future data center energy constraints but not direct HPC/AI systems research.
