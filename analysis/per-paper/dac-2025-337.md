# NoiseZO: RRAM Noise-Driven Zeroth-Order Optimization for Efficient Forward-Only Training
**Venue:** DAC

## Problem
RRAM compute-in-memory (CIM) training requires gradient computation overhead; RRAM write noise complicates training convergence.

## Motivation
Energy-efficient DNN training on edge devices via CIM requires eliminating backpropagation and leveraging inherent hardware noise as an optimization primitive.

## Method
NoiseZO maps neural networks to dual RRAM arrays and exploits intrinsic RRAM write noise as zeroth-order perturbations for gradient-free training; fine-grained perturbation control enhances accuracy; training requires only two forward passes per update.

## Key Novelty
First framework turning RRAM write noise into a feature for zeroth-order gradient estimation during training.

## Contributions
1. Forward-only training eliminates backpropagation on RRAM CIM
2. 21x energy reduction vs. CMOS implementations
3. Perturbation control strategy for high training accuracy
