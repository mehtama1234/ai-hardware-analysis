# CVMAX: Accelerator Architecture with Polar Form Multiplication for Complex-Valued Neural Networks

**Authors:** Hyunwuk Lee, Sungbin Kim, Sungwoo Kim, Won Woo Ro  
**Venue:** DAC 2025

## Summary
CVMAX is a software-hardware co-design for energy and area-efficient Complex-Valued Neural Network (CVNN) acceleration. Using polar-form quantization with shift quantization and dedicated hardware, it achieves 75% energy reduction and 4.44x speedup versus conventional CVNN accelerators.

## Key Contributions
- Polar-form quantization reducing bit width and complexity
- Shift quantization exploiting polar multiplication efficiency
- Dedicated hardware accelerator for CVMAX operations
- 75% energy reduction and 4.44x speedup

## Technical Approach
CVMAX represents complex numbers in polar form (magnitude and phase) rather than rectangular form (real and imaginary). This representation reduces quantization bit width and simplifies multiplication (magnitude multiply, phase add) versus rectangular multiplication complexity. Shift quantization further reduces complexity. Custom hardware implements these polar operations efficiently.
