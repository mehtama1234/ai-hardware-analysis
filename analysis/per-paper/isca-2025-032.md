# isca-2025-032 — NetCrafter: Tailoring Network Traffic for Non-Uniform Bandwidth Multi-GPU Systems

**Venue:** ISCA  
**Confidence:** low (abstract only)

## Problem
Hierarchical multi-GPU systems use high-bandwidth intra-group links but lower-bandwidth inter-group links, creating severe bottlenecks at the inter-group fabric. Conventional GPU communication treats flits uniformly, leaving bandwidth partially unutilized while generating unnecessary speculative traffic.

## Motivation
As ML workloads demand more GPUs, hierarchical non-uniform bandwidth interconnects are the practical scaling path, but the bandwidth imbalance between intra- and inter-group links creates disproportionate performance degradation at the slower inter-group fabric.

## Method
NetCrafter applies three complementary techniques at the inter-group network boundary: (1) flit stitching coalesces partially-filled flits going to the same destination to maximize bandwidth utilization; (2) flit trimming drops speculative prefetch flits not yet needed, eliminating unnecessary traffic; (3) flit sequencing reorders flits so latency-sensitive critical flits reach their destinations ahead of bulk traffic. No GPU compute pipeline changes are required.

## Key Novelty
Unified traffic-shaping layer combining flit-level stitching, demand-driven trimming of speculative flits, and priority-aware sequencing to exploit non-uniform bandwidth structure of hierarchical multi-GPU systems.

## Contributions
1. Identification of three inefficiency classes: partial flit utilization, unnecessary speculative fetches, and latency-insensitive ordering.
2. Flit stitching to coalesce compatible partially-filled flits.
3. Flit trimming to drop speculative flits not yet demanded.
4. Flit sequencing to prioritize latency-sensitive flits.
