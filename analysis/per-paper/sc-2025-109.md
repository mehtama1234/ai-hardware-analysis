# The Cost of Teaching Operational ML

**Venue:** SC | **ID:** sc-2025-109

## Summary
Analysis of infrastructure requirements and costs for graduate-level operational ML education, demonstrating comprehensive end-to-end ML systems engineering curriculum on Chameleon Cloud testbed.

## Problem & Motivation
ML education typically focuses on model development but neglects operational skills: distributed training, deployment, monitoring, and automation. These HPC/systems-grounded skills require substantial computing resources and comprehensive infrastructure setup, making them difficult to teach at scale.

## Key Findings
- **Total compute requirement:** 186,692 compute instance hours for course (191 students)
- **Cost projection:** ~$250 per student on commercial clouds (~$50K total)
- **Coverage:** Distributed training, reproducible experiments, automated deployment, continuous monitoring
- **Feasibility:** Demonstrated viability of comprehensive ML systems curriculum with open materials

## Hardware & Systems
- **Platform:** Chameleon Cloud testbed
- **Configuration:** On-demand distributed computing clusters
- **Model:** Research infrastructure for educational access

## Techniques
- Distributed training frameworks
- Experiment tracking and reproducibility tools
- Model deployment and serving
- Continuous monitoring and logging
- Automated retraining pipelines

## Contributions
1. First comprehensive cost analysis of operational ML education
2. Detailed instrumentation of resource consumption per assignment type
3. Commercial cloud cost projection framework
4. Open educational materials enabling course replication
5. Empirical evidence for HPC computing in ML education

## Limitations
- Course-specific implementation limits direct generalizability
- Chameleon Cloud infrastructure differs from commercial platforms
- Analysis excludes instructor time and operational overhead
- Cost projections based on commercial rates, not actual commercial usage
