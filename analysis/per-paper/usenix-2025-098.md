# Accelerating Model Training on Ascend Chips: An Industrial System for Profiling, Analysis and Optimization

**Problem:** Model training on Ascend accelerators suffers from performance inefficiencies that require systematic identification through profiling. Industrial-scale deployments need comprehensive analysis tools to optimize hardware utilization for training workloads.

**Method:** The system integrates performance profiling to monitor Ascend behavior during training, analytical techniques to identify bottlenecks, and optimization methods to accelerate kernels. It combines hardware-level performance counters with software-level optimization strategies targeting training efficiency.

**Novelty:** An integrated industrial-grade profiling and optimization framework specifically designed for model training acceleration on Ascend chips.
