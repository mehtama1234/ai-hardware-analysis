# SetupKit: Efficient Multi-Corner Setup/Hold Time Characterization Using Bias-Enhanced Interpolation and Active Learning

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-274

## Problem
Accurate setup/hold time characterization relies on potentially millions of SPICE simulations across diverse process-voltage-temperature (PVT) corners, creating a major bottleneck lasting weeks or months. Existing methods suffer from slow search convergence and inefficient exploration in multi-corner settings.

## Motivation
To drastically reduce characterization time using statistical intelligence and active learning to intelligently guide expensive SPICE simulations to the most informative corners.

## Method
SetupKit accelerates timing characterization through three techniques working together. It uses BEIRA, a search method that applies statistical error modeling to guess and refine solutions more quickly. It estimates good starting points by analyzing the circuit design itself. Most importantly, it employs active learning with a Gaussian Process (machine learning that learns from test results to predict which future tests will be most revealing) to focus simulations on the most informative manufacturing conditions and temperatures, avoiding redundant tests.

## Key Novelty
SetupKit uses a Gaussian Process (machine learning that learns from test results) to actively predict which manufacturing conditions and temperatures will be most informative to test, reducing the total number of simulations needed for multi-corner characterization.

## Contributions
- BEIRA (bias-enhanced interpolation) method for accelerated search convergence
- Active learning framework learning PVT-timing correlations
- Gaussian Process-based corner selection strategy
- 2.4× overall CPU time reduction (720→290 days on single core) for 16 PVT corners
