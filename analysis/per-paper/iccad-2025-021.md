# GAIA: Glass-Aware I/O Middleware

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-021

## Problem
Quartz glass archival storage has hybrid mechanical-optical architecture (XY mechanical, Z optical) introducing unique performance bottlenecks that conventional I/O scheduling cannot address.

## Motivation
Glass storage offers multi-century durability and radiation resistance, requiring specialized middleware to unlock performance potential.

## Method
The system coordinates three strategies to handle the storage hardware's unique design. Zigzag Data Placement arranges data to align with the mechanical stage's side-to-side motion, reducing wasted movement. Z-Axis First Placement prioritizes the fast optical axis before slower mechanical movements. Shortest Moving Time First scheduling selects operations based on predicted movement time, processing those requiring the least motion first.

## Key Novelty
This is the first I/O middleware designed specifically for glass archival storage systems that combine mechanical and optical movement.

## Contributions
- Zigzag data placement algorithm aligned to mechanical stage motion
- Z-axis-first placement strategy for optical traversal optimization
- SMTF scheduling based on movement time prediction
- 82% read latency reduction vs traditional schedulers
