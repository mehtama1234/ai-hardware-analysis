# ApproxGNN: A Pretrained GNN for Parameter Prediction in Design Space Exploration for Approximate Computing

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-214  
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Design space exploration for approximate accelerators requires expensive evaluation of QoR-cost tradeoffs for many parameter configurations.

## Motivation
Efficient parameter prediction accelerates design space exploration and enables rapid prototyping of approximate accelerators.

## Method
Graph Neural Network trained to predict quality-of-result and cost metrics. Transfer learning approach with pre-trained model. Generalizes across multiple approximate computing scenarios.

## Key Novelty
First pre-trained GNN for approximate computing design space exploration with transfer learning capability.

## Contributions

1. GNN model for QoR/cost prediction in approximate computing
2. Pre-trained transfer learning approach
3. 50% MSE improvement over conventional methods
