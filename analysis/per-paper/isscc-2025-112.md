# isscc-2025-112: 27GHz Fractional-N Sub-Sampling PLL with Fast Locking

**Problem:** Mm-wave fractional-N PLLs face DTC range enlargement from prescalers and prolonged locking times in sub-sampling mode.

**Method:** This sub-sampling PLL uses a phase detector that samples both rising and falling edges of the oscillator—this doubles the timing information, shrinking the tuning range without prescaler circuits (which traditionally reduce frequency but widen the tuning range). To lock quickly in fractional-N mode (where non-integer frequency ratios slow normal feedback), the design measures remaining frequency errors with a Time-to-Digital Converter and corrects them beyond what the reference clock can reach, settling in under 2 microseconds.

**Key Novelty:** Polarity-reversible SSPD + TDC-based FLL enabling reduced DTC range and sub-microsecond fractional locking.

**Main Results:** 27GHz, 57.9fsrms jitter, -249.7dB FoM, 1.98µS locking time.

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only analysis)
