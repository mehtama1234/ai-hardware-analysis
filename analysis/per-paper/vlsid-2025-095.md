# CMOSP18 FD-SOI Technology Based MCU Achieving High Performance of 1.2GHz Using High Speed, Optimized Leakage & High Density Tightly Coupled Memory (TCM)

**Venue:** VLSID 2025  
**Authors:** Praveen Verma, Anuj Dhillon, Ashfaque Ahmed, Yagnesh Vaderiya, Chandan Singh, Veenita Kumari, Harshit Sharma

## Problem Statement
Microcontroller units require both high performance (>1GHz clock rates) and low power consumption. Tightly coupled memories (TCM), which are embedded in close proximity to processor cores, critically determine system performance. Optimizing TCM for simultaneous high speed and low leakage while managing area is challenging, particularly across different physical proximity zones to the core.

## Key Contributions
1. **1.2 GHz Operation**: Demonstrates high-frequency MCU operation in 18nm FD-SOI technology
2. **16% Timing Improvement**: 16% reduction in access and setup time vs. competitor TCM designs
3. **33% Leakage Reduction**: Significant power savings through leakage-optimized memory instances
4. **17% Area Efficiency**: Reduced area footprint compared to competitive implementations
5. **Dual-Variant Optimization**: Location-aware TCM design with distinct optimizations for near and far placement
6. **High-Density Bitcells**: Reduced RC delay and improved performance through density optimization

## Technical Approach
- **Memory Architecture**: Two TCM instance variants optimized for different physical placements
  - **Near-Core Variant**: Performance-optimized for reduced access time and setup time
  - **Far-Core Variant**: Leakage-optimized with reduced supply voltage and optimized gate sizes
- **Bitcell Design**: High-density bitcell implementation to minimize area and RC delay
- **Technology**: CMOSP18 FD-SOI (28nm FinFET-like) with body bias advantages
- **Integration**: Embedded within 1.2 GHz MCU core
