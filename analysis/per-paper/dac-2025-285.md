# X-SAT: An Efficient Circuit-Based SAT Solver

**Venue:** DAC | **Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem & Motivation
Circuit equivalence verification is critical but slow with general-purpose SAT solvers; existing CNF-based approaches miss circuit-specific structure.

## Core Method
CDCL algorithm with structural elimination reducing variables/clauses while preserving circuit topology + circuit-aware branching heuristics for arithmetic patterns.

## Key Contribution
Circuit-specific branching heuristics and structural elimination exploiting arithmetic circuit patterns.

## Results
- Outperforms prior circuit solvers on all benchmarks
- Matches/exceeds SOTA CNF-based solvers on complex arithmetic instances
