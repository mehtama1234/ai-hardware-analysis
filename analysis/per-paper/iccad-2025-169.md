# HyperEF 2.0: Spectral Hypergraph Coarsening via Krylov Subspace Expansion and Resistance-Based Local Clustering

**Authors:** Hamed Sajadinia, Zhuo Feng

**Venue:** ICCAD

## Summary
HyperEF 2.0 improves spectral hypergraph coarsening and partitioning through expanded Krylov subspace methods and resistance-based local clustering, achieving state-of-the-art VLSI partitioning quality.

## Key Contributions
- Expanded Krylov subspace approach improving effective resistance approximation
- Resistance-based local clustering for better cluster balance
- State-of-the-art performance on VLSI hypergraph benchmarks
- Up to 4.5× speedup over prior flow-based clustering methods
