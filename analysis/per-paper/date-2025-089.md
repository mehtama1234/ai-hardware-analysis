# Multi-Sensor Data Fusion for Enhanced Detection of Laser Fault Injection Attacks in Cryptographic Hardware: Practical Results

**Venue**: DATE  
**Primary Theme**: laser-attack detection  

## Problem
Detecting laser fault injection attacks on cryptographic hardware deployed in FPGAs. Laser attacks offer high precision and temporal control, making them difficult to detect with single sensors.

## Method
Low-cost detection scheme using Time-to-Digital Converters (TDCs) to sense IR voltage drops induced by laser illumination. Multi-sensor fusion approach with minimum two sensors improves detection reliability and reduces false alarms.

## Key Novelty
Multi-sensor TDC-based fusion achieving high detection rate with minimal false alarms against laser fault injection.

## Contributions
- TDC-based sensing of laser-induced transients
- Multi-sensor fusion scheme for reliable detection
- Practical validation on Xilinx Artix-7 FPGA with real laser setup
