# LeanAttention: Hardware-Aware Scalable Attention for the Decode-Phase of Transformers

**ID:** mlsys-2025-037 | **Venue:** MLSYS 2025 | **Confidence:** high

## Problem
LLM decode-phase attention has critically low GPU occupancy because FlashAttention-2 only parallelizes over batch and heads (Nq=1 for decode), and FlashDecoding's fixed-split causes imbalanced loads and quantization inefficiencies.

## Method
Applies Stream-K style decomposition to attention: context length is linearized into fixed-size LeanTiles, then divided equally among all CTAs regardless of head/batch boundaries. The associativity of the softmax re-scaling operator enables exact reduction of partial outputs from unevenly-sized splits, achieving near-100% SM occupancy.

## Key Novelty
Proves softmax re-scaling's associativity to enable Stream-K arbitrary work partitioning in attention, achieving near-100% GPU occupancy for all problem sizes and context lengths.

## Metrics
- **Speedup:** Average 1.73x over FlashDecoding on A100 (max 2.18x at 256k ctx); average 1.52x on H100 (max 2.53x)
- **Energy:** More efficient than FlashDecoding at >128k context

## Baselines
FlashAttention-2, FlashDecoding, FlashInfer
