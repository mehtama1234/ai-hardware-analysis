# isscc-2025-178: Compact Resistive Temperature Sensor with Time-Domain Amplification

**Title:** 27.5: A 4,100µm2 Wire-Metal-Based Temperature Sensor with a Fractional-Discharge FLL and a Time-Domain Amplifier with ±0.2°C Inaccuracy (3σ) from -40 to 125°C and 45fJ·K2 Resolution FoM in 28nm CMOS

**Problem:** Integrated temperature sensors need compact area, high accuracy, and low-voltage operation in advanced processes.

**Approach:** Wire-metal resistor with fractional-discharge FLL and time-domain amplifier, eliminating active-device biasing.

**Key Result:** ±0.2°C accuracy over -40 to 125°C, 45fJ·K² FoM, 4,100µm² area, 0.9V compatible.

**Hardware:** ASIC (integrated temperature sensor).

**Tags:** temperature-sensor, resistive, low-voltage, compact, high-accuracy, integrated-sensing
