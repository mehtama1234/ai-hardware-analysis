# SmaRTLy: RTL Optimization via Logic Inferencing and Structural Rebuilding

**Venue:** DAC | **ID:** dac-2025-182

## Summary
SmaRTLy advances RTL optimization for multiplexer trees by exploiting logical relationships and structural redundancy that traditional tools like Yosys miss. Achieves 8.95% additional area reduction on standard benchmarks and 47.2% more reduction on industrial designs (>1M gates).

## Key Results
- **Area Reduction:** 8.95% vs. Yosys on IWLS-2005/RISC-V; 47.2% more reduction on industrial >1M-gate designs
- **Method:** The approach uses logic inferencing—analyzing how signals logically depend on each other—to identify redundant multiplexers, which are circuit structures that select inputs based on control signals. It eliminates these unnecessary multiplexers entirely. For the remaining multiplexers, it restructures them by leveraging the discovered signal dependencies, reducing the total number of logic gates in the design.
