# Accelerated Spatio-Temporal Bayesian Modeling for Multivariate Gaussian Processes

**Venue:** SC | **ID:** sc-2025-110

## Summary
DALIA: GPU-accelerated framework for Bayesian inference on multivariate Gaussian processes using hierarchical distributed parallelism, enabling unprecedented scaling to hundreds of supercomputer nodes.

## Problem & Motivation
Multivariate Gaussian processes model complex interdependent phenomena but face severe computational bottlenecks in high-dimensional spatio-temporal applications. Existing methods lack scalability for real-world data volumes and resolution requirements.

## Key Findings
- **Weak-scaling improvement:** 100× over state-of-the-art methods
- **Strong-scaling speedup:** 1000× on 496 GH200 superchips (Alps supercomputer)
- **Parameter expansion:** 8× larger parameter space than previous approaches
- **Real-world validation:** 48-day air pollution study with refined spatial resolution

## Hardware & Systems
- **GPUs:** NVIDIA GH200 superchips
- **System:** Alps supercomputer
- **Scale:** 496 superchips (distributed-memory configuration)
- **Architecture:** Hierarchical GPU + CPU parallelism

## Techniques
- **INLA (Integrated Nested Laplace Approximation):** Efficient Bayesian inference
- **Sparse inverse covariance formulation:** Reduced memory footprint
- **Block-dense GPU computation:** Optimized GPU utilization
- **Hierarchical parallelism:** Triple-layer distributed scheme
- **Multivariate GP modeling:** Complex spatial-temporal correlations

## Limitations
