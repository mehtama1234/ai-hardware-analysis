# Self-Attention to Operator Learning-based 3D-IC Thermal Simulation

**Venue:** DAC | **ID:** dac-2025-125

## Core Problem
Efficient thermal simulation for 3D ICs with high power densities; PDE solvers are accurate but slow for iterative design.

## Method
SAU-FNO combines self-attention and U-Net with FNO to capture long-range thermal dependencies and local high-frequency features; transfer learning fine-tunes low-fidelity data.

## Key Novelty
Hybrid self-attention + U-Net + FNO preserving high-frequency information with transfer learning on low-fidelity training data.

## Contributions
- SAU-FNO architecture (self-attention + U-Net + FNO)
- Transfer learning enabling low-fidelity training
- State-of-the-art thermal prediction
- 842x speedup over FEM
