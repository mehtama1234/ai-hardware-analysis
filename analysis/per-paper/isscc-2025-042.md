# isscc-2025-042: VSWR-Resilient Phased-Array PA

**Title:** An Ultra-Compact Wideband Load-Insensitive Complex-Cascode LC-Neutralized Power Amplifier for 4:1-VSWR-Resilient Operations in Large-Scale Phased Arrays

**Venue:** ISSCC

## Core Contribution
LC-neutralized complex-cascode PA achieving 4:1 VSWR resilience and <7dB gain flatness across frequency and scanning angles for phased arrays.

## Key Metrics
- **VSWR Resilience:** 4:1 (vs. unmatched variation >7dB)
- **Gain Variation:** <7dB across frequency/angle
- **Scanning Angle:** >60° support

## Problem
Phased-array mutual coupling creates 4:1 VSWR at PA input, degrading output power, gain, efficiency, and complicating calibration.

## Method
The power amplifier uses a multi-stage circuit architecture with inductors and capacitors arranged to actively cancel impedance mismatches (called LC-neutralized complex-cascode topology). In large phased arrays, antennas electromagnetically interfere with each other, producing severe impedance variations represented as 4:1 VSWR. This design makes the amplifier resilient to these variations, maintaining stable gain, stable 1-dB compression output power, and stable efficiency across different frequencies and scanning angles beyond 60°, all in a compact form.
