# Run-time Energy-Efficiency Optimization for AI and HPC Workloads

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-133
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Power management at exascale is critical for environmental/cost impact, but most methods require deep application knowledge.

## Motivation
GPU-dominated datacenters for AI/HPC need application-agnostic power optimization with minimal overhead.

## Method
Runtime power manager: spectral analysis detects repetitive patterns, traces per-pattern energy, gradient-descent optimizer finds optimal GPU frequency.

## Key Novelty
Application-agnostic runtime power optimization via pattern detection and per-pattern energy tuning

## Contributions
1. 15% energy reduction for equivalent compute
2. No overhead
3. Minimal execution time impact
4. Validated on diverse AI applications
