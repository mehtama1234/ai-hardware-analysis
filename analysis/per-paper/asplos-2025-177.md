# asplos-2025-177: Syno: Structured Synthesis for Neural Operators

**Venue:** ASPLOS  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
NAS and tensor compilers are limited to composing or optimizing existing manually designed operators and cannot discover fundamentally new operator designs. There is no systematic method to automatically synthesize novel neural operators that satisfy structural constraints required for trainability and hardware efficiency.

## Motivation
Manually designed neural operators bottleneck both prediction accuracy and execution performance, and the space of possible operators is too large and complex for human intuition to explore.

## Method
Syno works by defining small, composable building blocks that operate on tensor dimensions (the multi-dimensional arrays used in neural networks) and enforcing rules that keep model training stable—like ensuring dimensions match between operations and follow the associative property (where the order doesn't affect the result). To avoid searching redundant designs, the system uses expression canonicalization to identify and prune equivalent forms. It then uses stochastic tree search (a Monte Carlo-style method of randomly exploring design branches) to navigate the enormous design space while respecting input/output dimension constraints, and compiles and evaluates the resulting operators on real hardware.

## Key Novelty
Syno is an end-to-end system that automatically discovers neural operators by assembling small, reusable components operating on tensor dimensions (multi-dimensional arrays) and using stochastic tree search (random exploration of design possibilities) to find operators that outperform both hand-designed ones and those from automated neural architecture search.

## Contributions
- Fine-grained tensor-dimension primitives ensuring trainability and enabling expression canonicalization
- Guided synthesis flow generating valid operators matched to specified input/output dimension constraints
- Efficient stochastic tree search algorithm for neural operator design space exploration
- Discovered operators achieving 1.37x to 2.06x speedups with <1% accuracy loss on NAS-optimized models
