# asplos-2025-177: Syno: Structured Synthesis for Neural Operators

**Venue:** ASPLOS  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
NAS and tensor compilers are limited to composing or optimizing existing manually designed operators and cannot discover fundamentally new operator designs. There is no systematic method to automatically synthesize novel neural operators that satisfy structural constraints required for trainability and hardware efficiency.

## Motivation
Manually designed neural operators bottleneck both prediction accuracy and execution performance, and the space of possible operators is too large and complex for human intuition to explore.

## Method
Syno defines fine-grained primitives on tensor dimensions that enforce trainability properties (dimension matching, associativity). Expression canonicalization prunes redundant candidates. A guided synthesis flow constrains valid operators to specified input/output dimension sizes, and stochastic tree search (Monte Carlo-style) efficiently explores the large design space. Resulting operators are compiled and evaluated on real hardware.

## Key Novelty
End-to-end neural operator synthesis framework using fine-grained tensor-dimension primitives and stochastic tree search to automatically discover novel operators outperforming manually designed and NAS-optimized baselines.

## Contributions
- Fine-grained tensor-dimension primitives ensuring trainability and enabling expression canonicalization
- Guided synthesis flow generating valid operators matched to specified input/output dimension constraints
- Efficient stochastic tree search algorithm for neural operator design space exploration
- Discovered operators achieving 1.37x to 2.06x speedups with <1% accuracy loss on NAS-optimized models
