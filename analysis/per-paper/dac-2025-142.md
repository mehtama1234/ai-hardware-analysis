# PiSPICE: Post-Layout SPICE Acceleration via Parasitic Identification

## Problem
Post-layout SPICE simulation of advanced-node ICs involves billions to trillions of parasitic nodes, creating prohibitively high computational cost that slows design closure and verification cycles.

## Method
PiSPICE uses adjoint sensitivity analysis on pre-layout circuits to identify critical parasitic elements and eliminate non-critical ones. Only critical parasitics are retained in post-layout simulation; model order reduction minimizes their impact while non-critical parasitics are pruned entirely.

## Key Results
- 17.27x simulation speedup vs commercial Spectre simulator
- <0.78% error margin compared to Spectre
- Avoids expensive sensitivity computation on billion-node post-layout circuits
