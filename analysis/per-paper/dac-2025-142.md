# PiSPICE: Post-Layout SPICE Acceleration via Parasitic Identification

## Problem
Post-layout SPICE simulation of advanced-node ICs involves billions to trillions of parasitic nodes, creating prohibitively high computational cost that slows design closure and verification cycles.

## Method
PiSPICE uses adjoint sensitivity analysis (a mathematical technique for finding which components affect results) on pre-layout circuits (before physical layout) to identify critical parasitic elements (unwanted electrical components that appear in densely packed circuits). Only these critical parasitics are kept in the post-layout simulation; model order reduction (a technique that simplifies their representation) minimizes their computational impact while non-critical parasitics are removed entirely. This reduces simulation scale from billions or trillions of nodes to just the essential ones.

## Key Results
- 17.27x simulation speedup vs commercial Spectre simulator
- <0.78% error margin compared to Spectre
- Avoids expensive sensitivity computation on billion-node post-layout circuits
