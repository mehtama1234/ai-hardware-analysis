# Enhancing Reliability and Energy Efficiency in Network-on-Chip Architectures through Hybrid Sorting Algorithm-Based Core Mapping

**Paper ID:** vlsid-2025-069
**Venue:** VLSID
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
NoC task-to-core mapping significantly impacts system reliability and energy efficiency; traditional mapping approaches are suboptimal.

## Motivation
Reliable, energy-efficient NoCs require optimized task mapping strategies that reduce communication overhead and adapt to varying workloads.

## Method
Hybrid sorting algorithm combining Bitonic sorter (high-throughput parallel sorting for initial task placement) and Selection sorter (refines mapping for reliability/energy, dual-mode adaptive operation). FPGA evaluation on real and synthetic benchmarks.

## Key Novelty
Hybrid Bitonic-Selection sorting approach for NoC core mapping; dual-mode adaptive operation tuned to reliability and energy constraints.

## Contributions
- Hybrid Bitonic-Selection sorting-based core mapping algorithm
- Reduced communication overhead
- Dual-mode adaptive workload management
- Enhanced system reliability
- Optimized energy consumption
