# Enhancing Reliability and Energy Efficiency in Network-on-Chip Architectures through Hybrid Sorting Algorithm-Based Core Mapping

**Paper ID:** vlsid-2025-069
**Venue:** VLSID
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
NoC task-to-core mapping significantly impacts system reliability and energy efficiency; traditional mapping approaches are suboptimal.

## Motivation
Reliable, energy-efficient NoCs require optimized task mapping strategies that reduce communication overhead and adapt to varying workloads.

## Method
This approach combines two sorting techniques to assign tasks to cores in a Network-on-Chip efficiently. First, Bitonic sorting—a high-throughput parallel sorting method—quickly places tasks across cores as a starting point. Second, Selection sorting (which picks the best option one at a time) refines these placements, switching between two modes to optimize for either reliability or energy efficiency depending on system needs. The method was tested on an FPGA with both real workloads and synthetic benchmarks.

## Key Novelty
The core idea is a two-stage hybrid sorting approach for assigning tasks to Network-on-Chip cores: fast Bitonic sorting provides an initial placement, and then Selection sorting refines it using dual-mode adaptation that prioritizes either reliability or energy efficiency.
