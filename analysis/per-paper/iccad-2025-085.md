# JSA-CIM: A Joint-Sparse AdderNet Compute-In-Memory Accelerator for Energy-Efficient Edge AI Applications

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-085

## Problem
Existing CIM designs for AdderNet fail to exploit inherent model sparsity in both activations and weights, missing substantial opportunities for energy efficiency improvement in edge AI deployment.

## Motivation
AdderNet offers significant computational advantages over CNNs through efficient Sum-Of-Absolute-Differences operations, but realizing energy-efficient CIM deployment requires exploiting joint sparsity patterns in both activation and weight dimensions.

## Method
JSA-CIM is an 8T-SRAM-based digital CIM for efficient AdderNet inference exploiting joint-sparsity of activations and weights in bit-serial fashion. Architecture-level features include sparsity-aware CIM macro exploiting word-level activation sparsity; circuit-level includes novel 8T-SRAM bitline architecture exploiting both activation and bit-level weight sparsity; robust sparsity-gated minimum selection for reliable and efficient bit-serial minimum operation.

## Key Novelty
Joint-sparsity exploitation in both activation and weight dimensions through specialized 8T-SRAM bitline architecture for energy-efficient AdderNet CIM computation.

## Contributions
- Sparsity and kernel-size-aware CIM macro for flexible architecture configuration and throughput optimization
- Novel 8T-SRAM bitline architecture exploiting word-level activation and bit-level weight sparsity
- Robust sparsity-gated minimum selection circuit for reliable bit-serial minimum operations
- 65nm implementation achieving 170.7 GOPS throughput, 1.8 TOPS/mm² area efficiency, 75.98 TOPS/W energy efficiency
