# Extended-Reach TIA for Optical Receivers

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**ID:** isscc-2025-244

## Problem
Co-packaged optical receivers require extended PD-TIA reach (0.2 inch vs. 0.03 inch in prior work) for thermal/mechanical/field-replacement flexibility, but long interconnects degrade signal quality.

## Solution
4×112Gb/s PAM-4 linear TIA with active-input-termination (AIT-TIA) and signal-interpolation single-ended-to-differential converter achieves 0.2-inch PD-TIA reach at 0.61pJ/b efficiency.

## Key Innovation
Active-input-termination TIA enabling 0.2-inch PD-TIA reach (6.7× improvement) while maintaining low energy.

## Results
- **Data rate:** 4×112 Gb/s PAM-4
- **PD-TIA reach:** 0.2 inch (6.7× improvement)
- **Energy efficiency:** 0.61 pJ/b

## Hardware Target
ASIC

## Techniques
Circuit design, signal processing, interconnect
