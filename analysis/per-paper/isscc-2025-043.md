# isscc-2025-043: Sub-THz Switch-Loaded VCO

**Title:** A 224GHz 19.9% TR Varactor-Less VCO Utilizing a Multi-Section Switch-Loaded Coupled-Line Resonator

**Venue:** ISSCC

## Core Contribution
Varactor-less 224GHz VCO achieving 19.9% tuning range via multi-section switch-loaded coupled-line resonator.

## Key Metrics
- **Frequency:** 224GHz (sub-THz)
- **Tuning Range:** 19.9%
- **Architecture:** Multi-section switch-loaded coupled-line

## Problem
Varactors and switched-capacitors suffer from low Q-factor and limited tuning range at sub-THz frequencies (>100GHz).

## Method
The oscillator tunes its frequency by using electronic switches to selectively activate inductors (magnetic coils) within a coupled-line resonator—a transmission line structure that naturally oscillates at specific frequencies. By switching different inductors in or out of the circuit, the resonant frequency shifts across a 19.9% range. This design replaces varactors (voltage-controlled capacitors), which perform poorly above 100GHz and suffer from low signal quality; the switched-inductor approach maintains high signal quality (Q-factor) throughout the tuning range at 224GHz.
