# isscc-2025-072

**Title:** 9.5 A Sub-1V, 50mV Dropout LDO Using Pseudo-Impedance Buffer with Phase-Margin Improvement Design

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Problem:** Sub-1V analog LDOs struggle to support wide load ranges (light to heavy); current-mirror buffers degrade performance at light loads due to high K ratios needed for heavy load support.

**Key Contribution:** Pseudo-impedance buffer decouples buffer resistance from load current, enabling wide load coverage in sub-1V without stability compromises.

**Method:** Pseudo-impedance buffer replacing conventional current-mirror, single-stage architecture, phase-margin improvement design avoiding Miller compensation.

**Hardware:** ASIC power regulator

**Metrics:** Sub-1V, 50 mV dropout, wide load current support

**Techniques:** Circuit design, power

**Primary Theme:** Sub-1V LDO with wide-load pseudo-impedance buffer
