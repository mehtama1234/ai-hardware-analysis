# DCDiff: Enhancing JPEG Compression via Diffusion-based DC Coefficients Estimation

**Problem:** Dropping DC coefficients at camera and reconstructing via statistics introduces errors; these propagate through the image.

**Method:** End-to-end diffusion model for DC coefficient estimation with mask-based correction of distribution-violating locations.

**Key Result:** Improved compression performance over baselines; minimal downstream task impact; compatible with low-cost JPEG processors.

**Hardware Target:** CPU  
**Workloads:** vision  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
