# ISSCC 2025-202: Granite Rapids-D—Dual-SKU Xeon 6 for Edge & vRAN

## Core Contribution
Intel Xeon 6 SoC (Granite Rapids-D) offering dual optimization strategies: compute-optimized for vRAN/edge data-center and rugged-edge SKU (-40°C to 85°C operation) with integrated security and flexible networking.

## Technical Approach
- **Dual-SKU Strategy**:
  - *Compute-optimized*: High scalar/parallel throughput, PCIe5, low-latency memory for vRAN and edge servers
  - *Edge-optimized*: Full rugged-temperature dynamic range, enterprise-grade robustness
- **Security**: Confidential AI-enabled (TEE-based trusted execution)
- **Connectivity**: Multi-protocol ethernet, accelerator support, PCIe5 for high-bandwidth I/O
- **Thermal**: Full dynamic temperature range support on edge SKUs

## Performance Metrics
- **Temperature Range (Edge)**: -40°C to 85°C Tcase (full dynamic thermal support)
- **Memory**: Low-latency, high-bandwidth (exact specs not provided)
- **Interconnect**: PCIe 5.0
- **Security**: Confidential AI support (TEE)

## Baselines
- Prior Xeon Scalable Processors (less rugged thermal range)
- Telecom/vRAN ASICs (less flexible, higher porting cost)

## Hardware Impact
Enables Intel to compete in edge-compute and vRAN segments (traditionally ASIC/telecom-dominated) with flexible, programmable Xeon platform. Dual-SKU strategy reduces fragmentation vs. separate product lines.
