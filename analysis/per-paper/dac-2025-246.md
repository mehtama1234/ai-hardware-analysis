# dac-2025-246: LMM-IR: Large-Scale Netlist-Aware Multimodal Framework for Static IR-Drop Prediction

## Summary
Multimodal ML framework representing SPICE netlists as 3D point clouds and fusing with imagery for fast IR drop prediction, achieving best F1 score and lowest MAE vs. ICCAD 2023 contest winners.

## Key Idea
Encode circuit topology as 3D point clouds in netlist transformer to scalably predict voltage drops via multimodal fusion.

## Contributions
- Netlist-to-3D-point-cloud representation
- Multimodal netlist+image fusion
- Best F1 vs. ICCAD 2023 winners
- Lowest MAE among approaches

## Limitations
Deployment to real design flows not demonstrated.
