# Uncertainty Disentanglement for Multimodal Foundation Models in Robotic Systems

**Venue:** MLSys 2025 | **ID:** mlsys-2025-011 | **Confidence:** high

## Problem
Multimodal foundation models in robotic systems conflate perception uncertainty and decision uncertainty, preventing targeted mitigation of either type.

## Method
Conformal prediction quantifies perception uncertainty; FMDP with Kripke structures and temporal logic handles decision uncertainty. Active sensing triggered on high perception uncertainty; automated refinement triggered on high decision uncertainty. Uses LLaVA-1.5-7B and CLIP; evaluated on Carla simulator and real-world driving.

## Novelty
First framework to formally disentangle perception uncertainty (conformal prediction) from decision uncertainty (FMDP + temporal logic) in multimodal robotic systems.

## Metrics
- 40% reduction in output variability
- 5% improvement in specification satisfaction
- 95%+ temporal logic compliance
