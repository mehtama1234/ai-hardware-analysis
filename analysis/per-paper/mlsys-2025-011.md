# Uncertainty Disentanglement for Multimodal Foundation Models in Robotic Systems

**Venue:** MLSys 2025 | **ID:** mlsys-2025-011 | **Confidence:** high

## Problem
Multimodal foundation models in robotic systems conflate perception uncertainty and decision uncertainty, preventing targeted mitigation of either type.

## Method
Conformal prediction creates calibrated prediction sets for multimodal inputs, measuring uncertainty from noisy or ambiguous sensor data. Decision uncertainty is modeled using Formally-specified Markov Decision Processes (FMDP)—mathematical decision models that map possible states (using Kripke structures) and verify behavior follows formal rules via temporal logic specifications. When sensor uncertainty is high, the system automatically requests additional observations; when decision uncertainty is high, it automatically refines its model. The approach was tested on LLaVA-1.5-7B and CLIP models using the Carla driving simulator and real-world driving data.

## Novelty
First framework to formally disentangle perception uncertainty (conformal prediction) from decision uncertainty (FMDP + temporal logic) in multimodal robotic systems.
