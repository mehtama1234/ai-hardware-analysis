# isscc-2025-073

**Title:** A 3.47 NEF 175.2dB FOMs Direct Digitization Front-End Featuring Delta Amplification for Enhanced Dynamic Range and Energy Efficiency in Bio-Signal Acquisition

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Problem:** Bio-signal acquisition (EEG) requires low input-referred noise (50–60 nV/√Hz) and high artifact rejection (100+ mV); conventional approaches sacrifice one for the other.

**Key Contribution:** Delta amplification in VCO-DSM achieves 3.47 NEF (low noise) + enhanced dynamic range, simultaneous noise and artifact immunity.

**Method:** The circuit uses a direct-to-digital approach with delta amplification—a circuit design that extends the input range—in the first stage, breaking the traditional tradeoff between low noise and artifact rejection. Instead of amplifying first and saturating under large artifacts, this keeps the signal linear across a wide range while a VCO-based delta-sigma modulator (an analog-to-digital converter) digitizes it. This enables the front-end to reject 100+ millivolt artifact spikes while maintaining 50-60 nanovolts per square root Hertz noise, and achieves a 3.47 NEF (noise efficiency factor).

**Hardware:** ASIC

**Metrics:** NEF 3.47, FoMs 175.2 dB, IRN 50–60 nV/√Hz, >100 mV dynamic range

**Techniques:** Circuit design, analog

**Primary Theme:** Low-noise, high-DR bio-signal front-end
