# dac-2025-026: YAP - Hybrid Bonding Yield Model

**Title:** YAP: Yield Modeling and Simulation for Advanced Packaging

**Venue:** DAC 2025

**Core Issue:** Cu-Cu hybrid bonding yield prediction requires accounting for multiple failure mechanisms; simulation too slow for design optimization.

**Key Contribution:** Near-analytical yield model (W2W/D2W); 10,000x faster than simulation with identical accuracy; enables bonding pitch and process control co-optimization.

**Hardware Target:** Chiplet, packaging

**Workload:** Physical design, manufacturing

**Metrics:**
- Runtime speedup: 10,000x
- Accuracy: matches simulation

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)
