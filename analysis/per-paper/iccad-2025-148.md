# PCBFormer: Understanding 3D Structure of RealWorld PCB Traces for S-Parameter Prediction

**Venue:** ICCAD
**ID:** iccad-2025-148
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
S-parameter extraction for PCB traces via electromagnetic simulation is computationally expensive and becomes a bottleneck with iterative design changes in component placement.

## Motivation
Efficient S-parameter prediction enables faster signal integrity analysis and PCB design iteration.

## Method
The framework uses artificial intelligence to predict how signals behave in circuit boards, replacing slow computer simulations. It learns by analyzing 3D structures where traces (copper wires) run on multiple layers and interact electromagnetically—the way electric fields from one trace influence others nearby. By understanding these multi-layer interactions, it predicts S-parameters (measurements of signal behavior) across frequencies from DC to 1 gigahertz.

## Key Novelty
Using artificial intelligence to model multi-layer electromagnetic interactions in circuit boards, replacing slow computer simulation for S-parameter prediction.

## Contributions
- Multi-layer 3D PCB structure modeling in deep learning
- Capture of electromagnetic coupling across traces and layers
- 0.86 R² score across 210 S-parameters for realistic PCBs
- High accuracy replacement for electromagnetic simulation
