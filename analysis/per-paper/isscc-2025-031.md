# isscc-2025-031: Background-Light-Robust Flash LiDAR

**Title:** SPAD Flash LiDAR with Chopped Analog Counter for 76m Range and 120klx Background Light

**Venue:** ISSCC

## Core Contribution
Direct time-of-flight LiDAR using single-photon avalanche diodes (SPADs) with chopped analog counter achieving 76m range under extreme background light (120klx).

## Key Metrics
- **Range:** 76m
- **Background Light Tolerance:** 120klx
- **Performance:** High frame rate with improved fill-factor

## Problem
Prior flash LiDAR systems struggle with simultaneous range, depth resolution, and frame-rate under strong sunlight; background-light noise dominates.

## Method
dToF sensor with chopped analog counter suppressing background interference via analog noise cancellation.
