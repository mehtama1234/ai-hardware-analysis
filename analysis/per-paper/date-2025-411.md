# Compromising DNNs: RowPress Attack Vulnerability

**Venue:** DATE | **ID:** date-2025-411

## Problem
RowPress attacks can flip targeted bits in DRAM; impact on DNNs unexplored. Existing RowHammer mitigations are ineffective against RowPress, creating a new vulnerability.

## Motivation
DNN security and robustness; emerging DRAM attack vectors threaten AI systems in shared computing environments.

## Approach
DRAM-profile-aware attack methodology capturing distinct bit-flip patterns of RowPress vs RowHammer. Evaluation across 11 DNN architectures on Samsung DRAM.

## Key Innovation
First comprehensive evaluation of RowPress attacks on DNNs demonstrating faster degradation than RowHammer.

## Results
- RowPress causes faster DNN performance degradation than RowHammer
- Existing RowHammer mitigations are ineffective
- Evaluated on 11 DNN architectures, multiple datasets

## Target Hardware
CPU (DRAM-based systems)

## Techniques
Security, reliability analysis

## Workload
CNN (generic DNNs)

**Low** (abstract-only analysis)
