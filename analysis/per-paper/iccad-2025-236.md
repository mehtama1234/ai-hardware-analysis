# Revolution or Hype? Seeking the Limits of Large Models in Hardware Design

**Paper ID:** iccad-2025-236  
**Venue:** ICCAD

## Problem

Recent breakthroughs in Large Language Models and Large Circuit Models have created excitement about AI's potential in EDA, but significant skepticism remains about practical capabilities versus inflated expectations.

## Motivation

The EDA community needs authoritative guidance on realistic capabilities and limitations of AI models in hardware design to make informed investment and adoption decisions.

## Method

Panel paper synthesizing perspectives from leading experts in academia and industry to critically examine practical capabilities, fundamental limitations, and future prospects of large AI models in circuit design. Frames debate on reliability, scalability, and interpretability while evaluating whether these models meaningfully outperform or complement traditional EDA methods.

## Key Novelty

Foundational overview paper providing authoritative perspective on capabilities and limitations of LLMs and large circuit models in EDA applications.

## Contributions

- Critical analysis of practical capabilities and limitations of large AI models in EDA
- Examination of reliability concerns in AI-assisted hardware design
- Discussion of scalability and interpretability challenges
- Perspective on complementarity vs. replacement of traditional EDA methods
