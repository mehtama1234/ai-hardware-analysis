# CROP - LLM-powered VLSI design tuning via retrieval-augmented generation for parameter optimization

CROP automates VLSI design parameter optimization using retrieval-augmented generation integrated with large language models. By retrieving relevant historical design solutions and applying LLM-based reasoning, the system intelligently recommends parameter configurations that achieve 9.9% power reduction compared to manual tuning. This approach reduces design effort by leveraging learned patterns from past designs while maintaining the domain expertise necessary for complex design decisions.
