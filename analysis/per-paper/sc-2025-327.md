# Towards Supporting QIR: Steps for Adopting the Quantum Intermediate Representation

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-327
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Quantum computing intermediate representations (IRs) are critical for optimization, but adoption pathways are unclear; converting existing simulators to support QIR requires significant effort.

## Motivation
QIR (built on LLVM) offers standardization benefits; understanding adoption steps reduces barriers to broader quantum simulator support of QIR and classical control flow.

## Method
Demonstration: convert quantum circuit simulator to QIR runtime. Outline multiple adoption approaches. Benchmark performance vs original simulator. Show classical control flow support.

## Key Novelty
Clear roadmap for QIR adoption; empirical evidence that transition introduces no performance penalty and enables new capabilities (classical control flow).

## Contributions
1. Step-by-step guide for QIR runtime adoption
2. Quantum circuit simulator with QIR support
3. Performance equivalence with original simulator
4. Classical control flow support
