# BlasPart: A Deterministic Parallel Partitioner for Balanced Large-Scale Hypergraph Partitioning

## Problem
Large-scale hypergraph partitioning (VLSI, HPC) must balance partition quality with deterministic outputs across thousands of partitions.

## Solution
Recursive multilevel bisection with deterministic parallel guarantees and level-dependent balance constraints. Maintains reproducibility while scaling to 4096-way partitioning.

## Impact
3.33x speedup vs Mt-KaHyPar-SDet; improved balance and scalability for large industrial VLSI/HPC workloads.
