# ReMaP: Macro Placement by Recursively Prototyping and Periphery-Guided Relocating

**Venue:** DAC | **Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem & Motivation
Expert macro placement is time-consuming; placement directly impacts timing (WNS/TNS) and dataflow. Experts place memory/arithmetic blocks peripherally to minimize wiring and congestion.

## Core Method
Two-phase approach: ABPlace (angle-based analytical) arranges macros along ellipse optimizing dataflow and avoiding overlaps → rough periphery distribution. Heuristic positions macros along chip boundaries mimicking expert practice. Recursive refinement + parameter autotuning improve quality.

## Key Contribution
Angle-based analytical placement + periphery-guided relocation matching expert macro floorplanning practices.

## Results
- 34.15% WNS improvement
- 65.39% TNS improvement
- 8.75% additional improvement via autotuning
