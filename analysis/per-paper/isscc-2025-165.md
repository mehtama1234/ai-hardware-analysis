# isscc-2025-165: Two-Stage Power Converter for Data Center PoL

**Title:** A 12A 89.3% Peak Efficiency and 26mV Undershoot 12-to-1V Two-Stage Converter with Regulated Resonant Switched-Capacitor Regulators

**Problem:** Data centers and processors need efficient power delivery at 12A output, sub-1V supply, with minimal voltage droop.

**Approach:** Two-stage regulation—intermediate bus converter (12V→intermediate) + switched-capacitor point-of-load regulator using regulated resonant operation.

**Key Result:** 89.3% peak efficiency, 26mV undershoot, supporting fast transient response.

**Hardware:** ASIC (power delivery circuit).

**Tags:** power-delivery, dc-dc-converter, efficiency, point-of-load, two-stage, low-voltage
