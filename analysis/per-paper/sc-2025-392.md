# Large-Message All-to-All Communication at Frontier Scale

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-392
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
At exascale, all-to-all communication becomes latency-dominated even for large messages, making it a critical bottleneck for collective operations.

## Motivation
MPI_Alltoall is a fundamental communication pattern in HPC applications; vendor implementations may not be optimized for extreme scale (65K+ tasks).

## Method
The approach uses GPU-aware Message Passing Interface (a communication system that lets GPUs exchange data directly, without going through the CPU). On 8,192 computers, it optimizes all-to-all communication (where every computer sends data to every other one) by handling small and large messages as separate problems, using different strategies for each. Instead of just maximizing throughput (how much data moves per second), it cuts latency—the time it takes to initiate communication—which is the critical bottleneck when thousands of nodes coordinate.

## Key Novelty
The central insight is that optimizing for latency (communication startup time) rather than throughput matters most for all-to-all communication at exascale, and this approach outperforms the hardware vendor's implementations with 65,536 parallel tasks on Frontier.

## Contributions
1. Two GPU-aware all-to-all implementations targeting different message size ranges
2. Performance characterization on Frontier at record scale (65536 tasks, 8192 nodes)
3. Guidance for MPI_Alltoall_init optimization on exascale systems
