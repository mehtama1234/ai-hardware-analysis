# Evaluating HPK for Running Cloud-Native Workloads on Slurm Clusters

**ID:** sc-2025-062 | **Venue:** SC

## Problem Statement
Integrating cloud-native and HPC workloads requires separate resources; leads to inefficient resource partitioning

## Motivation
Applications increasingly combine cloud-native and HPC processing steps; current separate platforms waste resources and complicate job management

## Method
HPK framework enabling unmodified cloud-native workload execution on main HPC cluster (Slurm)

## Key Novelty
Unified execution of cloud-native and HPC workloads on single cluster without resource partitioning

## Contributions
- HPK framework for cloud-native workload support on HPC clusters
- Preservation of existing HPC job management and accounting
- No workload modification required
