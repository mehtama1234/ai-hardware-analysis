# isscc-2025-174: Symbol-Power-Tracking Supply Modulator for 5G

**Title:** 9.4 A 102ns/V 94.3%-Peak-Efficiency Symbol-Power-Tracking Supply Modulator for 5G NR Power Amplifiers

**Problem:** 5G RFPAs need dynamic supply tracking within cyclic-prefix duration (1.17µs), but traditional approaches suffer slow speed, large passives, and efficiency loss.

**Approach:** Split-capacitor supply modulator with optimized charging and pre-charging achieving 102ns/V tracking speed and 94.3% efficiency.

**Key Result:** Meets FR1 symbol-duration constraints without degraded efficiency or large external components.

**Hardware:** ASIC (power tracking circuit).

**Tags:** 5g, power-tracking, rfpa, supply-modulation, efficiency, fast-transient
