# Quantum Virtual Machines

**Problem:** Quantum hardware is heterogeneous and scarce, with limited qubit availability across different platforms. Existing approaches lack a portable abstraction layer for executing quantum algorithms across diverse quantum backends while efficiently managing qubit allocation and error mitigation.

**Method:** The paper proposes a quantum virtual machine that provides an intermediate representation for quantum operations and compiles high-level quantum programs to physical qubit sequences. The VM includes a runtime layer for qubit allocation, scheduling across multiple quantum backends, and coordination of classical-quantum execution with error mitigation strategies.

**Novelty:** A hardware-agnostic quantum virtual machine abstraction that enables portable quantum program execution with improved qubit utilization through intelligent scheduling and allocation.
