# VLSI Design and Experimental Demonstration of Photonic Interposers in Thin-Film Lithium Niobate

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-100

## Problem
Optimizing photonic interposer performance requires accounting for optical timing skew, propagation loss, routing/placement blockages, and seamless integration with EDA tools—challenges for which mature design automation does not exist.

## Motivation
Enable energy-efficient clock distribution and high-bandwidth communication among chiplets via photonic interposers with mature EDA tool integration.

## Method
Photonic-to-electronic and electronic-to-photonic IC transformations enabling use of EDA tools for photonic IC design. Design optical clock trees and optical routers. Fabricate and experimentally validate photonic interposer in thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN).

## Key Novelty
First photonic-electronic transformation enabling use of industry-standard EDA tools for photonic interposer design; experimental TFLN photonic interposer demonstration.

## Contributions
- 13.85% optical timing skew improvement vs standalone OCT design tools
- 128x128 electro-optical router design automation in <40 minutes
- Experimental TFLN photonic interposer demonstration
- 2.16 dB optical loss skew measured experimentally
- Phase-adaptive optical power modulation for loss reduction
