# Destination Earth: The Climate Change Adaptation Digital Twin

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-128
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Traditional climate modeling at high resolution (km-scale) is computationally infeasible; digital twin for climate adaptation requires multi-decadal simulations.

## Motivation
Climate change adaptation planning requires high-resolution long-term climate projections; Destination Earth initiative targets operational digital twin.

## Method
The approach runs three interconnected climate models—which simulate the atmosphere, oceans, ice sheets, and other systems working together—to project decades of future climate worldwide. These simulations run on two of Europe's most powerful supercomputers (LUMI and MareNostrum5) to handle the enormous calculation needed to model the planet at 5-kilometer resolution (fine enough to capture details like mountain valleys and coastal features). The team also demonstrated that scaling to 1-kilometer resolution is technically feasible.

## Key Novelty
The first fully working digital twin of Earth's climate, simulating decades-long global changes at kilometer-scale resolution on European supercomputers.

## Contributions
1. 5 km resolution multi-decadal projections
2. 0.6 simulated years/day throughput
3. 6.6 PB climate data portfolio
4. 97 simulated days/day at 1 km on full machine
