# Destination Earth: The Climate Change Adaptation Digital Twin

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-128
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Traditional climate modeling at high resolution (km-scale) is computationally infeasible; digital twin for climate adaptation requires multi-decadal simulations.

## Motivation
Climate change adaptation planning requires high-resolution long-term climate projections; Destination Earth initiative targets operational digital twin.

## Method
Multi-decadal global climate projections at 5 km resolution using three coupled Earth system models on LUMI and MareNostrum5; scaling to 1 km demonstrated.

## Key Novelty
First operational multi-decadal climate digital twin at km-scale resolution on EuroHPC systems

## Contributions
1. 5 km resolution multi-decadal projections
2. 0.6 simulated years/day throughput
3. 6.6 PB climate data portfolio
4. 97 simulated days/day at 1 km on full machine
