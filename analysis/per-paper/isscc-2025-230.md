# isscc-2025-230: High-Voltage Instrumentation Amplifier with Superior CMRR

**Problem:** Industrial control requires gain-adjustable instrumentation amplifiers handling large differential inputs (±24V) with substantial common-mode offsets (±10V), high CMRR (>120dB), high input impedance (>10MΩ), and bandwidth.

**Approach:** 36V current-balancing instrumentation amplifier achieving ±24V differential and ±10V CM input handling with 140dB CMRR maintained across all gain settings.

**Key novelty:** Current-balancing topology achieving 140dB CMRR at all gain settings.

**Metrics:** 5.6MHz bandwidth, >10MΩ input impedance, ±24V differential / ±10V CM range.

**Tags:** instrumentation-amplifier, high-voltage, cmrr, industrial, gain-adjustable, sensor-conditioning
