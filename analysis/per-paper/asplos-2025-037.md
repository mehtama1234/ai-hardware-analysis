# H-Houdini: Scalable Invariant Learning

**ID:** asplos-2025-037  
**Venue:** ASPLOS  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)  
**Primary Theme:** Scalable invariant learning for large hardware formal verification

## Problem
Formal hardware verification via invariant learning (e.g., IC3/PDR) does not scale to large hardware designs because the invariant search space grows exponentially with state size, causing state-of-the-art tools to time out on real-world RTL designs.

## Motivation
Hardware bugs discovered post-silicon are extremely costly; formal verification offers correctness guarantees but is limited to small designs, leaving the majority of modern processor and SoC designs unverified by automated techniques.

## Method
H-Houdini extends the Houdini invariant-learning algorithm with scalability improvements targeting large hardware designs. The likely approach involves decomposing the hardware design into manageable sub-modules or abstract components, learning invariants compositionally (e.g., assume-guarantee or abstraction-refinement), and propagating learned invariants across the design hierarchy to avoid full-state enumeration. Specific algorithmic details are not disclosed in the available abstract.

## Key Novelty
Scalable extension of Houdini-style invariant learning to large hardware designs that previously exceeded the capacity of existing formal verification tools.
