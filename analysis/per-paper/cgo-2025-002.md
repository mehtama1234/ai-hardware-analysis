# ASDF: A Compiler for Qwerty, a Basis-Oriented Quantum Programming Language

**Venue:** CGO 2025 | **Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)

## Problem & Motivation
Compiling basis-oriented quantum languages requires circuit synthesis from basis translations and automatic adjoint/predicated function specialization—challenges not addressed by circuit-oriented compilers.

## Technical Approach
High-level quantum IR implemented in MLIR framework handles basis translation to circuit generation and automatic specialization. Produces OpenQASM 3 or QIR for simulation or hardware execution with comparable fault-tolerant costs.

## Key Novelty
First IR specifically for basis-oriented quantum languages, enabling efficient compilation from higher-level abstractions.

## Key Results
- Comparable fault-tolerant resource cost to circuit-oriented compilers
- Handles basis translations and predicated/adjoint specialization automatically

## Hardware & Techniques
- **Target:** ASIC (quantum hardware)
- **Techniques:** Compiler, IR design, basis translation
