# isscc-2025-027: Easy-Drive Pipelined-SAR ADC with Fast Calibration

**An Easy-Drive 16MS/s Pipelined-SAR ADC Using Split Coarse-Fine Input-Buffer-Sampling Scheme**

## Summary
16 MS/s pipelined-SAR ADC with split coarse-fine input-buffer sampling enabling rail-to-rail 3.6 V input capability from 1.8/0.9 V supply, plus fast PVT-robust background gain calibration.

## Technical Approach
- **Split buffer**: Coarse-fine input sampling reducing capacitive loading and slew requirements
- **No predictor**: Direct operation without external driver circuits or high supply
- **Calibration**: Metastability-detector based gain calibration with input dithering for PVT robustness
- **Performance**: 79.4 dB SNDR, 99.4 dB SFDR at Nyquist

## Key Innovation
Split coarse-fine input-buffer scheme enabling easy driving without external circuits or high supply, combined with fast metastability-based background calibration robust across PVT.

## Contributions
- Split coarse-fine buffer eliminating drive requirement
- Rail-to-rail 3.6 V input from 1.8/0.9 V supply
- Fast PVT-robust background gain calibration
- Input-dither probability control for convergence

## Target Workloads
Instrumentation, audio acquisition, sensor interfaces

## Limitations
- Calibration convergence time not specified
- Power consumption and area not detailed
- Noise contribution of calibration loop unclear
