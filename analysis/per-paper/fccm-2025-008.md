# DRSA: Accelerating Macro Placement on Commercial FPGAs

**Problem:** FPGA macro-component placement in DynaRapid creates a bottleneck where the existing greedy placement strategy causes significant frequency degradation, limiting the practical benefits of macro-based fast compilation.

**Method:** DRSA replaces greedy placement with simulated annealing optimization specifically tailored for macro-level components in DynaRapid. The simulated annealing algorithm iteratively refines macro positions to minimize frequency loss while maintaining fast placement execution, navigating the placement design space more effectively than greedy approaches.

**Novelty:** Simulated annealing-based macro placer designed for DynaRapid that eliminates frequency degradation while preserving the fast compilation benefits of macro-hierarchical design.
