# SDM-PEB: Spatial-Depthwise Mamba for Enhanced Post-Exposure Bake Simulation

**Problem:** PEB simulation needs precise 3D modeling of photoacid diffusion and inhibitor reactions to maintain resist profile accuracy in lithography.

**Method:** Spatial-depthwise Mamba attention with customized selective scan and state space models for capturing multi-scale 3D dependencies in diffusion processes.

**Key Result:** Outperforms previous methods in accuracy and efficiency (validated against commercial rigorous models).

**Hardware Target:** GPU, ASIC  
**Workloads:** HPC, database  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
