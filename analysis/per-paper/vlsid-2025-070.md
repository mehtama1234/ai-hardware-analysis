# 8GHz Multi-Phase Ring VCO Design with Wide Tuning Range for SerDes Applications in 6nm FinFET Process

**Paper ID:** vlsid-2025-070
**Venue:** VLSID
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
SerDes applications require voltage-controlled oscillators with wide frequency tuning range, low phase noise, and operation across PVT variations.

## Motivation
6nm FinFET technology offers improved performance but requires careful design of analog circuits; need for low-noise, wide-tuning VCOs for high-speed I/O.

## Method
8-phase pseudo Differential Ring Oscillator design in 6nm FinFET (0.8V ±5%, -40 to +125°C). Two Basic Delay Cell architectures (BDC-1, BDC-2) proposed and compared.

## Key Novelty
Multi-phase ring VCO design optimized for 6nm FinFET; comparison of delay cell architectures for wide tuning range.

## Contributions
- BDC-1 design: 4GHz FTR (5.5-9.5 GHz), -78.43 dBc/Hz phase noise @ 1MHz offset
- BDC-2 design: 3.29GHz FTR (6-9.29 GHz), -83.41 dBc/Hz phase noise @ 1MHz offset
- Voltage tuning range: 0.16-0.64V (20-80% of supply)
- Average power: BDC-1 19.5mW, BDC-2 25.6mW
- 8-phase output for SerDes
