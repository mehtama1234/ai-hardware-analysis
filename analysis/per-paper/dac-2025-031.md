# Warpage-Aware Generative Floorplanning for Reliable Advanced Packaging

**Problem:** Thermal warpage in heterogeneous advanced packaging materials degrades system performance but is not addressed by conventional floorplanning algorithms.

**Key Idea:** Use transformer-based encoding to learn floorplan graph representations, then apply parallel decoding with warpage-aware legalization to minimize die displacement and warpage jointly.

**Approach:** Generative learning with integrated thermal-mechanical constraint modeling during die placement legalization.

**Results:** 9.9% warpage improvement and 8.3% wirelength reduction over prior state-of-the-art.

**Theme:** AI-guided floorplanning that explicitly optimizes for packaging reliability under warpage.
