# EarDVFS: Environment-Adaptable RL-based DVFS for Mobile Devices

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-098

## Problem
Conventional DVFS methods struggle with mobile device demands including device-specific thermal calibrations, ambient environment impact, and non-processor component thermal effects.

## Motivation
Improve power efficiency across diverse thermal environments while maintaining performance on mobile devices.

## Method
EarDVFS: environment-adaptable RL-based DVFS with:
- Proactive throttling combining traditional and RL-based methods
- Tracking of ambient and non-processor temperatures
- Environment-robust RL parameter design
- Device-specific configuration awareness

## Key Novelty
First environment-adaptable RL-DVFS considering ambient and non-processor components for mobile device thermal management.

## Contributions
- 21.6% average power efficiency improvement (up to 49.6%)
- Maintains performance across varying thermal environments
- Proactive throttling combining strengths of traditional and RL-based methods
- Extensive ablation studies validating each RL component
