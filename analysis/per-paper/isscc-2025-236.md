# isscc-2025-236: Dual-Mode SRAM CIM for Floating-Point Training

**Problem:** SRAM CIM macros target inference; on-device training requires backpropagation with transposed weights. Prior T-CIM designs waste area (separate FF/BP), use low-precision integer (accuracy loss), or suffer PVT sensitivity (analog CIM).

**Approach:** 28nm digital SRAM CIM macro with dual-mode transpose enabling FF and BP via single MAC circuit set. Floating-point format preserves training accuracy; approximate computing mode available for speed/efficiency trade-off.

**Key novelty:** Dual-mode transpose SRAM CIM reusing MAC circuits for FF and BP phases while supporting floating-point precision.

**Metrics:** 192.3TFLOPS/W energy efficiency for training and inference.

**Tags:** cim, training, sram, floating-point, edge-ai, accuracy
