# isscc-2025-060

**Title:** A Dual-Input Bidirectional 3-Level Battery Charger with Coarse-Fine VCF Balancing and Wide VCR for Foldable Mobile Applications

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Problem:** Foldable phones need compact, high-density chargers supporting simultaneous wired + wireless charging and reverse power sharing.

**Key Contribution:** Seamless buck-to-boost mode transition via 3-level topology + coarse-fine VCF balancing without auxiliary circuits.

**Method:** The charger uses a circuit design with three voltage levels and two separate input switches—one for plugged-in charging and one for wireless charging. When charging the battery, the circuit steps down the input voltage (called buck conversion); when the battery powers an external device, it steps up the voltage (called boost conversion); and when idle, it does nothing. The charger keeps current stable across all these modes by adjusting the switching frequency, using both coarse (rough) and fine (precise) tuning. Because the circuit handles a very wide range of input voltages, it doesn't need separate circuit designs for high or low voltage inputs—a single design works for both wired and wireless charging.

**Hardware:** ASIC power management IC

**Techniques:** Power delivery, circuit design

**Primary Theme:** Bidirectional multi-level charging for foldable phones
