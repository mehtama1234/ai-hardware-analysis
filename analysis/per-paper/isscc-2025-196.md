# ISSCC 2025-196: 8–28GHz Multi-Phase Clock Generator

## Core Contribution
Dual-feedback ring-oscillator architecture that generates 8-phase clocks from 8–28GHz with low jitter and phase error, eliminating costly PLL-divider topologies for high-speed transceivers.

## Technical Approach
- **DLL + Ring Oscillator**: Cascaded topology where DLL expands input clock to multiple phases, then RO injection-locked by DLL
- **Feedback Benefit**: RO's autonomous feedback suppresses phase error and jitter vs. standalone DLL chains
- **Frequency Tuning**: Covers 8–28GHz in 28nm CMOS without fT-limited PLL divider
- **Phase Count**: 8-phase output suitable for PAM-4 and advanced modulation

## Performance Metrics
- **Frequency Range**: 8–28GHz (3.5x tuning)
- **Phases**: 8-phase
- **Jitter/Phase Error**: Low (exact quantification not in abstract)

## Baselines
- PLL + divider (reaches fT limits, costly, infeasible in advanced nodes)
- Quadrature VCOs (4 phases only, mismatch-sensitive)
- Poly-phase filters (4 phases, mismatch-sensitive)
- DLL chains (high phase error accumulation)

## Hardware Impact
Enables 224Gb/s PAM-4 and beyond wireline transceivers without resorting to inefficient PLL dividers or limited quadrature approaches. Scalable to higher frequencies as process improves.
