# date-2025-012: BOSON-1: Understanding and Enabling Physically-Robust Photonic Inverse Design

## Problem
Nanophotonic inverse-designed structures are numerically optimal but difficult to fabricate and highly sensitive to physical variations. Discrete material distributions with numerous local optima cause gradient-based methods to converge suboptimally.

## Method
BOSON-1 formulates inverse design as a fabrication-restricted, discrete, probabilistic optimization problem. Key techniques include: (1) optimization directly in the fabricable subspace, (2) dense target-enhanced gradient flows to mitigate local optima, and (3) conditional subspace optimization strategy with adaptive sampling-based robust optimization.

## Contributions
- 74.3% post-fabrication performance improvement over prior methods
- Adaptive sampling reduces prohibitive runtime for variation robustness
- Gradient-based optimization constrained to fabricable subspace

## Results
On three representative photonic device benchmarks, BOSON-1 delivers fabricable structures with best convergence and performance under realistic variations compared to prior work.
