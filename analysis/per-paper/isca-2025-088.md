# Constant-Rate Entanglement Distillation for Fast Quantum Interconnects

**ID:** isca-2025-088 | **Venue:** ISCA | **Confidence:** low (abstract only)

## Problem
Existing quantum interconnect protocols cannot simultaneously achieve high fidelity and high rate of entanglement generation under realistic noisy local operations, blocking distributed quantum computing. Current distillation methods either have vanishing rate or cannot tolerate physical noise levels.

## Motivation
Fault-tolerant distributed quantum computing requires constant-rate, high-fidelity entanglement across interconnects; all practical proposals fall short, preventing modular large-scale quantum computer construction.

## Method
A sequence of two-way entanglement distillation protocols based on quantum error-detecting codes with increasing code rate. Iterated two-way distillation rounds are combined with standard fault-tolerance techniques to yield a constant fraction of raw pairs converted to high-fidelity logical pairs. Numerical optimization minimizes practical overhead under memory constraints.

## Key Novelty
First constant-rate (in expectation) entanglement distillation protocol for noisy quantum interconnects, via chaining quantum error-detecting codes of increasing rate in a two-way distillation ladder.

## Contributions
1. Constant-rate entanglement distillation scheme tolerating noisy local operations
2. Theoretical proof of constant rate in expectation
3. Numerical optimization to reduce overhead under memory constraints
4. Demonstrated improvement over existing quantum interconnect methods for fault-tolerant regimes
