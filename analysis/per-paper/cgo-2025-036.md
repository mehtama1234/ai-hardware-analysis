# Combining MLIR Dialects with Domain-Specific Architecture for Efficient Regular Expression Matching

**Venue:** CGO | **Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Regular expression pattern matching is computationally intensive and sequential in data dependency; general-purpose CPUs/GPUs are inefficient while frozen hardware accelerators lack pattern flexibility.

## Core Innovation
Using MLIR's dialect-based compilation framework to enable DSA-level optimizations for regex matching, bridging abstraction gap between patterns and hardware execution.

## Method
Combines MLIR's multi-level IR with two domain-specific dialects: one abstractly representing regular expressions and another targeting the DSA instruction set. A redesigned DSA architecture maximizes parallelization by exploiting structured compilation infrastructure optimizations across abstraction levels.

## Key Contributions
- Multi-level MLIR compilation strategy with two domain-specific dialects for regex and DSA targeting
- Redesigned DSA architecture maximizing parallelization
- Up to 2.26x speedup and 2.30x energy efficiency improvement

## Results
- Speedup: 2.26x
- Energy efficiency: 2.30x
- Resource usage: improved
