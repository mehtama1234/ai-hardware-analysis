# ChipletEM: Physics-Based EM Signoff for 2.5D/3D Chiplet Integration

**Venue:** DAC 2025  
**Confidence:** abstract-only

## Summary

ChipletEM is a comprehensive physics-based electromigration signoff tool for 2.5D/3D chiplet systems. It couples FVM-based electrical-thermal simulation with FDTD stress solver and analytical TSV thermal modeling, achieving 0.61% nucleation error and 2.4% growth error vs. FEM.

## Key Contributions

- **Coupled Multi-Physics**: Simultaneous modeling of EM, thermal stress, void growth, IR drop, and Joule heating
- **TSV-Aware**: First EM tool explicitly accounting for Through Silicon Via electromigration with non-uniform thermal distribution
- **Accurate Simulation**: FVM-FDTD coupling at each timestep, 0.61% nucleation and 2.4% growth phase errors
- **Improvement vs. SOTA**: 17% error reduction compared to AFD method (from 22.22% to 5.24%)

## Technical Focus

Addresses unique EM challenges in chiplet systems including TSV current paths, inter-die thermal gradients, and coupled stress-thermal-EM interactions. Enables accurate reliability signoff for advanced integrations.
