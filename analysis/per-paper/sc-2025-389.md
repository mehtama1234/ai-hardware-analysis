# HPC-R1: Characterizing R1-like Large Reasoning Models on HPC

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-389
**Confidence:** low

## Problem

Limited understanding of Large Reasoning Model (LRM) training performance and system inefficiencies on HPC systems; complex multi-stage workflows challenging to optimize.

## Motivation

Comprehensive LRM training characterization on production HPC systems (Perlmutter) needed to guide future HPC-AI system design.

## Method

HPC-R1: characterization of LRM training stages (SFT, GRPO-based RL, generation, distillation) on NERSC Perlmutter; detailed performance analysis.

## Key Novelty

First comprehensive LRM training characterization on Top500 HPC system; 19 key observations across all training stages.

## Contributions

1. Comprehensive LRM training characterization on Perlmutter
2. Analysis of SFT, GRPO-based RL, generation, and distillation
3. 19 key observations and system inefficiency identification
4. Recommendations for future HPC-AI system design
