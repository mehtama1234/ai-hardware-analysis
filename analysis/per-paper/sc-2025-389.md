# HPC-R1: Characterizing R1-like Large Reasoning Models on HPC

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-389
**Confidence:** low

## Problem

Limited understanding of Large Reasoning Model (LRM) training performance and system inefficiencies on HPC systems; complex multi-stage workflows challenging to optimize.

## Motivation

Comprehensive LRM training characterization on production HPC systems (Perlmutter) needed to guide future HPC-AI system design.

## Method

The study measured large reasoning model training across four stages on NERSC Perlmutter, a top-500 supercomputer: supervised fine-tuning (teaching the model with labeled examples), group relative policy optimization (reinforcement learning that rewards better outputs), generation, and distillation (compressing the model). Researchers tracked processor utilization, memory efficiency, and speed through each stage to identify performance bottlenecks and system inefficiencies.

## Key Novelty

For the first time, researchers comprehensively measured how large reasoning models train on a top-500 supercomputer and identified 19 key performance insights across all training stages.

## Contributions

1. Comprehensive LRM training characterization on Perlmutter
2. Analysis of SFT, GRPO-based RL, generation, and distillation
3. 19 key observations and system inefficiency identification
4. Recommendations for future HPC-AI system design
