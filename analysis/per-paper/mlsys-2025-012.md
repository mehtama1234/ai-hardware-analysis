# Distributed Submodular Subset Selection at Billion Scale

**Venue:** MLSys 2025 | **ID:** mlsys-2025-012 | **Confidence:** high

## Problem
Submodular subset selection for dataset pruning requires centralizing all data in one machine's memory, infeasible at billion-point scale.

## Method
Distributed bounding algorithm using grow/shrink steps computes per-worker upper/lower bounds on marginal gains without centralizing data. Multi-round partition-based greedy with adaptive repartitioning aggregates global selections. Implemented on Apache Beam; scales to 13 billion points.

## Novelty
Bounding algorithm that avoids centralizing data by computing provably tight distributed bounds on submodular marginal gains.

## Metrics
- 98% of centralized greedy quality at 32 communication rounds
- Scales to 13 billion data points
