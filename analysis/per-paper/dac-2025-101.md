# Invited: EDA for Heterogeneous Integration

**Venue:** DAC 2025 (Invited Paper)  
**Confidence:** low (position paper)

## Problem
Heterogeneous chiplet-based systems require new EDA tools and methodologies beyond traditional monolithic design. Chiplet disaggregation, multi-physics analysis, physical design, and infrastructure standardization are all nascent or missing.

## Key Insight
Chiplet design demands co-optimization across system partitioning, substrate choices, thermal management, and interchiplet communication—requiring integrated EDA frameworks beyond traditional point tools.

## Four Core EDA Challenges
1. **Chiplet Disaggregation & Co-Optimization:** Map system designs to chiplets with optimal technology/substrate choices
2. **Multi-Physics & Multi-Scale Analysis:** From ML-driven early-stage to full-fidelity thermomechanical signoff
3. **Physical Design for HI:** Chiplet placement, routing, thermal/power delivery on substrates
4. **Infrastructure:** Chiplet libraries, data formats, standards, design kits

## Impact
Establishes roadmap for EDA tool and methodology development enabling mainstream chiplet-based system design.
