# J2Place: A Multiphase Clocking-Oriented Length-Matching Placement for Rapid Single-Flux-Quantum Circuits

**Venue:** ICCAD | **ID:** iccad-2025-051

## Problem
RSFQ circuits require inserting numerous D flip-flops substantially increasing area and energy. Existing placement tools do not support multiphase clocking RSFQ circuits, missing opportunity to reduce DFF overhead.

## Motivation
Reduce circuit area and energy dissipation in superconducting RSFQ circuits through multiphase clocking placement.

## Method
The approach introduces two new cell designs (TFFDO and TFFDE) that work with two-phase clock signals, reducing the number of D flip-flops (storage elements) needed in the circuit. Clock signals are then distributed across the circuit column-by-column using maximum flow, an algorithm that finds optimal routing paths. The circuit components are arranged vertically using dynamic programming — a systematic optimization technique that minimizes wire length while respecting fixed ordering constraints. Finally, components are reordered based on wire lengths to further reduce total wiring overhead.

## Key Novelty
A placement framework designed specifically for multiphase-clocked RSFQ circuits that combines new cell designs (TFFDO and TFFDE) with wire-length-aware ordering to reduce D flip-flop overhead.

## Contributions
- Novel TFFDO and TFFDE cells simplifying multiphase clock distribution
- Maximum flow-based method for systematic clock distribution
- Length-aware reordering and DP-based wirelength minimization for placement
