# AutoNTT: Automatic Architecture Design and Exploration for Number Theoretic Transform Acceleration on FPGAs

**Problem:** NTT is a major computational bottleneck in FHE systems, but there lacks a systematic design automation tool to explore various NTT accelerator architectures that support diverse FHE parameters such as different polynomial sizes, modulo sizes, and reduction methods.

**Method:** AutoNTT automatically generates optimized NTT accelerators in HLS supporting multiple architecture patterns (iterative, dataflow, hybrid) and reduction methods. The tool explores polynomial sizes from 2^10 to 2^17 and modulo sizes from 28-64 bits, applying optimizations such as polynomial and twiddle factor buffer reduction and simplified butterfly unit interconnections.

**Novelty:** Automatic generation and systematic exploration of multiple NTT accelerator architectures via HLS to support diverse FHE parameters and reduction methods.
