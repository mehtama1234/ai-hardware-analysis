# CREST-CiM: Cross-Coupling-Enhanced Differential STT-MRAM for Robust Computing-in-Memory in Binary Neural Networks

**Problem:** Standard MRAM-based computing-in-memory suffers from low distinguishability of stored weights (+1/-1), resulting in poor sense-margin and vulnerability to process variations.

**Method:** Uses two cross-coupled magnetic tunnel junctions (MTJs) per bitcell to store complementary weights, achieving 8100x high-to-low current ratio for robust differential sensing.

**Key Contribution:** 3.4x higher sense-margin and 27.6% better read-disturb-margin with only 7.9% area overhead.

**Results:** Near-software accuracy on ResNet-18/CIFAR-10, 10.7% improvement over 2T-2MTJ baseline.
