# CREST-CiM: Cross-Coupling-Enhanced Differential STT-MRAM for Robust Computing-in-Memory in Binary Neural Networks

**Problem:** Standard MRAM-based computing-in-memory suffers from low distinguishability of stored weights (+1/-1), resulting in poor sense-margin and vulnerability to process variations.

**Method:** CREST-CiM stores each binary weight using two magnetic tunnel junctions—tiny devices that switch between magnetic states to encode data—connected together per memory cell, one storing +1 and one storing −1. Cross-coupling them (linking their magnetic states) dramatically increases the difference between the electrical current when reading the +1 state versus the −1 state, achieving an 8100-fold ratio. This much larger difference makes the reading circuit much more reliable, allowing it to correctly identify weights despite manufacturing variations and other hardware imperfections.

**Key Contribution:** 3.4x higher sense-margin and 27.6% better read-disturb-margin with only 7.9% area overhead.

**Results:** Near-software accuracy on ResNet-18/CIFAR-10, 10.7% improvement over 2T-2MTJ baseline.
