# dac-2025-023: DIAS - Attention Sparsity with Hierarchical PIM

**Title:** DIAS: Distance-based Attention Sparsity for Ultra-Long-Sequence Transformer with Tree-like Processing-in-Memory Architecture

**Venue:** DAC 2025

**Core Issue:** Attention bandwidth and KV cache capacity bottleneck long-context LLM inference; sparsity methods hurt accuracy at 1M+ tokens.

**Key Contribution:** Distance-based irregular sparsity (DIAS) + hierarchical PIM (TreePIM) for KV cache; 75x speedup on 1M-token Llama3-405B with <1% accuracy drop.

**Hardware Target:** PIM (processing-in-memory)

**Workload:** LLM inference, attention, transformer

**Metrics:**
- Speedup: 75x vs SOTA
- Sequence length: 1M tokens
- Accuracy drop: <1%
- Model: Llama3-405B

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)
