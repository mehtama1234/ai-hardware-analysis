# Revisit Choice Network for Synthesis and Technology Mapping

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-022

## Problem
Choice networks constructed via simulation and SAT solving often contain low-quality choices that don't contribute to effective technology mapping.

## Motivation
High-quality choice networks improve circuit optimization and technology mapping results.

## Method
The CRISTAL framework builds choice networks in three steps. First, it searches for representative logic cones—typical patterns in the logic that needs hardware mapping. Second, it uses equality saturation (exploring every mathematically equivalent form of a structure and different ways to reorganize it) to generate diverse alternatives. Third, it ranks candidate choices by quality and keeps only the best ones, discarding the low-quality options that initial simulation and SAT solving would normally retain.

## Key Novelty
CRISTAL improves choice networks for technology mapping by using equality saturation to explore different structural forms, then selecting only the highest-quality options rather than keeping all simulation results.

## Contributions
- Novel CRISTAL methodology for Boolean choice network construction
- Representative logic cone search technique
- Equality saturation for structural mutation and diversity
- Priority-ranking choice selection and validation
