# sc-2025-040: Improving Supercomputer Usage with Aging Awareness

## Summary
Proposes aging-aware scheduling strategies to extend device lifetime in HPC systems through frequency scaling, trading performance for reliability and reduced environmental footprint. Evaluates robustness to aging model uncertainty.

## Problem
- Device aging impacts environmental footprint (lifecycle carbon)
- High GPU demand makes availability critical
- Extending device lifetime reduces cost and environmental impact
- Aging is strongly temperature-dependent (Arrhenius equation)

## Method
1. **Aging model**: Temperature-frequency relationship for device aging
2. **Scheduling algorithm**: Dynamic frequency reconfiguration to optimize:
   - Total FLOPs executed before failure
   - Device lifetime extension
3. **Baseline**: Compare with constant-frequency scheduling
4. **Robustness**: Evaluate performance under different aging models

## Key Innovations
1. **Aging-aware scheduling**: First to incorporate aging lifetime models in HPC scheduling
2. **Performance-reliability trade-off**: Quantifies FLOP gains vs. lifetime extension
3. **Robustness analysis**: Evaluates algorithm performance under model inaccuracy
4. **Practical relevance**: Addresses real constraint (GPU scarcity)

## Hardware Targets
- CPUs (Intel, AMD)
- NVIDIA GPUs
- AMD GPUs
- Generic HPC accelerators

## Results (from abstract)
- Aging-aware scheduling can significantly increase total FLOP output
- Trade-off: Performance reduction acceptable for lifetime extension
- Robustness to aging model inaccuracy is critical consideration
