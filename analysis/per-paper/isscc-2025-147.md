# ISSCC-2025-147: Fast-Settling Low-Spur mmWave LO for JCAS

**Problem:**  
Joint Communication-and-Sensing (JCAS) systems using SC-OFDM require LOs with fast frequency-hopping settling (<18ns for IEEE 802.11ay) and low spurs; traditional LOs cannot meet both stringent requirements.

**Solution:**  
60GHz SSB mixer-based LO with integrated LC oscillator (ILO) filter; I/Q calibration and rapid frequency-hop architecture achieve sub-nanosecond settling with -56dBc worst-case spurs.

**Key Results:**  
- Sub-nanosecond settling time
- -56 dBc worst-case spur level
- 60GHz operation for JCAS

**Key Innovation:**  
First SSB-mixer LO achieving sub-ns settling while maintaining low-spur performance through ILO filtering, enabling mmWave SC-OFDM frequency hopping.
