# isscc-2025-134: Miniature Wireless OECT-Based Electrochemical Sensing SoC

**Problem:** Miniaturized electrochemical sensor implants for long-term biomarker monitoring face challenges in achieving high sensitivity, wide dynamic range, and spatial resolution while remaining small enough for implantation; traditional three-electrode (3E) systems and ISFETs suffer from low scalability and limited channel integration.

**Motivation:** Chronic disease management (autoimmune, cardiovascular, cancer) requires continuous monitoring of inflammatory cytokines (TNF-alpha, IL-6); miniaturized wireless implant systems enable less-invasive long-term monitoring.

**Method:** The millimeter-scale SoC integrates 16 channels of organic electrochemical transistors (OECTs) for direct biomarker sensing with wireless readout, replacing conventional 3E/ISFET approaches to enable higher scalability and reduced system size while managing OECT high static current demands through optimized sensor interface design.

**Key Novelty:** Integrated wireless 16-channel OECT-based electrochemical sensing SoC achieving 0.1pM limit-of-detection with millimeter-scale form factor.

**Contributions:**
- Millimeter-sized wireless electrochemical sensing SoC
- 16-channel OECT-based biomarker detection
- 0.1pM limit-of-detection sensitivity
- Optimized sensor interface managing OECT power consumption
