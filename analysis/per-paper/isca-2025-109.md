# isca-2025-109 — Phi: Leveraging Pattern-based Hierarchical Sparsity for High-Efficiency Spiking Neural Networks

**Venue:** ISCA  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Spiking Neural Network (SNN) accelerators exploit binary activation sparsity (0-1 spikes) to skip zero computations, but they ignore the structured distribution patterns present in spike activations, leaving significant compute savings unexploited. Existing designs treat each non-zero spike independently, incurring redundant runtime multiply-accumulate operations.

## Motivation
SNNs promise energy-efficient neuromorphic inference, but without exploiting inherent spike pattern structure, the achievable speedup and energy savings over conventional DNN accelerators are limited, stalling the transition of SNNs to practical deployment.

## Method
Phi introduces a two-level hierarchical sparsity framework for SNNs. Level 1 is vector-wise sparsity: spike activations are represented using a small set of pre-defined patterns selected offline via k-means clustering, enabling offline pre-computation of pattern-weight products so that most runtime multiply-accumulates are eliminated. Level 2 is element-wise sparsity: a highly sparse residual matrix (the complement of the Level 1 approximation) handles remaining activations, further reducing computation while preserving accuracy. A pattern-aware fine-tuning technique is applied to increase Level 2 sparsity. The Phi hardware architecture processes both levels on-the-fly, with dedicated datapaths for each sparsity tier.

## Key Novelty
A two-level hierarchical sparsity scheme that decomposes spike activations into pre-computable pattern vectors plus a highly sparse residual, enabling offline pattern-weight precomputation to eliminate the bulk of SNN runtime arithmetic.

## Contributions
- Observation and characterization of structured pattern distributions in SNN binary spike activations.
- Two-level (vector-wise + element-wise) hierarchical sparsity framework with k-means-based pattern selection and pattern-aware fine-tuning.
- Phi hardware architecture with dedicated datapaths for both sparsity levels, processing activations on-the-fly.
- 3.45x speedup and 4.93x energy-efficiency improvement over state-of-the-art SNN accelerators.
