# isscc-2025-011: Compact SD-PWM Source-Driver IC

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)

## Problem
High-resolution mobile displays require many source-driver channels, increasing area and cost. Conventional interpolation sub-DACs suffer from nonlinearity (Gm) or require area-hungry capacitors/high-voltage devices.

## Approach
10-bit source-driver IC with **delta-sigma PWM (SD-PWM)** for low-voltage digital interpolation:
- Replaces analog interpolation circuits with digital modulation
- Reduces capacitor and HV transistor overhead
- Maintains high linearity

## Key Contribution
SD-PWM enables compact low-voltage DAC (1884μm²/channel) without auxiliary capacitors or high-voltage blocks.

## Performance
- Resolution: 10-bit
- Area: 1884 μm²/channel
- Interpolation: Low-voltage delta-sigma modulation
- Application: Mobile OLED/LCD displays
