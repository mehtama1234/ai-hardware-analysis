# ISSCC 2025-205: High-Density Shield-Less Die-to-Die Transceiver

## Core Contribution
Single-ended SBD transceiver achieving 64Gb/s per wire (10.5Tb/s/mm/layer) with integrated echo, NEXT, and FEXT cancellation, enabling shield-less ultra-high-density chiplet interconnects.

## Technical Approach
- **Signaling**: Single-ended simultaneous bi-directional (SBD) at 64Gb/s per wire
- **Echo Cancellation**: Analog circuit suppressing near-end reflection from local transmitter
- **Crosstalk Cancellation**: Dual approach for NEXT (near-end) and FEXT (far-end) coupling
- **Channel**: Shield-less on-chip 3mm copper routing (no ground shielding saves area)
- **Modulation**: Likely NRZ or low-complexity PAM (exact not specified)

## Performance Metrics
- **Data Rate**: 64Gb/s per wire
- **Throughput**: 10.5Tb/s/mm/layer (SBD doubles effective bandwidth vs. unidirectional)
- **Channel**: 3mm on-chip routing
- **Bit Error Rate**: <10^-16 (margin-bound for systems, ~4+ dB margin typical)
- **Technology**: 28nm CMOS

## Baselines
- Ground-shielded channels (4–6Tb/s/mm/layer typical, doubled area)
- Prior SBD with echo cancellation only (vulnerable to NEXT/FEXT)
- Digital equalization (higher latency, power overhead)

## Hardware Impact
Enables ultra-dense chiplet networks for AI/HPC multi-die systems (100+ Tb/s intra-package). Removes ground shield tax, allowing more signal wires in same footprint—critical for high-bandwidth low-latency L3 cache and memory stacks.
