# Ab-initio Quantum Transport with the GW Approximation, 42, 240 Atoms, and Sustained Exascale Performance

**ID**: sc-2025-376
**Venue**: SC
**Confidence**: low

## Problem
Computational intractability of quantum transport with electron-electron interactions

## Motivation
Nanodevice design requires advanced quantum effects modeling

## Method
The method pairs NEGF (non-equilibrium Green's functions, a quantum technique for tracking electron flow) with the GW approximation (a more accurate way to account for how electrons interact with each other). It handles the enormous computational burden by using spatial domain decomposition—splitting the system into smaller regions, solving each separately, then combining the results. This let them maintain full quantum accuracy while simulating 42,240 atoms at exascale speed.

## Key Novelty
First successful demonstration of GW-approximation NEGF for NRFET-scale quantum transport simulations at exascale performance.
