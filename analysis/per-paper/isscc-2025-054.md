# isscc-2025-054: Integrated-Driver Optical Retimer for AI/ML I/O

**Title:** A 212Gb/s PAM-4 Retimer with Integrated High-Swing Optical Driver and Chip-to-Module Long Reach Capability of 40dB in 5nm FinFET

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Core Problem:**
Hyperscale AI/ML systems need 200+Gb/s optical I/O; chip-to-module paths have 40dB loss; external laser drivers add power, cost, and complexity.

**Method:**
4-lane 212.5Gb/s/lane PAM-4 retimer with integrated high-swing optical driver on TX, receiver-side 40dB C2M equalization, all within power budget.

**Key Innovation:**
Integrated high-swing optical driver eliminating external laser driver.

**Metrics:**
- **Per-Lane Rate:** 212.5 Gb/s
- **Total Throughput:** 850 Gb/s (4-lane)
- **Modulation:** PAM-4
- **C2M Equalization:** 40 dB
- **Process:** 5 nm FinFET

**Architecture:** PAM-4 retimer + integrated laser driver

**Techniques:** Interconnect design, circuit design

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)
