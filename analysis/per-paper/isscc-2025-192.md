# isscc-2025-192: Low-Latency 200Gb/s Heterogeneous Transceiver

**Problem**: AI data-centers need 200G/lane links, but linear-drive pluggable optics face severe VSR channel loss (-14dB at 200G), and retimed solutions with DSP introduce unacceptable latency for AI inference.

**Solution**: Heterogeneous 0.13µm SiGe BiCMOS + 28nm CMOS transceiver with non-ADC receiver architecture. SiGe analog front-end handles VSR equalization without DSP; 28nm CMOS provides clock/data recovery, eliminating latency penalty.

**Key Results**:
- 200Gb/s PAM-4 transmission
- Non-ADC architecture (no DSP latency)
- VSR insertion loss (-14dB) handled in analog domain
- Energy efficiency comparable to retimed solutions
- Significantly lower latency for AI inference

**Hardware**: ASIC (heterogeneous)  
**Application**: AI data-center / pluggable optics  
**Confidence**: high (detailed abstract)
