# ExactMap: Enhancing Delay Optimization in Parallel ASIC Technology Mapping

**Paper ID:** iccad-2025-231  
**Venue:** ICCAD

## Problem

Existing sequential ASIC technology mappers neglect load information during mapping, leading to significant discrepancy between estimated and actual cell delays and suboptimal performance. Sequential algorithms cannot exploit modern multicore and GPU parallelism.

## Motivation

Timing-critical applications require faster optimization with better delay prediction; modern hardware supports massive parallelism that existing sequential mappers cannot leverage.

## Method

Proposes ExactMap, a parallel ASIC technology mapper that: (1) considers load information of intermediate solutions to identify critical nodes, (2) selects delay-oriented cells based on load-aware delay estimation, (3) employs dual recovery method for both delay and area optimization, and (4) integrates optimization within configuration-level parallelism framework on GPU.

## Key Novelty

First parallel ASIC technology mapper incorporating load-aware delay estimation and achieving 32% average delay reduction with 65.33× speedup on GPU.

## Contributions

- Load-aware delay estimation enabling better delay-critical cell selection
- Dual recovery method balancing delay and area optimization
- GPU-based configuration-level parallelism achieving 65.33× speedup
- 32% average delay reduction with only 3% area penalty vs. ABC synthesis tool
