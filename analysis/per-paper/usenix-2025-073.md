# GREYHOUND: Hunting Fail-Slows in Hybrid-Parallel Training at Scale

**Problem:** Fail-slows (stragglers) are transient slowdowns in large-scale hybrid-parallel GPU training lasting from sub-minutes to nearly ten hours, caused by slow computations or communications due to contention, device degradation, or network congestion. Current manual detection and checkpoint-restart failover approaches are time-consuming and ineffective.

**Method:** GREYHOUND automatically detects when GPUs or network links slow down during hybrid-parallel training by continuously monitoring performance, achieving over 99% accuracy. When slowdowns occur due to device degradation, system contention, or network congestion, GREYHOUND automatically applies multiple corrective strategies that adapt to the specific slowdown, without requiring engineers to pause training and restart from a checkpoint. The system responds on-the-fly during training, eliminating manual intervention and the downtime of checkpoint-restart failover.

**Novelty:** GREYHOUND automatically fixes GPU and network slowdowns using multiple strategies without manual intervention or checkpoint-restart.
