# GREYHOUND: Hunting Fail-Slows in Hybrid-Parallel Training at Scale

**Problem:** Fail-slows (stragglers) are transient slowdowns in large-scale hybrid-parallel GPU training lasting from sub-minutes to nearly ten hours, caused by slow computations or communications due to contention, device degradation, or network congestion. Current manual detection and checkpoint-restart failover approaches are time-consuming and ineffective.

**Method:** GREYHOUND automatically identifies slow GPUs and communication links through a detection mechanism achieving >99% accuracy. It applies a novel multi-level mitigation strategy to address identified stragglers without human intervention or checkpoint-restart overhead. The system monitors during hybrid-parallel training and adaptively responds to detected slowdowns.

**Novelty:** An automated multi-level mitigation mechanism that handles stragglers without requiring checkpoint-restart failover or manual operator intervention.
