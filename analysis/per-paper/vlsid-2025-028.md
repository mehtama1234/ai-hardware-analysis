# An Innovative Solution to Improve Ultra Low Voltage Writability and Leakage in GPU SRAMs

**Venue:** VLSID

## Problem
GPU SRAM scaling to 3nm fails at ultra-low voltage (ULV); write failures and high leakage due to bit-cell scaling

## Motivation
LLM training demands GPU scaling; mobile power optimization requires ULV operation; SRAM scaling bottleneck

## Method
The paper presents circuit modifications to 3nm GPU SRAM bit cells—the tiny storage units in graphics processors—to solve two problems that arise at ultra-low voltage (reduced power for energy efficiency): write failures and leakage current (power wasted through unwanted electrical flow). By adjusting how transistors are sized and connected within each cell, the approach improves the cell's ability to reliably store new data while reducing power loss.

## Key Novelty
The innovation is applying circuit design changes to 3nm GPU SRAM bit cells to enable reliable writes and reduce power leakage when operating at ultra-low voltage.
