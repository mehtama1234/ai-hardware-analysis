# Device-Algorithm Co-Design of Ferroelectric Compute-in-Memory In-Situ Annealer for Combinatorial Optimization Problems

**Venue:** DAC | **Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem & Motivation
Combinatorial optimization requires O(n²) Ising energy computation and complex exponential calculations; hardware costs and iteration overhead are prohibitive for large instances.

## Core Method
DG FeFET-based CiM crossbars with novel incremental-E transformation reducing VMV from O(n²) to O(n); simplified fractional approximation of exponential annealing factors enables in-situ computation.

## Key Contribution
Incremental-E transformation reducing complexity to O(n) paired with ferroelectric CiM substrate for native in-situ annealing.

## Results
- 1503–1716× energy reduction on 3000-node Max-Cut
- 8.08–8.15× speedup
- 98% success rate vs 50% for other annealers
