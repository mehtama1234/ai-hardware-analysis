# dac-2025-428: Similarity-Aware Attention Acceleration for 3D Video Generation

**Problem:** 3D video generation >75% attention compute incurs high latency/energy.

**Motivation:** Video compression exploits patch similarity; token similarity in attention can reduce compute similarly.

**Method:** SimPicker co-design combines (1) speculation-based similarity at frame/token level, (2) LUT-based FP-INT multiplier, (3) adaptive grouping for workload balance.

**Key Novelty:** Speculation-based similarity detection + LUT-based FP-INT multiplication for efficient 3D attention.

**Contributions:**
- Coarse- and fine-grained similarity exploitation with dynamic adaptation
- LUT-based FP-INT multiplier reducing bank conflicts
- 5.21x–1.45x speedup vs A100; 17.92x–1.63x energy efficiency

**Hardware Target:** GPU, ASIC

**Workloads:** Diffusion, vision

**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
