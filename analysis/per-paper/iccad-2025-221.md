# When Semi-Supervised LVM Meets Frequency-Based Critical Layout Pattern Selection

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-221  
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Identifying critical lithography patterns for process optimization requires manual annotation, which is time-consuming and subjective.

## Motivation
Automated pattern criticality detection reduces manual effort and improves consistency in lithography pattern selection.

## Method
The approach uses semi-supervised learning (training on a mix of labeled examples and unlabeled data to reduce manual work) combined with Vision Transformers, an AI model designed to understand images by breaking them into visual patches and learning their relationships. It analyzes lithography patterns in two ways at once: by looking at the images directly and by examining their frequency characteristics (which reveal repetitive structures and potential defects). This dual perspective helps identify which patterns are hardest to manufacture reliably.

## Key Novelty
Instead of manual expert annotation, it combines semi-supervised learning with Vision Transformers and frequency analysis to automatically detect which lithography patterns are critical for manufacturing quality.

## Contributions

1. Semi-supervised ViT-based pattern classifier
2. Frequency-based criticality analysis
3. Eliminates manual pattern annotation
4. Replaces manual process
