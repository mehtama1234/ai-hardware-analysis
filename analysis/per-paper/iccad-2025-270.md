# CNOT Oriented Synthesis for Small-Scale Boolean Functions Using Spatial Structures of Parallelotopes

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-270

## Problem
Quantum circuit scalability is limited by gate fidelity and qubit counts in the NISQ era, restricting physical implementations to small-scale circuits. Existing logic network structures for quantum circuit synthesis neglect the spatial structure intrinsic to Boolean functions.

## Motivation
To enhance synthesis efficiency and reduce circuit complexity for small-scale Boolean functions by leveraging spatial structures encoded in parallelotopes.

## Method
SSHR (Spatial Structure-based Hypercube Reduction) extracts global spatial features from parallelotopes embedded in hypercubes to minimize Multi-Control Toffoli gates. Two variants are introduced: SSHR-H uses heuristic functions to accelerate runtime, while SSHR-I integrates an Integer Linear Programming solver to maximize spatial structure utilization.

## Key Novelty
Uses spatial structure of Boolean functions encoded by parallelotopes to access a broader optimization space for quantum circuit synthesis.

## Contributions
- Novel SSHR synthesis method tailored for small-scale Boolean functions (≤8 qubits)
- Two variants with different optimization strategies: heuristic-based and ILP-based approaches
- 56% and 81% reductions in CNOT gate counts compared to ESOP and XAG methods
