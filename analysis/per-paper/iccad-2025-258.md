# CorDBA: Corners Decoupled Bayesian Approach for yield optimization

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**Paper ID:** iccad-2025-258

## Problem Statement
Yield optimization for advanced nodes is challenging due to limited resources and high cost of transistor-level simulation in yield estimation.

## Motivation
Process variations create complex yield-performance relationships; simulations are expensive at advanced nodes, limiting optimization iterations.

## Method
CorDBA avoids expensive transistor-level simulations by separating yield improvement from yield measurement. It uses statistical process-variation corners (predicted worst-case manufacturing scenarios) to identify where circuits might fail. Then it optimizes using max-minimum optimization, which focuses on making even the worst-performing chips as robust as possible. This multi-stage approach lets designers refine designs without constantly re-running expensive simulations.

## Key Novelty
The key idea is decoupling—improving yield without expensive simulations by using statistical process predictions and optimizing for the worst-performing chips.

## Contributions

1. 3.6× SPICE simulation speedup
2. 134.7× robustness improvement
3. Multi-stage sequential optimization
