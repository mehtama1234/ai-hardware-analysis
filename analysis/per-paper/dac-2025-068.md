# Towards Training Robustness Against Dynamic Errors in Quantum Machine Learning

**Venue:** DAC | **Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Quantum machine learning training assumes static error rates in QNNs, but quantum noise is dynamic across time and qubits, causing performance degradation even when aggregate error rates appear similar.

## Motivation
NISQ-era quantum machine learning requires robust training strategies that account for dynamic, heterogeneous error conditions across different qubits and time-varying noise profiles.

## Method
A novel noise-aware training strategy dynamically adjusts to standard and fatal error conditions during QNN optimization, incorporating low-complexity fatal-error detection to identify problematic error patterns and adapt training accordingly.

## Key Novelty
Dynamic noise-aware training for quantum neural networks that adapts to time-varying and qubit-specific error conditions.

## Contributions
- Novel dynamic NAT strategy addressing time-varying and heterogeneous quantum noise
- Low-complexity fatal-error detection method for QNN optimization
- Maintains competitive performance with leading NAT methods across varying error scenarios
- Improved robustness against dynamic error conditions in NISQ devices

## Metrics
- Accuracy: Competitive performance with leading NAT methods

## Baselines
- Static noise-aware training methods, Leading NAT approaches

## Limitations
Evaluation on simulated quantum noise without real quantum hardware.
