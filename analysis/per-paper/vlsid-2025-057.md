# 2D Thermal Contour Modeling of 14 nm SOI FinFET Using Machine Learning for Efficient Thermal Profile Prediction

**ID**: vlsid-2025-057 | **Venue**: VLSID | **Confidence**: low (abstract only)

## Problem Statement
SOI FinFETs experience severe self-heating issues due to poor thermal conductivity of the buried oxide (BOX) in silicon-on-insulator substrates. FEM-based thermal simulations are computationally expensive, limiting design iteration and thermal optimization cycles.

## Motivation
Thermal management is critical for device reliability and performance at advanced nodes. ML-based thermal surrogate models could enable rapid thermal-aware device design without expensive FEM simulations.

## Method
- **Approach**: FEM-Supplemented ML (FSML)
- **Models**: 
  - Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) for spatial feature extraction
  - Variational Autoencoder (VAE) for compact thermal representation
- **Training Data**: 2D thermal contours from Synopsys TCAD FEM simulator
- **Technology**: 14 nm SOI FinFET
- **Evaluation Metrics**: MSE, accuracy, loss, IoU, SSIM
