# dac-2025-119: VQT-CiM – Ferroelectric CiM Accelerator for Vector-Quantized Transformers

## Quick Summary
VQT-CiM uses vector quantization (RVQ+PVQ) to convert dynamic transformer attention VMMs into static operations, enabling write-free FeFET-based CiM acceleration. Achieves 3.54× energy efficiency and 4.53× throughput vs. NVM-based CiM designs.

## Key Insight
Quantize transformer keys/values to fixed codebooks so attention matrix multiplications become pure reads on CiM crossbars—eliminating expensive write-back cycles and compute-write-compute dependencies.

## Hardware & Technique
- **Target**: ASIC, CiM (Compute-in-Memory)
- **Techniques**: Quantization, near-data-processing, circuit-design (ferroelectric)
- **Workloads**: LLM inference, transformers, attention

## Results
- 3.54× energy efficiency gain
- 4.53× throughput improvement
- vs. NVM-based CiM transformer baselines
