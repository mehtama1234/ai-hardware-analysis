# MMCircuitEval: A Comprehensive Multimodal Circuit-Focused Benchmark for Evaluating LLMs

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**Paper ID:** iccad-2025-267

## Problem Statement
Existing LLM evaluation benchmarks lack comprehensive coverage of EDA tasks; multimodal LLMs need assessment across diverse circuit design stages and abilities.

## Motivation
MLLMs show promise for EDA automation, but comprehensive evaluation across design stages from front-end to back-end is missing.

## Method
MMCircuitEval assembles 3,614 question-answer pairs combining text and circuit diagrams, drawn from textbooks, datasheets, and technical documentation reviewed by experts. These pairs cover both digital and analog circuits across the entire circuit design process—from basic knowledge questions through design specifications, front-end work (initial design), and back-end work (physical layout). Each question is categorized by design stage, circuit type, the skill it tests, and difficulty level.

## Key Novelty
This is the first comprehensive benchmark combining text and circuit diagrams for electronic circuit design, spanning all design stages and both digital and analog circuits.

## Contributions

1. 3614 curated QA pairs
2. Expert review for accuracy
3. Categorization by stage/type/ability/difficulty
