# Investigating Impact of Bit-flip Errors in Control Electronics on Quantum Computation

**Venue:** VLSID

## Problem
Bit-flip errors in FPGA control electronics (quantum pulse memory) degrade quantum gate fidelity

## Motivation
FPGA controls quantum systems; environmental stresses cause bit flips; need robust representation for quantum gates

## Method
Analyze bit-flip sensitivity in floating-point vs fixed-point quantum pulse representations; test on IBM simulators

## Key Novelty
Fixed-point representation shows superior robustness to bit-flips vs floating-point for quantum control

## Contributions
- Floating-point sensitivity analysis (~200% TVD increase)
- Fixed-point robustness demonstration
- Data representation guidance
