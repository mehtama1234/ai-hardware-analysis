# Uncertainty-Aware Energy Management for Wearable IoT Devices with Conformal Prediction

**Venue:** DAC | **ID:** dac-2025-126

## Core Problem
Energy management for battery-powered wearable IoT devices with stochastic ambient energy harvesting; existing point predictions ignore uncertainty.

## Method
Conformal prediction framework with provable coverage guarantees for energy harvest; Monte Carlo sampling-based energy management exploring uncertainty regions; lightweight ML aggregation for near-optimal decisions.

## Key Novelty
Conformal prediction with quantified uncertainty for probabilistic energy management decisions.

## Contributions
- Conformal prediction for energy harvest with provable >90% coverage
- Monte Carlo sampling-based energy decision optimization
- Lightweight ML aggregation model
- Average 2 J distance from optimal Oracle
