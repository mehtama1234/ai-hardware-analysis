# Uncertainty-Aware Energy Management for Wearable IoT Devices with Conformal Prediction

**Venue:** DAC | **ID:** dac-2025-126

## Core Problem
Energy management for battery-powered wearable IoT devices with stochastic ambient energy harvesting; existing point predictions ignore uncertainty.

## Method
The approach uses conformal prediction—a technique that generates ranges of possible future energy harvest with mathematical guarantees they'll contain the actual value a set percentage of the time. Instead of making a single energy-management guess, it tests multiple decisions against many possible energy scenarios sampled from these ranges (Monte Carlo sampling), then combines the results using a lightweight machine-learning model to determine a near-optimal energy strategy.

## Key Novelty
The core idea is using conformal prediction to quantify energy-harvest uncertainty and enable energy-management decisions based on ranges of probable values rather than single guesses, with mathematical guarantees the ranges cover real outcomes.

## Contributions
- Conformal prediction for energy harvest with provable >90% coverage
- Monte Carlo sampling-based energy decision optimization
- Lightweight ML aggregation model
- Average 2 J distance from optimal Oracle
