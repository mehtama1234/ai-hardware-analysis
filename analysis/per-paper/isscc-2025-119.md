# isscc-2025-119: UCIe Chiplet Interface with Matched-Delay Clock Gating

**Problem:** Dynamic clock gating in chiplet interconnects causes voltage droop-induced Di/Dt surge, leading to sampling misalignment and bit errors.

**Method:** A 32Gb/s, 64-lane UCIe interface uses matched-delay architecture (internal pathways with identical timing characteristics) to maintain synchronized clock and data signals during dynamic power gating. When power switches on or off, voltage droops (temporary dips in supply voltage) normally cause timing delays to shift unpredictably, misaligning clock and data at the sampling point. The matched-delay design ensures both signals experience the same delay shifts, keeping them aligned and preventing bit errors from voltage variations.

**Key Novelty:** Matched-delay clock gating architecture eliminating droop-induced sampling errors in high-density UCIe interfaces.

**Main Results:** 32Gb/s per lane, 10.5Tb/s/mm density, 0.6pJ/b efficiency, robust dynamic power-gating.
