# Grasp: Group-based Prediction of Activation Sparsity for Fast LLM Inference

**Venue:** DAC | **Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem & Motivation
ReLU-fied LLMs enable sparsity by skipping zero-activation computations, but existing prediction methods struggle with distribution outliers, limiting skipping efficiency and speedup.

## Core Method
Grasp: magnitude-based, training-free sparsity predictor for ReLU-fied LLMs. Groups vector elements by distribution and explicitly handles outliers for more accurate per-element impact estimation. Enables efficient zero-activation identification and memory/compute skipping.

## Key Contribution
Group-based magnitude prediction with outlier handling for improved activation sparsity in ReLU-fied LLMs.

## Results
- 11% higher sparsity prediction accuracy than SOTA
- 1.85× speedup vs dense inference
