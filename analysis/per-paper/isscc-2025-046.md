# isscc-2025-046: 3nm Dual-Port SRAM with Far-End Write-Assist

**Title:** 29.5 A 3nm 3.6GHz Dual-Port SRAM with Backend-RC Optimization and a Far-End Write-Assist Scheme

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Core Problem:**
Dual-port SRAM at 3nm scaling suffers from severe RC-induced delays, complex asynchronous dual-clock timing, and marginal write operations requiring novel assist techniques.

**Method:**
Array dual-rail voltage architecture (VDI for periphery, VDDM for arrays) with novel far-end write-assist circuit and refined wakeup scheme to maintain voltage relationships and write margins.

**Key Innovation:**
Far-end write-assist scheme enabling sub-500mV write margin in dual-port SRAM.

**Metrics:**
- **Vmin:** 495 mV @ 95% yield (−40°C)
- **Frequency:** 3.6 GHz @ 1.0V
- **Cell Area:** 0.052 µm² (improved DTCO scaling vs. SP-SRAM)

**Architecture:** Array dual-rail with peripheral/array voltage domains

**Techniques:** Circuit design, memory-system optimization, power management

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)
