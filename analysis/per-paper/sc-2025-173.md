# Core Hours and Carbon Credits: Incentivizing Sustainability in HPC

**Venue:** SC

## Problem
HPC users are largely unaware of energy consumption and lack motivation to choose efficient resources; resource provider efficiency efforts miss user-side optimization

## Motivation
Incentivize user-driven sustainability through energy-aware accounting methods

## Method
The researchers surveyed 300 HPC users to understand their energy awareness. They created two billing approaches—one charging based on direct energy consumption, another based on carbon emissions—to test whether charging users for computing resources would motivate them to choose more efficient options. Both approaches were evaluated through computer simulations and with actual user studies.

## Key Novelty
Directly charging users for their energy consumption—rather than just showing them feedback—reduced energy use by 40%.

## Contributions
- Evidence that only 30% of HPC users are energy-aware
- Two novel multi-resource accounting methods (energy and carbon-based)
- User study showing 40% energy reduction under cost-based incentives
