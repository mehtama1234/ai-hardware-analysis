# micro-2025-091: A Probabilistic Perspective on Tiling Sparse Tensor Algebra

**Venue:** MICRO 2025  
**Authors:** Ritvik Sharma, Zi Yu Xue, Nathan Zhang, Rubens Lacouture, Fredrik Kjolstad, Sara Achour, Mark Horowitz  
**Confidence:** low (title/abstract only — abstract body was unavailable)

## Problem
Tiling sparse tensor algebra for cache efficiency is difficult because effective tile size and cache behavior depend on sparse data distribution, which varies across inputs and is unknown at compile time.

## Motivation
Sparse tensor operations underlie graph analytics, scientific computing, and ML workloads; poor tiling decisions cause cache thrashing and memory bandwidth bottlenecks.

## Method
The paper takes a probabilistic approach to sparse tensor tiling, modeling sparsity patterns probabilistically to predict cache behavior and guide tile-size selection at compile time or runtime. Specific algorithmic details not available from the abstract alone.

## Key Novelty
A probabilistic framework for analyzing and selecting tile sizes in sparse tensor algebra that accounts for input-dependent sparsity distributions.

## Contributions
1. Probabilistic model for cache behavior of sparse tensor algebra under varying sparsity patterns
2. Tiling strategy guided by sparsity probability models rather than static heuristics
