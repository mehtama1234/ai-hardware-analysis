# Pathfinder: Constructing Cycle-accurate Taint Graphs for Analyzing Information Flow Traces

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-011

## Problem
Hardware information flow tracking generates massive violation traces requiring manual analysis of hundreds/thousands of signals to identify root causes.

## Motivation
Automated root cause identification in IFT traces dramatically reduces verification burden for detecting security vulnerabilities.

## Method
Pathfinder constructs taint graphs (maps showing how information flows through a circuit at each clock cycle) by combining two analysis techniques: taint tracking (which follows how data moves) and self-composition (which compares system states to spot differences). The system creates these graphs at cycle-accurate precision, tracking information flow at each step. From violation traces (records of unwanted information flows), it automatically extracts only the relevant signals, eliminating the need to manually analyze hundreds or thousands of signals.

## Key Novelty
It automatically generates cycle-accurate maps of information flow from violation records, eliminating the manual analysis work that would otherwise require searching through hundreds of signals.

## Contributions
1. Theoretical foundation unifying taint tracking and self-composition
2. Automated taint graph generation from simulators or formal model checkers
3. Signal relevance extraction reducing manual analysis 1.6-769.9x
4. Case studies demonstrating effectiveness on constant-time, Trojans, Spectre violations
