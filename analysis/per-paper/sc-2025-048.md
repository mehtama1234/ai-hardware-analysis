## Motivation
Support SC25 Reproducibility Initiative; evaluate artifact quality of DRIM-ANN paper

## Method
The reproducibility evaluation independently tests the DRIM-ANN approximate nearest neighbor search engine by re-running its experiments following SC25 Artifact Description and Evaluation standards. Researchers verify that the code, datasets, and experimental procedures match what was published, checking whether others can achieve the same performance results. The process documents what reproduces correctly, where results differ, and what information was missing or unclear in the original paper. This systematic verification confirms whether DRIM-ANN's claimed speedups and accuracy on DRAM-PIMs (memory chips with built-in processors) hold up under independent testing.

## Key Novelty
This work validates whether the DRIM-ANN approximate nearest neighbor search engine's results can be independently reproduced by other researchers.
