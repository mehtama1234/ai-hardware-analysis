# Swift: Fast Performance Tuning with GAN-Generated Configurations

**Problem:** Manual system configuration tuning requires extensive expertise and exhaustive search over large configuration spaces. Traditional methods like grid search and random search are inefficient at discovering optimal performance settings across different hardware platforms and workloads.

**Method:** Swift uses two competing neural networks (a Generative Adversarial Network, or GAN) to explore performance configurations efficiently. One network proposes candidate settings, while the other evaluates their quality; through this adversarial process, they iteratively find good configurations. This approach is faster than trying every possible combination because the generator learns to focus on promising areas rather than searching blindly.

**Novelty:** Swift uses two competing neural networks—one generating candidate system configurations and one judging their quality—instead of traditional exhaustive search methods to find optimal performance settings.
