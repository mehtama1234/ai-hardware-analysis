# hpca-2025-098 — Exploring the Performance Improvement of Tensor Processing Engines through Transformation in the Bit-weight Dimension of MACs

**Venue:** HPCA  
**Authors:** Qizhe Wu, Huawen Liang, Yuchen Gui, Zhichen Zeng, Zerong He, Linfeng Tao, Xiaotian Wang, Letian Zhao  
**Confidence:** low (abstract only)

## Problem
Existing TPE designs for GEMM focus on dataflow and operand reuse at the matrix level but neglect optimization opportunities within the MAC microarchitecture. No systematic framework exists for reasoning about and transforming the bit-level structure of MACs.

## Motivation
As GEMM becomes the dominant compute primitive for AI, TPEs are increasingly critical for GPUs and DSAs, making MAC-level efficiency gains multiplicatively impactful.

## Method
The paper introduces a mathematical notation (bit-weight dimension) that exposes the internal structure of multiply-accumulate (MAC) circuits—the core units that multiply and sum numbers. By systematically transforming how bits flow through these circuits, the authors derive four optimization techniques that improve timing performance, silicon area, or power consumption. The design is implemented in hardware simulation at 28-nanometer scale and tested on four standard tensor processor architectures plus a new bit-slice design.

## Key Novelty
A mathematical notation for the bit-weight dimension (the bit-level structure of multiply-accumulate circuits) enables four independent processor microarchitecture optimizations in addition to existing dataflow strategies.

## Contributions
- Novel bit-weight dimension perspective and finer-grained TPE notation
- Four optimization techniques targeting PE microarchitecture PPA
- 1.27-1.56x area efficiency and 1.04-1.56x energy efficiency across four classic TPE architectures
- 12.10x energy efficiency and 2.85x area efficiency vs. Laconic (bit-slice)

## Metrics
- **Energy efficiency:** 1.04-1.56x vs. classic TPEs; 12.10x vs. Laconic
- **Area efficiency:** 1.27-1.56x vs. classic TPEs; 2.85x vs. Laconic
- **Process:** SMIC-28nm
