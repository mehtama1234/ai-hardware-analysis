# e-boost: Boosted E-Graph Extraction with Adaptive Heuristics and Exact Solving

**ID:** iccad-2025-245

## Problem
E-graph extraction for circuit synthesis requires expensive ILP solving. Existing methods are computationally limited and do not scale well.

## Motivation
Faster e-graph extraction enables quicker circuit optimization and synthesis; accelerating this bottleneck improves design productivity.

## Method
Hybrid framework combining parallelized heuristics, adaptive pruning strategies, and ILP solving. Heuristics generate candidates; ILP refines optimal solutions. Achieves 558× speedup over pure ILP; 7.6-8.1% area improvement in synthesized circuits.

## Key Novelty
Adaptive pruning combined with parallel heuristics and ILP for accelerated e-graph extraction with improved synthesis quality.

## Contributions
- Parallelized heuristic e-graph extraction
- Adaptive pruning framework
- ILP integration for optimization
- 558× speedup over pure ILP
