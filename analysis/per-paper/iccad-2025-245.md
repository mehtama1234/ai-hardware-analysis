# e-boost: Boosted E-Graph Extraction with Adaptive Heuristics and Exact Solving

**ID:** iccad-2025-245

## Problem
E-graph extraction for circuit synthesis requires expensive ILP solving. Existing methods are computationally limited and do not scale well.

## Motivation
Faster e-graph extraction enables quicker circuit optimization and synthesis; accelerating this bottleneck improves design productivity.

## Method
The method combines three techniques: parallelized heuristics (fast approximation methods) generate candidate solutions quickly, adaptive pruning (smart elimination of unlikely good options based on what works) narrows the search space, and ILP solving (exact optimization that finds the mathematically best solution) refines the remaining candidates. This hybrid approach achieves a 558× speedup over pure ILP solving and produces circuits that are 7.6-8.1% smaller in area.

## Key Novelty
The core innovation is using adaptive pruning (smart elimination that learns what works) to focus expensive exact optimization on the most promising circuit candidates generated by parallel approximations.
