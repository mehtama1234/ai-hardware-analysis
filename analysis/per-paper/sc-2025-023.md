# Peachy Parallel Assignments (EduHPC 2025)

## Overview
Curation of three high-quality, instructor-tested parallel computing assignments balancing biological inspiration, simulation, and deep learning motivation.

## Problem & Motivation
- **Challenge**: Limited availability of adoptable, high-quality parallel computing assignments; instructors need inspiration and practical resources
- **Opportunity**: Curated assignments from Edu* workshops increase adoption and inspire students
- **Scope**: Diversity of assignment types (simulation, cellular automata, ML)

## Assignments

### 1. Fairy Rings (Cellular Automata)
- **Problem**: Biologically-motivated variation of Game of Life
- **Parallelization**: Spatial decomposition for cellular automata
- **Motivation**: Natural/biological systems

### 2. Flooding Simulation
- **Problem**: Simulate water flow over uneven terrain with active rainfall
- **Parallelization**: Stencil-based computation
- **Motivation**: Environmental modeling, physical simulation

### 3. Softmax Function (Deep Learning)
- **Problem**: Implement softmax in parallel
- **Parallelization**: Linear algebra, reduction operations
- **Motivation**: ML/AI connection to parallel computing

## Design Principles
- **Tested**: Demonstrated effectiveness in real courses
- **Inspiring**: Motivational problem domains (biology, environment, ML)
- **Adoptable**: Designed for easy integration by other instructors
- **Diverse**: Multiple programming paradigms and applications

## Pedagogy Insights
- Problem motivation drives student engagement
- Diverse domains (biology, simulation, ML) appeal to different learners
- Real-world applications aid comprehension

## Limitations
