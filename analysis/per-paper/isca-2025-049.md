# AQB8: Energy-Efficient Ray Tracing Accelerator through Multi-Level Quantization

**ID:** isca-2025-049
**Venue:** ISCA
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Ray tracing BVH traversal is bottlenecked by DRAM bandwidth consumed by 32-bit floating-point bounding boxes, and prior 8-bit compression schemes still require decompression back to FP32 for intersection tests, retaining the arithmetic cost of complex FP32 operations.

## Motivation
Real-time high-fidelity ray tracing requires massive ray-object intersection tests whose memory traffic and arithmetic cost scale with scene complexity, making energy and bandwidth efficiency critical for GPU RT accelerators.

## Method
AQB8 constructs a quantized BVH tree using a multi-level quantization scheme where bounding boxes are encoded as 8-bit integers at multiple hierarchy levels, enabling intersection tests to operate directly on low-bit integers without decompression. The hardware replaces FP32 arithmetic units with area-efficient integer units for BVH traversal, eliminating both DRAM bandwidth waste and expensive FP32 arithmetic. A hierarchical error-compensation mechanism propagates quantization bounds across BVH levels to prevent false-negative ray-box misses.

## Key Novelty
Multi-level quantization of BVH bounding boxes enabling end-to-end integer arithmetic during BVH traversal without any FP32 decompression step.

## Contributions
1. Multi-level quantization technique for constructing quantized BVH trees that preserves intersection correctness across hierarchy levels.
2. RT accelerator hardware (AQB8) that operates entirely on 8-bit integers during BVH traversal, eliminating FP32 arithmetic units.
3. 70% reduction in DRAM accesses and 49% energy reduction over modern GPU RT accelerators across diverse scenes.
4. 27% hardware area reduction and 1.82x performance speedup versus GPU RT baselines.
