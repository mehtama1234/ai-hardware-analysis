# isscc-2025-113: Ultra-Low-Noise Zoomed Capacitance-to-Digital Converter

**Problem:** VCO-based time-domain delta-sigma CDCs face kT/C noise exacerbation at reduced OSR and lack efficient feedforward compensation for VCO's fused integrator-quantizer.

**Method:** Zoom SAR + VCO delta-sigma fine stage; chopping-based kT/C cancellation requiring minimal amplification; add-then-subtract phase-domain lead-compensation for stable loops.

**Key Novelty:** Chopping-based kT/C cancellation with efficient phase-domain lead-compensation enabling OTA-free high-order VCO delta-sigma.

**Main Results:** 185.2dB FoMs, 8.7aFrms noise, fast measurement time, high energy efficiency.

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only analysis)
