# isscc-2025-113: Ultra-Low-Noise Zoomed Capacitance-to-Digital Converter

**Problem:** VCO-based time-domain delta-sigma CDCs face kT/C noise exacerbation at reduced OSR and lack efficient feedforward compensation for VCO's fused integrator-quantizer.

**Method:** The circuit measures capacitance in two stages: a fast rough estimate using SAR (successive approximation register, a binary-search measurement technique), then precise refinement based on oscillator timing (VCO). To reduce thermal noise that worsens at low measurement speeds, it uses chopping—repeatedly flipping the signal to shuffle noise away—requiring only minimal signal amplification, unlike conventional methods that need power-hungry amplifiers. It stabilizes the feedback loop using phase-domain lead-compensation (an 'add-then-subtract' technique for timing adjustments), enabling a stable high-order loop without reducing measurement accuracy and eliminating the need for separate amplifier circuits (OTAs).

**Key Novelty:** Chopping-based kT/C cancellation with efficient phase-domain lead-compensation enabling OTA-free high-order VCO delta-sigma.

**Main Results:** 185.2dB FoMs, 8.7aFrms noise, fast measurement time, high energy efficiency.

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only analysis)
