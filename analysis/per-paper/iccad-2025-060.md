# EqMap: FPGA LUT Remapping using E-Graphs

**Venue:** ICCAD

## Problem
FPGA technology mapping creates a gap between SAT-based exact synthesis and heuristic cut enumeration, resulting in suboptimal LUT counts and circuit depths.

## Motivation
Decades of research on technology mapping still leaves substantial room for improvement despite understanding the computational complexity.

## Method
E-graph driven compiler that enumerates circuit rewritings using e-graph representation, bridging the gap between exact SAT-based synthesis and practical heuristic methods to explore the optimization space more comprehensively.

## Key Novelty
E-graph based approach to FPGA technology mapping that combines exact and heuristic reasoning for improved LUT optimization.

## Contributions
- 12% average LUT reduction over vendor tools
- Never increases circuit depth
- Practical runtime for large designs
- Reusable compiler infrastructure for RTL equivalence checking and QoR auditing

## Hardware Target
FPGA

## Technique Categories
EDA, Compiler

## Metrics
- **Area:** 12% LUT reduction
- **Circuit Depth:** No increase

## Baselines

- SAT-based exact synthesis
- Heuristic cut enumeration

## Limitations
Evaluation limited to specific benchmark sets; practical applicability depends on design characteristics.
