# NeuraLUT-Assemble: Hardware-Aware Assembling of Sub-Neural Networks for Efficient LUT Inference

**Problem:** Existing LUT-based neural network designs suffer from accuracy degradation because the fan-in required by neurons is limited by the exponential scaling of LUT resources with input width, forcing reliance on extremely sparse models that limit connectivity.

**Method:** NeuraLUT-Assemble combines mixed-precision quantization with assembly of larger neurons from smaller LUT units to increase connectivity while keeping individual LUT fan-in manageable. Skip-connections across entire LUT structures improve gradient flow during training. This architecture achieves competitive accuracy compared to fully-connected MLP models.

**Novelty:** Hardware-aware assembly of sub-neurons from smaller LUT units combined with mixed-precision to overcome fan-in limitations.
