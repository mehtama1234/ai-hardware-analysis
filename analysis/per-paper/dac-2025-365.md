# DSPlacer: DSP Placement for FPGA-based CNN Accelerator

## Summary
Automated DSP placement framework for CNN accelerators on FPGAs, addressing timing closure through GCN identification and ILP-based optimization.

## Key Results
- **32% WNS improvement** vs Vivado 2020.2
- **65% WNS improvement** vs AMF-Placer 2.0

## Core Innovation
Combined GCN-based datapath identification, min-cost-flow assignment, and ILP cascade legalization for comprehensive DSP placement automation.

## Technique
GCN identifies datapath DSPs, min-cost-flow optimizes compact layout, ILP enforces cascade constraints and preserves datapath information between PL and PS.
