# isscc-2025-053: NeuroPilot Dual-Direction CIM Path-Finder

**Title:** 14.7 NeuroPilot: A 28nm, 69.4fJ/node and 0.22ns/node, 32×32 Mimetic-Path-Searching CIM-Macro with Dynamic-Logic Pilot PE and Dual-Direction Searching

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Core Problem:**
Path-finding in autonomous micro-robots and VLSI routing suffers from inefficient single-direction searching, poor DFF-based storage (power/area/leakage), and limited processing windows for large maps.

**Method:**
32×32 mimetic-CIM macro with dual-direction searching (DDS), dynamic-logic pilot PE with outspread pre-charge (OPC), and 3-step coarse/fine-path (TsCFP) flow for large-map navigation.

**Key Innovation:**
Dual-direction searching with dynamic-logic PE + automated coarse/fine pathfinding flow.

**Metrics:**
- **Search Rate:** 3,670 M nodes/s
- **PE Area:** 213.6 µm²
- **Energy:** 69.4 fJ/node
- **Latency:** 0.22 ns/node
- **Process:** 28 nm

**Architecture:** 32×32 CIM macro with integrated SRAM + dynamic-logic PEs

**Techniques:** Dataflow, scheduling, circuit design

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)
