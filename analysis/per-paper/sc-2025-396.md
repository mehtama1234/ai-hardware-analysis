# Network Replay and Consistency Across Testbeds

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-396
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Shared network testbeds introduce variability (jitter, loss) that degrades experiment fidelity and reproducibility; difficult to quantify testbed quality.

## Motivation
Precise network behavior reproduction is essential for reliable systems research; current testbeds lack tools to measure and compare consistency.

## Method
Choir records real network traffic and plays it back at 100 Gbps on ordinary computers, allowing researchers to recreate the exact same network conditions repeatedly. This accurate replay eliminates the randomness (jitter and packet loss) that normally makes results unpredictable on shared lab networks (called testbeds). Choir includes a consistency metric—a measurement showing whether different testbeds behave identically when given the same traffic—so researchers can assess testbed reliability.

## Key Novelty
Choir is the first tool to replay network traffic at 100 Gbps on commodity hardware while quantitatively measuring whether different testbeds produce consistent results.

## Contributions
1. 100 Gbps network replay engine for commodity hardware
2. Novel consistency metric for testbed comparison
3. Empirical demonstration that shared testbeds (e.g., FABRIC) have significantly higher variability than local bare-metal systems
