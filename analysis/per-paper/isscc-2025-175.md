# isscc-2025-175: 12GS/s 16× Time-Interleaved SAR ADC

**Title:** 24.8 A 12GS/s 9b 16× Time-Interleaved SAR ADC in 16nm FinFET

**Problem:** RF direct sampling and phased arrays need high-speed, high-SNDR ADCs without complex multi-supply integration.

**Approach:** 16-channel time-interleaved SAR ADC using standard 1.8V/0.8V supplies, 16nm FinFET process.

**Key Result:** 12GS/s, 48.1dB SNDR at 5.3GHz, 160mW, 0.25mm², simplified integration.

**Hardware:** ASIC (ADC).

**Tags:** adc, sar-adc, time-interleaved, high-speed, rf-sampling, phased-array
