# Generative Latent Diffusion for Efficient Spatiotemporal Data Reduction

**Venue:** SC  
**Theme:** Data Compression with Generative Models  
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Generative models offer compression but lack controllability and reconstruction accuracy for practical data compression; existing compressors (SZ3) have limited compression ratios

## Motivation
Scientific simulations produce massive spatiotemporal datasets; improving compression ratios while maintaining reconstruction accuracy enables better data management

## Methodology
Latent diffusion combining VAE with conditional diffusion model; compresses keyframes into latent space for interpolation of remaining frames; eliminates per-frame latent storage

## Key Novelty
First to combine VAE+diffusion for spatiotemporal compression; keyframe-only latent storage achieves higher compression ratios than prior generative methods

## Contributions
- VAE+conditional diffusion architecture for spatiotemporal compression
- Keyframe-based latent storage reducing storage overhead
- 10x compression ratio vs SZ3 across datasets
- 63% better performance vs learning-based methods at same error
