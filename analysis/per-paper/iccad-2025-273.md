# EI-TR: A Versatile Exponential Integrator Framework for Transient Analysis of Generic Nonlinear Circuits

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-273

## Problem
Application of exponential integrator (EI) methods to generic nonlinear circuits faces numerical instability and difficulty in combining with Newton iterations.

## Motivation
To provide a more efficient and stable alternative to backward differentiation formulas (BDF) for transient circuit simulation.

## Method
Exponential integrator methods (mathematical techniques for simulating circuit behavior over time) normally become numerically unstable when applied to nonlinear circuits. This framework fixes that using two techniques: implicit regularization—adjusting the system's mathematical properties (eigenvalues) to prevent a problematic numerical separation that causes instability—and truncated rational approximation to represent nonlinear behavior as a simplified mathematical function. By solving the linear and nonlinear parts separately rather than nested together, it reduces the three-level iteration structure of earlier methods to a single Newton iteration loop. This makes exponential integration methods as practical to use as conventional circuit simulation techniques.

## Key Novelty
Combining implicit regularization and truncated rational approximation to stabilize exponential integrator methods for nonlinear circuits.

## Contributions
- Novel EI-TR framework for generic nonlinear circuit transient analysis
- Implicit regularization scheme avoiding system partition instability
- 3× reduction in Newton iterations compared to EI-NK
- 6.5× larger time steps compared to traditional trapezoidal methods
