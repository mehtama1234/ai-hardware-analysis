# mlsys-2025-028 — Venn: Resource Management for Collaborative Learning Jobs

**Venue:** MLSYS 2025

**One-line problem:** Reduce average job completion time for multiple concurrent federated/collaborative learning jobs competing for overlapping pools of ephemeral, heterogeneous edge devices.

## Method
Venn is a centralized scheduler for federated learning that uses two algorithms. Intersection Resource Scheduling (IRS) groups jobs by device type, sorts jobs within each group by remaining work (shortest first), and allocates devices to the group with the most waiting jobs relative to available resources, prioritizing groups needing scarce hardware (O(max(m log m, n^2)) complexity). A tier-based matcher then partitions devices into V capability levels and randomly assigns jobs to tiers, only engaging tier-matching when it reduces job completion time (1 + c > V + c*g). Fairness logic adjusts job priorities to prevent starvation.

## Key Novelty
It formalizes and solves the Intersection Resource Scheduling problem for federated learning—where job device needs can overlap, contain, or nest within each other—using a strategy aware of device contention (how often multiple jobs need the same hardware) that jointly reduces job startup delays and response collection time.

## Metrics
- Up to 1.88x JCT improvement vs random matching
- Consistent improvements across 5 workload scenarios (Even/Small/Large/Low/High contention)
- No degradation in final model accuracy
- Negligible scheduling overhead at scale
