# mlsys-2025-028 — Venn: Resource Management for Collaborative Learning Jobs

**Venue:** MLSYS 2025

**One-line problem:** Reduce average job completion time for multiple concurrent federated/collaborative learning jobs competing for overlapping pools of ephemeral, heterogeneous edge devices.

## Method
Venn introduces a contention-aware centralized resource manager. Intersection Resource Scheduling (IRS) groups jobs by device requirements, sorts within each group by remaining demand (shortest-first), and greedily allocates intersected devices across groups based on queue-to-resource ratios, prioritizing groups with scarcer resources. A tier-based device matching algorithm partitions eligible devices by hardware capability, conditionally assigning higher-performance tiers when JCT improvement justifies scheduling delay increase. A fairness knob prevents starvation.

## Key Novelty
First CL resource manager formalizing and solving the Intersection Resource Scheduling problem — where eligible device sets may overlap, nest, or be disjoint across jobs — with a joint contention-aware heuristic that optimizes both scheduling delay and response collection time.

## Metrics
- Up to 1.88x JCT improvement vs random matching
- Consistent improvements across 5 workload scenarios (Even/Small/Large/Low/High contention)
- No degradation in final model accuracy
- Negligible scheduling overhead at scale
