# SC-2025-316: Reproducibility Report for SC25 Paper ThirstyFLOPS: Water Footprint Modeling and Analysis Toward Sustainable HPC Systems

## Summary
This is a reproducibility report from the SC25 Reproducibility Initiative Committee evaluating the artifact associated with the ThirstyFLOPS paper on water footprint modeling for sustainable HPC systems. The report assesses the quality, completeness, and reproducibility of the published work.

## Key Contributions
1. **Independent artifact evaluation** of ThirstyFLOPS water footprint model
2. **Reproducibility assessment** of sustainability measurements on HPC systems
3. **Artifact quality documentation** for future research use

## Technical Scope
- Evaluation of ThirstyFLOPS paper's artifact (code, data, experimental setup)
- Validation of water usage modeling methodology
- Assessment of reproducibility on different HPC systems

## Hardware & Systems
- Various HPC systems used in evaluation
- Data center infrastructure
- Computing centers with diverse cooling strategies

## Evaluation Details
- **Committee**: SC25 Reproducibility Committee
- **Evaluation criteria**: Artifact description and evaluation appendix standards
- **Focus**: Validation of water footprint measurement methodology

## Limitations & Scope
- Meta-evaluation paper without original research contributions
- Specific findings depend on ThirstyFLOPS paper contents
- Generalization of water usage models to different data center types not discussed
- System-specific parameters and assumptions need documentation
