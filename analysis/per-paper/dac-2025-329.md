# BitPattern: Enabling Efficient Bit-Serial Acceleration of Deep Neural Networks through Bit-Pattern Pruning

## Problem
Bit-serial DNN accelerators suffer from workload imbalance and inefficient memory access due to unstructured bit sparsity.

## Solution
Algorithm-hardware co-design: (1) bit-pattern pruning identifies regular patterns, merges similar patterns for load balancing, (2) co-designed accelerator with dedicated pattern decoder exploits structured sparsity.

## Impact
1.72x memory reduction; 2.11x speedup and 1.86x energy saving vs SOTA bit-serial accelerators with negligible accuracy loss.
