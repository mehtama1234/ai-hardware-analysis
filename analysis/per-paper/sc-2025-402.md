# Effective Node-Level Anomaly Detection in HPC Systems via Coarse-Grained Clustering and Fine-Grained Model Sharing

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-402
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Large-scale HPC systems experience performance degradation and failures; anomaly detection at node-level is challenged by massive scales, frequent job transitions, and complex patterns.

## Motivation
Unexpected failures impact scientific research; unsupervised anomaly detection avoids manual labeling overhead and adapts to evolving system behavior.

## Method
NodeSentry: unsupervised framework combining coarse-grained clustering and fine-grained model sharing for scalable node-level anomaly detection.

## Key Novelty
Novel model sharing strategy reduces training overhead 45.69% while achieving F1-score >0.876; 0.560 average improvement over baselines.

## Contributions
1. Coarse-grained clustering and fine-grained model sharing architecture
2. F1-score >0.876 on real HPC datasets
3. 45.69% reduction in training overhead vs. baselines
4. Open-source codebase and HPC-specific clustering adjustment tool
