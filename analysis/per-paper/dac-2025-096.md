# FLAG: FPGA-Based Low-Latency GNN Inference Service with Vector Quantization

## Problem
Real-time GNN inference serving is bottlenecked by intensive preparation (neighbor aggregation, feature computation) and neighborhood explosion (exponential neighbor set growth across layers), making it difficult to meet latency SLAs.

## Method
FLAG separates online and offline computation. Offline preprocessing precomputes and vector-quantizes hidden embeddings. FPGA accelerator performs lightweight online aggregation using the precomputed compressed embeddings, avoiding neighborhood explosion.

## Key Innovation
Vector-quantized embedding preprocessing enables FPGA-efficient GNN inference without neighborhood expansion overhead.

## Contributions
- Offline preprocessing with vector quantization for embedding compression
- FPGA accelerator for lightweight aggregation
- Elimination of neighborhood explosion via precomputation
- 154x-333x speedups on multiple GNN models

## Motivation
GNN serving (recommendations, knowledge graphs) requires low end-to-end latency; current systems cannot meet SLAs due to preparation overhead.
