# Accelerating Electro-Thermal Co-Analysis via Coarse-to-Fine Physics-Informed Neural Networks

**ID:** iccad-2025-039  
**Venue:** ICCAD

## Problem
Conventional electro-thermal co-simulation using iterative PDE solutions is computationally expensive and time-consuming for 3D IC designs.

## Motivation
Electro-thermal coupling has become critical for 3D IC design; fast analysis methods are needed during iterative design optimization.

## Method
Instead of repeatedly solving complex equations for electricity and heat flow (which is computationally expensive), this approach trains neural networks to respect physics laws—called physics-informed neural networks or PINNs. The method works in two stages: a coarse model predicts electrical potential and temperature across the entire 3D chip design, then a fine-grained model focuses on regions that need higher resolution. The training is optimized for fast convergence, combining learned patterns with physical constraints to ensure accuracy.

## Key Novelty
A two-stage neural network that first predicts electrical and thermal behavior across an entire 3D chip design, then refines specific regions of interest for higher precision.

## Contributions
1. Physics-informed neural network framework for electro-thermal co-analysis
2. Coarse-to-fine model hierarchy for global and local accuracy
3. Efficient training strategy accelerating convergence
4. 0.10-0.19% mean relative error with 3-4 orders of magnitude efficiency improvement versus commercial tool
