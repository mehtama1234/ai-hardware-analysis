# Orthrus - Dual-loop System-technology Co-optimization

Orthrus presents a dual-loop optimization framework that combines Bayesian optimization for sample-efficient parameter refinement and differential evolution for global exploration, enabling efficient system-technology co-optimization. The framework simultaneously optimizes application characteristics, system architecture parameters, and semiconductor technology choices across a complex interdependent design space. By coupling these complementary optimization algorithms, Orthrus reduces the computational burden of exploring multi-dimensional co-optimization landscapes while improving convergence quality.
