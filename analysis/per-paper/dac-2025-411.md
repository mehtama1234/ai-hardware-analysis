# Learning-Aided Safe Controller Synthesis with Formal Guarantees

**Problem:** ML-based controllers achieve good simulation performance but lack formal safety guarantees for safety-critical systems.

**Method:** The approach trains a controller using reinforcement learning (learning through trial-and-error feedback) to decide what actions to take, then uses a neural network to propose safety barriers—mathematical descriptions of regions where the system is guaranteed safe. Rather than requiring perfectly certified barriers upfront, the method verifies each candidate barrier through efficient computation. Importantly, this verification works with constraints in their actual complex form, without needing the simplifying approximations that previous methods required.

**Key Result:** Handles original non-convex BC constraints without relaxation; demonstrated on series of benchmarks.

**Hardware Target:** CPU, GPU  
**Workloads:** database  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
