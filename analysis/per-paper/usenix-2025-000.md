# SpaceExit: Enabling Efficient Adaptive Computing in Space with Early Exits

**Problem:** Space-based systems face severe power and thermal constraints that limit inference workloads on spacecraft and satellites. Existing neural network inference approaches consume excessive computation without adapting to available resources.

**Method:** SpaceExit implements early-exit neural network architectures that terminate computation at intermediate layers when confidence thresholds are met. The system dynamically adjusts exit thresholds based on available power and thermal budgets in the space environment. Hardware optimizations minimize the overhead of early-exit detection and decision logic.

**Novelty:** Adaptive early-exit mechanisms specifically tuned for space computing constraints with dynamic threshold adjustment based on orbital power and thermal conditions.
