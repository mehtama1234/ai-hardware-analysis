# SpaceExit: Enabling Efficient Adaptive Computing in Space with Early Exits

**Problem:** Space-based systems face severe power and thermal constraints that limit inference workloads on spacecraft and satellites. Existing neural network inference approaches consume excessive computation without adapting to available resources.

**Method:** SpaceExit designs neural networks (AI systems trained to recognize patterns) that can stop calculating early. Normally they run through all their layers (stages of computation), but SpaceExit lets them stop partway when they're confident enough in their answer. The system adjusts how confident they need to be before stopping based on the current power and cooling available in space—tighter constraints mean earlier stopping and lower energy use. Hardware optimizations reduce the overhead from checking when to stop.

**Novelty:** Neural networks that automatically adjust when they stop calculating based on available power and cooling in space.
