# isscc-2025-020: Ultra-Low-Power Neural Signal Processor for Speech-Decoding BMI

**A 3.9mW 200words/min Neural Signal Processor in Speech Decoding for Brain-Machine Interface**

## Summary
Dedicated neural signal processor achieving real-time speech decoding at 200 words/min for implantable brain-machine interfaces at 3.9 mW—65x power reduction vs. general-purpose CPU.

## Technical Approach
- **Pipeline**: Phone-probability inference (NN) → language-model beam-search word decoding
- **Specialization**: Hardware accelerated NN inference and LM lookup
- **Power**: 3.9 mW ultra-low-power suitable for implanted operation

## Key Innovation
First hardware accelerator for speech-decoding BMI achieving practical word rates and implantable power budgets, demonstrating specialized accelerator efficiency for biomedical inference.

## Contributions
- Real-time 200 words/min speech decoding
- Ultra-low 3.9 mW power consumption
- 65x power reduction vs. CPU-based approach
- Practical implantable BMI demonstration

## Target Workloads
Brain-machine interfaces, paralysis assistance, neural prostheses

## Limitations

- Per-word latency not specified
