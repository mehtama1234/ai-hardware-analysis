# isscc-2025-166: Skin-Inspired Neuromorphic Sensing Platform

**Title:** Skin-Inspired Electronics: An Emerging Sensing and Computing Platform

**Problem:** Tactile sensing systems lack dense integration and neuromorphic processing for realistic sensory feedback in prosthetics and wearables.

**Approach:** Neuromorphic platform combining dense tactile sensor arrays, neuromorphic encoding circuits, and neuromodulation for natural feedback.

**Key Result:** Seamless sensory feedback for prosthetic limbs enabling dexterous object manipulation without visual reliance.

**Hardware:** ASIC/SoC (neuromorphic sensing platform).

**Tags:** tactile-sensing, neuromorphic, prosthetics, wearable, dense-array, skin-inspired
