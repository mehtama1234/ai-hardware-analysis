# hpca-2025-017 — Mithril: A Scalable System for Deep GNN Training

**Venue:** HPCA  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Distributed full-graph deep GNN training cannot scale to many layers because communication volume grows proportionally with layers in graph-parallel approaches, overwhelming inter-GPU links.

## Motivation
Deep GNNs achieve higher expressiveness but existing graph-parallel training systems cannot scale due to O(layers) communication blow-up across GPU partition boundaries.

## Method
Mithril applies layer-level model parallelism: each GPU owns a disjoint set of consecutive GNN layers and processes all graph nodes for those layers, reducing communication by a factor of the layer count. Graph-chunk pipelining with embedding speculation breaks cross-chunk dependencies. A hybrid layer+graph parallelism handles large graphs and improves GPU utilization.

## Key Novelty
First layer-level model parallelism for full-graph GNN training with embedding speculation to resolve cross-layer dependencies in pipelined execution.

## Contributions
- Layer-level model parallelism for deep GNN training, cutting communication by a factor equal to GNN depth.
- Embedding speculation breaking inter-chunk dependencies for pipeline execution.
- Hybrid layer-parallel + graph-parallel scheme for large graphs.
- Up to 22.89x (avg 6.78x) communication reduction; up to 10.21x speedup on commodity Ethernet.

## Hardware Target
GPU

## Technique Category
parallelism, interconnect, scheduling

## Workloads
GNN

## Metrics
- Speedup: up to 2.34x (avg 1.49x) on InfiniBand; up to 10.21x (avg 7.16x) on commodity Ethernet vs. graph parallelism
- Communication volume: up to 22.89x (avg 6.78x) reduction per epoch

## Baselines
Graph parallelism (graph-partitioned distributed GNN training)

## Limitations
Embedding speculation may introduce approximation errors for very deep GNNs or high-heterogeneity graphs; convergence analysis is empirical.
