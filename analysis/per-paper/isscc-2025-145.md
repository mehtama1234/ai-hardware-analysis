# ISSCC-2025-145: Compact Multi-Modal Gas Sensor Stack

**Problem:**  
Wearable and ingestible gas sensors require miniaturization and low power, but traditional wired transducers require large packaging; prior MEMS-CMOS stacks lack multi-gas capability.

**Solution:**  
Heterogeneous MSS-CMOS stack integrating Membrane-type Surface-stress Sensor transducers on CMOS analog frontend and digital readout; 16-channel design at 3.5×3.5mm2 with 1.47mW/ch.

**Key Results:**  
- 16-channel multi-modal gas sensing
- 3.5 × 3.5 mm² form factor
- 1.47 mW/channel power
- Compact stack without external wiring

**Key Innovation:**  
First multi-modal MSS-CMOS heterogeneous stack achieving 16-channel density, enabling deployment in wearables and ingestible diagnostics.
