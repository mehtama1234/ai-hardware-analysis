# isscc-2025-238: Continuous-Time Noise-Shaping CDC

**Problem:** High-resolution CDCs for IoT environmental monitoring (humidity, pressure) face kT/C and flicker noise limits. ΔΣM CDCs achieve high resolution via oversampling/noise-shaping but power consumption remains limited.

**Approach:** Continuous-time noise-shaping ΔΣM-based CDC using CT integration directly in capacitance domain, avoiding sampling-clock jitter.

**Key novelty:** Continuous-time noise-shaping SAR CDC achieving high resolution with superior FoM via CT integration.

**Metrics:** 189.3dB figure-of-merit, 14.5fJ/conversion-step energy efficiency.

**Tags:** capacitance-converter, noise-shaping, iot, sensor, energy-efficient, high-resolution
