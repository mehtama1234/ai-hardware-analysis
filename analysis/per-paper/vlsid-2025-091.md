# Startup Circuit for Relaxation Oscillators with Low Functional Current and Minimal Area

**Venue:** VLSID 2025  
**Authors:** Anubhav Srivastava, Sadique Mohammad Iqbal, Divya Tripathi, Saurabh Goyal

## Problem Statement
Ultra-low-power oscillators used in IoT and sensor interfaces suffer from extended startup times, increasing time-to-ready and power overhead. Existing startup mechanisms either increase functional current consumption or require significant area, making them impractical for sub-100nW applications.

## Key Contributions
1. **3x Faster Startup**: Reduces startup time from 466 µs to 150 µs across process, voltage, and temperature variations
2. **Current Preservation**: Maintains functional current at 52 nanoamperes without increase
3. **Minimal Area Overhead**: Implemented using current mirrors and digital logic for lightweight circuit
4. **Robust Operation**: Validated across ±10% voltage margin and -40°C to +125°C temperature range
5. **Post-Trim Stability**: ±5% frequency variation across voltage and temperature

## Technical Approach
- **Startup Mechanism**: Open-loop current injection to charge internal nodes, followed by closed-loop transition once frequency stabilizes
- **Implementation**: Current mirrors and digital control circuits for mode switching
- **Optimization**: Leverages ring oscillator topology with minimal area overhead
- **Target**: 32 kHz oscillator in 16nm FinFET technology

## Hardware Target
