# isscc-2025-075: High-Efficiency Hybrid Boost Converter

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
High voltage conversion ratios in conventional boost converters suffer from very short turn-on pulses, voltage stress on inductors/transistors, and inherent right-half-plane zeros that limit transient response.

## Motivation
LiDAR transceivers for autonomous driving and motor drivers for drones/robots demand higher voltages (12-36V) and faster transient responses than conventional converters.

## Method
Inductor-in-the-middle hybrid topology reduces voltage stress on switches, eliminates RHP zero constraints, and maintains continuous input/output currents.

## Key Novelty
Inductor-in-the-middle architecture solving RHP zero limitation in high-ratio boost conversion.

## Contributions
- 93% peak efficiency (12-36V conversion)
- Fast transient response without RHP zero
- Continuous input/output currents
- Improved reliability for edge applications

## Metrics
- Peak efficiency: 93%
- Voltage range: 12-36V output
