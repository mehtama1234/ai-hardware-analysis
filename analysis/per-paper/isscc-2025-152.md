# isscc-2025-152: Adaptive Asynchronous Flash LiDAR

**Problem:** Flash LiDAR constrained by large hTDC footprint reducing spatial resolution; frame-based sensors bottlenecked by worst-case SNR; background light intensity degrades conventional designs.

**Motivation:** Autonomous vehicles and robotics need high-resolution 3D sensing with adaptive performance across variable illumination and frame rates.

**Method:** Asynchronous event-driven readout with pixelwise ToF validation and background-light-adaptive threshold. On-chip control adjusts pixel sensitivity and event rates dynamically; time-gated photon counting with integrated filtering enables 5–250fps variable frame rates.

**Key Novelty:** Background-light-adaptive pixelwise ToF validation with on-chip integrated control for variable-rate asynchronous flash LiDAR.

**Contributions:**
- 160×90 spatial resolution
- Dynamic 5–250fps frame rate adaptation
- Background-light-adaptive threshold
- Integrated on-chip control (no external drivers)

**Hardware Target:** ASIC, sensor

**Technique Categories:** circuit-design, sensor-design, near-data-processing

**Workloads:** vision

**Metrics:** 160×90 pixels, 5–250fps, background-adaptive

**Baselines:** Scanning LiDAR, fixed-frame-rate sensors, conventional photon counters

**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
