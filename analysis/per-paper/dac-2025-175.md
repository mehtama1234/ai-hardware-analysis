# Resilient Federated Learning on Embedded Devices with Constrained Network Connectivity

**Problem:** Federated learning on bandwidth-constrained embedded devices suffers from high communication overhead.

**Solution:** Adaptive federation with gradient-similarity-based selective transmission and compression for limited-bandwidth clients.

**Results:** 60-78% bandwidth savings vs. state-of-the-art; validated on real hardware.

**Theme:** Communication-efficient federated learning for edge devices.
