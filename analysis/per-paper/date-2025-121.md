# Designing Secure Space Systems

**Venue**: DATE  
**Primary Theme**: Lifecycle-integrated space system security  

## Problem
Space systems increasingly rely on commercial off-the-shelf hardware and software to accelerate development and reduce costs, but COTS components introduce vulnerabilities and expand the attack surface, creating cybersecurity risks for critical space assets.

## Method
The paper outlines a complete approach to building secure space systems by thinking about cybersecurity from the very start of development, not adding it at the end. It covers secure design practices (building security into how the system is built), thorough testing to find vulnerabilities, strategies to keep systems working even under attack, and industry standards. By weaving cybersecurity into every phase—from figuring out what the system needs to do, through design, testing, and deployment—it prevents security problems from being overlooked.

## Key Novelty
The key idea is treating cybersecurity as integral to every stage of space system development, from requirements to deployment, rather than as an afterthought.

## Contributions
- Framework for integrating cybersecurity throughout space system lifecycle
- Secure engineering practices adapted for space asset development
- Comprehensive testing and validation methodologies for space security
- Cyber resiliency strategies for operational space systems
- Role of standardization in enabling consistent security posture
