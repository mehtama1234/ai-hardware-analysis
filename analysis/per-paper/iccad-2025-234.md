# 3D Acceleration for Mixture-of-Experts and Multi-Head Attention Spiking Transformers with Dynamic Head Pruning

**Paper ID:** iccad-2025-234  
**Venue:** ICCAD

## Problem

Spiking Neural Networks offer energy-efficient, brain-inspired computing, but lack hardware support for highly parallel distributed processing required by mixture-of-experts models. Conventional 2D CMOS integration cannot efficiently support MoE-based spiking transformers.

## Motivation

Energy-efficient AI demands brain-inspired approaches like SNNs combined with model parallelism from MoE; 3D integration enables efficient spatial distribution of expert processing.

## Method

Introduces first 3D hardware architecture for MoE and multi-head attention spiking transformers. Leverages 3D integration with memory-on-logic and logic-on-logic stacking for expert distribution. Implements dynamic head pruning in multi-head attention to reduce computation. Optimizes for energy efficiency and latency through stackable circuitry exploiting 3D interconnect benefits.

## Key Novelty

First 3D hardware architecture and design methodology for spiking transformer networks with MoE and dynamic attention pruning, demonstrating significant energy and latency improvements.

## Contributions

- 3D hardware architecture supporting spatially distributed MoE processing
- Memory-on-logic and logic-on-logic stacking for efficient MoE layout
- Dynamic head pruning for multi-head attention energy reduction
- Significant energy efficiency and latency improvements over 2D CMOS
