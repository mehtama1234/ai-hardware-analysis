# McPAL: Scaling Unstructured Sparse Inference with Multi-Chiplet HBM-PIM Architecture

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Monolithic PIM chips cannot scale to very large LLMs (70B+) due to memory footprint and power/yield constraints of single dies.

## Key Insight
Multi-chiplet PIM with in-memory butterfly network and hierarchical scaling (3D vertical + 2.5D horizontal) avoids monolithic bottlenecks.

## Method
McPAL breaks down sparse weight matrices (collections of numbers where most are zero, allowing skipped computations) into irregular sparse vectors, then routes active computations through an in-memory butterfly network (an efficient traffic-distribution circuit) connected to processing-in-memory arrays (chips that do calculations where the data is stored). It scales vertically by stacking memory chips in 3D-HBM to minimize data travel distance, and horizontally via a 2.5D IO chiplet that handles inter-chip connections, avoiding the power and yield constraints of a single massive die.

## Contributions
- Multi-chiplet PIM architecture for LLM scaling
- Butterfly network for unstructured sparse routing
- 3D-HBM vertical scaling
- 2.5D IO chiplet horizontal scaling
- 1.57–3.12× speedup vs. A100, 10.43–35.66× energy efficiency
