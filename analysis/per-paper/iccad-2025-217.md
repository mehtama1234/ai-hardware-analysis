# LMLitho: A Large Vision Model-Driven Lithography Simulation Framework

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-217  
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Lithography simulation is computationally expensive, slowing design validation and optimization for advanced technology nodes.

## Motivation
Fast, accurate lithography simulation enables rapid design iteration and advanced-node preparation.

## Method
The framework trains a large vision model—an AI system that processes and understands images—on 100,000 examples of lithography patterns paired with their simulated results. A vision transformer architecture (a type of neural network designed to understand images) learns to directly map chip design patterns to their expected outputs. Because it learns general principles rather than memorizing specific examples, it can work on advanced technology nodes that weren't part of its original training data.

## Key Novelty
This is the first approach using a large vision model for lithography simulation, achieving 120 times faster results than commercial simulation tools.

## Contributions

1. Vision model-based lithography simulator
2. 120x speedup vs commercial simulation tools
3. Superior generalization to advanced nodes
4. Trained on 100k diverse patterns
