# LMLitho: A Large Vision Model-Driven Lithography Simulation Framework

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-217  
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Lithography simulation is computationally expensive, slowing design validation and optimization for advanced technology nodes.

## Motivation
Fast, accurate lithography simulation enables rapid design iteration and advanced-node preparation.

## Method
Large vision model trained on 100k lithography patterns for simulation. Vision transformer architecture for pattern-to-output mapping. Generalizes to advanced technology nodes beyond training data.

## Key Novelty
First large vision model-based approach to lithography simulation achieving 120x speedup over commercial tools.

## Contributions

1. Vision model-based lithography simulator
2. 120x speedup vs commercial simulation tools
3. Superior generalization to advanced nodes
4. Trained on 100k diverse patterns
