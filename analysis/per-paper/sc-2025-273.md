# Benchmarking Cutting-Edge Scientific Error-Bounded Lossy Compressors on Correlation-Based Rate-Distortion

**Venue:** SC 2025  
**Authors:** Ziwei Qiu, Jinyang Liu, Kai Zhao, Robert Underwood, Sheng Di

## Problem & Motivation

Scientific error-bounded lossy compressors are essential for managing storage and I/O costs in large-scale simulations and data processing. However, existing benchmarking efforts focus narrowly on error-value metrics (PSNR), overlooking correlation-based quality measures (SSIM, Error Autocorrelation, Pearson Coefficient) that are equally critical for preserving scientific accuracy and interpretability of decompressed data.

## Approach

A comprehensive benchmark study evaluating seven state-of-the-art scientific lossy compressors across six representative scientific datasets. Evaluation employs diverse data quality metrics:

- **Error-Value Metrics:** PSNR (traditional)
- **Correlation-Based Metrics:** SSIM (2 configurations), Error Autocorrelation, Pearson Coefficient

## Key Novelty

- **Holistic Quality Assessment:** First systematic benchmark including correlation metrics beyond traditional PSNR.
- **Cross-Dataset Analysis:** Reveals diverged performance characteristics across different scientific domains.
- **Practical Guidelines:** Provides actionable guidance for compressor selection based on domain-specific quality requirements.

## Contributions

- Comprehensive benchmark of 7 scientific lossy compressors
- Evaluation across 6 representative scientific datasets
- Multi-metric quality assessment framework
- Empirical evidence of performance divergence across metrics
- Selection guidelines for practitioners

## Hardware Target

- General-purpose CPUs
- Scientific computing clusters
- Storage subsystems (NAS, archival)

## Workloads

- Scientific simulation I/O reduction
- Large-scale data archival
- Storage optimization in HPC facilities

## Results

- **Key Finding:** No single compressor achieves optimal performance across all quality metrics within a dataset or across datasets within a metric
- Significant metric divergence observed for correlation-based measures
- Guidelines enable informed compressor selection

## Limitations & Questions
