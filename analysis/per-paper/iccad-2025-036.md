# Adder-DCIM: A Parallel Bit-Flexible Digital CIM Accelerator Joint Model Compression Framework for AdderNet Inference

**ID:** iccad-2025-036  
**Venue:** ICCAD

## Problem
Digital CIM accelerators face power and area overhead from multiplication, difficulty in exploiting fine-grained sparsity, and throughput degradation from bit-serial architecture, especially for edge deployment.

## Motivation
Edge AI requires low-power, low-latency, low-cost accelerators; digital CIM offers promise but faces practical efficiency challenges.

## Method
Adder-DCIM combines: (1) CIM-friendly model compression with operator decomposition, lossless fine-grained sparsity, and KLD-based mixed-precision quantization, (2) synchronous parallel DCIM architecture with mixed-precision support, (3) bit-flexible minimal selector circuit for efficient computation.

## Key Novelty
Joint co-design of model compression and bit-flexible parallel digital CIM architecture replacing multiplication with addition for improved efficiency.

## Contributions
1. CIM-friendly model compression framework with operator decomposition and lossless sparsity
2. KLD-based Cin-wise mixed-precision quantization
3. Synchronous parallel DCIM architecture for throughput improvement
4. Bit-flexible minimal selector circuit for mixed-precision computation
5. 255-243 TOPS/W@4-4.4bit with minimal accuracy loss (0.34-0.7%)

## Metrics
- Speedup: 20.7× improvement in energy efficiency × throughput density for ResNet50
- Energy/TOPS: 134 TOPS/W peak; 255 TOPS/W@4.2bit, 243 TOPS/W@4.4bit ResNet inference
- Area: 6.49 TOPS/mm² peak throughput density at INT8
- Accuracy: 0.34% loss on CIFAR10 ResNet20, 0.7% on ImageNet ResNet50

## Baselines

## Limitations
Evaluation limited to CNNs (ResNet); transformer and other neural network architectures not evaluated.
