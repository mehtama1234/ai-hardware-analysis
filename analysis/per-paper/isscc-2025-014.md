# isscc-2025-014: 400MHz Symbol-Power-Tracking Supply Modulator

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)

## Problem
5G FR2 (mmWave) PAs suffer from high PAPR in OFDM with complex modulation (QAM), causing power inefficiency at average levels. Envelope tracking (ET) limits bandwidth to ~200MHz; higher modulation bandwidth requires alternative supply-tracking approaches.

## Approach
**400MHz symbol-power-tracking (SPT) supply modulator** with:
- SPT tracks symbol-level power (higher bandwidth than continuous envelope)
- **SPT-adaptive-biasing network:** real-time transistor bias adjustment
- Maintains efficiency across power-back-off levels

## Key Contribution
400MHz SPT with adaptive biasing enables high-bandwidth supply tracking for mmWave CMOS PA efficiency.

## Performance
- Bandwidth: 400 MHz (2× prior ET limit)
- Application: 5G NR FR2 mmWave PA
- Technique: Symbol-level power tracking + adaptive bias
