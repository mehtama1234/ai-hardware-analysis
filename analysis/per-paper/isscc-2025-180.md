# isscc-2025-180: Via-Programmable DNN-Processor Fabrication

**Problem**: Wearable AI-IoT devices require custom DNNs, but each ASIC incurs substantial mask costs, making it infeasible to support diverse applications.

**Solution**: Via-programmable neuron array (VPNA) base chip where DNN layers are realized by adjusting via placements without changing photomasks. Bit- and neuron-serial circuits (BNSC) reduce routing overhead by 1/1024; sparse binary networks shrink DNN size to <20 tiles.

**Key Results**:
- 1/40 mask cost reduction
- 8.4x energy efficiency on EEG tasks
- Routing wire area reduction: 1/1024

**Hardware**: ASIC + CIM  
**Workload**: EEG inference (wearable)  
**Confidence**: low (abstract only)
