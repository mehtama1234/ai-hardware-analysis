# Ph.D. Project: A Novel Compilation-Based Approach for Generating Sparse Tensor Accelerators

**Problem:** Sparse tensor irregularity challenges GPU/NPU acceleration, and current point-wise manual FPGA design methodologies do not scale efficiently across diverse sparse scenarios.

**Method:** Proposes a compiler framework with three components: (1) a schedule-primitive-based DSL for sparse accelerator specification; (2) a two-stage mapping mechanism based on sparse meta-operations to flexibly map dataflow to hardware; (3) a heuristic design space exploration strategy guided by workload characteristics to automatically generate high-performance accelerators.

**Novelty:** Compiler-driven automatic generation of reconfigurable sparse tensor accelerators using schedule-primitive DSL and sparse meta-operations mapping.
