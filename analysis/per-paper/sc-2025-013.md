# ThirstyFLOPS: Water Footprint Modeling and Analysis Toward Sustainable HPC Systems

**Venue:** SC
**Paper ID:** sc-2025-013
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Water footprint of HPC systems is poorly understood despite growing water-intensiveness from cooling and power generation; environmental impact underexplored

## Motivation
HPC systems consume massive water for cooling; power generation also water-intensive; water scarcity is growing concern; sustainability metrics needed for HPC planning

## Method
ThirstyFLOPS framework: region-specific metrics (WUE, PUE, EWF); analysis of four representative HPC systems (Marconi, Fugaku, Polaris, Frontier) with real-world data; impact assessment of water scarcity and nuclear energy

## Key Novelty
Comprehensive water footprint analysis framework for HPC; region-specific sustainability metrics; guidance for water-aware HPC infrastructure design
