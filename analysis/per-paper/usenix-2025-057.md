# HotRAP: Hot Record Retention and Promotion for LSM-trees with Tiered Storage

**Problem:** Frequently accessed records in LSM-trees are compacted to slower storage over time, and existing promotion algorithms are inefficient at moving them back to fast storage, leading to degraded read performance.

**Method:** HotRAP uses an on-disk LSM-tree to track the hotness of individual keys and implements three distinct promotion pathways to efficiently move hot records from slow storage to fast storage with minimal delay. Built on RocksDB, the system maintains hot records in fast storage while they remain frequently accessed.

**Novelty:** On-disk LSM-tree structure for tracking record hotness combined with three promotion pathways to timely elevate frequently accessed records between tiered storage levels.
