# QFactory: Accelerating Quantized Large Language Model Serving with Qtile Graphs

**Problem:** Quantized LLM inference suffers from suboptimal dataflow scheduling and kernel execution patterns that fail to fully exploit the performance benefits of quantization. Existing serving systems lack specialized compilation strategies for the unique compute and memory characteristics of quantized operations.

**Method:** QFactory introduces Qtile Graphs, a tile-based graph intermediate representation that captures the quantized operation dataflow. The compiler applies graph-level optimizations including kernel fusion, memory-aware scheduling, and operation tiling to maximize hardware utilization and reduce communication overhead during inference.

**Novelty:** The Qtile Graph representation is specifically designed to express and optimize the dataflow patterns of quantized operations, enabling systematic compilation-time fusion and scheduling optimizations.
