# isscc-2025-088: Tomahawk5 AI/ML Network Switch

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Next-generation AI/ML data centers demand 51.2Tb/s switching (2x previous); monolithic integration at scale with diverse connectivity options is challenging.

## Motivation
Explosive AI/ML growth drives need for high-capacity, flexible data center switching infrastructure.

## Method
Monolithic switch integrating 51.2Tb/s fabric with integrated Peregrine SerDes supporting DAC, front-panel optics, LPO, and co-packaged optics in 5nm.

## Key Novelty
Integrated Peregrine SerDes enabling flexible multi-protocol connectivity in monolithic 51.2Tb/s design.

## Contributions
- 51.2Tb/s switching (2x predecessor)
- Integrated Peregrine SerDes
- Multi-protocol optical/copper support (DAC/FPO/LPO/CPO)
- 5nm monolithic integration

## Metrics
- Switching: 51.2 Tb/s
- Technology: 5nm
- Improvement: 2x vs. Tomahawk4
