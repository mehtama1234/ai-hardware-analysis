# SpectrePrefetch: Undermining Cache-Centric Secure Speculation with Modern Hardware Prefetchers

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-080

## Problem
Cache-centric secure speculation defenses fail to address security vulnerabilities exploitable through hardware prefetchers during speculative execution. Prefetchers can become transmission media for leaking secrets through their patterns and confidence signals.

## Motivation
Understanding and mitigating transient execution attacks that exploit hardware prefetchers is critical for securing modern CPUs against information leakage through speculative execution paths.

## Method
SpectrePrefetch develops an attack framework that encodes transient secrets into hardware prefetching patterns and confidence signals during speculative execution. Two attack variants exploit:
1. **Prefetching patterns** by probing cache state
2. **Prefetcher confidence** by probing prefetcher state

The attacks are demonstrated on Intel CPUs and Gem5 simulator with a proposed non-deterministic prefetching defense maintaining performance.

## Key Novelty
First framework exploiting hardware prefetchers as covert channels during speculative execution, demonstrating that prefetchers undermine existing cache-centric secure speculation defenses.

## Contributions
- Attack framework demonstrating secret leakage through prefetching patterns and confidence at 31.25 Kbps with 98.87% accuracy
- Theoretical and empirical evidence that prefetchers defeat cache-centric secure speculation defenses
- Analysis showing that restrictive defenses like MuonTrap lose substantial performance benefits
- Low-cost non-deterministic prefetching defense maintaining average performance on SPEC2017 benchmarks
