# Accuracy Is Not Always We Need: Precision-Aware Bayesian Yield Optimization

**Authors:** Jing Kou, Zidong Chen, Liang Zhang, Haiyan Qin, Wang Kang, Wei W. Xing  
**Venue:** DAC 2025

## Summary
This paper proposes a precision-aware yield optimization framework that intelligently adapts simulation budget allocation based on predicted design quality. Using a Continuous AutoRegression model and multi-fidelity strategy, it converges with <1,000 simulations, achieving 10x cost reduction versus high-fidelity approaches while improving design quality and robustness.

## Key Contributions
- Precision-aware yield optimization with adaptive resource allocation
- Continuous AutoRegression model for yield-fidelity relationship
- Multi-fidelity acquisition strategy balancing exploration and exploitation
- 10x simulation cost reduction with improved final design quality

## Technical Approach
The framework uses a CAR model to characterize how simulation fidelity affects yield estimation accuracy. A multi-fidelity acquisition strategy then allocates the simulation budget across candidates based on a Figure of Merit (quality predictor), prioritizing high-potential designs. This prevents wasteful high-fidelity simulation of low-quality designs.
