# MuSTNet: SAT-based Exact Multi-Stage Transistor Network Synthesis with Placement Awareness

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-207

## Problem
Complex gate synthesis typically focuses on single-stage networks, neglecting multi-stage opportunities and transistor placement, limiting optimization potential.

## Motivation


## Method
The approach uses a SAT solver (a constraint-satisfaction engine that finds exact optimal solutions) to design transistor networks. Rather than optimizing one layer of logic gates at a time, it considers multiple layers together, revealing deeper optimization opportunities. Critically, it accounts for physical transistor placement on the chip—not just logical correctness—enabling simultaneous reduction of both transistor count and chip area.

## Key Novelty
This is the first SAT-based method that optimizes transistor networks across multiple stages while directly accounting for physical transistor placement on the chip.

## Contributions
- Multi-stage hierarchy for deeper functional optimization
- Placement-aware transistor network minimization
- 18% transistor count reduction for 4-input functions
- 21.4% area reduction vs traditional cell-level design
