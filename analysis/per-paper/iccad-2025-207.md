# MuSTNet: SAT-based Exact Multi-Stage Transistor Network Synthesis with Placement Awareness

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-207

## Problem
Complex gate synthesis typically focuses on single-stage networks, neglecting multi-stage opportunities and transistor placement, limiting optimization potential.

## Motivation


## Method
SAT-based exact synthesis framework with multi-stage hierarchy for deeper optimization and placement-aware transistor network minimization for simultaneous count and area reduction.

## Key Novelty
First multi-stage SAT-based transistor network synthesis incorporating placement constraints.

## Contributions
- Multi-stage hierarchy for deeper functional optimization
- Placement-aware transistor network minimization
- 18% transistor count reduction for 4-input functions
- 21.4% area reduction vs traditional cell-level design
