# hpca-2025-085 — MLPerf Power: Benchmarking the Energy Efficiency of Machine Learning Systems from μWatts to MWatts for Sustainable AI

**Venue:** HPCA  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
There is no standardized, cross-platform methodology for benchmarking the energy efficiency of ML systems spanning the full deployment range from sub-milliwatt IoT devices to megawatt-scale datacenter clusters. Existing benchmarks focus on throughput/latency without capturing power consumption in a reproducible, comparable way across heterogeneous hardware.

## Motivation
Rapid ML adoption is driving massive energy consumption growth, and without standardized power benchmarks, system designers and operators lack actionable metrics to guide optimization for sustainable AI.

## Method
MLPerf Power extends the MLPerf benchmark suite with standardized power measurement rules and best practices developed by a consortium of 20+ industry organizations. The methodology defines measurement protocols for power metering at different granularities (device, rack, facility) and applies them to representative ML workloads (inference and training) across edge, mobile, and datacenter deployment scales. 1,841 reproducible measurements were collected from 60 systems, enabling cross-architecture energy-efficiency comparisons.

## Key Novelty
The first industry-wide, consortium-developed benchmarking framework that unifies ML energy-efficiency measurement across the full deployment spectrum from microwatts to megawatts.

## Contributions
- Standardized power measurement methodology and rules for ML systems across all deployment scales
- A dataset of 1,841 reproducible energy-efficiency measurements from 60 diverse ML systems
- Cross-architecture analysis revealing performance vs. energy-efficiency trade-offs from edge to cloud
- Open benchmark infrastructure enabling future sustainable AI research and standardization

## Hardware Targets
GPU, NPU, ASIC, CPU, SoC

## Technique Categories
power, scheduling

## Workloads
LLM-inference, CNN, vision, speech

## Metrics
- 1,841 measurements across 60 systems; power range from μW to MW

## Baselines
- MLPerf Inference v3.x systems without power measurement

## Limitations
The methodology relies on self-reported measurements by submitters, which may introduce variability in measurement fidelity across organizations.
