# asplos-2025-002: Early Termination for Hyperdimensional Computing Using Inferential Statistics

**Venue:** ASPLOS  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
HDC inference can be terminated early to reduce computation, but existing heuristic early-termination methods lack statistical guarantees and may either unacceptably degrade classification accuracy or terminate inference later than necessary.

## Motivation
HDC is a promising lightweight paradigm for edge inference on resource-constrained and emerging hardware; the lack of statistical guarantees in current early-termination methods limits deployability in accuracy-sensitive applications.

## Method
Applies inferential statistics (hypothesis testing) to HDC classification to derive a principled early-termination criterion. The termination decision is formulated as a statistical test on the similarity scores of HDC class hypervectors, computing confidence bounds and stopping inference when the test rejects ambiguity at the required statistical confidence, yielding provable accuracy-loss bounds.

## Key Novelty
First application of formal inferential statistics with provable confidence bounds to govern early termination in hyperdimensional computing classifiers.

## Contributions
- Statistically grounded early-termination framework for HDC classifiers with provable accuracy guarantees.
- Formulation of HDC classification confidence as a hypothesis test over hypervector similarity scores.
- Demonstration of reduced inference computation vs. heuristic methods while meeting accuracy targets.
