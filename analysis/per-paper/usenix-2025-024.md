# Identifying and Analyzing Pitfalls in GNN Systems

**Problem:** Graph Neural Network systems suffer from unexplored performance bottlenecks, design inefficiencies, and implementation issues that limit their practical effectiveness. Existing GNN frameworks have common pitfalls that impact scalability and correctness.

**Method:** The paper conducts empirical analysis and systematic benchmarking of GNN system implementations. It profiles existing GNN frameworks to identify common bottlenecks through performance characterization and comparative evaluation.

**Novelty:** Systematic empirical identification and categorization of design pitfalls in production GNN systems and frameworks.
