# Leveraging GPU for Better Detailed Placement Quality

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-128

## Problem
Detailed placement has approached practical limits of CPU-based wirelength optimization; leveraging GPU parallelism to further improve placement quality remains underexplored.

## Motivation
Further wirelength and routability improvements in detailed placement enable better overall design optimization.

## Method
GPU-based detailed placement flow featuring Simultaneous Row and Order Assignment (SROA) step that integrates dynamic programming-based row assignment with heuristic-based cell position adjustment; enables efficient exploration of larger solution spaces.

## Key Novelty
SROA algorithm for integrated row assignment and cell positioning leveraging GPU parallelism.

## Contributions
- Simultaneous Row and Order Assignment (SROA) algorithm for detailed placement
- GPU-accelerated exploration of enlarged solution space
- Preserved routability with improved wirelength metrics

## Metrics
- **Detailed Routing Wirelength Reduction:** 1.35%
- **Via Count Reduction:** 1.03%
- **Routability:** Maintained

## Limitations
Marginal improvements in already-optimized metrics; further gains may be limited.
