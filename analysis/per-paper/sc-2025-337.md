# Modular Architecture for High-Performance and Low Overhead Data Transfers

**ID:** sc-2025-337  
**Venue:** SC 2025  
**Authors:** Rasman Mubtasim Swargo, Engin Arslan, Md. Arifuzzaman

## Problem & Motivation

Traditional data transfer tools (rsync, GridFTP, etc.) rely on **fixed, static configurations** that cannot adapt to:
- Dynamic network conditions
- Changing system load
- Variable workload patterns

**Result:** Resource underutilization and unpredictable transfer times.

## Solution: AutoMDT

A **modular data transfer architecture** powered by adaptive RL-based optimization:

### Architecture
- **Modular design:** Decouples I/O (read/write) from network tasks
- **RL agent (PPO):** Learns optimal concurrency tuning for each component
- **Lightweight simulator:** Enables offline training (~45 min) without impacting production

### Adaptation
- Captures complex buffer dynamics
- Responds to changing conditions in real-time
- No online training overhead

## Results

| Metric | Improvement |
|--------|------------|
| Convergence speed | 8x faster |
| Transfer completion time | 68% reduction |
| Training overhead | 45 minutes (offline) |

## Key Insight

By decoupling optimization of read/network/write concurrency and using a simulator-trained RL agent, **data transfer becomes adaptive** without requiring domain expertise or manual tuning.

## Implications

Enables practical, autonomous optimization of large-scale data movements across WAN links, critical for distributed science.
