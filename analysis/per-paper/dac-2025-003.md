# Late Breaking Results: Decentralized Voting-Based Attestation for IoT Devices

**Problem:** Traditional remote attestation relies on single trusted verifier, creating single point of failure.

**Method:** Instead of relying on a single authority to verify if an IoT device is trustworthy, this protocol spreads verification across many independent computers in a blockchain network—each checks the device and votes on whether it's legitimate. All the votes and verification results are recorded permanently on the blockchain, creating a complete and unchangeable history that auditors can review. This distributed approach removes the single weak point that attackers could exploit.

**Key Contribution:** Eliminates single-point-of-failure through decentralized voting on blockchain.

**Results:** Prototype on three NVIDIA Jetson devices with up to 15 attestation nodes.
