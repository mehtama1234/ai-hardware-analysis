# Late Breaking Results: Decentralized Voting-Based Attestation for IoT Devices

**Problem:** Traditional remote attestation relies on single trusted verifier, creating single point of failure.

**Method:** Voting-based blockchain protocol distributes verification across all nodes; creates immutable traceable attestation history.

**Key Contribution:** Eliminates single-point-of-failure through decentralized voting on blockchain.

**Results:** Prototype on three NVIDIA Jetson devices with up to 15 attestation nodes.

**Tags:** iot-security, remote-attestation, blockchain, decentralized, iot-devices, verification
