# Maximizing Energy Efficiency in SNNs via Dynamic Joint Pruning

**Problem:** SNN deployment requires aggressive pruning, but existing methods optimize single sparsity forms, missing joint optimization opportunities.

**Key Idea:** Dynamically prune both spatiotemporal spike sparsity and weight sparsity by combining multi-stage masking, temporal attention batch normalization, and real-time SOP-driven coefficient adjustment.

**Approach:** Three-component framework: TABN for time-adaptive normalization, multi-stage thresholding for neuron firing control, and dynamic importance weighting based on computational impact.

**Results:** 126.38× synaptic operation (SOP) compression on CIFAR-10 with minimal accuracy degradation.

**Theme:** Joint spatiotemporal and weight pruning for neuromorphic energy efficiency.
