# FUSIS: Fusing Surrogate Models and Importance Sampling for Efficient Yield Estimation

**Venue:** DATE  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Core Idea
Combine surrogate models and importance sampling: train Deep Kernel SVM to approximate the yield indicator function, use it as the IS proposal distribution to guide efficient sampling, and apply correction factors for accuracy.

## Problem
Yield estimation faces tradeoff: surrogates are fast but inaccurate; importance sampling is accurate but expensive.

## Method
- **Deep Kernel SVM:** Learns indicator function (yield/fail classification)
- **IS proposal distribution:** Derived from SVM to accelerate convergence
- **Correction factor:** Adjusts yield estimate to compensate for surrogate errors
- Evaluated on SRAM and analog circuits

## Key Results
- **24.84% accuracy improvement** (8.67% average) vs state-of-the-art
- **29.54x speedup** (10.30x average)
