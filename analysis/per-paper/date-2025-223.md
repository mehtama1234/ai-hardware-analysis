# FUSIS: Fusing Surrogate Models and Importance Sampling for Efficient Yield Estimation

**Venue:** DATE  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Core Idea
Combine surrogate models and importance sampling: train Deep Kernel SVM to approximate the yield indicator function, use it as the IS proposal distribution to guide efficient sampling, and apply correction factors for accuracy.

## Problem
Yield estimation faces tradeoff: surrogates are fast but inaccurate; importance sampling is accurate but expensive.

## Method
FUSIS merges two techniques that usually involve choosing between them: it trains a Deep Kernel SVM model (a machine-learning classifier that predicts whether a chip design passes or fails), then uses those predictions to guide importance sampling—a statistical method that concentrates computational effort on scenarios most likely to fail, enabling faster convergence to an accurate yield estimate. A correction factor adjusts the final yield prediction to account for any systematic errors the SVM introduces. The approach was tested on SRAM and analog circuits.

## Key Results
- **24.84% accuracy improvement** (8.67% average) vs state-of-the-art
- **29.54x speedup** (10.30x average)
