# Photonic Interconnect for Accelerated Computing: Celestial AI Photonic Fabric Module (hotchips-2025-037)

## Summary
World's first SoC with integrated in-die optical I/O, enabling direct photonic interconnects for AI accelerator cluster communication.

## Analysis
- **Problem**: AI scale-up systems need on-die optical I/O to overcome electrical interconnect bandwidth and power density limitations
- **Method**: Monolithic integration of photonic modulators, detectors, waveguides with electrical compute logic on single SoC die
- **Key Novelty**: First SoC with integrated in-die optical I/O for direct photonic communication without external optics
- **Hardware**: SoC, photonic
- **Techniques**: Interconnect, circuit design
- **Workloads**: LLM training/inference, transformers
