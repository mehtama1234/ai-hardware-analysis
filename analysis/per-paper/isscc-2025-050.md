# isscc-2025-050: High-Speed Pipelined ADC with Gated-LMS Calibration

**Title:** 24.1 A 12b 3GS/s Pipelined ADC with Gated-LMS-Based Piecewise-Linear Nonlinearity Calibration

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Core Problem:**
Open-loop residue amplifiers in pipelined ADCs introduce gain errors and nonlinearity; polynomial calibration at high sampling rates (3GS/s) incurs prohibitive overhead.

**Method:**
Piecewise-linear (PWL) background calibration via gated LMS filters with adaptive thresholds extracts coefficients without polynomial computation; dual-path amplification improves settling.

**Key Innovation:**
LMS-based PWL calibration enabling efficient high-speed nonlinearity correction without polynomial overhead.

**Metrics:**
- **Resolution:** 12 bits @ 3 GS/s
- **SNDR:** 58.8 dB
- **Power:** 32.5 mW
- **FoM_S:** 165 dB; **FoM_W:** 15.2 fJ/conv-step

**Architecture:** Pipelined ADC with dual-path amplification & background LMS calibration

**Techniques:** Circuit design, adaptive filtering

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)
