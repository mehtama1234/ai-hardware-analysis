# SC-2025-157: Microgrid Optimization for Sustainable Data Centers

## Summary
Framework for optimizing on-site microgrid composition (solar, wind, storage) for data centers by co-simulating workloads, renewable generation, and embodied/operational carbon.

## Problem
- Data center microgrids lack systematic design tools
- Tradeoff between embodied carbon (infrastructure) and operational emissions unclear
- Microgrid sizing decisions complex, impact long-term sustainability
- Existing tools ignore embodied carbon in renewable systems

## Solution: Integrated Co-Simulation
Extends Vessim (energy-compute simulator) with:
1. **NREL SAM models**: Realistic renewable generation profiles (solar, wind, geothermal)
2. **Multi-horizon optimization**: Explore configurations across time scales
3. **Dual objective**: Minimize operational + embodied emissions
4. **Workload integration**: Capture compute-energy interdependencies

## Framework Components
- Computing workload simulator
- Renewable generation models (location-dependent)
- Energy storage dynamics
- Carbon accounting (operational + manufacturing)

## Optimization Approach
Black-box optimization to explore:
- Renewable capacity ratios
- Storage sizing and chemistry
- Dispatch strategies
- Grid interconnection levels

## Applications
- Data center planning (greenfield or retrofit)
- Renewable energy portfolio design
- Regional sustainability assessments

## Limitations
- Abstract only; no concrete optimization results
- Scalability for multi-MW systems unclear
- Policy constraints (grid codes) not discussed
