# PopFetcher: Towards Accelerated Mixture-of-Experts Training Via Popularity Based Expert-Wise Prefetch

**Problem:** Mixture-of-Experts training suffers from substantial All-to-All communications and imbalanced computation workloads due to sparse expert activation, severely degrading training efficiency.

**Method:** PopFetcher predicts expert popularity using a lightweight sliding-window technique to identify high-demand experts, then prefetches them asynchronously during non-MoE computations. It formulates end-to-end training latency as an optimization problem with a pruning strategy to derive the globally optimal prefetching scheme, while prioritizing All-to-All communication during the backward pass.

**Novelty:** Popularity-aided prefetching with formally-optimized pruning strategy derived from end-to-end training latency model.
