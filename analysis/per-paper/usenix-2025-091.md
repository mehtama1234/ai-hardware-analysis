# PopFetcher: Towards Accelerated Mixture-of-Experts Training Via Popularity Based Expert-Wise Prefetch

**Problem:** Mixture-of-Experts training suffers from substantial All-to-All communications and imbalanced computation workloads due to sparse expert activation, severely degrading training efficiency.

**Method:** PopFetcher predicts which experts will be heavily used by tracking recent activity patterns, then loads them into memory early—while the system is working on other tasks—so they're ready when needed. It treats the total training time as a math problem, figuring out exactly which experts are worth prefetching by eliminating unnecessary ones. During the backward pass—when the network learns by updating its weights—the system prioritizes communication across all processors, preventing bottlenecks.

**Novelty:** It predicts which experts will be needed from recent usage patterns, then mathematically determines which ones should be loaded in advance to minimize training time.
