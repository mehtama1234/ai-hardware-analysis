# NSTherm: An Error-Bounded Network-Stochastic Fusion Thermal Simulator for Geometry-Adaptable Chiplets via Diffeomorphic Mapping and Neural-Guided Variance Reduction

**Venue:** ICCAD
**ID:** iccad-2025-141
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Fast thermal simulation across varying chiplet geometries requires balancing computational cost (deterministic solvers), convergence speed (stochastic methods), and error guarantees (neural networks lack bounds).

## Motivation
Efficient thermal simulation is critical for iterative chiplet shape optimization in thermally constrained designs.

## Method
NSTherm combines a neural network—trained to predict heat distribution rapidly—with stochastic methods that use random sampling to solve equations reliably. To handle different chip shapes without retraining, it uses diffeomorphic mapping (a mathematical technique for smoothly transforming one shape into another while preserving structure). The neural network guides the stochastic method to reduce noise in results, while the stochastic calculations verify the neural predictions and prove accuracy bounds.

## Key Novelty
NSTherm's core idea is pairing a fast neural network with stochastic verification through mathematical shape-deformation (diffeomorphic mapping) to guarantee accurate thermal simulation across varying chip geometries.

## Contributions
- Diffeomorphic mapping for operator networks handling geometry variations
- Neural-stochastic fusion for variance reduction and error bounds
- 10.69-23.04x speedup vs. COMSOL
- 5.20-11.87x speedup vs. traditional stochastic methods
