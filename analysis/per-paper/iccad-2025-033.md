# Synthesis of Standard Cells of Minimum Delay

**ID:** iccad-2025-033  
**Venue:** ICCAD

## Problem
Conventional standard cell synthesis prioritizes area minimization at the top level, yielding suboptimal timing for high-performance chips where delay is the critical constraint.

## Motivation
At advanced technology nodes, minimizing cell delay is essential for implementing high-performance chips and improving clock frequency.

## Method
The paper proposes a delay-driven cell synthesis approach with three steps: (1) critical-path-driven transistor placement via search tree enumeration with pruning, (2) optimal transistor folding using dynamic programming to maximize diffusion sharing, and (3) optimal critical-net-driven in-cell routing formulated as an SMT problem.

## Key Novelty
Inverting traditional synthesis optimization hierarchy to prioritize cell delay minimization over area minimization.

## Contributions
1. Critical-path-driven transistor placement with effective pruning for enumeration
2. Optimal transistor folding via dynamic programming for diffusion-sharing maximization
3. Optimal critical-net routing via SMT formulation
4. Cell delay improvements of up to 9.3% enabling 7.2% average frequency increase while maintaining power and area
