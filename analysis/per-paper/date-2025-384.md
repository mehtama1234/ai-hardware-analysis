# SHWCIM: Scalable Heterogeneous Workload Computing-in-Memory Architecture

**Venue:** DATE | **ID:** date-2025-384

## Problem
SRAM-based CIM designs suffer from limited resource utilization, restricted multi-functionality, and constrained memory capacity.

## Motivation
CIM offers energy benefits but lacks flexibility; adding reconfigurability enables heterogeneous workload support while maintaining near-data-processing efficiency.

## Approach
HWCIM (SRAM-based CIM core) + SHWCIM (CIM-capable Coarse-Grained Reconfigurable Architecture) for diverse workload support. Evaluated in SMIC 55nm.

## Key Innovation
Scalable heterogeneous CIM architecture combining SRAM cores with CGRA for flexible multi-functionality.

## Results
- 1.6x lower power vs. prior CIM
- 2.8x higher energy efficiency vs. prior CIM
- 4.1x smaller area vs. prior CIM
- 105.9x speedup vs. CGRA
- 2-5x less energy than Nvidia A40

## Target Hardware
CIM, ASIC

## Techniques
Near-data processing, memory-system, dataflow

## Workload
CNN, Transformer (realistic workloads)
