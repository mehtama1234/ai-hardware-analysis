# isscc-2025-025: Ultra-Low-Power Gm-C GNSS Receiver

**A Gm-C RF Quadrature-Current-Generation Technique with 40dB IRR in 0.65V 2mW Multi-Mode CMOS GNSS Receiver**

## Summary
Ultra-low-power multi-mode GNSS receiver using Gm-C RF quadrature-current-generation achieving 40 dB image-rejection ratio at 0.65 V supply with only 2 mW total power.

## Technical Approach
- **Gm-C quadrature**: Transconductor-based signal quadrature generation, not LO-based
- **Phase accuracy**: Reduced parasitic sensitivity vs. switch-based approaches
- **IRR**: 40 dB image-rejection ratio via robust I/Q generation
- **Power**: 0.65 V supply, 2 mW operation

## Key Innovation
Gm-C quadrature-current-generation eliminating parasitic sensitivity of conventional switch-based quadrature, achieving high IRR in signal-quadrature topology without complex calibration.

## Contributions
- Gm-C robust quadrature-current technique
- 40 dB IRR in signal-quadrature receiver
- Multi-mode GNSS support (multiple standards)
- Ultra-low-power wearable operation

## Target Workloads
Wearable GNSS, location tracking, low-power positioning
