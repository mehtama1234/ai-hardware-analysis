# SparseLUT: Sparse Connectivity Optimization for Lookup Table-Based Deep Neural Networks

**Problem:** Existing LUT-based DNN approaches (LogicNets, PolyLUT, NeuraLUT) rely on fixed sparsity masks determined by random seed optimization, which is inefficient and computationally expensive. SparseLUT addresses the need for deterministic, efficient connectivity optimization without requiring extensive random seed tuning.

**Method:** SparseLUT uses a connectivity-centric training technique with a non-greedy strategy that dynamically optimizes network structure during training. The method prioritizes pruning of less significant connections while strategically regrowing alternative ones, enabling efficient convergence to target sparsity. This approach operates without random seed optimization and incurs no additional hardware overhead.

**Novelty:** Non-greedy connectivity-centric training that eliminates random seed optimization through dynamic pruning and strategic regrowth of connections in LUT-based DNNs.
