# SparseLUT: Sparse Connectivity Optimization for Lookup Table-Based Deep Neural Networks

**Problem:** Existing LUT-based DNN approaches (LogicNets, PolyLUT, NeuraLUT) rely on fixed sparsity masks determined by random seed optimization, which is inefficient and computationally expensive. SparseLUT addresses the need for deterministic, efficient connectivity optimization without requiring extensive random seed tuning.

**Method:** During training, SparseLUT removes weak connections between neurons and tests new ones in their place, continuously searching for the best network structure. This active approach finds good configurations directly, eliminating the computational waste of trying many random seed combinations. The method achieves efficient training with no additional hardware overhead.

**Novelty:** SparseLUT optimizes which connections stay by pruning (removing) weak connections and regrowing (testing) new alternatives during training, eliminating random seed optimization.
