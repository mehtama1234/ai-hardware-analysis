# dac-2025-082: Power-Constrained Printed Neuromorphic Hardware Training

**Problem:** Printed neuromorphic circuits must operate under severe power budgets while maintaining accuracy; penalty-based methods require 150+ runs for Pareto-optimal solutions.

**Method:** Augmented Lagrangian optimization—a mathematical technique that directly enforces power constraints—is built into the training process, so the network learns to stay within a power budget while maintaining accuracy all in one run. Traditionally, finding good power-accuracy tradeoffs requires 150+ separate training attempts; this method does it once. The approach was tested on 13 benchmarks using different activation functions (p-tanh and p-Clipped_ReLU).

**Key Novelty:** Augmented Lagrangian formulation enabling single-run Pareto-optimal power-accuracy tradeoff generation versus penalty-based 150+ runs.

**Hardware Target:** analog  
**Technique Category:** power, circuit-design  
**Workloads:** neuromorphic, low-power-ml  

**Metrics:**
- Accuracy-to-power: 52-59x improvement
- Max accuracy: 81.82% at high power

**Confidence:** low (abstract only)
