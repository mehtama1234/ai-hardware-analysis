# Qiwu: Exploiting Ciphertext-Level SIMD Parallelism in Homomorphic Encryption Programs

**Venue:** CGO | **Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Fully Homomorphic Encryption (CKKS) enables secure computation but reduction operations create large bubbles (gaps) in ciphertexts that waste resources despite plaintext-level SIMD packing.

## Core Innovation
Introducing ciphertext-level fusion for bubble elimination in FHE programs, complementing plaintext-level SIMD parallelism.

## Method
Qiwu performs ciphertext-level vectorization by fusing multiple ciphertexts containing bubbles to eliminate gaps. A DSL specifies zero bubbles in inputs and nonzero bubbles in outputs; data-flow analysis tracks bubble patterns and cost-benefit assessment guides fusion decisions.

## Key Contributions
- Ciphertext-level vectorization approach (Qiwu) for fusing ciphertexts to eliminate bubbles
- DSL for specifying bubble patterns and data-flow analysis for tracking them
- Implementation in FHE compiler: up to 18.0x speedup on CPUs (geometric mean 3.4x)

## Results
- Max speedup: 18.0x
- Geometric mean speedup: 3.4x

## Limitations
Evaluation limited to 4 applications; GPU performance and scalability to larger programs not discussed.
