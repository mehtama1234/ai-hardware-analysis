# FLB: Fine-grained Load Balancing for Lossless Datacenter Networks

**Problem:** Existing fine-grained load balancing schemes designed for lossy datacenter networks are counterproductive in lossless environments with PFC (Priority Flow Control), causing head-of-line (HoL) blocking that worsens congestion rather than alleviates it.

**Method:** FLB employs threshold-free rerouting to balance traffic load and improve link utilization during normal operation, and uses timely congested flow isolation to eliminate HoL blocking on non-congested flows when congestion occurs. The scheme integrates with RDMA/RoCE and coordinates with PFC to avoid pathological rerouting patterns.

**Novelty:** Threshold-free rerouting combined with congested flow isolation specifically designed to eliminate PFC head-of-line blocking while maintaining load balancing effectiveness.
