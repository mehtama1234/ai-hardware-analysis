# TimeFloats: Train-in-Memory with Time-Domain Floating-Point Scalar Products

**Venue:** VLSID

## Problem
Compute-in-memory (CIM) lacks floating-point training capability; conventional ADC/DAC costly in advanced nodes

## Motivation
DNN training in memory attractive for efficiency; floating-point precision needed; ADC/DAC power/complexity limiting

## Method
TimeFloats: time-domain floating-point scalar products avoiding ADC/DAC; pure digital building blocks in 15nm CMOS

## Key Novelty
Time-domain floating-point CIM for training without conventional converters; 22.1 TOPS/W efficiency

## Contributions
- 8-bit floating-point scalar products
- Time-domain processing
- 22.1 TOPS/W energy efficiency
- ADC/DAC elimination
