# ASTRA: Automatic Sizing of Transistors with Reasoning Agents

**Paper ID:** iccad-2025-232  
**Venue:** ICCAD

## Problem

Advancing technology nodes have increased transistor sizing complexity in analog circuit design. Existing AI techniques lack integrated domain expertise, resulting in slow convergence and inefficient optimization.

## Motivation

Analog circuit design automation is critical for modern IC development, but existing AI approaches are purely data-driven without access to domain knowledge like gm/ID methodology.

## Method

ASTRA automatically sizes transistors by connecting AI models, stored engineering knowledge, and Bayesian Optimization (a method that learns from each simulation to make better guesses, requiring fewer tests). It works in two stages: first, it retrieves relevant design rules and uses AI reasoning to narrow down feasible transistor sizes using the gm/ID methodology (which relates transistor gain and current); second, it uses Bayesian Optimization to refine the sizes by focusing on critical transistors the AI identified. The system integrates these components through a messaging protocol that lets them work together seamlessly while fitting into existing design tools.

## Key Novelty

ASTRA combines AI reasoning, engineering knowledge, and Bayesian Optimization to automatically size transistors, reducing required simulations by up to 4.35×.

## Contributions

- MCP-based integration of LLMs with domain knowledge bases for circuit design
- RAG-driven design initialization using gm/ID methodology
- LLM-guided critical transistor identification with BO-based optimization
- Up to 4.35× fewer simulations and 2.36× performance improvements on real circuits
- Open-source framework compatible with multiple sizing algorithms
