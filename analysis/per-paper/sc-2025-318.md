# Usability Evaluation of Cloud for HPC Applications

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-318
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Unclear whether cloud environments (Azure, AWS, GCP) provide equivalent performance and usability for HPC workloads compared to on-premises HPC clusters.

## Motivation
Cloud dominates economically; scientific workflows need portability and reproducibility; need empirical evidence to guide HPC migration decisions.

## Method
They tested 11 standard supercomputing benchmark programs on three cloud platforms—AWS, Azure, and GCP—comparing results against dedicated supercomputers at Lawrence Livermore National Lab. The tests used different combinations of CPUs and GPUs across 6 environment configurations, scaling up to 28,672 CPUs and 256 GPUs. By running identical workloads on both cloud and traditional systems, they could directly measure whether clouds delivered equivalent performance and usability.

## Key Novelty
Testing whether AWS, Azure, and GCP can match traditional supercomputers for scientific computing by running 11 benchmarks at scale—up to 28,672 CPUs and 256 GPUs.

## Contributions
1. Cross-cloud empirical evaluation of HPC workload performance (Azure, AWS, GCP)
2. Systematic scaling study up to 28K CPUs and 256 GPUs
3. Best practices for running HPC in cloud environments
