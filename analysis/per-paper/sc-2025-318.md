# Usability Evaluation of Cloud for HPC Applications

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-318
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Unclear whether cloud environments (Azure, AWS, GCP) provide equivalent performance and usability for HPC workloads compared to on-premises HPC clusters.

## Motivation
Cloud dominates economically; scientific workflows need portability and reproducibility; need empirical evidence to guide HPC migration decisions.

## Method
Cross-platform usability study: 11 HPC proxy apps/benchmarks across 3 clouds, 6 environments, CPU/GPU configs, scaling to 28,672 CPUs and 256 GPUs, benchmarked against LLNL on-premises clusters.

## Key Novelty
Comprehensive comparative study of cloud-HPC convergence across multiple platforms, scaling factors, and workload types.

## Contributions
1. Cross-cloud empirical evaluation of HPC workload performance (Azure, AWS, GCP)
2. Systematic scaling study up to 28K CPUs and 256 GPUs
3. Best practices for running HPC in cloud environments
