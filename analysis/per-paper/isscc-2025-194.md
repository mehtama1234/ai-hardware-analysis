# isscc-2025-194: Self-Calibrating Polar Switched-Capacitor Power Amplifier

**Problem**: Wireless systems (sub-6G, WiFi) need high-power, high-efficiency PAs with minimal linearity distortion; traditional designs require external DPD modules, and SCPAs suffer AM-PM distortion at high frequencies from transistor switching delays.

**Solution**: Polar SCPA with in-cell fast slope-to-phase self-calibration. Edge detectors in each cell detect waveform timing errors; slope-to-phase tuner adjusts edges in real-time to compensate AM-PM distortion. Asymmetrical 4-to-1 transformer balances impedance and efficiency.

**Key Results**:
- 27.7dBm output power at 4.7GHz
- 37.8% PAE (high efficiency)
- 5.8° AM-PM distortion (low linearity error)
- Eliminates external DPD
- Fast calibration loop supporting wideband modulation

**Hardware**: ASIC  
**Application**: Sub-6G / WiFi transmitter  
**Confidence**: low (abstract only)
