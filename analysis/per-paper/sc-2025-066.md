# UpANNS: Enhancing Billion-Scale ANNS Efficiency with Real-World PIM Architecture

**ID:** sc-2025-066 | **Venue:** SC

## Problem Statement
Billion-scale ANNS inefficient: CPU bottlenecked by bandwidth, GPU underutilizes resources; poor energy efficiency

## Motivation
AI systems need efficient billion-scale ANNS for RAG-LLMs and real-time recommendation serving

## Method
UpANNS framework: architecture-aware data placement, dynamic resource management, co-occurrence optimized encoding, early-pruning for top-k

## Key Novelty
Near-linear scalability for billion-entry ANNS on PIM; 4.3x higher QPS than Faiss, 2.3x better energy efficiency than GPU

## Contributions
