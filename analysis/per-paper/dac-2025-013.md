# Fast End-to-End Simulation and Exploration of Many-RISCV-Core Baseband Transceivers for Software-Defined Radio-Access Networks

**Problem:** RTL simulation of many-core SDR processors is prohibitively slow, hindering design-space exploration for 5G baseband processing.

**Method:** Static binary translation simulator with approximate timing model for 1024-core RISC-V cluster; integrated with wireless channel models for realistic 5G OFDM detection simulation.

**Key Contribution:** 3 orders of magnitude speedup over RTL; enables rapid end-to-end performance analysis.

**Results:** Single-threaded: 9.5s-3min per OFDM symbol; 73-121x speedup with 128-thread parallelization.
