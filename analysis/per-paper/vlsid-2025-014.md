# LAMA: A Latency Minimum Resource Constraint Accelerator for CNN Models

**Venue:** VLSID
**Authors:** Sutirtha Bhattacharyya, Fedrick Nongpoh, Karthik Maddala, E. Bhawani Eswar Reddy, Chandan Karfa

## Problem Statement
Deep learning models, particularly Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), have become crucial in various domains due to their wide applications and exceptional performance

## Key Innovation
Novel novel approach

## Method
LAMA works by providing a configurable design that lets engineers adjust how much hardware resources the accelerator uses versus how fast it executes CNNs. Different applications have different priorities—some need maximum speed while others have tight limits on chip size or power consumption—so this tunability lets each designer optimize for their situation. Rather than buying a fixed accelerator, they can configure it to balance performance and resource use according to their specific constraints.
