# ISSCC 2025-209: Low-Cost CMOS Terahertz Source with Matching-Sheet Radiation

## Core Contribution
232–260GHz CMOS THz amplifier-multiplier chain achieving 11.1dBm radiated power via low-cost patterned dielectric matching sheet, enabling practical on-chip THz generation for 6G/imaging without expensive silicon lenses.

## Technical Approach
- **Front-End**: Multiplier chain generating THz from lower-frequency CMOS oscillators
- **Power Amplifier**: Broadband gain-peaking PA using high-power FinFET (HyPowerFF) technology
- **Radiation**: Back-side emission via slot/dipole antennas on silicon substrate
- **Matching Sheet**: Patterned dielectric film applied to chip backside, reducing total-internal-reflection losses and improving radiation efficiency
- **Array**: On-chip amplifier-multiplier array for power combining

## Performance Metrics
- **Frequency**: 232–260GHz (28GHz bandwidth, 12% fractional)
- **Radiated Power**: 11.1dBm (12.6W equivalent, orders-of-magnitude improvement vs. prior CMOS THz sources at mW level)
- **Radiation Efficiency**: Improved (exact % not specified, but matching sheet addresses Si-air impedance mismatch)
- **Technology**: CMOS with HyPowerFF high-power transistors

## Baselines
- Front-side patch antenna CMOS sources (25% efficiency, 5% bandwidth, mW power)
- Back-side with silicon lens (high assembly cost, cm-scale bulky)
- Discrete/hybrid THz modules (expensive, not integrated)

## Hardware Impact
Democratizes chip-scale THz generation for 6G transceivers, compact imaging systems, and integrated spectroscopy. Matching-sheet approach reduces assembly cost and enables planar packaging, making THz-on-chip practical for commercial products.
