# Opto-ViT: Architecting a Near-Sensor Region of Interest-Aware Vision Transformer Accelerator with Silicon Photonics

**Venue:** ICCAD
**ID:** iccad-2025-144
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Vision Transformers have substantial compute and memory demands, hindering deployment in energy and bandwidth-constrained scenarios.

## Motivation
Silicon photonics-based acceleration can enable real-time, energy-efficient vision transformer inference at the edge.

## Method
Opto-ViT splits computation between photonic (light-based) and electronic processors: the photonic half uses lasers called VCSELs and microring resonators—optical devices that manipulate light—to rapidly multiply large matrices, while the electronic half handles operations that are difficult to implement with light, such as nonlinear functions and normalization. A lightweight network examines the image and identifies which regions matter, then skips processing the irrelevant patches. The vision transformer model is prepared for this hybrid approach through quantization-aware training (learning while simulating the lower precision the photonic hardware will use) and matrix decomposition (splitting large matrices into smaller pieces that fit the photonic constraints).

## Key Novelty
A photonic hardware accelerator positioned near image sensors that speeds up vision transformers on edge devices by skipping irrelevant patches and splitting computation between photonic and electronic processors.

## Contributions
- Hybrid electronic-photonic architecture for ViT acceleration
- Lightweight MGNet for region-aware patch pruning
- Quantization-aware training and matrix decomposition for photonic constraints
- 100.4 KFPS/W with 84% energy savings and <1.6% accuracy loss
