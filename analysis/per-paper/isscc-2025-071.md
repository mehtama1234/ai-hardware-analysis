# isscc-2025-071

**Title:** 4.2 A 1.8-to-3.0GHz Fully Integrated All-In-One CMOS Frequency Management Module Achieving -47/+42ppm Inaccuracy from -40 to 95°C and -150/+70ppm After Accelerated Aging

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Problem:** Crystal-based frequency references are bulky; on-chip RC-based references suffer poor aging (±1030 ppm drift); no prior work integrates on-chip reference into synthesizer for true FMM.

**Key Contribution:** First fully integrated FMM (RC reference + synthesizer) achieves crystal-like accuracy (±47/±42 ppm) and aging specs (±150/±70 ppm post-aging).

**Method:** On-chip RC reference with improved stability techniques, integrated synthesizer, 1.8–3.0 GHz output.

**Hardware:** ASIC, SoC

**Metrics:** ±47/±42 ppm accuracy (-40 to +95°C), ±150/±70 ppm post-aging

**Techniques:** Circuit design

**Primary Theme:** Crystal-free fully integrated frequency management
