# 333-eDRAM - 3T Embedded DRAM Leveraging Monolithic 3D Integration

**Authors:** David Kong, Shvetank Prakash, Jedrzej Kufel, Georgios Kyriazidis, Yasmine Omri, David Verity, Emre Ozer, Vijay Janapa Reddi  
**Venue:** DAC 2025

## Summary
333-eDRAM leverages monolithic 3D integration of three different transistor types to overcome fundamental eDRAM design trade-offs. Using IGZO FETs for retention, CNFETs for speed, and silicon CMOS for peripherals, it achieves 1.96x better EDP and 5.15x better EADP versus conventional silicon eDRAM.

## Key Contributions
- Heterogeneous-material 3D eDRAM architecture (IGZO + CNT + Si)
- Simultaneous optimization of density, retention, endurance, access speed, energy, and leakage
- 1.96x EDP improvement over silicon eDRAM
- 5.15x EADP improvement over silicon eDRAM
- Full physical layout at 7nm demonstrating manufacturability

## Technical Approach
The design uses three FET types optimized for different functions: IGZO for ultra-low leakage (retention), carbon nanotube for high drive current (access speed), and silicon for efficient peripherals. Monolithic 3D stacking integrates these layers. Validation includes cycle-accurate simulation on ARM Cortex-M0 with Embench benchmarks plus SPICE modeling calibrated to experimental data.
