# APSQ: Additive Partial Sum Quantization with Algorithm-Hardware Co-Design

## Problem
High-precision partial sum (PSUM) storage in input/weight stationary DNN accelerators causes excessive memory bandwidth and power consumption (~69% of power), a bottleneck overlooked by prior compression methods.

## Solution
APSQ integrates PSUM accumulation into the quantization framework, compressing partial sums to INT8 through a grouping strategy and reconfigurable hardware. Near-lossless compression on BERT, Segformer, EfficientViT, and LLaMA2-7B.

## Impact
28-87% energy reduction across models. First systematic treatment of PSUM quantization as a primary target in DNN accelerators.
