# hpca-2025-023 — Bit-slice Architecture for DNN Acceleration with Slice-level Sparsity Enhancement and Exploitation

**Venue:** HPCA  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Existing bit-slice DNN accelerators exploit sparsity only at coarse granularity — either input or weight sparsity independently — failing to jointly leverage both at fine bit-slice chunk granularity, leaving large computational savings unrealized.

## Motivation
Bit-slice architectures offer high reconfigurability for mixed-precision DNN inference, but fine-grained joint input+weight sparsity potential is underutilized due to hardware complexity.

## Method
BADA's front-end unit generates bit-slices and selectively gathers non-zero slices using packed-nonzero selection, forwarding them to a signed MAC back-end. Computation is skipped for (a) all-zero bit-slice chunks (coarse) and (b) any operand pair with either a zero input or weight slice (fine-grained joint). Two algorithmic optimizations: (1) 8-bit data bit-slice representation with no hardware overhead; (2) training-time weight distribution narrowing to increase zero-valued higher-order slices.

## Key Novelty
Joint exploitation of both input and weight sparsity at bit-slice granularity using front-end non-zero selection, combined with training-aware weight distribution narrowing to amplify higher-order slice sparsity.

## Contributions
- BADA architecture: coarse- and fine-grained joint input+weight bit-slice sparsity exploitation.
- Front-end non-zero slice selection enabling skip for any zero-slice operand pair.
- 8-bit data bit-slice representation without additional hardware overhead.
- Training-time weight narrowing; 2.67x throughput, 1.52x area, 2.15x energy efficiency vs. LUTein.
