# ISSCC-2025-149: Dual Fused Temperature/VDD Sensor for Chiplet Monitoring

**Problem:**  
Heterogeneous 3D chiplets face thermal hotspots and workload-dependent supply voltage gradients; spatiotemporal thermal and power delivery monitoring via separate sensors is costly.

**Solution:**  
Dual fused temperature and VDD sensor in 65nm CMOS via sensor fusion; achieves 2.4°C and 9mV (±3σ) inaccuracy without external references.

**Key Results:**  
- 2.4°C temperature accuracy (±3σ)
- 9 mV VDD accuracy (±3σ)
- Robust to VDD noise and scaling
- No external calibration references

**Key Innovation:**  
First sensor fusion combining temperature and VDD measurement in single compact IP, enabling distributed thermal and power-delivery monitoring for chiplets.
