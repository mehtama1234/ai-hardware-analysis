# isscc-2025-214: Fully Integrated Hybrid Energy-Harvesting with Coil-Sharing

## Quick Summary
An inductor-less, capacitor-less energy-harvesting platform combining piezoelectric and electromagnetic transducers, with a coil-sharing scheme that reuses the EM coil across all power-conversion stages.

## Problem
Current energy-harvesting designs require external power components:
- High-quality off-chip inductors (SSHI rectifiers for PE)
- Off-chip switched capacitors or inductors (volume/cost penalty)
- Multiple separate inductors/capacitors in different stages

This increases system volume and cost, limiting widespread IoT/edge-AI deployment.

## Method
Hybrid PE-EM platform where the EM harvester's inherent coil serves as:
1. Boost converter inductor (EM rectification)
2. Inductor for DC-DC conversion
3. Inductor for output regulation

Eliminated need for separate external inductors/capacitors through architectural coil-sharing.

## Key Novelty
Coil-sharing architecture that enables fully integrated energy harvesting without external power components.

## Contributions
- Hybrid PE-EM harvesting on single platform
- Inductor-less, capacitor-less design
- Coil-sharing across multiple conversion stages
- Reduced volume and cost
