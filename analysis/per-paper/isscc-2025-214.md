# isscc-2025-214: Fully Integrated Hybrid Energy-Harvesting with Coil-Sharing

## Quick Summary
An inductor-less, capacitor-less energy-harvesting platform combining piezoelectric and electromagnetic transducers, with a coil-sharing scheme that reuses the EM coil across all power-conversion stages.

## Problem
Current energy-harvesting designs require external power components:
- High-quality off-chip inductors (SSHI rectifiers for PE)
- Off-chip switched capacitors or inductors (volume/cost penalty)
- Multiple separate inductors/capacitors in different stages

This increases system volume and cost, limiting widespread IoT/edge-AI deployment.

## Method
The platform combines two types of energy harvesters—piezoelectric (which converts vibration into electricity through material deformation) and electromagnetic (which converts motion into electricity using a coil in a magnetic field). Rather than using separate inductors and capacitors (electronic components that store and regulate electrical energy) at each power-conversion stage, it reuses the electromagnetic coil as a shared inductor for all three stages: rectification (converting AC to DC power), DC-DC conversion (adjusting voltage levels), and regulation (stabilizing the output voltage). This coil-sharing synchronous rectification scheme means one component does multiple jobs, eliminating the need for external inductors and capacitors.

## Key Novelty
A coil-sharing scheme that eliminates external inductors and capacitors by reusing the electromagnetic harvester's coil across all power-conversion stages.

## Contributions
- Hybrid PE-EM harvesting on single platform
- Inductor-less, capacitor-less design
- Coil-sharing across multiple conversion stages
- Reduced volume and cost
