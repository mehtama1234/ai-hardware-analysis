# hpca-2025-014 — SoMa: Identifying, Exploring, and Understanding the DRAM Communication Scheduling Space for DNN Accelerators

**Venue:** HPCA  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
As on-chip buffers grow large enough to hold most single-layer weights, single-layer dataflow optimization no longer reduces DRAM bandwidth significantly. Multi-layer fusion and cross-layer DRAM communication scheduling remain poorly characterized.

## Motivation
DRAM bandwidth is the persistent bottleneck for DNN accelerators; larger SRAMs now enable multi-layer fusion, making cross-layer prefetch/store scheduling a large, unexploited opportunity.

## Method
Tensor-centric Notation (TCN) formally represents DRAM communication scheduling schemes across fused layer groups. An end-to-end compiler (SoMa) explores the space by jointly optimizing prefetch timing, store scheduling, and layer fusion for diverse accelerators. Deployed as a compiler for a commercial accelerator product.

## Key Novelty
Tensor-centric Notation that formally spans the DRAM communication scheduling space for multi-layer fused DNN execution, enabling compiler-driven exploration beyond single-layer dataflow.

## Contributions
- Identification of multi-layer DRAM communication scheduling as the key untapped optimization beyond single-layer dataflow.
- TCN representation and parsing method for the full scheduling space.
- SoMa compiler: 2.11x perf improvement and 37.3% energy reduction vs. Cocco.
- LLM scheduling analysis and DSE; open-sourced at GitHub.
