# sc-2025-212: Beyond End-to-End - LLMs in Scientific Problem Solving

## Overview
Evaluates multimodal LLMs on decomposed scientific question-answering tasks to expose reasoning bottlenecks and integration failures.

## Key Problem
End-to-end benchmarks for scientific MLLMs hide where and why models fail. Both Gemini 2.5 Pro and Qwen2.5-VL-32B fail on high-resolution visual data without external tools or scripting.

## Approach
Decompose scientific QA into fine-grained subtasks (coordinate mapping, pixel computation) to enable detailed assessment of where reasoning breaks down. Evaluate on high-resolution visual data.

## Key Findings
- Models can solve individual subtasks but fail to integrate them
- Large deviations emerge in end-to-end reasoning despite individual competence
- Scripting or tool use necessary for accurate answers

## Contributions
- Fine-grained evaluation framework for scientific QA
- Analysis of reasoning integration failures in state-of-the-art MLLMs
- Evidence supporting need for agent-based or multi-model approaches

## Evaluated Models
- Gemini 2.5 Pro
- Qwen2.5-VL-32B-Instruct
