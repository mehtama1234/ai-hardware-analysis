# SC-2025-165: SYCL Performance Portability for Low-Bit GEMV

## Summary
Performance portability study comparing SYCL vs CUDA/HIP implementations of low-bit fused matrix-vector operations (GEMV) across NVIDIA and AMD GPUs, identifying specific optimization barriers.

## Problem
- SYCL promises cross-platform GPU performance but gaps exist
- Low-bit operations (INT8, FP8) lack SYCL evaluation
- Performance portability barriers not well understood
- Prevents adoption of vendor-neutral programming models

## Study Design
**Kernels**: Low-bit fused GEMV (INT8, FP8 types)

**Implementation**: CUDA, HIP, SYCL versions

**GPU Coverage**:
| Tier | NVIDIA | AMD |
|------|--------|-----|
| **Datacenter** | A100 | MI100/MI250 |
| **Desktop** | RTX3090 | MI250 |
| **Laptop** | RTX4050 | MI28 |

## Root Causes of Performance Gaps

### 1. Loop Unrolling
- Compiler handling differs (NVCC vs LLVM)
- SYCL less predictable than vendor models

### 2. Kernel Dispatch Overhead
- SYCL dispatch larger than CUDA/HIP
- Impacts small-kernel performance

### 3. Sum Reduction
- Inefficient reduction in SYCL
- Vendor models have specialized optimizations

## Implications
- SYCL compilers need improvement (loop unrolling, reduction)
- Dispatch overhead strategy needed
- Vendor-specific paths may be necessary for peak performance

## Limitations
- Abstract only; no specific performance numbers
- Single kernel type (GEMV) studied
- No proposed solutions yet
