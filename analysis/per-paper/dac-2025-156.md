# Late Breaking Results: Automated Topology Generation for Power Amplifier Designs

**Authors:** Lida Kouhalvandi, Sercan Aygun, M. Hassan Najafi, Arman Roohi  
**Venue:** DAC 2025

## Summary
This work automates power amplifier design by using a BiLSTM deep neural network to predict optimal circuit topology (matching networks) for a given HEMT. Multi-objective Pareto optimization refines hyperparameters. Demonstrated on GaN HEMTs achieving 40 dBm output, 50%+ PAE, and 10+ dB gain.

## Key Contributions
- BiLSTM classifier for automated PA topology selection
- Multi-objective Pareto hyperparameter tuning
- Method agnostic to specific HEMT models
- Validated designs achieving 40 dBm, 50%+ PAE, 10+ dB gain

## Technical Approach
A bidirectional LSTM network is trained to classify and recommend optimal PA topologies given HEMT characteristics. The input/output matching networks are predicted by the classifier. Multi-objective optimization (Pareto front-based) optimizes the DNN architecture itself (layers, neurons).
