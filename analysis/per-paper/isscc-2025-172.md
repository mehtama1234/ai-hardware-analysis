# isscc-2025-172: Multi-Channel IR-UWB Transceiver with Shared Antennas

**Title:** 10.4 A 2-TRX IR-UWB Transceiver with Shared Antennas Supporting Channels 5 to 12 in Compliance with IEEE 802.15.4/4z Standards

**Problem:** UWB needs multi-channel support to handle interference in crowded 6GHz, while minimizing cost via antenna sharing.

**Approach:** Fully integrated 2-TRX IR-UWB with optimized CMOS RF switches supporting channels 5-12, shared TX/RX antennas.

**Key Result:** Reduced insertion loss vs prior CMOS, full integration eliminates external switches, IEEE 802.15.4/4z compliant.

**Hardware:** ASIC/SoC (UWB transceiver).

**Tags:** ir-uwb, transceiver, multi-channel, shared-antenna, rf-switch, iot
