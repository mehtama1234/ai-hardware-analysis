# isscc-2025-069

**Title:** 19.3 A Fractional-N PLL with 34fsrms Jitter and -255.5dB FoM Based on a Multipath Feedback Technique

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Problem:** Advanced wireless (4K-QAM) demands sub-100 fs jitter PLLs; prior designs use expensive high-reference frequencies (150–500 MHz), impractical for mass deployment.

**Key Contribution:** Multipath feedback technique in analog PLL achieves 34 fs RMS jitter at practical 80 MHz reference frequency (7.5x lower than prior work).

**Method:** Analog PLL with multipath feedback, parallel feedback paths reduce charge-pump dead-zone and spurs, improved phase margin.

**Hardware:** ASIC

**Metrics:** 34 fs RMS jitter, -255.5 dB FoM, 7.3–9.5 GHz output

**Techniques:** Circuit design

**Primary Theme:** Low-reference-freq PLL for cost-effective wireless
