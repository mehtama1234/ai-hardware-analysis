# isscc-2025-087: Flexible Ka-Band Satellite Phased-Array Receiver

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Small satellite constellations require diverse RF receivers: lightweight satellites need low-power single-polarization; heavier satellites need high-speed dual-polarization from independent sources.

## Motivation
Cost-effective LEO satellite constellations demand flexible, adaptable RF front-ends for diverse mission profiles and power budgets.

## Method
256-element Ka-band phased-array receiver with switch-type quadrature-hybrid-first architecture enabling mode switching (single/dual-circular/linear polarization).

## Key Novelty
Switch-type quadrature hybrid enabling flexible polarization modes with 50% power reduction for single-polarization.

## Contributions
- 256-element Ka-band phased-array
- Switch-type quadrature hybrid
- 50% power reduction for single-polarization
- Dual-independent signal reception

## Metrics
- Array elements: 256
- Frequency: Ka-band
- Power reduction: 50% for single-polarization
