# CLASS: A Controller-Centric Layout Synthesizer for Dynamic Quantum Circuits

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-024

## Problem
Traditional quantum layout synthesis focuses on circuit depth optimization; ignores classical controller communication time, critical for Dynamic Quantum Circuits with runtime measurements and conditional operations.

## Motivation
Dynamic quantum circuits require controller-to-controller communication optimization beyond traditional device-centric depth minimization.

## Method
Hypergraph-based modeling of distributed control system; heuristic-based graph partitioning algorithm optimizes controller communication latency.

## Key Novelty
First controller-centric layout synthesizer addressing inter-controller communication in distributed quantum control systems.

## Contributions
- Controller-centric design methodology for DQC layout
- Hypergraph-based modeling of distributed control systems
- Heuristic-based graph partitioning algorithm
- Up to 100% communication latency reduction with only 2.10% avg operation increase
