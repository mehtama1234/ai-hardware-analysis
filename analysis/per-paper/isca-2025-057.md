# QR-Map: A Map-Based Approach to Quantum Circuit Abstraction for Qubit Reuse Optimization

**Venue:** ISCA 2025  
**Authors:** Hyungseok Kim, Enhyeok Jang, Seungwoo Choi, Youngmin Kim, Won Woo Ro  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Identifying optimal qubit reuse opportunities in quantum circuits via mid-circuit measurements is NP-hard in the general case; existing frameworks either suffer from exponential search complexity or produce non-optimal results due to non-deterministic heuristics.

## Motivation
Quantum devices have severely limited qubit counts, so efficient mid-circuit qubit reuse directly determines whether practical algorithms fit on near-term hardware, but current compilation tools cannot guarantee optimality without prohibitive runtime.

## Method
QR-Map extracts and aligns two-qubit gates into a map structure, making dependency detection deterministic and tractable. The framework identifies reuse opportunities by analyzing aligned gate maps, ensuring qubit savings without excessive idle time, while jointly optimizing gate serialization depth and crosstalk reduction through a polynomial-time map-based search.

## Key Novelty
A map-based circuit abstraction that aligns two-qubit gates to expose qubit reuse dependencies deterministically, replacing exponential DAG search with a structured, optimal framework.

## Contributions
- QR-Map framework for deterministic, near-optimal qubit reuse without exponential search.
- 20% average qubit count reduction vs state-of-the-art.
- 25% circuit depth reduction and 22% SWAP insertion reduction.
- Balanced optimization of gate serialization depth and crosstalk.
