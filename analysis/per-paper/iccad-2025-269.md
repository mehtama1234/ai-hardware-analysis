# Seeing Through Designs: Attention-Based Knowledge Transfer for Preference-Guided Microarchitecture Search

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**Paper ID:** iccad-2025-269

## Problem Statement
Microarchitecture design search faces large search spaces and lengthy validation flows; design knowledge reuse across architectures is limited by PPA metric discrepancies.

## Motivation
Cross-architecture knowledge transfer can accelerate design discovery while meeting user-specified PPA targets.

## Method
Uses cross-attention networks to capture microarchitectural topology and design tool configuration interdependencies for knowledge adaptation across architectures, complemented by uncertainty-guided optimization for preference-based search.

## Key Novelty
Attention-based cross-architecture knowledge transfer with uncertainty-guided preference-based microarchitecture search.

## Contributions

1. 68.16% higher hypervolume
2. 3.85× speedup to target hypervolume
3. Discovery of user-specified PPA targets SOTA missed
