# isscc-2025-000: Fine-Grained Thermal Profiling in High-Density SoCs

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Localized thermal hotspots in power-dense SoCs and 3D-stacked systems with integrated voltage regulators create fine-grained temperature gradients exceeding 10°C across 100μm scales, limiting performance and reliability of AI/ML accelerators.

## Approach
Develops low-voltage micron-scale thermal sensors integrated into 16nm CMOS. Demonstrates profiling on a buck converter and SoC load-current emulator to capture spatial-temporal hotspot patterns.

## Key Contribution
Micron-scale thermal sensors enable real-time, fine-grained (100μm) thermal monitoring—critical for managing AI/ML accelerator hotspots in dense systems.

## Technical Details
- Sensor resolution: ~100μm spatial granularity
- Validated on 3D-integrated systems with on-chip voltage regulators
- Runtime monitoring for dynamic hotspot detection
