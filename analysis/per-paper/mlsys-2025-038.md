# MAS-Attention: Memory-Aware Stream Processing for Attention Acceleration on Edge Devices

**ID:** mlsys-2025-038 | **Venue:** MLSYS 2025 | **Confidence:** high

## Problem
On resource-constrained edge neural accelerators, attention acceleration methods (FLAT, oneDNN) execute MatMul and softmax sequentially on MAC and VEC units, leaving one unit idle at all times.

## Method
Semi-synchronous MAC-VEC stream processing: two streams of tiled tasks run in parallel (tiled MatMul on MAC, tiled softmax on VEC). A multi-tiered tiling scheme is optimized offline via MCTS + Genetic Algorithm. A proactive buffer overwrite strategy prevents cache overflow by selectively overwriting K or V tiles and reloading them, prioritizing softmax completion.

## Key Novelty
First attention acceleration for edge accelerators exploiting MAC-VEC heterogeneous parallelism via semi-synchronous streams, with proactive cache overwrite preventing pipeline stalls under constrained on-chip memory.

## Metrics
- **Speedup:** Up to 2.75x over FLAT (simulated, geomean 1.70x); up to 1.76x on real Huawei DaVinci NPU (geomean 1.42x)
- **Energy:** 54% reduction vs FLAT (simulated, geomean 18.55%)

## Baselines
Layer-Wise (unfused), Soft-Pipe, FLAT, TileFlow, FuseMax
