# dac-2025-111: Energy-Efficient Large-Scale Vector Similarity Search in NAND-Flash via Hybrid Matching

## Quick Summary
Hybrid-M is a 3D NAND-based in-memory VSS architecture that combines exact-search and approximate-search modes. ES mode filters out non-target vectors before expensive AS-mode comparisons, reducing energy by 67–83% vs. AS-only approaches for FSL and ANNS workloads.

## Key Insight
Dual-mode MCAM filtering: use low-cost exact matching to eliminate candidates before higher-cost approximate similarity, dramatically reducing wasted comparisons.

## Hardware & Technique
- **Target**: ASIC, PIM (Processing-in-Memory)
- **Techniques**: Near-data-processing, memory-system optimization, approximation
- **Workloads**: LLM inference, recommendation systems

## Results
- 67–83% energy reduction (FSL/ANNS)
- Comparable accuracy to AS-only baseline
- Baseline: MCAM-based VSS in AS mode
