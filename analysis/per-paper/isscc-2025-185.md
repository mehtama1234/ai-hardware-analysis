# isscc-2025-185: Sigma-Floating Switched-Capacitor AC-DC Converter

**Problem**: IoT/smart-home devices need compact, integrated AC-DC converters with high efficiency and large voltage conversion ratios; traditional inductive converters are bulky, linear regulators inefficient, and passive SC converters lack regulation.

**Solution**: Sigma-floating switched-capacitor topology with three-mode active rectifier enabling regulated 1.52W output from 85-230VAC mains, reducing idle-phase losses.

**Key Results**:
- 81.3% peak efficiency
- 1.52W output power (>1000x prior SC approaches)
- Regulated 3.3-4.6VDc output
- Integrated on-chip

**Hardware**: ASIC  
**Application**: IoT / smart-home power delivery  
**Confidence**: low (abstract only)
