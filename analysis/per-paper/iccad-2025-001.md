# (Invited Paper) AnaFlow: Agentic LLM-based Workflow for Reasoning-Driven Explainable and Sample-Efficient Analog Circuit Sizing

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-001

## Problem
Analog circuit sizing remains a handcrafted, time-consuming process prone to errors, requiring excessive simulation cycles and lacking explainability in automated approaches.

## Motivation
Automating analog design with explainability and sample efficiency enables faster iteration and wider adoption of AI-driven circuit design tools.

## Method
Multiple AI agents (powered by large language models) analyze the circuit's structure and design goals, then iteratively adjust the parameter values to meet performance targets. They use an adaptive simulation strategy—each design test teaches them something, and they learn from successful and failed attempts to decide which simulations to run next, reducing wasted effort. At each step, the agents explain their reasoning, so engineers can understand and verify each design choice.

## Key Novelty
AI agents (powered by large language models) design analog circuits efficiently while explaining their choices, by learning from previous design attempts to optimize which simulations to run next.

## Contributions
1. Agentic LLM workflow for circuit topology interpretation and design goal understanding
2. Adaptive simulation strategy for high sample efficiency
3. Learning mechanism that avoids past mistakes and accelerates convergence
4. Explainable design space exploration tool for analog circuit optimization
