# (Invited Paper) AnaFlow: Agentic LLM-based Workflow for Reasoning-Driven Explainable and Sample-Efficient Analog Circuit Sizing

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-001

## Problem
Analog circuit sizing remains a handcrafted, time-consuming process prone to errors, requiring excessive simulation cycles and lacking explainability in automated approaches.

## Motivation
Automating analog design with explainability and sample efficiency enables faster iteration and wider adoption of AI-driven circuit design tools.

## Method
Multi-agent LLM workflow interprets circuit topology and design goals, iteratively refines parameters via adaptive simulation strategy that learns from optimization history. Agents provide human-interpretable reasoning at each step.

## Key Novelty
LLM-agent-based framework for sample-efficient analog sizing with inherent explainability, adapting simulation strategy based on prior optimization mistakes.

## Contributions
1. Agentic LLM workflow for circuit topology interpretation and design goal understanding
2. Adaptive simulation strategy for high sample efficiency
3. Learning mechanism that avoids past mistakes and accelerates convergence
4. Explainable design space exploration tool for analog circuit optimization
