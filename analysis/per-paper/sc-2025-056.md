# What to Support When You're Compressing: The State of Practice Gaps and Opportunities for Scientific Data Compression

**ID:** sc-2025-056  
**Venue:** SC  
**Confidence:** high

## Problem
Lossy compression widely adopted but impact on scientific outcomes poorly characterized; mismatch between app needs and compressor capabilities

## Motivation
Two decades of lossy compression use in HPC requires systematic assessment of real-world practice and emerging gaps

## Method
The researchers surveyed 9 supercomputing domains. For each, they documented three things: the data the application processes (its workload), what compression it specifically needs, and what current compressors can actually provide. This revealed the gaps—where applications need compression features that current tools don't offer.

## Key Novelty
The paper identifies where compression tools fall short in meeting scientific application needs and provides 24 actionable recommendations to address the gaps.

## Contributions
1. Characterizes applications' needs across 9 domains
2. Documents compressor capability gaps
3. Provides best practices for applications and facilities
4. Identifies research opportunities
