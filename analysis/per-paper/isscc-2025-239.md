# isscc-2025-239: CORDIC-Less Digital Polar Transmitter

**Problem:** Digital polar transmitters require CORDIC for I/Q-to-polar conversion but CORDIC's nonlinearity expands AM/PM output bandwidth, challenging wideband implementations. Switched-capacitor PAs suffer low backoff efficiency; existing AM/PM methods introduce nonlinearity requiring DPD.

**Approach:** CORDIC-less polar transmitter using intrinsically linear 1-bit DSM-based PA with edge-selection-based phase modulation, avoiding CORDIC and achieving linearity without DPD.

**Key novelty:** CORDIC-less architecture with 1-bit DSM PA and edge-selection phase modulation, eliminating CORDIC nonlinearity.

**Metrics:** 50MS/s 256-QAM, >20% PAE without DPD.

**Tags:** transmitter, polar, dsm, digital, power-efficient, linearity
