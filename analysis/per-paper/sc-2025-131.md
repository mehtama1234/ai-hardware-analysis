# Characterizing Performance, Power, and Energy of AMD CDNA3 GPU Family

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-131
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
AMD CDNA3 (MI300X, MI325X) GPU performance, power, and energy characteristics are not well characterized for HPC/AI workloads.

## Motivation
CDNA3 is new generation for HPC and generative AI; understanding real performance/power/energy is critical for procurement and optimization.

## Method
Researchers independently tested AMD's MI300X and MI325X GPUs using focused tests (microbenchmarks) that measure specific capabilities—compute speed, memory access speed, and data throughput—plus real AI and scientific computing applications. This approach reveals both peak performance and real-world behavior while tracking speed, power consumption, and energy efficiency.

## Key Novelty
This provides the first comprehensive independent testing of AMD's MI300X and MI325X GPUs to measure their speed, power consumption, and energy efficiency.

## Contributions
1. Microbenchmark characterization (CU, memory, bandwidth)
2. Real-world application performance
3. Energy efficiency analysis
