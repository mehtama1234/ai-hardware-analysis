# Late Breaking Results: Utilization of Hybrid Threshold-Voltage Flip-flops for Power Recovery

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Leakage power dominates at advanced nodes; multi-VT optimization avoids critical paths where timing constraints prevent low-VT cells.

## Key Idea
Hybrid-VT flip-flops with different VT types in master/slave latches enable independent setup time and clock-to-Q control.

## Method
Flip-flops store data in two sequential parts: master and slave latches. Using slower transistors reduces power but makes both input timing (setup time) and output speed (clock-to-Q delay) worse together. This design uses faster transistors in the slave latch (to meet timing requirements on critical paths) and slower, power-saving transistors in the master latch (where input timing is less strict). This decoupled control enables power reduction throughout the design while satisfying all timing constraints.

## Contributions
1. Novel hybrid-VT flip-flop cell design
2. Independent delay control per latch
3. 8.97% additional leakage reduction with zero timing impact

ASIC (standard cell library)
