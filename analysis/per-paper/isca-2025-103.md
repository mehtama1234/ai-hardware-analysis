# ARTERY: Fast Quantum Feedback using Branch Prediction
**Venue:** ISCA | **Confidence:** low (abstract only)

## Problem
Quantum feedback in dynamic circuits introduces significant latency because each feedback branch must wait for measurement results to propagate through classical decoding and control, blocking gate execution and degrading circuit fidelity. The dynamic nature of mid-circuit measurement prevents speculative execution under current quantum control hardware.

## Motivation
Dynamic quantum circuits increasingly rely on fast mid-circuit feedback for error correction and algorithm execution, but the latency of measurement-to-gate feedback (dictated by readout and classical processing) has become a critical performance limiter.

## Method
ARTERY applies branch prediction to quantum feedback by combining two sources of information: historical statistics of branch outcomes and a real-time readout-pulse trajectory predictor that analyzes the in-flight pulse shape to predict the qubit state before full demodulation is complete. A reconciled branch predictor fuses these two signals to allow downstream gates to begin executing speculatively. Hardware support includes a scalable inter-FPGA backplane for feedback routing, a trigger mechanism for dynamic instruction timing, and an adaptive pulse sampling technique to maximize bandwidth.

## Key Novelty
Applying classical branch prediction—fusing historical branch statistics with real-time readout-pulse trajectory analysis—to speculatively pre-execute quantum gates before mid-circuit measurement results are finalized.

## Contributions
- Reconciled branch predictor combining historical branch statistics with real-time readout-pulse trajectory prediction, achieving over 90% prediction accuracy.
- 2.07x speedup in quantum feedback latency over state-of-the-art methods.
- 1.24x fidelity improvement due to reduced gate blocking.
- Scalable inter-FPGA backplane connection with a feedback trigger mechanism and adaptive pulse sampling for hardware bandwidth maximization.
