# dac-2025-276: BS-PDN-Last—Multifunctional Backside Power Delivery

**Problem:** Traditional PDN-first flows reserve backside for power, leaving suboptimal signal routing and performance trade-offs.

**Motivation:** Maximizing PPA gains requires rethinking design order to exploit multifunctional backside layers.

**Method:** BS-PDN-last defers power routing to post-signal-routing stage, allowing performance optimization first, then adapting power delivery to remaining resources. Vector-based IR-drop analysis.

**Key Novelty:** Post-signal-routing PDN design to unlock multifunctional backside utilization.
