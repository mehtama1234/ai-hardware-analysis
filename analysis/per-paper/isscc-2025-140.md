# ISSCC-2025-140: EMI-Compliant Automotive Touch AFE

**Problem:**  
Automotive touch-screen sensors must comply with CISPR 25 EMI standards while maintaining high SNR and frame rate, but typical driving signals (200-500kHz) radiate harmonics violating limits.

**Solution:**  
Pipelined dual-frequency modulation combined with sine2 waveform shaping in the AFE; achieves CISPR 25 Class 5 compliance without FFT overhead.

**Key Results:**  
- 10.5 mW total AFE power
- CISPR 25 Class 5 compliance
- Maintained SNR and frame rate

**Key Innovation:**  
Sine2 waveform shaping with pipelined dual-frequency modulation eliminates FFT overhead while achieving harmonic attenuation required for EMI compliance.
