# AI Analog Circuit Design Agents: On Knowledge Extraction and Transfer with Knowledge Graphs

**Authors:** Karthik Somayaji N. S, Peng Li

**Venue:** ICCAD

## Summary
ADO-KT enables topology-agnostic knowledge transfer for analog circuit design using LLMs and knowledge graphs, allowing design insights from one circuit topology to be applied to others.

## Key Contributions
- LLM-enabled knowledge graph generation for analog design knowledge extraction
- Quality refinement process for knowledge graph optimization
- Topology-agnostic knowledge transfer enabling design reuse across circuit types
- Significant improvements in design quality and data efficiency
