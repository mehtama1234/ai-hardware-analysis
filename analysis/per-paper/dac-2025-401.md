# dac-2025-401: Mitigating Routability Problems in Complementary-FET-based VLSI Designs

**Problem:** CFET stacking reduces cell height but creates routability challenges: limited pin accessibility and BEOL routing congestion at sub-5nm scales.

**Method:** The framework uses pin-extended cells (standard cells with connection points extended further outward) to improve accessibility to routing pins despite the compressed vertical space in CFET designs. It also strategically places blockages in the circuit layout, preventing circuits from being packed into certain areas to reduce the crowding of routing wires in the limited back-end-of-line resources.

**Key Novelty:** Pin-extended cells and placement blockages specifically for CFET routability.

**Contributions:**
- Pin-extended cell library
- Placement blockage strategy
- End-to-end CFET design framework
- DRV elimination with maintained area

**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
