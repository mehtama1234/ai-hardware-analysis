# dac-2025-391: Truly Pre-Routing Timing Prediction via Considering Power Delivery Network

**Problem:** Pre-routing timing prediction overlooks power delivery network (PDN) effects (IR drop, routing congestion), reducing practical utility in real-world design flows.

**Method:** To predict timing before routing while accounting for power delivery effects, the approach creates two AI models: one learning how voltage drops in the power network (IR drop) affect timing, and another learning how routing constraints affect timing. These models process three data sources: the circuit's logical connections (netlist), its physical layout, and its power delivery network structure. Pareto optimization combines them by fairly balancing their contributions so none dominates the prediction. This is tested on TSMC 16nm circuits.

**Key Novelty:** Pareto optimization-based multimodal fusion capturing PDN effects in pre-routing timing.

**Contributions:**
- IR drop and routing congestion-aware encoders
- Pareto optimization for balanced multimodal fusion
- Large-scale evaluation at TSMC 16nm
- SOTA performance improvements
