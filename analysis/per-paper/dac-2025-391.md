# dac-2025-391: Truly Pre-Routing Timing Prediction via Considering Power Delivery Network

**Problem:** Pre-routing timing prediction overlooks power delivery network (PDN) effects (IR drop, routing congestion), reducing practical utility in real-world design flows.

**Method:** Two specialized encoders (IR drop-aware, congestion-aware) fuse multimodal data (netlist, layout, PDN); Pareto optimization balances utilization across modalities.

**Key Novelty:** Pareto optimization-based multimodal fusion capturing PDN effects in pre-routing timing.

**Contributions:**
- IR drop and routing congestion-aware encoders
- Pareto optimization for balanced multimodal fusion
- Large-scale evaluation at TSMC 16nm
- SOTA performance improvements
