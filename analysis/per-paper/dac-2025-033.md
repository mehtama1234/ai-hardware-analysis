# RUPlace: Unified Placement and Routing Optimization

**Problem:** Conventional placement algorithms decouple routing, creating suboptimal designs where routing congestion becomes a bottleneck in advanced VLSI.

**Key Idea:** Use ADMM to alternately optimize global routing and incremental placement, grounding the formulation in bilevel optimization and Wasserstein distance metrics.

**Approach:** Convex programming model for cell inflation determines optimal placement; unified objective minimizes both congestion and wirelength through coordinated routing-placement iteration.

**Results:** Superior congestion reduction vs. OpenROAD, Xplace 2.0, and DREAMPlace 4.1 with competitive wirelength and runtime.

**Theme:** Theoretically-grounded unified placement-routing co-design for congestion and wirelength.
