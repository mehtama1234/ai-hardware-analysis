# isscc-2025-068

**Title:** A 70dB SNDR 80MHz BW Filter-Embedded Pipeline-SAR ADC Achieving 172dB FoMs with Progressive Conversion and Floating-Charge-Transfer Amplifier

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Problem:** Nyquist ADCs require sharp anti-aliasing filters (bulky, noisy); ΔΣ modulators hit bandwidth wall; filter-embedded SAR ADCs limited to tens of MHz bandwidth.

**Key Contribution:** Progressive conversion + floating-charge-transfer (FCT) amplifier enables 80 MHz bandwidth (4–5x prior filter-SAR designs) with high efficiency.

**Method:** This design embeds anti-aliasing filtering (the sharp frequency cutoff needed before digitization) directly into the analog-to-digital converter's core circuit, eliminating a separate bulky filter. A staged conversion approach overlaps multiple conversion steps so the filtering operations don't slow things down. Between these overlapped stages, a floating-charge-transfer amplifier efficiently amplifies and reshapes the signal at high speed without needing extra buffering circuitry. The design is fabricated in 28nm CMOS.

**Hardware:** ASIC (28nm)

**Metrics:** 70.1 dB SNDR, 80 MHz BW, 4.9 mW, Schreier FoM 172.2 dB

**Techniques:** Circuit design, quantization, filter design

**Primary Theme:** Filter-embedded pipeline-SAR for wideband RF
