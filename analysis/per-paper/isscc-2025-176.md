# isscc-2025-176: Neural-Network-Driven Crystal-Less FM Transmitter

**Title:** A Crystal-Less Frequency-Modulation Transmitter IC with Joint Neural-Network-Driven Modulation and Coding for Low-Power Connectivity

**Problem:** IoT transmitters require low power but suffer from PLL complexity/crystal cost or unreliable crystal-less alternatives.

**Approach:** Crystal-less FM transmitter using free-running LC oscillator with neural-network co-optimization of modulation and coding to compensate for drift/phase noise.

**Key Result:** Eliminated crystal overhead, maintained link reliability through intelligent waveform adaptation.

**Hardware:** ASIC (IoT transmitter).

**Tags:** iot, transmitter, crystal-less, neural-network, fm-modulation, low-power
