# Half-Height Double-Row CFET Standard Cells for Area Optimized Placement in A7 CMOS Node

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-097

## Problem
Double-Row CFET architecture enables cell track height scaling but introduces limitations on standard cell layout design and block-level area optimization.

## Motivation
Achieve significant area scaling in advanced CFET technology nodes through optimized standard cell design and placement strategies.

## Method
Half-Height Double-Row CFET (stacked transistors in compact rows) lets designers freely adjust standard cell layouts and then optimize cell placement during the final routing stage of chip design. This two-stage approach—flexible cell design followed by block-level optimization—achieves better space efficiency than fixed designs. The technique stays within realistic manufacturing limits for the metal interconnection layers.

## Key Novelty
First half-height stacked-transistor design enabling area optimization at both cell and block levels of chip design.

## Contributions
- 50% area reduction on standard cell layouts
- 10% block-level area scaling on ARM Cortex-M0 32-bit core at 90% utilization
- 14% projected block-level scaling with enriched standard cell library
- High flexibility in standard cell layout design
