# dac-2025-284: CIMFlow—Framework for Digital CIM Design and Evaluation

**Problem:** Digital CIM architectures lack comprehensive design and evaluation frameworks; capacity constraints make CIM adoption and optimization difficult.

**Motivation:** CIM addresses memory wall for energy-efficient DNN acceleration; unified framework reduces design complexity.

**Method:** CIMFlow bridges compilation and simulation with flexible ISA design. Advanced partitioning and parallelism strategies address CIM capacity constraints during compilation.

**Key Novelty:** Unified framework with partitioning strategies specifically addressing digital CIM capacity constraints.

**Contributions:**
- End-to-end CIMFlow workflow for DNN implementation
- Flexible ISA design connecting compilation and simulation
- Advanced partitioning for CIM capacity handling

**Confidence:** low (abstract-only; framework paper)
