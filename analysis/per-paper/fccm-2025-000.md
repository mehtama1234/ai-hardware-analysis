# FPGA-Based Approximate Multiplier for FP8

**Problem:** FP8 multiplication in neural networks incurs significant power consumption despite FP8's superior dynamic range compared to INT8. Existing FPGA-based 8-bit multiplier designs have high resource utilization.

**Method:** The authors conduct bit-level analysis of FP8 approximation methods. They implement a fine-grained optimized design on mainstream FPGAs (AMD and Altera) using primitives and templates combined with physical layout constraints. The design is evaluated for accuracy and resource utilization, then integrated into neural network inference to demonstrate power efficiency.

**Novelty:** First FPGA-based FP8 approximate multiplier design establishing a benchmark for low-precision floating-point approximate multipliers.
