# isscc-2025-013: 12.7Gb/s LPDDR5X with Advanced Transceiver

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)

## Problem
LPDDR5X speeds approach limits; demand for >10.7Gb/s/pin (e.g., LPCAMM2 for servers) requires new transceiver techniques. Lossy channels create severe timing margin challenges.

## Approach
16Gb LPDDR5X test chip with:
1. **4-phase self-calibration:** reduces clock phase skew below prior methods
2. **AC-coupled transceiver equalization:** three schemes enhance timing margins
3. Validated at 1.05V in 5th-gen 10nm DRAM process

## Key Contribution
4-phase calibration + AC-coupled equalization enables 12.7Gb/s/pin operation—beyond LPDDR5X baseline.

## Performance
- Density: 16 Gb
- Speed: 12.7 Gb/s/pin
- Voltage: 1.05 V
- Process: 5th-gen 10nm DRAM
- Improvement: Beyond 10.7 Gb/s/pin baseline
