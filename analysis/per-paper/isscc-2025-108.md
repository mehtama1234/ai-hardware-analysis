# isscc-2025-108: Always-Half-Inductor Hybrid Bidirectional Converter

**Problem:** Conventional bidirectional converters suffer from high inductor DCR losses and expensive HV transistor requirements for 2-cell battery systems.

**Method:** The converter uses a hybrid design that reduces the maximum current in its energy-storage inductor coil to half what conventional designs allow, cutting resistive power losses since they increase dramatically with current. It operates as a voltage booster (charging from USB) in one direction and as a voltage reducer (supplying power) in the other, both with reduced stress on the inductor. An adaptive system continuously measures power demand and adjusts the converter's operating point to maintain efficiency as conditions change.

**Key Novelty:** Always-half-inductor-current topology with adaptive optimization achieving 97.4% peak efficiency and reduced transistor stress.

**Main Results:** 97.4% peak efficiency, 6-8.4V battery voltage support, reduced inductor/transistor stress.
