# isscc-2025-108: Always-Half-Inductor Hybrid Bidirectional Converter

**Problem:** Conventional bidirectional converters suffer from high inductor DCR losses and expensive HV transistor requirements for 2-cell battery systems.

**Method:** Hybrid topology reducing peak inductor current by 50%; adaptive target current tracking optimizes operation; boost in forward mode, buck in OTG mode.

**Key Novelty:** Always-half-inductor-current topology with adaptive optimization achieving 97.4% peak efficiency and reduced transistor stress.

**Main Results:** 97.4% peak efficiency, 6-8.4V battery voltage support, reduced inductor/transistor stress.

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only analysis)
