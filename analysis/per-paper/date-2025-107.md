# CAS-PUF: Current-Mode Array-Type Strong PUF for Secure Computing in Area Constrained SoCs

**Venue**: DATE  
**Primary Theme**: Area-efficient strong PUF for IoT  

## Problem
Strong PUFs are essential for secure SoCs but traditional designs consume excessive area, preventing deployment in area-constrained IoT and mobile applications requiring hardware-based cryptographic key generation and device authentication.

## Method
Proposes Current-mode Array-type Strong PUF (CAS-PUF) based on a current source topology using only six minimum-size transistors; architecture is tolerant to power supply variation and enables efficient Challenge-Response Pair generation.

## Key Novelty
Minimal-transistor current-mode strong PUF achieving high CRP capacity with negligible area overhead.

## Contributions
- Six-transistor current-source PUF design reducing area by 20-72% versus prior strong PUFs
- Power supply variation tolerance with 96.45% reliability at ±10% fluctuation
- 19-53 orders of magnitude higher CRP capacity for equivalent area
- 97.69% reliability under temperature variation (0–80°C) with 50.01% uniqueness and 49.54% uniformity
