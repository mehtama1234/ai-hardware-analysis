# ISSCC-2025-139: Heterogeneously Integrated Active Probe for High-Speed Debugging

**Problem:**  
Debugging densely integrated microsystems requires probing at tight pitches with minimal signal distortion, but traditional 50Ω test equipment introduces excessive parasitic loading at microsystem interfaces.

**Solution:**  
Micromachined probe substrate heterogeneously integrated with high-performance RF sense amplifier; high-Z design minimizes parasitics while enabling direct signal acquisition from DC to 50GHz.

**Key Results:**  
- DC-to-50GHz measurement capability
- Non-disruptive probing at microsystem pitch
- Integrated on-probe RF sense amp eliminates external test equipment

**Key Innovation:**  
Heterogeneous integration of micromachined probe with RF sense amp enables high-Z non-disruptive probing, eliminating 50Ω test equipment bottleneck.
