# isscc-2025-038: Hybrid CNN-Transformer Accelerator

**Title:** A 28nm 0.22μJ/Token Memory-Compute-Intensity-Aware CNN-Transformer Accelerator with Hybrid-Attention-Based Layer-Fusion and Cascaded Pruning for Semantic-Segmentation

**Venue:** ISSCC

## Core Contribution
28nm hybrid CNN-Transformer accelerator achieving 0.22μJ/token for semantic segmentation via hybrid-attention layer-fusion and cascaded pruning.

## Key Metrics
- **Energy:** 0.22μJ/token
- **Technology:** 28nm
- **Token Length:** >16K TL

## Problem
ConvFormers suffer from (1) on-chip buffering limits at high sparsity, (2) infeasible K/V/weight simultaneous buffering in layer-fusion, (3) low sparsity in segmentation head.

## Method
Memory-compute-intensity-aware dataflow with hybrid-attention layer-fusion reducing K/V EMA redundancy, cascaded pruning extending to segmentation head.

## Confidence
Low (abstract-only analysis).
