# Matrix Is All You Need: Rearchitecting Quantum Chemistry to Scale on AI Accelerators

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-130
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Quantum chemistry has irregular computation patterns, fragmented hardware utilization, and limited reach on modern AI accelerators (GPUs).

## Motivation
Scientific computing mismatch with AI accelerator execution model (low-precision matrix ops); quantum chemistry needs GPU scaling.

## Method
Mako rearchitects quantum chemistry as matrix-aligned kernels with three components: KernelMako (ERI→MatMul via CUTLASS), QuantMako (physics-aware quantization), CompilerMako (kernel fusion/specialization).

## Key Novelty
Restructuring irregular scientific workload to align with AI accelerator matrix-op paradigm

## Contributions
1. 20x speedup on high-angular-momentum basis sets
2. 90% single-node efficiency, 70% across 64 GPUs
3. ubiquitin (1231 atoms) from days to 58 minutes
