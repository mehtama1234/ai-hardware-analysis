# Mixed-Signal Photonic Tensor Core with Photonic SRAM

## Problem
Traditional electrical von Neumann architectures suffer from memory-compute data movement bottlenecks and rising bitline/wordline capacitance at advanced nodes, limiting throughput and efficiency for AI/ML workloads.

## Method
Differential photonic SRAM (pSRAM) bitcell augments a scalable mixed-signal photonic tensor core for matrix multiplication using monolithic integrated photonic components. A novel 1-hot encoding electro-optic ADC (eoADC) converts multiplication outputs to digital bitstreams for electrical domain processing, eliminating memory-compute data movement.

## Key Results
- 4.10 TOPS computation speed
- 3.02 TOPS/W energy efficiency
- Fabrication-friendly integrated photonic components on GlobalFoundries 45SPCLO
