# VLSID 2025-040: Energy-Efficient 3:2 Compressor for Multipliers

## Summary
Novel two-stage stacking 3:2 compressor design achieving 47.30% power-delay product improvement. Multiplier improvements: 20.16% (4-bit), 35.84% (8-bit), 18.41% (16-bit).

## Problem
Multiplier efficiency depends on partial product addition. Existing 3:2 compressors derived from 4:2 designs are suboptimal for the multiplier context where input ratios differ.

## Method
- Two-stage stacking concept for compressor
- Minimized circuit size
- Optimized for 3:2 compression (3 inputs, 2 outputs)
- Applied to 4-bit, 8-bit, 16-bit multipliers
- Power-delay product metric

## Key Results
- **47.30% average PDP improvement** over existing 3:2 compressors
- **4-bit multiplier: 20.16% improvement**
- **8-bit multiplier: 35.84% improvement**
- **16-bit multiplier: 18.41% improvement**

## Applications
- General-purpose processors
- DSP accelerators
- Mobile processors
- Microcontrollers
- AI/ML accelerators

## Limitations
- Limited to 4-32 bit multipliers (larger multipliers not addressed)
- Design-specific results; applicability to other contexts unclear
- Simulation results only; no silicon implementation
- Technology node not specified
- No absolute power/delay metrics, only comparisons

## Hardware Targets
- General-purpose processors
- DSP and audio processors
- Mobile/embedded systems

## Analysis Notes
**Confidence: LOW** (abstract only)

Solid low-level optimization that could have widespread impact given multipliers are ubiquitous in processors. The 47% PDP improvement on the compressor itself is substantial. However, the multiplier-level improvements (18-36%) are more modest, suggesting the compressor is one component among many. The focus on smaller multipliers (≤32 bits) limits applicability to modern wide datapaths.
