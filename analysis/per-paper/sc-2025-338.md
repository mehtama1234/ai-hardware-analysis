# Reproducibility Report: Story of Two GPUs (H100 vs A100 Resilience)

**ID:** sc-2025-338  
**Venue:** SC 2025  
**Author:** Philippe Swartvagher (SC25 Reproducibility Committee)

## Purpose

Artifact evaluation and reproducibility assessment of SC25 paper **"Story of Two GPUs: Characterizing the Resilience of Hopper H100 and Ampere A100 GPUs"** by Cui et al.

## Scope

This is a **meta-paper** documenting:
- Artifact setup and validation
- Reproducibility of experimental results
- Evaluation methodology used by SC25 Reproducibility Initiative

## Target GPUs

- **NVIDIA H100 (Hopper)** — Latest generation
- **NVIDIA A100 (Ampere)** — Prior generation

## Contribution

Provides independent verification of GPU resilience characterization results, supporting confidence in the original findings.

## Implications

Reproducibility evaluation strengthens the credibility of GPU fault/resilience analysis, which is critical for designing robust HPC systems.
