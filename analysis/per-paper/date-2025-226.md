# LoLiPoP-IoT: Design and Simulation of Energy-Efficient IoT Devices

**Venue:** DATE  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Core Idea
Design sustainable IoT localization tag by modeling solar energy harvesting and electronics power consumption together, optimizing for autonomy and reducing electronic waste.

## Problem
IoT devices demand frequent battery replacement, creating maintenance burden and waste; energy autonomy is desirable but requires integrated simulation.

## Method
The researchers designed an IoT tag (a small device for locating wireless objects) that powers itself from harvested solar energy. They created a simulation combining models of energy generation from the solar panel and energy consumption from the electronics, including the nRF52833 microcontroller and DW3110 ultra-wideband transceiver (a radio module that determines location through wireless signals). They used publicly available simulation tools to optimize the design by testing tradeoffs: larger solar panels provide more power and longer device lifespan but require more physical area, while faster localization uses more energy.

## Key Results
- **Reduced photovoltaic panel area** for both 5-year lifespan and fully autonomous tags
- Demonstrated integrated energy harvesting + electronics platform simulation
- Open-tool framework for IoT energy optimization
