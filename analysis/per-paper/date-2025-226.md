# LoLiPoP-IoT: Design and Simulation of Energy-Efficient IoT Devices

**Venue:** DATE  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Core Idea
Design sustainable IoT localization tag by modeling solar energy harvesting and electronics power consumption together, optimizing for autonomy and reducing electronic waste.

## Problem
IoT devices demand frequent battery replacement, creating maintenance burden and waste; energy autonomy is desirable but requires integrated simulation.

## Method
- **Solar cell modeling:** From basic physics to circuit integration
- **Electronics simulation:** nRF52833 MCU and DW3110 UWB transceiver power analysis
- **Co-simulation:** Firmware and hardware using publicly available tools
- **Optimization:** Panel area vs lifespan vs localization latency tradeoffs

## Key Results
- **Reduced photovoltaic panel area** for both 5-year lifespan and fully autonomous tags
- Demonstrated integrated energy harvesting + electronics platform simulation
- Open-tool framework for IoT energy optimization
