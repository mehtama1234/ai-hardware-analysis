# isscc-2025-062

**Title:** 24.6 A Power- and Area-Efficient 4nm Self-Calibrated 12b/16GS/s Hierarchical Time-Interleaving ADC

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Problem:** Multi-channel RF systems need low-power, compact ADCs; thermal/area constraints demand extreme per-channel efficiency.

**Key Contribution:** Hierarchical time-interleaved architecture with integrated self-calibration achieves high SNDR (70+ dB) and suppressed spurs in 0.43 mm².

**Method:** Multiple sampling circuits take turns at staggered times (hierarchical time-interleaving), sharing the sampling workload across stages to achieve high-speed sampling with simpler individual circuits. Despite being designed identically, manufacturing imperfections make each circuit slightly different, causing mismatched measurement results. The chip automatically corrects these mismatches using progressive digital calibration — measuring and adjusting for errors stage-by-stage without any external manual tuning. Everything runs on-chip using 4nm FinFET transistors, eliminating external components and saving space and power.

**Hardware:** ASIC

**Metrics:** 12b/16GS/s, Schreier FoM 148 dB, area 0.43 mm²

**Techniques:** Circuit design, quantization

**Primary Theme:** Hierarchical time-interleaved ADC for wireless multi-channel RF
