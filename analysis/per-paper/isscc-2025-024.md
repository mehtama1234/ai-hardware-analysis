# isscc-2025-024: Single-Core Series-Resonance Oscillator with Ultra-Low Phase Noise

**An 8.1-to-9.9GHz Single-Core Pseudo-Series-Resonance Oscillator Achieving -128.7dBc/Hz PN at 1MHz**

## Summary
Single-core pseudo-series-resonance (PSR) oscillator achieving -128.7 dBc/Hz phase noise at 1 MHz offset, providing series-resonance benefits without multi-core mismatch or inverter-chain power overhead.

## Technical Approach
- **PSR topology**: Novel cross-coupled core providing current-controlled negative resistance in CMOS
- **Phase noise**: Series-resonance single-core operation without multi-core coupling
- **Power**: Minimal consumption vs. conventional multi-core and inverter-chain designs
- **Tuning**: 8.1–9.9 GHz range

## Key Innovation
First practical single-core CMOS series-resonance oscillator achieving -128.7 dBc/Hz phase noise, eliminating multi-core area/mismatch penalties and inverter-chain power overhead.

## Contributions
