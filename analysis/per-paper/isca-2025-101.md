# DREAM: Enabling Low-Overhead Rowhammer Mitigation via Directed Refresh Management
**Venue:** ISCA | **Confidence:** low (abstract only)

## Problem
JEDEC's Directed Refresh Management (DRFM) commands stall 8–32 DRAM banks simultaneously during Rowhammer mitigation, causing severe performance overheads (up to 49% slowdown) compared to the per-bank NRR assumed by prior work. Existing counter-based trackers that avoid these stalls require impractically large storage.

## Motivation
Rowhammer thresholds continue to fall (approaching 500 activations), making efficient yet JEDEC-standards-compliant mitigations critical for real products.

## Method
DREAM proposes two DRFM-aware variants. DREAM-R (randomized) delays issuing a DRFM after sampling an aggressor row so that other banks can also sample rows within the same window, amortizing the multi-bank stall across more mitigations per DRFM command. DREAM-C (counter-based) groups 32–256 randomly chosen rows across 32 banks into a "gang" that shares a single counter, reducing tracker storage by 8x versus Graphene while avoiding CAM lookups and preserving DRFM compatibility.

## Key Novelty
Exploiting DRFM's inherent multi-bank parallelism to either batch-amortize stalls (DREAM-R) or dramatically compress tracker storage via gang counting (DREAM-C), without requiring non-standard DRAM commands.

## Contributions
- DREAM-R: delayed DRFM issuance that reduces PARA overhead from 12.7% to 4.24% and MINT overhead from 15.9% to 2.1% under DRFMsb.
- DREAM-C: gang-based counter tracker achieving only 1 KB/bank storage at threshold 500, 8x lower than Graphene, with negligible slowdown.
- Security analysis bounding the safe delay window for DREAM-R to preserve correctness at a given Rowhammer threshold.
- Favorable comparison to PRAC demonstrating DREAM as a practical JEDEC-compliant alternative.

## Metrics
- DREAM-R reduces PARA slowdown from 12.7% to 4.24% (DRFMsb); DREAM-C reduces storage to 1 KB/bank (8x vs Graphene) with negligible slowdown at threshold 500
- Baseline: PARA with NRR 3.9%; DRFMab 49%

## Baselines
PARA (NRR/DRFMsb/DRFMab), MINT, Graphene, PRAC

## Limitations
Security guarantee of DREAM-R depends on bounding the delay interval, which may tighten constraints at very low future Rowhammer thresholds.
