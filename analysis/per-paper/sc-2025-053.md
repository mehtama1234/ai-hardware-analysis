# Characterizing the Performance of Parallel Data-Compression Algorithms across Compilers and GPUs

**ID:** sc-2025-053  
**Venue:** SC  
**Confidence:** high

## Problem
Compiler choice and GPU platform significantly affect data compression performance; unclear which combinations are optimal

## Motivation
Data compression critical for HPC; different compilers produce different performance characteristics requiring systematic study

## Method
The researchers tested 107,632 different compression algorithms (methods for shrinking data size) across three compilers (NVCC, Clang, HIPCC) and two GPU platforms (NVIDIA, AMD). For each algorithm-compiler-platform combination, they measured how fast the compression ran. By gathering this data at scale, they could identify which compiler-and-platform pairings produced the fastest compression for different algorithms.

## Key Novelty
The key finding is that compiler and GPU platform interact: the best-performing compiler depends on your GPU, and the best GPU depends on your compiler choice.

## Contributions
1. Evaluates 107,632 compression algorithm variants
2. Identifies compiler-specific performance patterns (Clang slow compression, fast decompression)
3. Provides guidance for compiler selection
