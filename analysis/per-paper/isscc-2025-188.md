# isscc-2025-188: Multimode Multiband RF Transceiver for LTE Cat1bis IoT

**Problem**: Cellular IoT (LTE Cat1bis) applications need cost-competitive transceivers with minimal die size and BoM; traditional designs require large area and external filtering for multi-band support.

**Solution**: 28nm CMOS transceiver with harmonic-rejection TX and spur-avoidance RX enabling multimode/multiband support with reduced external components and integrated filtering.

**Key Results**:
- Harmonic-rejection TX (reduced external filters)
- Spur-avoidance RX (simpler integrated filtering)
- Multiband support in single die
- Reduced BoM and cost

**Hardware**: ASIC  
**Application**: LTE Cat1bis cellular IoT  
**Confidence**: low (abstract only)
