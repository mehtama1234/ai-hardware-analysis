# Invited Paper: CMOS 2.0 - Redefining the Future of Scaling

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-008

## Problem
Traditional geometric scaling faces fundamental limits; geometric approach of scaling continues to be the only path with new paradigm needed.

## Motivation
Transitioning from geometric to heterogeneous 3D scaling enables continued power-performance-area gains beyond traditional roadmap scaling.

## Method
Instead of just shrinking transistors (which hits physical limits), CMOS 2.0 stacks multiple specialized processing layers vertically by bonding wafers together and processing from the back side. This requires new chip designs and new design software tools (called EDA—electronic design automation) to handle these 3D structures.

## Key Novelty
The core shift is from making transistors smaller to stacking specialized processing layers vertically in 3D as the new path for scaling.

## Contributions
1. CMOS 2.0 platform concept using 3D bonding and backside processing
2. Architecture design methods for optimal mapping to heterogeneous 3D technology
3. EDA infrastructure for supporting CMOS 2.0 design flows
4. Reliability analysis and mitigation strategies for 3D heterogeneous stacks
