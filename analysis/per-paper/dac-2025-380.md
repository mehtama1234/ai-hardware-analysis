# High Energy-efficiency and Low latency In-Memory Computing using Analog Accumulator and In-Memory ADC with shared References

**Venue:** DAC
**ID:** dac-2025-380

## Problem
In-memory computing array ADCs require high area overhead for data conversion; traditional approaches sacrifice linearity and energy efficiency.

## Motivation
In-memory analog-to-digital conversion consumes large area; efficient shared-reference designs can drastically reduce overhead while maintaining signal integrity.

## Method
A 256x128 in-memory array uses dual-8T SRAM bitcells for ternary weight storage and read-write decoupling. Bit-slicing with charge-sharing binary weighted accumulator (CHA) handles multi-bit input. Read WWL Under Drive enables low-current variation.

## Key Novelty
Reconfigurable in-memory ADC with shared references achieving 9x area overhead reduction and 1146 TOPS/W through efficient accumulation.

## Contributions
1. Reconfigurable in-memory ADC with shared references reducing area overhead by 9x (3% vs traditional)
2. Charge-sharing binary weighted accumulator (CHA) for low-latency multi-bit handling
3. Bit-slicing approach improving throughput by 1.9x and linearity by 23x vs pulse-width modulation
4. 1146 TOPS/W and 27 TOPS/mm^2 energy and area efficiency with <1% accuracy loss on vision/graph tasks
