# isscc-2025-002: IBM Telum II – Design-Technology Co-Optimization

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Designing 43B-transistor 5nm processors requires balancing power, performance, area, and reliability while managing massive concurrent EDA complexity across libraries, IP blocks, and gate-level logic.

## Approach
**Telum II** (modified Samsung 5nm bulk CMOS) introduces:
- Custom IBM standard cell and IP libraries co-optimized with process node
- **Concurrent hierarchical design:** arrays, regfiles, analog blocks, PHY, and custom logic designed simultaneously rather than sequentially
- Integrated abstraction and modeling layers for design data management

## Key Contribution
Concurrent hierarchical methodology enables simultaneous library-IP-gate development, enabling efficient design of massive-scale processors.

## Performance
- Die size: 600mm² (+13% vs Telum I)
- Transistors: 43B (+38%)
- Wire: 38km (+28%)
- Shape density: 1.3T (+25%)
- 18 BEOL layers
