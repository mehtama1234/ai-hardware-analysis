# EPiCarbon: A Carbon Modeling Tool for Electro-Photonic Accelerators

**Venue:** ICCAD | **ID:** iccad-2025-045

## Problem
Embodied carbon (manufacturing) is not quantifiable for photonic chips, yet constitutes dominant portion of total carbon footprint. Existing models cover CMOS but lack photonic chip embodied carbon estimation.

## Motivation
Photonic computing promises orders of magnitude better energy efficiency than CMOS, but true carbon benefit requires quantifying both operational and embodied carbon.

## Method
Developed first-ever embodied carbon model for photonic chips by analyzing fabrication energy and yield differences from CMOS. Created EPiCarbon tool integrating both operational and embodied carbon analysis for Electro-Photonic accelerators. Tool used to analyze state-of-the-art EPiC accelerators and identify strategies for carbon reduction.

## Key Novelty
First model to estimate embodied carbon of photonic chips, demonstrating 4.1x lower fabrication energy than CMOS.

## Contributions
- Embodied carbon model for photonic chips with fabrication energy and yield analysis
- EPiCarbon open-source tool for carbon footprint evaluation of electro-photonic accelerators
- Case study on ADEPT accelerator identifying carbon-reduction strategies
