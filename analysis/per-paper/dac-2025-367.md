# Ragnar: Exploring Volatile-Channel Vulnerabilities on RDMA NIC

## Summary
Comprehensive hardware-contention-based side-channel attacks on RDMA NICs in data centers via reverse engineering and covert-channel construction.

## Key Results
- **3.2x bandwidth** improvement over SOTA RDMA attacks (CX-5)
- **95.6% accuracy** fingerprinting operations on distributed databases

## Core Innovation
First systematic volatile-channel attack suite on RDMA NICs exploiting hardware contention at multiple granularities.

## Technique
Microbenchmark-driven reverse engineering of RDMA NIC behavior; construction of hardware-contention-based covert channels; fingerprinting via timing/bandwidth side-channels.
