# ISSCC-2025-144: High-Power All-Digital Base-Station Transmitter

**Problem:**  
Base stations dominate wireless operator energy (73%); analog PA-based transmitters suffer high quiescent currents. High-power all-digital transmitters require fine gate segmentation and glitch-free wideband operation.

**Solution:**  
8-phase multi-phase all-digital upconversion with dynamic retiming and glitch-free phase mapping; custom low-VT LDMOS MMIC with fine gate segmentation and high-density flip-chip integration.

**Key Results:**  
- 20W integrated output power (mMIMO-scale)
- 68% peak drain efficiency
- 62% peak system efficiency
- Wideband modulation support

**Key Innovation:**  
First DTX achieving mMIMO power levels with wideband modulation, proving all-digital transmitters are viable for base-station deployment.
