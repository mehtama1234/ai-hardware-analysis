# QUARK: Quantization-Enabled Circuit Sharing for Transformer Acceleration by Exploiting Common Patterns in Nonlinear Operations

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**Paper ID:** iccad-2025-266

## Problem Statement
Nonlinear operations in transformers significantly contribute to inference latency and hardware resource overhead in FPGA acceleration.

## Motivation
Common patterns in nonlinear activation functions offer circuit sharing opportunities; quantization enables efficient approximation.

## Method
QUARK exploits common patterns in nonlinear operations via quantization-enabled circuit sharing on FPGA, reducing hardware overhead while maintaining high accuracy through precise approximation design.

## Key Novelty
Quantization-enabled circuit sharing for nonlinear operations in transformers with high-quality approximation.

## Contributions

1. 1.96× end-to-end speedup over GPU
2. >50% nonlinear module overhead reduction
3. Accuracy preservation under ultra-low-bit quantization
