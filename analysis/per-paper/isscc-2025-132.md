# isscc-2025-132: Wideband GaN Power Amplifier with High Back-Off Efficiency

**Problem:** Wireless systems with hundreds of MHz bandwidth and complex modulations (high PAPR) require power amplifiers with large RF bandwidth, high back-off efficiency, good linearity, and compact size; gallium nitride (GaN) technology offers high power density but efficiency degrades significantly at back-off.

**Motivation:** Modern wireless standards (massive MIMO, satellite, 5G+) demand wideband PAs with maintained efficiency across wide dynamic range to maximize throughput and minimize power consumption.

**Method:** The GaN power amplifier implements a series-load conducting Gallium Nitride (SLCG) combined with Doherty-Continuum architecture to achieve high efficiency across wide frequencies (1.35-7.6GHz) and power back-off conditions.

**Key Novelty:** SLCG-Doherty-Continuum architecture enabling >38% efficiency at 6dB back-off over multi-octave frequency range.

**Contributions:**
- GaN power amplifier with series-load conducting architecture
- SLCG-Doherty-Continuum design for improved back-off efficiency
- >38% efficiency at 6dB back-off
- 1.35-7.6GHz multi-octave bandwidth coverage
