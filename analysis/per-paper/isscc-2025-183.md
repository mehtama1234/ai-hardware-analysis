# isscc-2025-183: Low-Power PAM-4 Receiver with Capacitive-Feedback Equalizer

**Problem**: PAM4 multi-level signaling increases throughput but reduces SNR, requiring complex equalization that adds power/area and introduces delay.

**Solution**: Delay-less capacitive-feedback equalizer (CFE) providing low-power PAM4 reception in 28nm CMOS without pipeline latency insertion.

**Key Results**:
- 0.3pJ/b energy efficiency at 32Gb/s
- 0.00288mm² active area
- Zero-latency equalization
- Simplified circuit vs DFE/FFE

**Hardware**: ASIC  
**Application**: Memory/IO interface  
**Confidence**: low (abstract only)
