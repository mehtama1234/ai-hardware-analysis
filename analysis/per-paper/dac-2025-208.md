# SNAPPIX: Efficient-Coding-Inspired In-Sensor Compression for Edge Vision

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only analysis)

## Problem
Edge vision sensors face severe energy bottleneck transmitting high-volume pixel data; conventional compression is task-agnostic and inefficient.

## Method
**Coded exposure (CE)** in-sensor compression learns task-agnostic sampling patterns via **efficient coding theory**, co-designs **downstream models** for CE pixel non-uniformity, and implements **lightweight hardware augmentations**.

## Key Novelty
Task-agnostic in-sensor compression with efficient coding and co-designed vision models reduces transmission energy dramatically.

## Results
- **15.4× energy reduction** vs state-of-the-art video methods
- Improved action recognition and video reconstruction
