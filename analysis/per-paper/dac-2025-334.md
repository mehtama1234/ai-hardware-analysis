# Late Breaking Results: Opera: An Open and Efficient Platform for Data-driven Synthesis of Analog Circuits
**Venue:** DAC

## Problem
Analog circuit synthesis is data-driven but bottlenecked by expensive circuit simulators in the synthesis loop; benchmarking methods requires exclusive access to commercial semiconductor technology.

## Motivation
Fast, open, benchmarkable analog circuit synthesis enables democratization of circuit design and accelerates iteration cycles for chip designers.

## Method
Designing analog circuits requires running expensive circuit simulators (SPICE software) many times to test and optimize designs, which is slow and costly. Opera replaces these expensive simulators with fast neural networks (called surrogate models) that predict how a circuit will behave without the computational cost. These neural network substitutes are integrated into a standard reinforcement learning environment (like OpenAI Gym, a training framework for optimization algorithms), so engineers can use existing tools to automatically search for better circuit designs. The entire platform is built on an open-source 45nm semiconductor process, allowing researchers to reproduce the results without paying for access to proprietary manufacturing technology.

## Key Novelty
Opera is the first open platform that replaces expensive circuit simulators with neural network substitutes and integrates them into standard reinforcement learning environments for analog circuit design.

## Contributions
1. 40x acceleration of data-driven synthesis flow via surrogate modeling
2. Benchmarkable platform using open-source semiconductor technology
3. Integration with standardized optimization frameworks (OpenAI Gym)
