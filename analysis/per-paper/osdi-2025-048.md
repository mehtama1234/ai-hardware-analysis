# Basilisk: Using Provenance Invariants to Automate Proofs of Undecidable Protocols

**Problem:** Verifying correctness of distributed protocols is challenging, especially for undecidable protocols where no complete algorithmic proof procedure exists. Manual proof construction is labor-intensive and error-prone.

**Method:** Basilisk uses provenance invariants—properties tracking data flow origins through protocol states—to guide automated proof generation. The system encodes protocol semantics and invariants into constraint-satisfaction formulations, leveraging automated solvers to generate proofs even for theoretically undecidable protocol classes by discovering structural properties.

**Novelty:** Applying provenance tracking as an automated proof basis for protocols that are theoretically undecidable by conventional methods.
