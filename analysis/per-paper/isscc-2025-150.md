# isscc-2025-150: Multi-Core Isolated DC-DC Converter

**Problem:** Integrated isolated DC-DC converters suffer from severe conducted EMI (150kHz–30MHz) and radiated EMI (30MHz–1GHz) due to PWM control and parasitic transformer capacitance.

**Motivation:** Miniaturized board-constrained systems need low-noise, high-efficiency isolated power without external filters.

**Method:** Multi-core converter with symmetrical inverter and embedded transformer; integrated ~1MHz PWM regulation + tens-of-MHz inverter switching with common-mode suppression.

**Key Novelty:** Monolithic integrated isolated converter design achieving both EMI compliance and efficiency in a single converter.

**Contributions:**
- 2W @ 53.2% peak efficiency
- CISPR-32 Class-B EMI compliance (no external filters)
- Sub-5mV output ripple

**Hardware Target:** ASIC

**Technique Categories:** power, circuit-design, packaging

**Metrics:** 53.2% peak efficiency, <5mV ripple, 2W power

**Baselines:** External EMI filter approaches, conventional PWM-only converters

**Confidence:** low (abstract-only analysis)
