# Physical Insights into the Leakage Mechanisms Governing the Scaling Trends in 4H-SiC Based Junctionless FETs

**VLSID 2025** | vlsid-2025-087

## Abstract
Wide bandgap 4H-SiC enables high-power/high-temp devices but leakage mechanisms differ from silicon. This work provides comprehensive analysis of sub-10nm 4H-SiC planar junctionless FET leakage via 2D calibrated simulation. Unlike silicon (dominated by L-BTBT GIDL), 4H-SiC leakage is controlled by: direct gate tunneling, and inefficient volume depletion. Provides design guidelines for channel length, thickness, oxide thickness enabling effective volume depletion in sub-10nm regime.

## Problem
- 4H-SiC scaling to sub-10nm requires understanding unique leakage physics
- Silicon design guidelines don't apply due to wider bandgap
- Sub-10nm low-leakage designs (IoT, automotive) need physics-based optimization

## Key Insight
4H-SiC wider bandgap makes band-to-band tunneling (dominant in Si) negligible; direct gate tunneling and inefficient volume depletion become limiting factors for sub-10nm leakage performance.

## Approach
1. 2D device simulation with careful calibration
2. Three-component leakage analysis:
   - Gate-induced drain leakage (GIDL) via band-to-band tunneling
   - Direct gate tunneling through oxide
   - Volume depletion efficiency
3. Silicon vs. 4H-SiC comparison
4. Design guideline extraction

## Silicon vs. 4H-SiC Comparison

| Mechanism | Silicon | 4H-SiC |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| **L-BTBT GIDL** | Dominant | Negligible (wide bandgap) |
| **Gate tunneling** | Secondary | Important |
| **Volume depletion** | Effective | Often inefficient |
