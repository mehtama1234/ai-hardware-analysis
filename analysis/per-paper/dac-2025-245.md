# dac-2025-245: MiniWear: Minimizing Flash Wear via Hybrid Persistent Cache for Extended EF-SMR Lifetime

## Summary
Proposes hybrid persistent cache with Flash-PC and SMR-PC tiers plus fine-grained scheduling and proactive balancing to reduce flash wear by up to 66.67% in EF-SMR systems without impacting performance.

## Key Idea
Distribute cache workload across hybrid flash/SMR tiers with adaptive scheduling to minimize flash endurance degradation.

## Contributions
- 66.67% flash wear reduction
- Tiered Flash-PC/SMR-PC organization
- Fine-grained scheduling strategy
- Proactive resource balancing

## Limitations
Throughput/latency trade-offs and scalability not addressed.
