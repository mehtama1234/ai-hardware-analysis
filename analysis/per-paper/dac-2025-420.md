# Blaze: An Efficient Bit-Sparse Attention Architecture With Workload Orchestration

**Problem:** Attention has high computational complexity and power; existing bit-sparsity methods (for CNNs) don't address irregular non-zero bit distribution and workload imbalance in attention tensors.

**Method:** Bit + value-level sparsity with Approximate-Computing-Based (ACB) workload orchestration to balance irregular loads, plus Leading-Booth enhancement and reconfigurable compute engine.

**Key Result:** 2.37-6.18x speedup vs SOTA; 9.69-43.96x energy efficiency vs SOTA; 1.58x vs SOTA bit-sparse accelerators.

**Hardware Target:** ASIC  
**Workloads:** LLM-inference, transformer, attention  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
