# Energy-Aware HPC Scheduling with LLM-Based Power Prediction

**ID:** sc-2025-237
**Venue:** SC
**Confidence:** low

## Problem

HPC energy consumption strains grid; need operational strategies to manage HPC as grid load

## Motivation

Theoretical energy-aware scheduling studied; lack practical implementations; grid integration critical

## Approach

LLM-based per-job power prediction; lightweight scheduling strategy; Slurm integration without core mods

## Key Novelty

Using learned representations from a language model (LLM embeddings), the approach predicts job power with 15% lower error than prior methods, enabling a 4.0 MWh shift of computing load to solar-peak hours without reducing throughput.

## Contributions

- LLM-based power prediction from job scripts
- Energy-aware scheduling without Slurm core modification
- Per-job power MAE reduction (15% vs SOTA)
- Grid-aware load shifting strategy
