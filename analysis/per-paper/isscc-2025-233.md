# isscc-2025-233: Hybrid PM-SAR Capacitance-to-Digital Converter

**Problem:** Capacitive sensors need wide-input-range (>10nF), fast-conversion (<10µs), high-resolution CDCs. SAR/ΔΣM suffer limited range (analog saturation); PM CDCs achieve wide range but incur long conversion times.

**Approach:** PM-SAR-Hybrid CDC combining period modulation (wide range via time-domain) with SAR conversion (fast resolution), eliminating saturation while achieving sub-10µs conversion.

**Key novelty:** Hybrid PM-SAR topology overcoming traditional range-vs-speed trade-off.

**Metrics:** 18.5nF input range, 6.1µs conversion time at 18.1pF, high resolution.

**Tags:** capacitance-converter, wide-range, fast-conversion, hybrid, sensor, energy-efficient
