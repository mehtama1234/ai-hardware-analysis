# Chronbench: An Incremental HDL Benchmark Suite

**Problem:** FPGA CAD tools compile entire designs from scratch, causing slow development iterations and limiting designer productivity. There is a lack of benchmarks encapsulating realistic HDL development histories, preventing research into sophisticated incremental compilation flows.

**Method:** Chronbench captures realistic HDL development as chronological, synthesizable commits in git repositories. The authors provide a tool to convert public repositories into benchmarks and fully characterize them by synthesizing, placing, and routing 170 commits to analyze development patterns, change magnitudes, and reuse potential.

**Novelty:** Encoding realistic HDL development histories as benchmark suites with chronological synthesizable commits in git repositories enables systematic research into incremental compilation.
