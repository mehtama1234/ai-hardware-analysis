# ISSCC-2025-142: Long-Reach PAM-4 200G Ethernet Transceiver

**Problem:**  
100G/lane Ethernet is transitioning to 200G/lane (224Gb/s) for AI data centers, requiring power-efficient, long-reach SerDes transceivers handling signal integrity challenges from cables and connectors.

**Solution:**  
Long-reach PAM-4 SerDes transceiver in 5nm FinFET with optimized analog front-end for signal compensation achieving >46dB channel loss at 212.5Gb/s with 2.2pJ/b analog power.

**Key Results:**  
- 224 Gb/s PAM-4 operation
- >46 dB channel reach
- 2.2 pJ/b analog power efficiency

**Key Innovation:**  
Efficient long-reach PAM-4 design enabling practical 200G/lane Ethernet deployment in dense data center interconnects.
