# DiSPlace - Transistor-level Placement with Diffusion-Sharing Optimization

DiSPlace introduces transistor-level placement optimized for diffusion-sharing, where adjacent transistors can share diffusion regions to reduce area and improve performance. By incorporating diffusion-sharing constraints into the placement algorithm, the tool achieves 18% wirelength reduction and 24% area savings. This circuit-aware approach to physical design demonstrates that transistor-level optimizations can deliver substantial improvements in chip efficiency and density.
