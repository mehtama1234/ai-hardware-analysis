# A Graph-Based Deep Reinforcement Learning Framework for Quantum Circuit Mapping with Look-Ahead Rewards and Biased Exploration

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**Paper ID:** iccad-2025-265

## Problem Statement
Quantum circuit mapping requires inserting SWAP gates to match hardware topology constraints; minimizing additional gates is crucial to reduce circuit error rates in NISQ systems.

## Motivation
NISQ hardware has strict two-qubit gate constraints; reducing SWAP overhead directly improves circuit fidelity and success rates.

## Method
LARBE uses graph neural networks to encode quantum circuits and applies deep reinforcement learning with look-ahead rewards for trading immediate vs future benefits and biased exploration incorporating heuristic rules.

## Key Novelty
Graph-based DRL framework with look-ahead rewards and biased exploration for quantum circuit mapping.

## Contributions

1. 5.83–17.57% improvement over SABRE
2. Heuristic-guided exploration acceleration
