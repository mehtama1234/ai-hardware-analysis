# A Graph-Based Deep Reinforcement Learning Framework for Quantum Circuit Mapping with Look-Ahead Rewards and Biased Exploration

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**Paper ID:** iccad-2025-265

## Problem Statement
Quantum circuit mapping requires inserting SWAP gates to match hardware topology constraints; minimizing additional gates is crucial to reduce circuit error rates in NISQ systems.

## Motivation
NISQ hardware has strict two-qubit gate constraints; reducing SWAP overhead directly improves circuit fidelity and success rates.

## Method
The system uses graph neural networks to encode the quantum circuit structure as an interconnected network, then applies deep reinforcement learning—an AI technique that learns through trial and error with rewards—to decide where to insert SWAP gates. Rather than optimizing just the immediate step, it uses look-ahead rewards to account for how each decision affects future steps. It also uses biased exploration, which means it guides its search using practical rules about what tends to work well in quantum computing, rather than trying purely random options.

## Key Novelty
It applies graph-based deep reinforcement learning that combines forward planning (look-ahead rewards) and guided search (using domain expertise) to solve quantum circuit mapping by finding optimal SWAP gate placements.
