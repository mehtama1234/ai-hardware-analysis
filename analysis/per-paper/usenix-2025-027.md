# Fast ACS: Low-Latency File-Based Ordered Message Delivery at Scale

**Problem:** Delivering messages with ordering and at-least-once delivery guarantees across clusters to thousands of consumers while maintaining low latency and enabling consumer-controlled pacing.

**Method:** Fast ACS uses two-sided communication (RPC) for inter-cluster messaging and one-sided communication (RMA) for intra-cluster delivery, with file-based storage providing ordering guarantees. The hybrid approach selects the appropriate primitive based on message distance to optimize end-to-end latency while maintaining ordering and delivery guarantees.

**Novelty:** Hybrid RPC/RMA communication strategy that optimizes message delivery latency by selecting the appropriate primitive based on inter-cluster versus intra-cluster distance.
