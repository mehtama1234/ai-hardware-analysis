# ISSCC-2025-135: Multiphase Subharmonic Switching RF Transmitter

**Problem:**  
Wide-bandwidth RF transmitters (3.2Gb/s) for K/Ka-band (24GHz) must achieve low EVM and high efficiency at both peak and back-off power levels, but traditional IQ architectures suffer 3dB power loss and prior subharmonic switching designs were limited to tens of MHz bandwidth.

**Solution:**  
Multiphase subharmonic switching (MP-SHS) transmitter with phase-shifted subharmonic LO dividers (suppressing spurs), current-shared phase-interpolator MUX for LO generation, and dual-rate hybrid DAC with noise shaping.

**Key Results:**  
- 22.3% PAE at peak, 20.3% PAE at PBO
- -30.8 dB EVM at 3.2 Gb/s
- 65nm CMOS implementation at 24GHz

**Key Innovation:**  
Phase-shifted subharmonic LO divider eliminates 3dB IQ loss while maintaining subharmonic spur attenuation through frequency-division phase injection.
