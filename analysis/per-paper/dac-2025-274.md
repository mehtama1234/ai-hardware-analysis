# dac-2025-274: RADiT—Redundancy-Aware Diffusion Transformer Acceleration

**Problem:** Diffusion Transformers require iterative sampling with redundant computations across timesteps; high latency and energy.

**Motivation:** DiTs are mainstream for image/video generation; reducing redundancy is critical for real-time inference.

**Method:** Blockwise input feature evaluation identifies redundancy; reuse results across timesteps via Dynamic Threshold Scaling Module (DTSM) and Compress and Compare Unit (CCU).

**Key Novelty:** Timestep-aware redundancy elimination via blockwise similarity and cross-timestep reuse.

**Contributions:**
- 1.8x image, 1.7x video speedup without quality loss
- 41-45.5% energy reduction
- 1.67-1.76x throughput improvement

**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

**Tags:** diffusion-transformer, redundancy-elimination, video-generation, timestep-similarity
