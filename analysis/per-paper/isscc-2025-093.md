# isscc-2025-093: Noise-Immune Analog Distribution LDO

## Summary
2A fully analog distribution LDO with noise-immune control signals for SoC thermal management, overcoming previous limitations of analog DISLDOs in noisy environments.

## Key Contributions
- Noise-immune analog signal distribution
- 2A distributed power delivery
- Even current distribution across MPs
- Lower static current than digital alternatives

## Hardware Target
SoC
