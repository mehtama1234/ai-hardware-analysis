# asplos-2025-118: PowerMove: Optimizing Compilation for Neutral Atom Quantum Computers with Zoned Architecture

**Venue:** ASPLOS  
**Confidence:** low (abstract only)  
**Primary Theme:** joint atom-movement-aware compilation for neutral atom quantum computers

## Problem
Neutral atom quantum computers (NAQCs) with zoned architectures (ZA) and qubit movement capabilities require coordinated gate scheduling, qubit positioning, atom movement, and inter-zone communication, but existing compilers cannot jointly optimize these dimensions, leading to poor fidelity and long execution times for quantum circuits.

## Motivation
NAQCs have emerged as leading platforms for scalable quantum computing due to hardware-native qubit movement and zoned architecture capabilities, but without compilers that exploit these features, the hardware advantages do not translate to practical circuit performance.

## Method
PowerMove is a compiler for NAQCs that jointly optimizes gate scheduling, qubit positioning, atom movement, and inter-zone communication for zoned architectures. The compiler introduces new optimization passes that model atom movement as a first-class resource and explore movement strategies jointly with gate ordering. For NISQ applications, PowerMove reduces atom movements and inter-zone communications while minimizing circuit depth. For FTQC, it replaces physical qubits with logical qubits encoded in quantum error correction (QEC) codes and applies analogous co-optimization, achieving a 4.78x reduction in execution time. The compilation algorithm achieves up to 216.9x compilation speedup over prior methods.

## Key Novelty
Jointly optimizing atom movement strategies alongside gate scheduling and qubit positioning for both NISQ and FTQC settings in neutral atom zoned architectures, achieving orders-of-magnitude fidelity improvement.

## Contributions
- PowerMove compiler jointly optimizing gate scheduling, qubit positioning, atom movement, and inter-zone communication for NAQC zoned architectures.
- Orders-of-magnitude fidelity improvements over state-of-the-art NAQC compilers.
- Up to 3.76x execution time reduction (NISQ) and 216.9x compilation speedup.
- Extension to FTQC with QEC-encoded logical qubits achieving 4.78x execution time reduction; open-sourced.
