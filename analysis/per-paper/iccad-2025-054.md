# ThermoPhoton: Fast 3D Thermal Simulation of Photonic Integrated Circuits via Operator Learning

**Venue:** ICCAD | **ID:** iccad-2025-054

## Problem
Silicon photonic integrated circuits face severe thermal crosstalk at high density. Traditional numerical solvers prohibitively slow for large-scale 3D simulation. Existing ML surrogates lack generalization to distributed heaters and layered PIC structures.

## Motivation
Enable fast, accurate thermal-aware design of next-generation high-density photonic chips.

## Method
Operator-learning neural architecture (ThermoPhoton) tailored for PIC thermal modeling: pseudo-3D source representation leveraging device stratification; zero coordinate shift encoding for physics-informed loss; attention mechanisms capturing sharp thermal gradients and crosstalk effects.

## Key Novelty
Physics-informed operator learning with pseudo-3D representation and ZCS encoding for PIC thermal modeling.

## Contributions
- Pseudo-3D source representation exploiting device stratification
- Zero coordinate shift encoding for improved physics-informed loss
- Attention-based operator learning capturing thermal gradients and crosstalk
