# dac-2025-454: Critical Proof Obligations for IC3 Verification

## Summary
Improves SAT-based IC3 hardware verification by introducing Critical Proof Obligations (CPOs) to prioritize and streamline proof obligation management, reducing verification time.

## Key Results
- Significant performance improvements on HWMCC benchmarks
- Better CPO discovery
- Improved lemma propagation

## Technical Approach
- **Critical Proof Obligations (CPOs)**: Identify high-impact states that need proving unreachable
- **CPO-Driven UNSAT Core Generation**: Extract minimal constraints  
- **CPO-Driven Proof Obligation Propagation**: Accelerate frame refinement

## Contribution
Demonstrates that selective focus on critical proof obligations can substantially improve IC3 performance, offering a practical lever for verification engineers to reduce design sign-off time.
