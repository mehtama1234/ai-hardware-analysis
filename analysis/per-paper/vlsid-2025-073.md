# Design of Manchester Carry Chain Hybrid Adder for MASH 1-1-1 Delta Sigma Modulator for Fractional-N Frequency Synthesizers

**Paper ID:** vlsid-2025-073
**Venue:** VLSID
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
MASH 1-1-1 Delta Sigma Modulators for fractional-N frequency synthesizers require high-speed, low-power adders with minimal worst-case delay.

## Motivation
Fractional-N synthesizers are critical for frequency synthesis; MASH DSM quality depends on adder performance (speed, power, jitter).

## Method
Novel high-speed hybrid adder combining Carry-Look-Ahead and Carry-Select using Domino Logic and Manchester Carry Chain topology. Includes negative-edge triggered low-power partially static flip-flop for sampling.

## Key Novelty
Hybrid CLA-Carry-Select topology optimized for DSM; Manchester Carry Chain in Domino Logic; application-specific flip-flop design.

## Contributions
- Hybrid CLA-Carry-Select adder for MASH DSM
- Manchester Carry Chain topology in Domino Logic
- Low-power partially static flip-flop for output sampling
- 181.9 ps worst-case delay at 100 MHz
- 202.52 µW average power consumption @ 100 MHz
- Designed and verified in Xilinx Vivado (Zedboard) and TSMC 65nm CMOS
