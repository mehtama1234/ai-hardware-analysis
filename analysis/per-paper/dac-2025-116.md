# dac-2025-116: ANGraph – GNN-Based Performance Prediction for Neuromorphic Hardware

## Quick Summary
ANGraph bridges the sim-to-silicon gap for asynchronous neuromorphic hardware using a GNN trained on over 1M gate-level samples. It predicts latency (76% RMSE reduction) and power (MAPE 0.88%) and generalizes across scales and process nodes without retraining.

## Key Insight
Graph neural networks can learn circuit patterns from gate-level simulation and transfer predictions across different neuromorphic designs, dramatically improving design space exploration accuracy.

## Hardware & Technique
- **Target**: ASIC, NPU
- **Techniques**: Scheduling, circuit-design prediction
- **Workloads**: RL (neuromorphic reinforcement learning)

## Results
- Latency: R² +0.69, RMSE -76% vs. simulator
- Power: R² 0.98, MAPE 0.88%
- Generalization: no retraining needed for new scales/nodes
