# ISSCC-2025-143: Voltage-Biased Low-Offset Hall Sensor

**Problem:**  
Traditional current-biased Hall sensors exhibit large offset errors (tens of µT), limiting their use in precision applications like compasses and current sensors.

**Solution:**  
Voltage-biased Hall sensor with novel dual-arrangement readout in standard 0.18µm CMOS; outputs current instead of voltage, enabling offset cancellation through custom frontend design.

**Key Results:**  
- 1.0 µT (3σ) offset
- 60 nT/√Hz noise floor
- 3x offset reduction and 5x noise improvement vs prior art

**Key Innovation:**  
Voltage-biased topology with optimized readout achieves dramatic offset and noise improvements over traditional current-biased approaches.
