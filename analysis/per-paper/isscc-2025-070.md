# isscc-2025-070

**Title:** 20.2 A 67μW/Channel, 0.13nW/Synapse/b Nose-on-a-Chip for Noninvasive Diagnosis of Diseases with On-Chip Incremental Learning

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Problem:** Portable electronic noses for breath-based disease detection struggle with accuracy drift across environments/patients; high power limits portability.

**Key Contribution:** On-chip incremental learning (0.13 nW/synapse/b) enables patient-specific adaptation without external cloud processing.

**Method:** Ultra-low-power E-nose front-end (67 μW/channel), on-chip neural network adaptation, local calibration to environment/patient signatures.

**Hardware:** ASIC, SoC

**Metrics:** 67 μW/channel, 0.13 nW/synapse/b

**Techniques:** Circuit design, power, near-data processing

**Primary Theme:** Portable E-nose with on-chip learning for health diagnosis
