# MetaSapiens: Real-Time Neural Rendering with Efficiency-Aware Pruning and Accelerated Foveated Rendering

**Venue:** ASPLOS 2025  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Point-Based Neural Rendering (PBNR / 3D Gaussian Splatting) is too expensive for real-time execution on mobile devices due to the large number of Gaussian primitives and full-resolution rendering requirements.

## Motivation
AR/VR and digital twin applications demand real-time photorealistic rendering on mobile hardware; current PBNR methods exceed mobile power and latency budgets.

## Method
MetaSapiens makes real-time neural rendering possible on phones by combining two techniques. First, it removes Gaussian primitives (small 3D shapes that build the image) that don't meaningfully matter, deciding which to keep based on visual importance and computational cost on the specific phone hardware. Second, it renders the center of your view in full detail and the edges in lower resolution or with fewer primitives, exploiting the fact that human peripheral vision can't perceive fine details, with both optimized together on mobile hardware.

## Key Novelty
The key innovation is combining efficient removal of 3D primitives with rendering that shows the center of vision in full detail and edges in lower quality, both working together on mobile devices.

## Contributions
- Hardware-cost-aware Gaussian pruning criterion for PBNR
- Accelerated foveated rendering reducing peripheral computation
- Real-time PBNR on mobile through combined pruning + foveation
