# MetaSapiens: Real-Time Neural Rendering with Efficiency-Aware Pruning and Accelerated Foveated Rendering

**Venue:** ASPLOS 2025  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Point-Based Neural Rendering (PBNR / 3D Gaussian Splatting) is too expensive for real-time execution on mobile devices due to the large number of Gaussian primitives and full-resolution rendering requirements.

## Motivation
AR/VR and digital twin applications demand real-time photorealistic rendering on mobile hardware; current PBNR methods exceed mobile power and latency budgets.

## Method
Two co-designed techniques: (1) efficiency-aware Gaussian pruning reduces the primitive count using a criterion balancing visual importance and hardware computational cost on the target platform; (2) accelerated foveated rendering exploits peripheral visual acuity reduction to render the foveal region at full quality and periphery at lower resolution/fewer Gaussians, cutting total compute. Both are co-designed with the mobile hardware execution model.

## Key Novelty
Co-designing efficiency-aware Gaussian pruning with hardware-targeted foveated rendering to achieve real-time PBNR on mobile.

## Contributions
- Hardware-cost-aware Gaussian pruning criterion for PBNR
- Accelerated foveated rendering reducing peripheral computation
- Real-time PBNR on mobile through combined pruning + foveation
