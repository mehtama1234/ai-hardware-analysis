# Separate but Together: Integrating Remote Attestation into TLS

**Problem:** Remote attestation and TLS are typically deployed as separate security mechanisms, requiring distinct verification flows. The challenge is cleanly integrating attestation verification into the TLS handshake without duplicating functionality or degrading performance.

**Method:** The approach extends the TLS handshake protocol to incorporate hardware attestation credentials (likely from TPM or CPU-based attestation). Attestation evidence is transmitted during certificate exchange, allowing clients to verify system integrity alongside TLS certificates through integrated handshake messages.

**Novelty:** Integration of remote attestation as a native component of TLS rather than requiring separate out-of-band attestation mechanisms.
