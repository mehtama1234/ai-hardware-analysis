# IceSpy: Reconfigurable Edge Accelerator for Scalable and Private Structural Health Monitoring

**Problem:** Structural Health Monitoring deployment is hindered by high costs from power-hungry wireless transmission and privacy concerns from data exposure. The challenge is implementing privacy-preserving data filtering at the edge while minimizing power consumption and deployment costs.

**Method:** IceSpy uses a programmable multi-chip systolic array of low-power COTS FPGAs to implement data filtering and local differential privacy at the edge. On-device filtering reduces wireless transmission overhead and power consumption. Local differential privacy removes sensitive information before transmission to untrusted servers. The systolic architecture enables efficient parallel processing of high-volume sensor streams.

**Novelty:** Combining local differential privacy with edge data filtering on a multi-chip FPGA systolic array to simultaneously achieve privacy preservation and 3× power reduction in structural health monitoring.
