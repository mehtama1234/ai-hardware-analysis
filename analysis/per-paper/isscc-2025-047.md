# isscc-2025-047: All-Passive mm-Wave LNA with Interferer-Cancellation Filter

**Title:** 5.10 A 3.5mW mm-Wave Low-Noise Active Bandpass Filter Employing an All-Passive Interferer-Cancellation Feedforward Path

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Core Problem:**
mm-wave receivers must balance low noise, high linearity, and sharp filtering; existing approaches sacrifice one or more metrics.

**Method:**
LNA with all-passive feedforward cancellation path that pre-filters before device nonlinearity, enabling sharp RF-domain filtering while maintaining noise and linearity performance.

**Key Innovation:**
All-passive interferer cancellation integrated into LNA for simultaneous low-noise, high-linearity filtering.

**Metrics:**
- **Power:** 3.5 mW
- **Tradeoff resolved:** Low noise + high linearity + sharp filter

**Target Applications:** Portable consumer electronics, AR/VR headsets

**Techniques:** Circuit design, power optimization, analog filtering

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)
