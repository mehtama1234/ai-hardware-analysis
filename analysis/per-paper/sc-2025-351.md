# EAS-Sim: A Framework and its Methodology for the Co-Design of Multi-Objective, Energy-Aware Schedulers for AI Clusters

**Paper ID:** sc-2025-351
**Venue:** SC
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Energy consumption is a critical operational cost in DL cluster datacenters, but schedulers ignore energy objectives

## Motivation
Modern HPC datacenters need multi-objective schedulers balancing performance, fairness, QoS, and energy efficiency

## Method
EAS-Sim is a simulation-based tool for designing job schedulers that handle multiple competing objectives in AI clusters. The methodology guides the design of different scheduler variants: Zeus for minimizing energy, Hades for ensuring fairness, AuraChronos for meeting service-level agreements, and Charon for respecting power budget limits.

## Key Novelty
The framework enables systematic design of job schedulers that optimize energy efficiency alongside fairness, service-level agreements, and power constraints.

## Contributions
- EAS-Sim simulation framework
- Four novel scheduler designs
- 8-10% energy reduction (Zeus) vs Pollux
- 7-8% energy reduction with fairness (Hades)
- 20x SLA miss rate improvement (AuraChronos)
- Power budget management (Charon)
