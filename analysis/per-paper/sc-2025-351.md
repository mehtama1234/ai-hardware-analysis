# EAS-Sim: A Framework and its Methodology for the Co-Design of Multi-Objective, Energy-Aware Schedulers for AI Clusters

**Paper ID:** sc-2025-351
**Venue:** SC
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Energy consumption is a critical operational cost in DL cluster datacenters, but schedulers ignore energy objectives

## Motivation
Modern HPC datacenters need multi-objective schedulers balancing performance, fairness, QoS, and energy efficiency

## Method
EAS-Sim framework + methodology for co-designing energy-aware schedulers: Zeus (energy), Hades (fairness), AuraChronos (SLA), Charon (power budget)

## Key Novelty
Systematic methodology for co-designing multi-objective, energy-aware schedulers with simulation framework

## Contributions
- EAS-Sim simulation framework
- Four novel scheduler designs
- 8-10% energy reduction (Zeus) vs Pollux
- 7-8% energy reduction with fairness (Hades)
- 20x SLA miss rate improvement (AuraChronos)
- Power budget management (Charon)
