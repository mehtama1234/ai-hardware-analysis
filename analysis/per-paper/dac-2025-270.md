# dac-2025-270: Efficient Continuous Logic Optimization with Diffusion Model

**Problem:** Logic synthesis optimization requires exhaustive search over exponentially large transformation sequences; existing Bayesian and RL methods are inefficient.

**Motivation:** Automating synthesis QoR optimization with better efficiency enables faster design iteration.

**Method:** The method searches for better designs by using gradient-based optimization (climbing uphill) in a continuous mathematical space, then uses a diffusion model (trained to convert solutions into valid synthesis sequences) to translate those continuous points back into actual transformations. A separate model predicts how good each design is based on area and delay, guiding the search. The diffusion model ensures every solution remains valid.

**Key Novelty:** Diffusion models bridge continuous optimization and discrete synthesis feasibility.

**Contributions:**
- 5-130x efficiency gain vs. Bayesian/RL methods
- Diffusion-guided continuous optimization framework
- Lower area/delay on benchmarks

**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
