# PHOENIX: Pauli-Based High-Level Optimization Engine for Instruction Execution on NISQ Devices

**Venue:** DAC
**ID:** dac-2025-381

## Problem
Variational quantum algorithms require compilation at gate level, missing high-level Pauli-based optimization opportunities for Hamiltonian simulation programs.

## Motivation
Hamiltonian simulation programs have distinct Pauli-based structure; high-level IR optimization can drastically reduce circuit depth and gate count.

## Method
PHOENIX operates on Pauli-based IR using binary symplectic form (BSF) to formally describe Pauli strings. It reformulates IR synthesis as BSF column-weight reduction via Clifford transformations, with heuristic simplification searching for optimal 2Q operators and TETRIS-like global ordering.

## Key Novelty
Pauli-level IR compilation with binary symplectic form enabling systematic global optimization of Hamiltonian simulation programs.

## Contributions
1. High-level Pauli-based IR for capturing program structure of Hamiltonian simulation
2. Binary symplectic form formulation enabling systematic BSF column-weight reduction
3. Heuristic algorithm for maximal Pauli simplification at each step via 2Q Clifford operators
4. Global ordering strategy balancing gate cancellation, circuit depth, and qubit routing
5. Outperforms SOTA VQA compilers across diverse backend ISAs and hardware topologies
