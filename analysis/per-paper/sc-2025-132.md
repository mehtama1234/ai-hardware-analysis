# AERIS: Argonne Earth Systems Model for Reliable and Skillful Predictions

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-132
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Weather forecasting with deterministic methods has spectral biases and poor ensemble calibration; high-resolution scaling is difficult.

## Motivation
Generative ML enables better Earth system modeling; diffusion models show promise but scaling to high resolution is challenging.

## Method
AERIS generates weather predictions using a diffusion transformer—a neural network that creates output by progressively refining random noise—scaled to 1.3 to 80 billion parameters at pixel-level resolution. The model uses Swin transformer blocks (which apply attention within local windows to reduce computation) combined with SWiPe, a parallelism technique that distributes computation via window-based, sequence-based, and pipeline-based splits across processors without extra communication overhead.

## Key Novelty
AERIS combines billion-parameter diffusion transformers for Earth systems weather prediction with SWiPe composable parallelism, a technique that coordinates three processor-split methods (window-based, sequence-based, and pipeline-based) without extra communication.

## Contributions
