# AERIS: Argonne Earth Systems Model for Reliable and Skillful Predictions

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-132
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Weather forecasting with deterministic methods has spectral biases and poor ensemble calibration; high-resolution scaling is difficult.

## Motivation
Generative ML enables better Earth system modeling; diffusion models show promise but scaling to high resolution is challenging.

## Method
AERIS: 1.3-80B parameter pixel-level Swin diffusion transformer with SWiPe parallelism technique (window+sequence+pipeline parallelism without extra communication).

## Key Novelty
Billion-parameter diffusion transformer for Earth systems + SWiPe composable parallelism for window-based transformers

## Contributions
1. 10.21 ExaFLOPS sustained / 11.21 peak on Aurora
2. 95.5% weak scaling, 81.6% strong scaling
3. Stable to 90-day forecasts
4. Outperforms IFS ENS
