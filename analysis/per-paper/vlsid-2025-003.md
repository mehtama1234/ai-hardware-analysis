# A Fully Autonomous 1.2A Auxiliary Buck DC-DC Converter for Fast Transient Load-on-Demand

**Venue:** VLSID
**Authors:** Shivam Agarwal, Sivasai Guddanti, Qadeer A. Khan

## Problem Statement
A fully autonomous auxiliary buck converter for supplying transient load current is proposed in this paper

## Key Innovation
Novel propose approach

## Method
A buck converter reduces voltage by rapidly switching transistors on and off in a controlled pattern. This design is fully autonomous, meaning internal feedback circuits continuously monitor the load's current demand and adjust the switching automatically without external commands. When the load suddenly demands more current (a transient spike), the converter detects this and instantly increases output to supply up to 1.2 amps, then returns to baseline when demand drops.
