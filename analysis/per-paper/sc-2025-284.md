# ChatEED: An agentic retrieval assistant for accelerator operators

**Venue:** SC 2025  
**Authors:** Aaron Zachary Reed, Claudio Bisegni, Sandesh Shrestha, Michelle Huang, Daniel Ratner (SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory)

## Problem & Motivation

Accelerator facility operators at SLAC maintain critical institutional knowledge through electronic logbooks (ELOGs), but the system is labor-intensive and information-poor: entries are time-consuming to create, often incomplete, filled with unexplained jargon, and inconsistently structured. With decades of accumulated records, manual information retrieval is inefficient and knowledge transfer to new operators is difficult.

## Approach

**ChatEED:** An agentic retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system designed through human-centered methodology:

1. **Operator-Needs Analysis:** Conducted interviews and workflow observations at SLAC.
2. **Key Capabilities:** 
   - Automated shift summarization
   - Real-time ELOG information retrieval
3. **Design Priorities:** Security, modularity, efficiency, transparency
4. **Architecture:** Agentic RAG enabling iterative refinement and context-aware retrieval.

## Key Novelty

- **Human-Centered Design:** System design driven by operator workflow and needs analysis.
- **Agentic RAG:** Goes beyond static retrieval to enable iterative information gathering and reasoning.
- **Operations-Specific:** Tailored to accelerator operations domain with sensitive information handling.
