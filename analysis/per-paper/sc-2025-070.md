# Reproducibility Report for SC25 Paper Moment: Co-optimizing Physical Communication Topology and Data Placement for Multi-GPU Out-of-core GNN Training

**ID:** sc-2025-070 | **Venue:** SC

## Problem Statement
Artifact evaluation and reproducibility verification

## Motivation
Ensure reproducibility and artifact quality of SC papers

## Method
The team obtained and evaluated the Moment paper's code and documentation describing a technique for optimizing two aspects together: the physical communication pathways between GPUs and where data is stored when training graph neural networks (neural networks designed for graph-structured data) on multiple GPUs with some data on disk because it exceeds GPU memory. They recreated the experimental environment and ran the training jobs as described, comparing their reproduced results against the original paper's reported performance to verify whether the work is reproducible.

## Key Novelty
This report verifies whether the Moment paper's approach of jointly optimizing GPU communication topology and data placement actually produces its claimed performance improvements for training graph neural networks across multiple GPUs with disk-based storage.
