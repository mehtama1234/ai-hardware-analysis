# isscc-2025-109: Fifth-Order Filtering 5G Receiver

**Problem:** 5G NR receivers with 200+ MHz bandwidth require aggressive blocker suppression while maintaining low noise figure and power efficiency.

**Method:** The receiver uses a five-stage filter (fifth-order filtering) at the input with steep roll-off exceeding 20dB per decade of frequency, which sharply rejects signals from nearby channels before they can interfere. It employs a mixer-first architecture where the incoming radio signal passes through this front-end filter before being converted to baseband (the low-frequency signal containing the data). To keep power consumption low while maintaining the low noise figure needed for weak-signal reception, the design uses stacked capacitors for passive gain—amplifying the signal without drawing extra current.

**Key Novelty:** Fifth-order filtering achieving 19dBm adjacent-channel IIP3 with improved power efficiency over prior mixer-first approaches.

**Main Results:** 200MHz bandwidth, 19dBm adjacent-channel IIP3, optimized NF/power trade-off.
