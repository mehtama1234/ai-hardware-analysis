# isscc-2025-109: Fifth-Order Filtering 5G Receiver

**Problem:** 5G NR receivers with 200+ MHz bandwidth require aggressive blocker suppression while maintaining low noise figure and power efficiency.

**Method:** Fifth-order filtering architecture using mixer-first topology with steep baseband input impedance roll-off (>20dB/dec); passive gain via capacitor stacking.

**Key Novelty:** Fifth-order filtering achieving 19dBm adjacent-channel IIP3 with improved power efficiency over prior mixer-first approaches.

**Main Results:** 200MHz bandwidth, 19dBm adjacent-channel IIP3, optimized NF/power trade-off.

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only analysis)
