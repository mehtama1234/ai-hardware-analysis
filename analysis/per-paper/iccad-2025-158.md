# COBRA - Binary Transformer Accelerator on FPGA with 1-bit Multiplier

COBRA presents an FPGA-based binary Transformer accelerator that uses 1-bit quantized weights and activations to replace expensive multiplications with simple binary operations. The system achieves 311× energy efficiency improvement over GPU-based Transformer inference while maintaining reasonable accuracy. This work demonstrates that aggressive quantization combined with specialized hardware design can enable efficient Transformer deployment on resource-constrained edge devices, opening new possibilities for ubiquitous AI inference.
