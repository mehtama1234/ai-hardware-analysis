# Enabling Decoder-only Language Model Inference on a CNN Accelerator

**ID:** iccad-2025-032  
**Venue:** ICCAD

## Problem
CNN accelerators cannot efficiently run decoder-only language models due to non-computational operations in attention (head merging/splitting, transpositions), lack of floating-point support for Softmax and RMSNorm, and memory-bound operations during generation that degrade throughput.

## Motivation
Reusing existing CNN accelerators for NLP avoids high cost of dedicated NLP hardware while leveraging already-deployed commercial infrastructure.

## Method
The paper proposes minimal hardware extensions to CNN accelerators including: (1) a method to execute multi-head attention without dedicated reshaping hardware, (2) a lightweight SIMD coprocessor for nonlinear operations (Softmax, RMSNorm), and (3) techniques to improve power efficiency during memory-bound generation stage.

## Key Novelty
Minimal architectural extensions enabling efficient transformer inference on CNN accelerators by addressing non-computational attention operations and floating-point requirements.

## Contributions
1. Efficient multi-head attention execution without dedicated reshaping hardware
2. Lightweight SIMD coprocessor design for nonlinear operations
3. Power-efficiency techniques for memory-bound generation stage
4. Demonstration that low-power CNN accelerators achieve GPU-comparable NLP inference throughput
