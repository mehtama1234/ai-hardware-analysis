# isscc-2025-015: Self-Propelling Microrobot with PT-Symmetric Energy Harvesting

**Fully Integrated Self-Propelling Microrobot in 180nm CMOS with Sub-GHz Parity-Time-Symmetry On-Chip Energy Harvesting and Traveling Wave Electroosmosis Actuation**

## Summary
First monolithic integration of parity-time-symmetry energy harvesting and electroosmotic actuation on a single 180nm CMOS die, enabling fully autonomous microbot motion without external power or control.

## Technical Approach
- **Energy harvesting**: PT-symmetric circuits at sub-GHz frequencies directly on CMOS
- **Actuation**: Traveling-wave electroosmotic channels for propulsion
- **Integration**: Monolithic design eliminating off-chip power coupling

## Key Innovation
PT-symmetric harvesting (lower sensitivity to load variations than magnetic coupling) coupled directly to electroosmotic actuation, avoiding need for external power or photovoltaic processes.

## Contributions
- First PT-symmetric XO in CMOS
- Integrated electroosmotic actuator design
- Fully autonomous microbot demonstration

## Target Workloads
Medical devices, micro-scale manipulation, implantable systems

## Limitations
- Speed, force output, operating duration metrics not disclosed
- Scalability to larger/faster robots unclear
