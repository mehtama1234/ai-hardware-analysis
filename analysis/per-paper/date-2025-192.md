# HyAtten: Hybrid Photonic-Digital Architecture for Accelerating Attention Mechanism

**Venue:** DATE  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Core Idea
Combine photonic and digital processing for attention acceleration by adaptively routing signals—low-resolution signals via fast photonic converters, high-resolution via digital circuits—to minimize signal conversion overhead.

## Problem
Photonic accelerators for transformers are limited by high ADC/DAC conversion overhead, negating their speed and energy advantages.

## Method
The system uses a signal comparator (a circuit that examines incoming data) to split the work between two paths. Low-precision signals use low-resolution photonic converters, which can process many operations in parallel through photonic circuits. High-precision signals bypass the slow conversion step and go straight to digital circuits instead. By routing each type of signal to the right processor, the system avoids the expensive conversion overhead that normally limits photonic speedup.

## Key Results
- **9.8x performance/area** vs state-of-the-art photonic transformer accelerator
- **2.2x energy-efficiency/area** improvement
