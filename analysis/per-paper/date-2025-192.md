# HyAtten: Hybrid Photonic-Digital Architecture for Accelerating Attention Mechanism

**Venue:** DATE  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Core Idea
Combine photonic and digital processing for attention acceleration by adaptively routing signals—low-resolution signals via fast photonic converters, high-resolution via digital circuits—to minimize signal conversion overhead.

## Problem
Photonic accelerators for transformers are limited by high ADC/DAC conversion overhead, negating their speed and energy advantages.

## Method
- **Signal Classifier:** Determines whether each signal can be processed by low-resolution photonic converters
- **Hybrid Processing:** Routes signals to photonic path (low-resolution) or digital path (high-resolution) based on classification
- Photonic circuits handle high-parallelism tasks; digital circuits handle precision-critical operations

## Key Results
- **9.8x performance/area** vs state-of-the-art photonic transformer accelerator
- **2.2x energy-efficiency/area** improvement

## Limitations
Evaluation limited to photonic systems; digital transformer and GPU comparisons absent.
