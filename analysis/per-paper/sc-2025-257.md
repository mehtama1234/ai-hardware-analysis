# Superconducting Architecture Modeling in gem5

## Problem
Superconducting electronics promise 100x clock speed but architectural implications remain unclear. Full-system modeling needed to quantify benefits alongside memory hierarchies and interconnects.

## Approach
- Extended gem5 with 100 GHz superconducting cores and 4 GHz cryogenic cores
- Integrated multilevel caches and SRNoC superconducting interconnect
- Evaluated on SPEC CPU2006 and compute-intensive workloads

## Key Findings
- **Compute-intensive**: up to 24x speedup
- **Memory-intensive**: only 1.2x (bottlenecked by room-temperature DRAM)
- **SRNoC interconnect**: 35-73x energy efficiency (narrow paths), but 1246x slowdown (wide paths)
- **Critical bottleneck**: 800 GB/s cache bandwidth requirement

## Implication
Superconducting technology best suited for domain-specific accelerators, not general-purpose CPUs. Performance gains depend heavily on workload memory access patterns and data widths. DRAM remains a critical bottleneck in heterogeneous systems.

## Themes
- Emerging computing substrates
- Full-system simulation
- Heterogeneous memory hierarchies
- Interconnect design challenges
