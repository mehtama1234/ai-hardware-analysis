# isscc-2025-052: Magnetic-Mode Octave-Bandwidth VCO

**Title:** 34.4 A 9.05-to-37.0GHz LO Generator with Magnetic Mode Switching and Tuning-Free Octave-Bandwidth Common-Mode Resonator Achieving >190.7dBc/Hz FoM

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Core Problem:**
Octave-bandwidth VCOs (>10GHz) struggle with simultaneous wide tuning range and low phase noise; multi-mode designs require manual mode switching and extensive tuning.

**Method:**
Magnetic mode switching with automatic tuning-free common-mode resonator tracking maintains resonance across 4.09:1 tuning range, eliminating complex manual control.

**Key Innovation:**
Automatic magnetic-mode switching + tuning-free octave-bandwidth resonator.

**Metrics:**
- **Frequency Range:** 9.05–37.0 GHz (4.09:1)
- **Phase Noise FoM:** >190.7 dBc/Hz

**Architecture:** Magnetic mode switching + automatic CM resonator tracking

**Techniques:** Circuit design, VCO design

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)
