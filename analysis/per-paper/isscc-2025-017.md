# isscc-2025-017: Power-Efficient Pipelined ADC with Parallel-SAR Sub-Quantizer

**A 14b 1GS/s Single-Channel Pipelined ADC with Parallel-Operation SAR Sub-Quantizer and Dynamic-Deadzone Ring Amplifier**

## Summary
14b 1GS/s pipelined ADC using parallel SAR sub-conversion to decouple timing from amplifier bandwidth, enabling low-power ring-amplifier design while maintaining 68.2 dB SNDR.

## Technical Approach
- **Parallel SAR**: Overlaps residue conversion with amplification, reducing stage count
- **Ring amplifier**: Dynamic-deadzone topology improves linearity and PVT robustness
- **Power**: 15.3 mW enabling Schreier FoM of 173.3 dB

## Key Innovation
Parallel-operation SAR decoupling conversion timing from residue amplification, enabling lower-power ring-amplifier designs without sacrificing SNDR or input swing.

## Contributions
- Parallel SAR scheme reducing amplifier bandwidth requirements
- Dynamic-deadzone ring-amplifier linearity improvement
- High SFDR (85.8 dB) with low power (15.3 mW)

## Target Workloads
Instrumentation, wireless communications, high-resolution analog frontend
