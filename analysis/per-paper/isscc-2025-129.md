# isscc-2025-129: On-Die Power Management with ML Calibration for 3nm CPU

**Problem:** High-performance CPUs for gaming face over-current excursions when peak current exceeds PMIC tolerance due to shorter power-budgeting intervals (<50µs at 90fps) and rising CPU clock speeds, causing system instability and performance degradation.

**Motivation:** Gaming workloads at 90+ fps demand low power-budgeting intervals; fast hardware-based power sensing is essential to enable dynamic core allocation and prevent current excursions while maintaining gaming performance and computing efficiency.

**Method:** The 3nm octa-core CPU integrates on-die power sensors with machine learning-based calibration to enable real-time power management at sub-50µs intervals, dynamically allocating tasks to cores based on instantaneous power budgets to prevent peak-current violations.

**Key Novelty:** On-die power sensors with silicon ML-based calibration for real-time peak-current management in multi-core CPUs.

**Contributions:**
- On-die power sensor integrated in 3nm octa-core CPU
- Machine learning-based sensor calibration for improved accuracy
- Fast power management at <50µs intervals for gaming workloads
- Prevention of peak-current excursions without performance loss
