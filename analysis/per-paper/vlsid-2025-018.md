# N-Well Patterning of P-Type CMOS Substrate for Improving Quality Factor of on-Chip Inductors at Millimeter Wave Frequencies

**Venue:** VLSID

## Problem
On-chip inductors at mmW frequencies suffer from eddy current and capacitive coupling losses

## Motivation
High Q inductors essential for mmW RF circuits; substrate losses limit performance; need loss reduction without resonance degradation

## Method
N-well patterning on p-type substrate; two patterning structures reduce eddy/capacitive currents in spiral inductors

## Key Novelty
N-well patterning approach maintains self-resonant frequency while reducing substrate coupling losses

## Contributions
- 77% Q improvement (structure 1)
- 16% Q improvement (structure 2)
- Inductance preservation
