# N-Well Patterning of P-Type CMOS Substrate for Improving Quality Factor of on-Chip Inductors at Millimeter Wave Frequencies

**Venue:** VLSID

## Problem
On-chip inductors at mmW frequencies suffer from eddy current and capacitive coupling losses

## Motivation
High Q inductors essential for mmW RF circuits; substrate losses limit performance; need loss reduction without resonance degradation

## Method
Creating patterned regions of N-well material (a semiconductor region type) in a p-type substrate shields spiral inductors from electrical losses at millimeter-wave frequencies. The patterns act as barriers that reduce eddy currents (unwanted circular electrical currents induced in the substrate) and capacitive coupling (unwanted electrical connections through the substrate). Two specific patterning structures suppress both types of losses simultaneously, improving inductor efficiency at high frequencies.

## Key Novelty
N-well patterning reduces substrate losses in millimeter-wave inductors without degrading their self-resonant frequency.
