# SRing: Sub-Ring Optical NoC Design

**Venue:** DATE | **ID:** date-2025-404

## Problem
Wavelength-routed optical ring routers lack application customization, resulting in high propagation loss from long signal paths and excessive splitter usage in power distribution networks.

## Motivation
Generic ring designs waste power and area; application-specific customization exploits communication patterns for efficiency gains.

## Approach
Sub-ring clustering (multiple short rings instead of one large ring) + MILP-based wavelength assignment to minimize power distribution splitters.

## Key Innovation
Sub-ring clustering topology with MILP-optimized wavelength assignment for application-specific optical NoCs.

## Results
- Reduced worst-case signal path length
- Lower insertion loss
- Reduced PDN splitter count
- Improved power efficiency

## Target Hardware
Photonic (optical NoC)

## Techniques
Interconnect design, dataflow, power optimization
