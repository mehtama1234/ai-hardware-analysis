# Barre: Empowering Simplified and Versatile Programmable Congestion Control in High-Speed AI Clusters

**Problem:** Network congestion in high-speed AI clusters severely limits collective communication throughput during distributed training and inference, requiring complex, inflexible congestion control policies tailored to specific cluster configurations.

**Method:** Barre provides a programmable abstraction layer for congestion control that allows users to define custom policies without low-level networking expertise. It simplifies policy implementation through higher-level primitives and enables versatile support for diverse workload patterns via in-network or end-host programmability mechanisms.

**Novelty:** A simplified, vendor-agnostic programmable framework that abstracts congestion control policy expression, enabling rapid prototyping and deployment of cluster-specific strategies without requiring switch or NIC firmware modifications.
