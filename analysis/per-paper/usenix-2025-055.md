# Barre: Empowering Simplified and Versatile Programmable Congestion Control in High-Speed AI Clusters

**Problem:** Network congestion in high-speed AI clusters severely limits collective communication throughput during distributed training and inference, requiring complex, inflexible congestion control policies tailored to specific cluster configurations.

**Method:** Barre simplifies congestion control policy writing by providing higher-level building blocks instead of requiring deep networking expertise. Users compose these blocks to create policies tailored to their specific cluster. The policies run either within network switches (in-network) or on end systems like GPUs and network cards (end-host).

**Novelty:** Barre lets you quickly develop and deploy custom congestion-control strategies across different hardware vendors without modifying switch or NIC firmware.
