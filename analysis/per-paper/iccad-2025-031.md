# Invited: IEEE DATC RDF-2025: Enabling an EDA Research Ecosystem

**ID:** iccad-2025-031  
**Venue:** ICCAD

## Problem
EDA research lacks standardized open-source infrastructures and benchmarking mechanisms that support academic reproducibility and commercial tool vendor collaboration.

## Motivation
Establishing accessible EDA research infrastructure and enabling industry-academia partnerships accelerates innovation in design automation.

## Method
IEEE CEDA DATC establishes: (1) a GitHub resource with OpenROAD-based research environment, (2) permission mechanisms and benchmarking for commercial P&R tools with vendor collaboration, and (3) ML EDA Commons initiative for machine learning in EDA.

## Key Novelty
First formal permission mechanism and public benchmarking results for commercial EDA tools published with vendor permission.

## Contributions
1. Accessible GitHub resource providing OpenROAD and OpenROAD-flow-scripts-based research environment
2. First-ever permission mechanism for commercial EDA tool benchmarking with published results
3. ML EDA Commons initiative supporting ML-driven EDA research
4. Community infrastructure supporting reproducible EDA research and open culture development
