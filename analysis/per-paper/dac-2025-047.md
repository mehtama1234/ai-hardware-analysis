# EPOC: An Efficient Pulse Generation Framework with Advanced Synthesis for Quantum Circuits

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Quantum optimal control has high computational overhead; gate-based pulse generation suffers from long latencies.

## Key Idea
Combine ZX-Calculus, circuit partitioning, and synthesis to enable fine-grained, parallel pulse generation with minimal overhead.

## Method
EPOC uses ZX-Calculus, a mathematical form that reveals equivalent ways to arrange a quantum circuit, to explore more parallelizable circuit organizations. The framework breaks pulse generation into smaller, more controllable units (finer granularity) and partitions the circuit into independent segments that can process in parallel. Synthesis techniques then optimize these segments into efficient pulse instructions, reducing both the computational cost of pulse generation and circuit execution time.

## Contributions
1. Novel ZX-Calculus + partitioning + synthesis framework
2. Increased parallelism through finer granularity
3. 31.74% latency reduction vs. prior work; 76.80% vs. gate-based methods
