# EPOC: An Efficient Pulse Generation Framework with Advanced Synthesis for Quantum Circuits

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Quantum optimal control has high computational overhead; gate-based pulse generation suffers from long latencies.

## Key Idea
Combine ZX-Calculus, circuit partitioning, and synthesis to enable fine-grained, parallel pulse generation with minimal overhead.

## Method
- ZX-Calculus for compact circuit abstraction
- Circuit partitioning for parallel processing
- Advanced synthesis for equivalent circuit representations
- Fine-grained pulse generation

## Contributions
1. Novel ZX-Calculus + partitioning + synthesis framework
2. Increased parallelism through finer granularity
3. 31.74% latency reduction vs. prior work; 76.80% vs. gate-based methods

## Workloads
Quantum circuit execution
