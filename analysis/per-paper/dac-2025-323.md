# ELF: Efficient Logic Synthesis by Pruning Redundancy in Refactoring

## Problem
Refactoring in logic optimization is computationally expensive; 98% of cut-based refactoring attempts fail to improve circuits.

## Solution
ML classifier preemptively prunes unsuccessful cut candidates before expensive resynthesis, eliminating wasted computation in the refactor operator.

## Impact
3.9x speedup vs ABC refactor on EPFL benchmarks and 10 large industrial designs.
