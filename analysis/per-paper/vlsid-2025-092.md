# Optimization of Sub-Threshold Standard Cells for Energy Efficient Designs

**Venue:** VLSID 2025  
**Authors:** Vardhan Suroshi, Karthik B. K, Vikram Kannur, Vinay Reddy, Madhura Purnaprajna

## Problem Statement
Contemporary ultra-low-power IC designs rely on subthreshold voltage operation to minimize energy consumption. However, standard foundry libraries are optimized for nominal voltage (1.2V) and fail to provide adequate performance in the subthreshold regime, requiring custom-optimized standard cell libraries.

## Key Contributions
1. **Specialized Cell Library**: Comprehensive subthreshold standard cell library optimized via SPEA-2 genetic algorithm
2. **44% Energy Efficiency Improvement**: Average improvement compared to conventional approaches
3. **Massive Energy Reduction**: 13.26x reduction vs. nominal voltage library at subthreshold
4. **Near-Threshold Improvement**: 1.49x better than foundry near-threshold library
5. **Validated on Real Workloads**: Tested on RISC-V processors and EPFL benchmark circuits
6. **Quantified Tradeoffs**: Clear characterization of area vs. energy tradeoffs

## Technical Approach
- **Optimization Method**: SPEA-2 multi-objective genetic algorithm for transistor sizing
- **Library Scope**: Complete set of standard cells (logic gates, storage elements)
- **Validation**: 
  - RISC-V processor synthesis
  - EPFL benchmark circuit synthesis
  - Comparison against nominal (1.2V) and near-threshold (600mV) baselines
- **Operating Point**: Subthreshold voltage regime (<500mV)
