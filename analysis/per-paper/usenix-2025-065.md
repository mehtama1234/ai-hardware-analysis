# Emulating Space Computing Networks with RHONE

**Problem:** Space Computing Networks (SCNs) research requires high-fidelity modeling of satellite and constellation behavior (power, thermal, network, computation states), but real satellite experimentation is prohibitively expensive.

**Method:** RHONE employs a two-phase approach: offline modeling builds power, thermal, orbit, network, and computation models using real satellite telemetries and hardware-in-the-loop chip mirroring; online execution runs container-based applications with these integrated models to achieve satellite and constellation-level fidelity.

**Novelty:** Combining real satellite telemetry data and hardware-in-the-loop chip mirroring to achieve high-fidelity satellite and constellation-level emulation while maintaining practical usability.
