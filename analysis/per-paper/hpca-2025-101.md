# hpca-2025-101 — Choco-Q: Commute Hamiltonian-based QAOA for Constrained Binary Optimization

**Venue:** HPCA  
**Authors:** Debin Xiang, Qifan Jiang, Liqiang Lu, Siwei Tan, Jianwei Yin (Zhejiang University)  
**Confidence:** high (full text)

## Problem
Existing QAOA approaches for constrained binary optimization (penalty-term or cyclic Hamiltonian) fail to encode arbitrary linear constraints, yielding near-zero success rates (~0.02-0.07%) and long end-to-end latency. Decomposing the resulting Hamiltonians into deployable quantum circuits requires exponential time and memory.

## Motivation
Constrained binary optimization (NP-hard: facility location, scheduling, graph coloring) is a prime target for quantum advantage, but the extremely low success rates of current QAOA designs eliminate any practical quantum benefit.

## Method
Choco-Q selects a driver Hamiltonian that commutes with the constraint operator ([C_hat, H_d] = 0), exploiting the Heisenberg picture to guarantee constraint expectation values remain invariant during quantum evolution — achieving 100% in-constraints rate for arbitrary linear equality constraints. Three optimization passes enable NISQ deployment:

1. **Hamiltonian serialization**: Decomposes the global commute Hamiltonian into independent local sub-Hamiltonians (Lemma 1 proves constraint satisfaction is preserved), reducing circuit depth from ~4000 to ~24.
2. **Equivalent decomposition**: Converts each local commute Hamiltonian into control-phase gates + CX gates with O(n) time complexity and O(n) circuit depth (Lemma 2), replacing exponential Trotter-Suzuki; achieves 10^6x decomposition time reduction.
3. **Variable elimination**: Removes variables with the most non-zero solution entries from the constraint matrix, reducing qubit count and circuit depth to ~100 at the cost of extra classical measurements.

Evaluated on IBMQ Fez (159-qubit Heron r2), Sherbrooke, Osaka (127-qubit Eagle r3) platforms, and A100 GPU simulator.

## Key Novelty
Using the commute Hamiltonian as the QAOA driver provides a universal hard-constraint encoding for arbitrary linear constraints, combined with a linear-complexity exact decomposition that makes it deployable on current NISQ hardware.

## Contributions
- First QAOA framework achieving 100% in-constraints rate for arbitrary linear equality constraints via commute Hamiltonian encoding
- Hamiltonian serialization reducing circuit depth from thousands to tens with provable constraint preservation (Lemma 1)
- Exact O(n) decomposition of local commute Hamiltonians into control-phase + CX gates, eliminating exponential Trotter approximation (Lemma 2)
- Variable elimination for further NISQ deployment; overall 235x success rate improvement and 4.69x end-to-end speedup over cyclic Hamiltonian QAOA

## Metrics
- **Speedup:** 4.69x end-to-end over cyclic Hamiltonian QAOA on real IBMQ hardware
- **Accuracy:** 235x improvement in success rate; 100% in-constraints rate; 658x improvement in approximation ratio gap
- **Other:** 10^6x decomposition time reduction; circuit depth from ~10^4 to ~100; 2.65x in-constraints rate on IBMQ

## Limitations
Circuit depth grows linearly with problem size and number of constraints, limiting scalability to problems within current NISQ qubit counts (tested up to 28 variables, 16 constraints).
