# Post-Variational Quantum Neural Networks on a Hybrid HPC-QC System

**ID**: sc-2025-368
**Venue**: SC
**Confidence**: low

## Problem
Training quantum neural networks on NISQ devices with limited resources

## Motivation
NISQ devices are limited; post-variational methods reduce circuit evaluations

## Method
Post-variational quantum neural networks use a hybrid classical-quantum approach to avoid repeatedly running the quantum circuit. Instead of the variational method's approach of measuring the quantum circuit many times to estimate parameter updates, this method uses the classical computer to compute how to improve parameters differently—handling expensive calculations that would normally require repeated quantum measurements. This reduces the demand on the quantum hardware, making training feasible on small, noisy quantum devices (NISQ devices).

## Key Novelty
It trains quantum neural networks on resource-limited quantum computers with better accuracy than variational methods, without needing to repeatedly evaluate the quantum circuit.
