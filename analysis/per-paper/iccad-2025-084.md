# Building an Open CGRA Ecosystem for Agile Innovation

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-084

## Problem
Closed, vertically-integrated CGRA development limits architectural exploration and innovation. Lack of open, modular CGRA ecosystems hinders community-driven research and real-world deployment of reconfigurable accelerators.

## Motivation
Modern AI and edge computing workloads require agile hardware-software co-design, which benefits from open ecosystems replacing closed vertically-integrated approaches with modular, community-driven architectures.

## Method
The paper presents an open CGRA ecosystem including:
1. **HyCUBE**, a CGRA with reconfigurable single-cycle multi-hop interconnect for efficient data movement
2. **PACE**, embedding HyCUBE within a RISC-V SoC for edge computing
3. **Morpher**, an open-source, architecture-adaptive CGRA design framework supporting design space exploration, compilation, simulation, and validation

The work emphasizes openness at every layer to lower innovation barriers.

## Key Novelty
Comprehensive open CGRA ecosystem with architecture-adaptive design framework enabling modular hardware specialization and cross-layer optimization without coupling to specific software implementations.

## Contributions
- HyCUBE CGRA with reconfigurable single-cycle multi-hop interconnect for flexible data movement
- PACE architecture integrating HyCUBE within RISC-V SoC for power-efficient edge computing
- Morpher open-source design framework supporting full design space exploration and compilation pipeline
- Demonstration of modular, community-driven CGRA development enabling reproducible research
