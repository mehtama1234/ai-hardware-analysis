# isscc-2025-158: LC-Resonator CTLE for High-Loss PAM-4

**Problem:** High-speed interconnects (100+ Gb/s AI/GPU, PCIe 7.0) require 52dB+ loss compensation over long channels. Analog CTLE power-hungry; DSP-based approaches lack energy-efficient broadband equalization.

**Motivation:** Data-center AI accelerators need long-reach interconnects; extreme loss compensation bottlenecks transceiver design.

**Method:** DSP-based 112Gb/s PAM-4 receiver with LC-resonator CTLE front-end enabling >52dB loss compensation without power-hungry analog inductors. Adaptive DSP backend for equalization and symbol detection. Analog data-path latency optimization. 4nm FinFET.

**Key Novelty:** LC-resonator CTLE achieving >52dB loss compensation in energy-efficient DSP PAM-4 receiver.

**Contributions:**
- 112Gb/s PAM-4
- >52dB loss compensation
- LC-resonator CTLE
- DSP adaptive equalization
- Analog latency reduction

**Hardware Target:** ASIC

**Technique Categories:** circuit-design, signal-processing, analog

**Metrics:** 112Gb/s, >52dB CTLE, 4nm

**Baselines:** Conventional analog CTLE, prior DSP PAM-4, lower-speed transceivers

**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
