# isscc-2025-158: LC-Resonator CTLE for High-Loss PAM-4

**Problem:** High-speed interconnects (100+ Gb/s AI/GPU, PCIe 7.0) require 52dB+ loss compensation over long channels. Analog CTLE power-hungry; DSP-based approaches lack energy-efficient broadband equalization.

**Motivation:** Data-center AI accelerators need long-reach interconnects; extreme loss compensation bottlenecks transceiver design.

**Method:** A 112Gb/s receiver uses an LC-resonator CTLE (continuous-time linear equalizer: a circuit using inductors and capacitors to compensate for signal loss over long channels) combined with a digital signal processor to recover signals weakened by over 52dB of loss. The LC-resonator front-end boosts weakened frequencies without excessive power consumption, while the digital processor performs adaptive equalization and decodes PAM-4 symbols (a 4-level encoding that packs more data per signal pulse). Built in 4nm FinFET (advanced transistor manufacturing process), the design minimizes latency through analog path optimization and achieves the energy efficiency needed for data-center interconnect deployment.

**Key Novelty:** LC-resonator CTLE achieving >52dB loss compensation in energy-efficient DSP PAM-4 receiver.

**Contributions:**
- 112Gb/s PAM-4
- >52dB loss compensation
- LC-resonator CTLE
- DSP adaptive equalization
- Analog latency reduction

**Hardware Target:** ASIC

**Technique Categories:** circuit-design, signal-processing, analog

**Metrics:** 112Gb/s, >52dB CTLE, 4nm

**Baselines:** Conventional analog CTLE, prior DSP PAM-4, lower-speed transceivers
