# Multi-Partner Project: Artificial Intelligence in Manufacturing Leading to Sustainability and the Consideration of Human Aspects (AIMS5.0)

**Venue**: DATE  
**Primary Theme**: AI and hardware for sustainable Industry 5.0  

## Problem
Industry 5.0 requires convergence of sustainability, digital autonomy, and human-centric design; translating these principles into practical hardware and tool implementations is challenging.

## Method
By connecting academic AI researchers directly with manufacturers, the project grounds AI solutions in real factory problems rather than lab settings. The resulting AI-driven tools and hardware are designed with two constraints built in from the start: they must keep humans meaningfully involved in decisions (not just executing automated steps), and they must reduce environmental impact. This co-design approach means technical capability and human/sustainability requirements shape each other.

## Key Novelty
Industry 5.0 AI systems can integrate automation hardware, digital autonomy (system flexibility and independence), environmental sustainability, and human involvement as mutually reinforcing rather than competing priorities.

## Contributions
- AI-driven hardware and tool chain development for manufacturing
- Methodologies addressing human aspects in industrial automation
- Bridge between academic research and practical industrial deployment
