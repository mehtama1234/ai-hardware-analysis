# isscc-2025-086: Efficient Vth-Tilting PUF

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
PUFs require stable process-mismatch entropy across PVT corners; conventional enrollment screening across multiple conditions increases test time/cost.

## Motivation
Secure key generation at scale needs efficient PUF designs working reliably with minimal overhead and test cost.

## Method
The device extracts a unique fingerprint from tiny manufacturing variations in how much voltage transistors need to turn on (threshold-voltage mismatches) in simple inverter circuits. It uses a technique called Vth-tilting — adjusting the measurement method for these threshold voltages — to streamline enrollment by testing only at standard operating conditions instead of across temperature and voltage extremes. The design adds three protections: power-gating (disabling power during idle to prevent aging), a balanced sampler (symmetrically measuring signals to resist eavesdropping), and laser-attack detection.

## Key Novelty
The core innovation is the Vth-tilting measurement scheme, which cuts enrollment test time by requiring screening only at standard voltage and temperature.

## Contributions
- 0.3127% BER at worst PVT
- KER <5.5×10^-20 with error correction
- Efficient Vth-tilting enrollment
- BTI mitigation and side-channel defense
