# isscc-2025-029: Ultra-Low-Power 32 kHz Crystal Oscillator

**A 0.36nW/0.9V 32kHz Crystal Oscillator Using Analog Regulation for Cross-Current Avoidance**

## Summary
Ultra-low-power 32 kHz crystal oscillator consuming 0.36 nW at 0.9 V supply via analog regulation eliminating inverter cross-currents, enabling direct battery operation without external regulation.

## Technical Approach
- **Regulation**: Analog-domain cross-current suppression in sustaining amplifier
- **Supply**: Direct 0.9–2 V battery operation without intermediate regulation
- **Power**: 0.36 nW ultra-low operation—10–100x improvement over pulsed-injection designs
- **Timing**: Time-domain regulation vs. voltage-domain switching

## Key Innovation
Analog regulation for cross-current avoidance enabling ultra-low-power XO operation directly from battery voltage, eliminating regulation-circuit overhead and complexity.

## Contributions
- Sub-0.5 nW power at 0.9 V supply
- Direct battery operation without external regulation
- 10–100x power improvement vs. pulsed-injection techniques
- Time-domain regulation methodology
