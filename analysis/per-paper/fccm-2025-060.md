# Ph.D. Project: Floorplan Quality Prediction for HLS Design Exploration on Multi-Die FPGA

**Problem:** Cross multi-die boundary delays and local congestion in HLS designs on multi-die FPGAs require iterative parameter tuning through Vitis to achieve better frequency performance, consuming significant time overhead.

**Method:** A graph neural network is trained to predict congestion (crowding of signals) and resource consumption for circuit layouts. Since predictions take milliseconds, designers can rapidly explore many layout options instead of iteratively running the slow Vitis design tool. This replaces manual parameter tuning with automated design space exploration.

**Novelty:** A graph neural network trained to predict floorplan quality eliminates the need for expensive iterative Vitis design runs.
