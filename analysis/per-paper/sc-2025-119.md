# Stability-preserving Lossy Compression for Large-scale Partial Differential Equations

**Venue:** SC | **ID:** sc-2025-119

## Summary
Stability-preserving lossy compression for PDE checkpoint/restart workflows, explicitly controlling physical invariants (kinetic/potential energy) to ensure numerical stability across multiple restart cycles.

## Problem & Motivation
Fault tolerance at exascale requires frequent checkpointing, but standard checkpoints consume enormous I/O bandwidth. Lossy compression reduces storage significantly but traditionally sacrifices physical fidelity. PDE systems with energy conservation requirements face divergence/oscillation risks from unconstrained compression losses.

## Key Findings
- **Write performance:** 8.4× improvement over baseline
- **Read performance:** 6.3× improvement
- **Numerical stability:** Relative L2 error ~2e-6 across multiple cycles
- **Energy preservation:** Explicit control maintains physical invariants
- **Scalability:** Validated on Frontier supercomputer at scale

## Hardware & Systems
- **Platform:** ORNL Frontier supercomputer
- **Scale:** Exascale HPC systems
- **Configuration:** Multi-node distributed-memory PDE simulation
- **I/O subsystem:** High-performance parallel I/O

## Techniques
- **Stability-preserving compression:** Energy perturbation control
- **Kinetic energy constraint:** Monitor and limit velocity field changes
- **Potential energy constraint:** Preserve configuration-dependent energy
- **Lossy algorithm:** Compression with fidelity guarantees
- **Multi-cycle validation:** Test across restart sequences
