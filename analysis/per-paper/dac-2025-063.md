# Centralized Training and Decentralized Control through the Actor-Critic Paradigm for Highly Optimized Multicores

**Venue:** DAC | **Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Distributed neural-network-based resource controllers suffer from conflicting control decisions and partial observability, significantly impairing learning and stability of control policies for multicore systems.

## Motivation
Dynamic voltage frequency scaling on multicores requires robust control that handles partial observability and coordinated resource allocation across cores, a challenge conventional distributed controllers fail to address.

## Method
A centralized neural-network critic evaluates global system behavior under temperature constraints while decentralized neural-network actors implement core-level DVFS decisions trained together via actor-critic reinforcement learning, resolving conflicting decisions and improving observability.

## Key Novelty
Centralized-training-decentralized-control actor-critic architecture for multicore DVFS, mitigating partial observability and decision conflicts.

## Contributions
- Novel actor-critic reinforcement learning framework for multicore DVFS control
- Centralized critic providing system-wide context to decentralized actors
- Average 20% and peak 34% performance improvements over state-of-the-art on real-world platform
- Improved stability of control policies under temperature constraints

## Metrics
- Performance: 20% average, 34% peak improvement

## Baselines
- State-of-the-art distributed neural-network resource controllers

## Limitations
Evaluation limited to DVFS without broader system-level integration.
