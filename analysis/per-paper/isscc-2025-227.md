# isscc-2025-227: Blocker-Tolerant Receiver with Multi-Level TAF

**Problem:** Crowded spectrum creates out-of-band (OOB) and alias-band blockers that degrade in-band EVM. Conventional time-approximation filtering has limited rejection and reconfigurability.

**Approach:** 28nm CMOS mixer-first receiver combining multilevel TAF (mTAF), VCO-based integrator with embedded digitalization, and N-path filtering with non-uniform sampling.

**Key novelty:** Multilevel TAF with VCO integrator achieving 65dB blocker rejection with high reconfigurability.

**Metrics:** -36/-31dB EVM with ±20/+24dBc blocker; 20dB improvement over prior designs.

**Tags:** blocker-tolerance, receiver, filtering, mmwave, 28nm, evm
