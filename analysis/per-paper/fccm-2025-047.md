# Reconfigurable Retina-Inspired Looming Detection

**Problem:** Detecting rapidly approaching objects and potential threats requires efficient real-time detection. Current systems lack specialized hardware optimized for retinal looming detection computation.

**Method:** A novel reconfigurable CMOS circuit is designed for looming detection based on retinal neuroscience principles. Performance is validated using real-world laboratory data and hardware-aware simulations. The circuit is implemented in GlobalFoundries' 22nm FD-SOI technology with measured energy efficiency of 0.36 pJ per looming spike for a 5×5 pixel kernel.

**Novelty:** A reconfigurable CMOS circuit specifically designed for retinal looming detection with in-sensor computation capabilities.
