# isscc-2025-225: Wideband mm-Wave Transceiver

**Problem:** Wideband mm-wave transceivers must simultaneously achieve low-noise wideband RX, high-efficiency wideband TX, and excellent TX/RX isolation. These conflicting requirements challenge conventional designs.

**Approach:** Single 65nm CMOS integration spanning 17.7-29.5GHz with co-designed RX (3.3dB NF), TX (20.2dBm OP1dB), and isolation circuits.

**Key novelty:** Unified wideband transceiver avoiding separate narrowband designs.

**Applications:** 5G, LEO SATCOM, JSAC.

**Tags:** mmwave, transceiver, 5g, satcom, wideband, cmos
