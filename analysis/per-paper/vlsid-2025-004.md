# AI-Driven Anomaly Detection in Oscilloscope Images for Post-Silicon Validation

**Venue:** VLSID
**Authors:** Kowshic A. Akash, Tobias Wulf, Torsten Valentin, Alexander Geist, Ulf Kulau, Sohan Lal

## Problem Statement
In the post-silicon validation process, different functionalities and boundaries of a system-on-chip (SoC) are tested, generating a large amount of data in the form of oscilloscope images, trace data, and log files

## Key Innovation
Novel first approach

## Method
The approach trains a Convolutional Autoencoder (CAE)—a neural network that learns to compress and reconstruct images—on real silicon test data from various post-silicon validation projects. During training, the autoencoder learns to replicate normal oscilloscope images seen during testing. When presented with new test images, the autoencoder attempts to reconstruct them; if the reconstruction significantly differs from the original, that difference indicates an anomaly requiring investigation.
