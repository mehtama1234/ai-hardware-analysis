# hpca-2025-033: ER-DCIM: Error-Resilient Digital CIM Architecture with Run-Time MAC-Cell Error Correction

**Venue:** HPCA  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Digital CIM SRAM cells are prone to errors at low-voltage (required for energy efficiency), but traditional SRAM ECC correcting single read-out rows is incompatible with DCIM's simultaneous multi-row MAC readout, leaving MAC-level errors uncorrected and degrading neural network accuracy.

## Motivation
Low-voltage operation is essential for DCIM energy efficiency, but voltage scaling increases SRAM error rates; uncorrected MAC errors substantially degrade inference accuracy, making reliable low-voltage DCIM unsolved.

## Method
ER-DCIM uses three complementary strategies to prevent errors during math operations in memory. First, it adds mathematical check-information (residue codes) to the computation that lets it catch and fix errors in the results as they happen—the first real-time error correction technique designed for DCIM. Second, it fixes defective SRAM cells gradually without interrupting the calculation. Third, it reuses the error-correction hardware that was built for low-voltage mode (where errors occur more easily) to boost performance at standard voltage instead, preventing wasted chip space.

## Key Novelty
Using residue codes to correct errors in multiply-accumulate results during DCIM computation, rather than at the SRAM cell level—the first real-time approach of this kind.

## Contributions
- Residue code-based real-time MAC-level error correction for DCIM (first of its kind).
- Progressive cell error correction avoiding computation stalls during SRAM repair.
- Mode switcher reconfiguring error-resilient logic for high-voltage throughput improvement.
- Accuracy maintained without loss in both low- and high-voltage modes.

## Metrics
- No accuracy degradation in low-voltage or high-voltage modes vs. baseline.
