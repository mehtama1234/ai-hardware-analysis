# dac-2025-400: Late Breaking Results: A Geometric Diffusion Model for Macro Placement Generation

**Problem:** Macro placement directly impacts circuit performance; traditional approaches struggle with connectivity preservation and placement constraints.

**Method:** MacroDiff uses diffusion-based generative modeling (an iterative refinement technique, similar to progressively denoising an image) to place large circuit blocks called macros. Instead of directly predicting where each macro should go, it first predicts the wirelength relationships—how far signals need to travel between components. This indirect representation automatically preserves how signals connect through the circuit, reduces the number of placement constraints, and naturally handles rotations and translations (different orientations and positions of the layout).

**Key Novelty:** Wirelength-based geometric representation in diffusion model for macro placement.

**Contributions:**
- Diffusion-based generative approach
- Wirelength-based connectivity preservation
- 91.6% overlap reduction
- 74.4% legalization displacement reduction, 7.0% HPWL improvement
