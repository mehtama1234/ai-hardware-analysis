# Battery-Free Distributed ML Edge Platform

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**ID:** isscc-2025-245

## Problem
Ultra-low-power edge sensors with ML must operate from 100nW harvested energy while supporting multimodal sensing and model adaptation. Current systems suffer from isolated monomodal operation and high NN accelerator power overhead.

## Solution
94.8nW battery-free platform integrating adaptive event-driven multimodal sensing with ultra-low-power NN accelerator, enabling distributed collaborative sensing with model adaptation for data distribution shifts.

## Key Innovation
Ultra-low-power distributed multimodal sensing with adaptive ML from 100nW energy harvesting.

## Results
- **Power consumption:** 94.8 nW (battery-free)
- **Operation:** Down to 100nW harvested energy
- **Capabilities:** Multimodal sensor fusion, adaptive inference, distributed collaboration

## Hardware Target
ASIC, SoC

## Techniques
Power efficiency, near-data processing, circuit design
