# isscc-2025-182: Zen 5 High-Performance x86-64 Microprocessor Core

**Problem**: x86 market demands high per-core performance and energy efficiency within fixed area budget while supporting diverse client/server/embedded workloads.

**Solution**: 55mm² 8-core CCX in TSMC 4nm with configurable FP256/FP512 SIMD paths, enhanced microarchitecture (branch prediction, I-cache prefetch, rename registers, TLB), and density-optimized Zen 5c variant.

**Key Results**:
- -16% IPC generational improvement
- 5.7GHz frequency
- 8.6B transistors per CCX
- 1MB L2 per core, 32MB shared L3

**Hardware**: CPU (8-core)  
**Workload**: General-purpose / desktop  
**Confidence**: low (abstract only)
