# ProtoCellLayout: Prototype-Guided Graph Learning for Accurate and Generalizable Standard Cell Layout PPA Estimation

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-134

## Problem
Standard cell layout PPA estimation requires rapid and precise methods; geometry-based approaches lack accuracy, SPICE simulations are slow, and ML methods don't capture layout-dependent effects and require massive training data.

## Motivation
Accelerate Design Technology Co-Optimization (DTCO) for advanced semiconductor process nodes.

## Method
The approach represents each standard cell as a Topology-Geometry Graph (TGG)—a combined representation capturing both the circuit's logical connections and its physical layout. Rather than training on massive datasets, it uses prototype learning: identifying recurring structural patterns in CMOS logic and common routing configurations, then reusing these patterns to predict power, performance, and area for new cells with minimal training data.

## Key Novelty
The core idea is representing circuits as Topology-Geometry Graphs that combine logic and layout, then using prototype learning to identify and reuse structural patterns across different cells.

## Contributions
- Topology-Geometry Graph (TGG) representation method integrating circuit and geometric features
- Prototype learning mechanism exploiting CMOS structural regularity
- Accurate PPA estimation from layout without excessive training data
