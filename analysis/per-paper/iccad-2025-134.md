# ProtoCellLayout: Prototype-Guided Graph Learning for Accurate and Generalizable Standard Cell Layout PPA Estimation

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-134

## Problem
Standard cell layout PPA estimation requires rapid and precise methods; geometry-based approaches lack accuracy, SPICE simulations are slow, and ML methods don't capture layout-dependent effects and require massive training data.

## Motivation
Accelerate Design Technology Co-Optimization (DTCO) for advanced semiconductor process nodes.

## Method
Topology-Geometry Graph (TGG) representation integrating circuit topology and geometric features; prototype learning mechanism leveraging CMOS logic structural regularity and routing pattern similarity for generalization across cells with minimal training data.

## Key Novelty
TGG representation capturing both topology and geometry; prototype learning for cross-cell generalization using structural regularity.

## Contributions
- Topology-Geometry Graph (TGG) representation method integrating circuit and geometric features
- Prototype learning mechanism exploiting CMOS structural regularity
- Accurate PPA estimation from layout without excessive training data

## Hardware Target
- ASIC

## Technique Categories
- physical-design
- EDA

## Metrics
- **Estimation Speedup:** <1 minute (vs hours for SPICE)
- **Percentage Error:** <3.5%
- **Layout Selection Accuracy:** >95%

## Baselines
- Geometry-based approaches
- SPICE simulations
- Prior ML methods

## Limitations
Evaluated only on industrial standard cell library; generalization to custom cells or different technologies unclear.
