# OmniFed: A Modular Framework for Configurable Federated Learning from Edge to HPC

**Paper ID:** sc-2025-361
**Venue:** SC
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Federated learning needs modular, configurable infrastructure supporting edge-to-HPC deployments with privacy

## Motivation
Heterogeneous FL systems require separation of concerns, mixed protocols, and privacy mechanisms in a unified framework

## Method
OmniFed: modular framework with topology/protocol config, pluggable privacy (DP/HE/SA), compression, multiple training algorithms

## Key Novelty
Configuration-driven modular FL framework supporting mixed protocols and pluggable privacy/compression across heterogeneous environments

## Contributions
- OmniFed modular FL framework
- Mixed topology and protocol support
- Pluggable privacy and compression
- Configuration-driven customization
