# OmniFed: A Modular Framework for Configurable Federated Learning from Edge to HPC

**Paper ID:** sc-2025-361
**Venue:** SC
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Federated learning needs modular, configurable infrastructure supporting edge-to-HPC deployments with privacy

## Motivation
Heterogeneous FL systems require separation of concerns, mixed protocols, and privacy mechanisms in a unified framework

## Method
OmniFed is a federated learning framework—where multiple computers train together while keeping data private—built from components you can mix and match. You configure how the computers connect (topology) and communicate (protocols), then select your privacy method: differential privacy (mathematically obscures individual data), homomorphic encryption (computes on encrypted numbers), or secure aggregation (combines results without revealing individual entries). The framework includes data compression to reduce network traffic and supports multiple training algorithms, working across edge devices and supercomputers.

## Key Novelty
OmniFed's core innovation is making federated learning modular—you can configure and swap different privacy protections, compression methods, and communication protocols rather than committing to a single fixed design.
