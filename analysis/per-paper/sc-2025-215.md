# sc-2025-215: Hybrid Quantum-HPC Cloud Workload Management Simulation

## Overview
Survey and simulation framework (HybridCloudSim) for workload management in hybrid quantum-HPC systems, addressing scheduling, resource allocation, and orchestration challenges.

## Key Problem
Hybrid quantum-HPC platforms require coordinating workflows across heterogeneous classical and quantum resources. Device variability, quantum noise, and distributed orchestration create novel challenges.

## Approach
1. Comprehensive survey of workload management in classical HPC, quantum clouds, and hybrid systems
2. Develop taxonomy categorizing approaches by: scheduling strategy, allocation model, orchestration, performance metrics
3. Implement HybridCloudSim simulation framework

## Contributions
- State-of-the-art survey of hybrid quantum-HPC workload management
- Taxonomy for categorizing management approaches
- HybridCloudSim: conceptual foundation and practical tool
- Framework for optimizing resource usage under realistic constraints

## Key Challenges Addressed
- Device variability (quantum and classical)
- Noise-prone quantum processors
- Synchronized orchestration across distributed systems

## Hardware Target
Quantum computers, HPC clusters, hybrid quantum-HPC platforms

## Confidence
Low (survey paper, limited specific experimental results in abstract)
