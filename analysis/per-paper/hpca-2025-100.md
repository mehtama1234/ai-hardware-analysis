# hpca-2025-100 — BOSS: Blocking algorithm for optimizing shuttling scheduling in Ion Trap

**Venue:** HPCA  
**Authors:** Xian Wu, Chenghong Zhu, Jingbo Wang, Xin Wang  
**Confidence:** low (abstract only)

## Problem
Shuttling operations in ion trap quantum computers cause significant fidelity loss and elongated execution times, especially at scale (4000+ two-qubit gates, 64-78 qubits).

## Motivation
Ion traps offer superior gate fidelity, coherence times, and connectivity, but shuttling overhead is a primary scalability bottleneck degrading both fidelity and throughput.

## Method
BOSS groups quantum operations (the instructions given to qubits) into blocks to minimize how many times ions need to be shuttled (moved) across the trap. By batching related operations together, the algorithm reduces the total shuttling overhead that would otherwise happen between every operation. It uses realistic experimental conditions, including sympathetic cooling (a technique where one cooled ion helps cool others nearby), to predict execution time and fidelity more accurately. Testing covers circuits with 4000+ two-qubit gates across 64-78 qubits.

## Key Novelty
BOSS cuts shuttling operations by up to 96.1% by grouping quantum operations based on which qubits they use, taking advantage of the fact that related operations often cluster on nearby qubits.

## Contributions
- BOSS blocking algorithm reducing shuttle operations by up to 96.1%
- Integration of sympathetic cooling parameters for realistic fidelity simulation
- Validation on circuits with up to 4000+ two-qubit gates and 64-78 qubits
