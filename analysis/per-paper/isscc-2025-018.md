# isscc-2025-018: Ultra-Low-Jitter Fractional-N Digital PLL

**A 65fsrms-Jitter and -272dB-FoMjitter, N 10.1GHz Fractional-N Digital PLL**

## Summary
Fractional-N DPLL achieving 65 fs RMS jitter at 10.1 GHz through quantization-error-compensating BBPD, eliminating thermal-noise overhead of conventional DTC-based approaches.

## Technical Approach
- **BBPD**: Bang-bang phase detector with built-in ΔΣ quantization error compensation
- **Calibration**: Orthogonal-polynomial LMS algorithm adapting across PVT variations
- **No DTC**: Eliminates DTC thermal noise by directly compensating ΔΣ Q-error

## Key Innovation
Direct quantization-error compensation in BBPD eliminates need for high-power DTC, achieving ultra-low jitter without DTC-induced thermal noise.

## Contributions
- Sub-100 fs jitter at 10.1 GHz
- Quantization-error-compensating BBPD architecture
- Orthogonal-polynomial LMS for robust PVT adaptation
- -272 dB FoMjitter demonstrating power-efficient design

## Target Workloads
High-speed frequency synthesis, wireline/wireless communications, timing recovery
