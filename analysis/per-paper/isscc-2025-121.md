# isscc-2025-121: Autonomous Lightweight Microactuator Driving System with Flying Solid-State Batteries

**Problem:** Small-scale flying electromechanical systems (microrobotics) require efficient high-voltage drive systems for electrostatic and piezoelectric actuators while meeting stringent weight (<1-2g) and power constraints for flight endurance (10s of minutes).

**Motivation:** Capacitive actuators demand reactive power (Cx V² F) in the 10s mW range; efficient energy management is critical for flight autonomy limited by micro-scale energy storage capacity.

**Method:** The system manages reactive energy delivery to capacitive loads by using flying solid-state batteries that enable integrated power conversion from low-voltage sources (3-5V) to high voltages (tens of volts), optimizing the voltage conversion ratio and reactive power efficiency.

**Key Novelty:** Flying solid-state battery integration for autonomous lightweight high-voltage power conversion in microrobotics applications.

**Contributions:**
- Lightweight autonomous microactuator driving system for flying systems
- Solid-state battery integration for high-voltage generation from low-voltage sources
- Efficient reactive power management for capacitive actuators
