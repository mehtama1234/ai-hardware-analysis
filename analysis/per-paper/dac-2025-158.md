# Quantum-Resistant Security: PQC Readiness and Research Challenges (Invited)

**Authors:** Ashish Kundu, Ramana Kompella  
**Venue:** DAC 2025

## Summary
This invited talk addresses post-quantum cryptography (PQC) readiness. It presents Quartz, a system for observability of quantum vulnerabilities in cryptography systems. Using ciphersuite dependency graphs and Cryptography Bill of Materials (CBOM), it maps quantum-unsafe cryptography usage across complex systems and supply chains.

## Key Contributions
- Quartz system for observability of quantum cryptographic vulnerabilities
- Ciphersuite dependency graphs for mapping cryptography usage and dependencies
- Cryptography Bill of Materials framework for supply-chain risk assessment
- Assessment methodology for PQC readiness across systems

## Technical Approach
Quartz provides observability into cryptographic suites by constructing dependency graphs showing how quantum-unsafe protocols flow through systems and supply chains. CBOM generation provides a structured view of risk exposure, enabling organizations to identify where migration to PQC standards (as announced by NIST) is most critical.
