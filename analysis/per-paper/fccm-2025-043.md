# Ph.D. Project: Holistic Partitioning and Optimization of CPU-FPGA Applications Through Source-to-Source Compilation

**Problem:** Hardware/software partitioning and High-level Synthesis (HLS) optimization are typically applied independently without leveraging their potential interplay. This leads to suboptimal offloading decisions that don't account for how partitioning choices affect optimization opportunities and vice versa.

**Method:** The approach uses an Extended Task Graph (ETG) representation of C/C++ applications automated through source-to-source compilation. It creates task clusters for FPGA offloading by simultaneously optimizing three objectives: maximizing applicable hardware optimizations within clusters, minimizing global communication costs between CPU and FPGA, and grouping tasks that share data within the same cluster.

**Novelty:** Unified holistic approach that combines hw/sw partitioning and HLS optimization as a single automated process through source-to-source compilation, enabling mutual influence between partitioning and optimization decisions.
