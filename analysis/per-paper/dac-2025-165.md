# BiNeuroRAM: Energy-Efficient ReRAM-Based PIM for Accurate Bipolar Spiking Neural Network Acceleration

**Problem:** ReRAM-based neuromorphic accelerators face high sensing power and accuracy degradation in SNN execution.

**Solution:** BiNeuroRAM combines ReRAM-PIM with spike-tracing bipolar neurons, low-power voltage sense amplifiers (LPVSA), and asynchronous control to eliminate power bottlenecks.

**Results:** 80.9% ImageNet accuracy (8.4% improvement), 14.7-58.2x lower read power, 2.08x throughput and 2.09x energy efficiency gains.

**Theme:** Neuromorphic acceleration via optimized memory sensing.
