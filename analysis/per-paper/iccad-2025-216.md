# Towards Multi-Objective Routing: A Novel Coreset-based Transfer Learning Framework

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-216  
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Routing optimization with multiple conflicting objectives (timing, power, wirelength) traditionally trades quality for optimization time.

## Motivation
Efficient multi-objective routing enables better design tradeoff exploration without excessive computation time.

## Method
A coreset is a small, representative subset of data that captures the key properties of a routing problem. The method combines this with transfer learning—reusing knowledge from previously solved routing problems to address new ones. Because routing problems often share common patterns, applying past solutions requires much less training data than solving each problem from scratch, while maintaining high-quality routes that balance competing objectives like timing (delay), power consumption, and wirelength (total length of connecting wires).

## Key Novelty
It combines coresets and transfer learning—sampling representative data and reusing prior solutions—to speed up circuit routing while optimizing multiple conflicting objectives.

## Contributions

1. Coreset-based transfer learning for routing
2. 5-8x training time reduction
3. Preserves routing quality across objectives
