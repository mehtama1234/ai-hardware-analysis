# ICCAD 2025-189: Connecting the D.O.T.S. - Design of Fluidic Circuit Boards for Multi-OoC Platforms

## Summary
This invited paper addresses standardization challenges in connecting multiple Organ-on-Chip (OoC) platforms. The authors present CAD tools and design methodologies for creating standardized microfluidic circuit board connectors, enabling reproducible and interoperable multi-OoC systems.

## Problem Statement
Current microfluidic chip integration approaches lack unified standards for connecting multiple OoC components. This fragmentation prevents seamless composition of complex, integrated biomedical systems and limits the scalability of multi-OoC platforms.

## Key Contributions
1. **CAD Tool Framework**: Automated design tools for standardized microfluidic circuit board connector specification
2. **Design Standards**: Unified specifications for multi-OoC platform connectivity
3. **Integration Methodology**: Systematic approach to combining multiple OoC components into coherent microfluidic systems
4. **Standardization Framework**: Enables reproducible and interoperable OoC platform design

## Hardware Target
- Microfluidic chips and circuits
- Organ-on-Chip (OoC) platforms
- Biomedical research hardware
- Multi-platform integrated systems

## Technical Approach
The work leverages design automation and CAD tools to establish standardized connector architectures for microfluidic circuit boards. This enables consistent design, fabrication, and integration of multiple OoC components.

## Significance
Standardization of microfluidic connectors addresses a critical bottleneck in translational biomedical research. By enabling modular composition and interoperability of OoC platforms, this work facilitates:
- Reproducible experimental systems
- Easier platform reuse and reconfiguration
- Accelerated development of complex multi-organ systems
- Improved collaboration across research groups
