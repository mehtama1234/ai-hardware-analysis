# isscc-2025-037: Crystal-Less Wake-Up Receiver

**Title:** A Crystal-Less BodyID with an Asynchronous Clockless Leakage-Powered Wake-Up Receiver and Over-the-Channel Clock Recovery

**Venue:** ISSCC

## Core Contribution
Battery-less wake-up receiver using asynchronous design and over-the-channel clock recovery, eliminating crystal oscillator and always-running clock.

## Key Metrics
- **Power Mode:** Leakage-powered idle
- **Clock Source:** Over-the-channel recovery (in-band)
- **Reference:** None (crystal-less)

## Problem
Prior ULP wake-up receivers require always-running clocks and external crystals, wasting power and requiring calibration references; battery-less nodes need sub-latency without external components.

## Method
Asynchronous clockless architecture deriving clock directly from incoming RF signal, powered by device leakage during idle.

## Confidence
Low (abstract-only analysis).
