# date-2025-025: Multi-Partner Project: Securing Future Edge-AI Processors in Practice (CONVOLVE)

## Problem
Edge-AI devices have limited computing and energy resources yet require high performance and security. Attackers with physical access can target cryptographic keys and neural network weights (IP).

## Method
CONVOLVE protects edge-AI devices by designing specialized hardware that guards against attackers with physical access—preventing them from stealing cryptographic keys (authentication and encryption secrets) or neural network weights (the trained AI model's intellectual property). The system treats security as a fundamental hardware design principle from the start, and carefully manages the device's limited power consumption and computing capacity.

## Contributions
- Holistic security-by-design framework for resource-constrained edge-AI
- Address cryptographic key and neural network weight protection
- Novel hardware technologies and integrated workflows

## Results
Project in intermediate stage; highlights security aspects including challenges encountered, pursued solutions, and early results for future edge-AI processor development.
