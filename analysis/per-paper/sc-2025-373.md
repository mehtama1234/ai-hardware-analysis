## Problem
Real-time transfer and analysis of high-throughput scientific data to remote facilities

## Motivation

## Method
The researchers tested the STZ compression framework by running it on scientific datasets and comparing compressed results to originals. They verified that STZ could process data in real time as it arrived, while retaining enough accuracy for analysis, confirming its usefulness for transferring large volumes of data to remote research facilities.

## Key Novelty
STZ processes scientific data as it flows, discarding less-important details to speed up transfer while keeping the measurements needed for research.
