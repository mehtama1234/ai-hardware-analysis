# iSEW: in-Sensor Embedded Watermarking for Secure Imaging

**Problem:** Digital images lack hardware-native tamper resistance and are vulnerable to post-capture modifications. Current watermarking approaches operate post-acquisition, adding computational overhead and remaining susceptible to removal attacks.

**Method:** The approach adds a watermark during the pixel-readout stage of a CMOS camera sensor, before the analog signal converts to digital (ADC conversion transforms the continuous signal into numbers). Instead of watermarking the finished image in software, the watermark is injected directly into the sensor's readout electronics, specifically the column amplifier circuit controlled by a secure pattern generator. Because the watermark is embedded at the hardware level during readout, before digitization, it becomes permanently mixed into every pixel's value without adding computational overhead, and cannot be separated or removed.

**Novelty:** The innovation is watermarking at the analog readout stage before digital conversion, directly built into the sensor hardware, creating a tamper-resistant mechanism.
