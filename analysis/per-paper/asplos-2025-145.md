# asplos-2025-145: Spindle: Efficient Distributed Training of Multi-Task Large Models via Wavefront Scheduling

**Venue:** ASPLOS  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Training multi-task, multi-modal (MT MM) foundation models is inefficient because their heterogeneous model components and diverse workloads across tasks and modalities create complex dependency patterns that existing parallelism strategies cannot jointly optimize.

## Motivation
Foundation models increasingly unify multiple tasks and modalities under a shared base with specialized components, causing severe resource under-utilization in standard training systems.

## Method
Spindle decomposes MT MM model execution into waves and applies heterogeneity-aware workload parallelization combined with dependency-driven wavefront scheduling to overlap execution across tasks and modalities.

## Key Novelty
Wavefront scheduling abstraction that jointly optimizes parallelization and dependency-driven scheduling for heterogeneous MT MM models.

## Contributions
- Wavefront scheduling formulation for heterogeneous MT MM wave decomposition
- Heterogeneity-aware workload parallelization strategy
- Dependency-driven execution scheduling overlapping waves across tasks
- Up to 71% speedup over state-of-the-art training systems
