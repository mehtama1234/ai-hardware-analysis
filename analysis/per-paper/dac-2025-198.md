# AutoClock: Automated Clock Management for Power-Efficient HLS Designs on FPGAs

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only analysis)

## Problem
FPGA HLS tools lack automated clock management; diverse clock resources and multi-domain complexity lead to inefficient power consumption.

## Method
AutoClock automatically manages clocks in FPGA designs by choosing which clock resources to use, assigning modules to separate clock domains (so different parts run at different speeds), and hierarchically gating clocks (turning them on and off in layers) to save power. It uses heuristics (practical rules of thumb) and constraint satisfaction (checking that all requirements are met) to find the best clock domain assignments and gating strategy, while preventing metastability issues (glitches from signals crossing between different clock speeds) and time-division multiplexing problems (when one clock signal is reused in complex ways).

## Key Novelty
Automatic optimization of clock domain assignments and hierarchical clock gating to prevent metastability glitches (from signals crossing between clock speeds) and time-division multiplexing problems (from reusing clocks in complex ways).

## Results
- **74.38% dynamic power reduction**
