# AutoClock: Automated Clock Management for Power-Efficient HLS Designs on FPGAs

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only analysis)

## Problem
FPGA HLS tools lack automated clock management; diverse clock resources and multi-domain complexity lead to inefficient power consumption.

## Method
AutoClock provides **automated clock resource allocation**, **module-to-domain assignment** (with metastability/TDM handling), and **hierarchical clock gating** to optimize the large design space.

## Key Novelty
Integrated multi-clock domain management for HLS that automatically addresses metastability and timing division multiplexing issues.

## Results
- **74.38% dynamic power reduction**
