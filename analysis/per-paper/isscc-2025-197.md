# ISSCC 2025-197: HOOP—Scalable Hybrid Converter Ring for HPC Power Delivery

## Core Contribution
Ring-topology DC-DC converter system with inherent auto-current-balancing across hundreds of parallel phases and phase-shedding capability, eliminating need for complex digital control in AI-era power delivery.

## Technical Approach
- **Power Ring (Φ0)**: Enables automatic inductor current balancing across converter phases
- **Back Ring (Φ0)**: Supports phase shedding for efficiency at part-load conditions
- **Hybrid Topology**: Extends charge-balance principles from single converters to parallel hybrid multi-phase arrays
- **Scalability**: Inherently scales to hundreds of phases without per-phase digital control

## Performance Metrics
- **Scalability**: Hundreds of parallel phases
- **Efficiency**: Improved via phase shedding at variable loads
- **Response**: Faster transient response to load changes
- **Complexity**: Reduces vs. digital current-balance controllers

## Baselines
- Conventional multi-phase converters requiring per-phase digital controllers
- Prior buck/three-level charge-balance (single converter only, not parallelizable)
- PLL-based current balancing approaches

## Hardware Impact
Addresses critical bottleneck in AI/HPC power delivery where tens to hundreds of point-of-load converters must balance current and respond to fast dynamic loads. Enables efficient, scalable power rails for next-generation accelerators.
