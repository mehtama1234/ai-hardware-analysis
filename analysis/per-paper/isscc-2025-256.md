# isscc-2025-256: Single-Stage Dual-Output Wireless Power Rectifier

**Title:** A 6.78MHz Single-Stage Regulating Rectifier with Dual Outputs Simultaneously Charged in a Half Cycle Achieving 92.2% Efficiency and 131mW Output Power

**Venue:** ISSCC 2025

**Problem:** Implanted biomedical devices require wireless power transfer with multiple independent outputs (low stable voltage for biopotential recording, high-bandwidth voltage for neurostimulation), but prior designs cascade separate stages with conflicting requirements.

**Key Innovation:** Single-stage dual-output rectifier topology with simultaneous per-half-cycle charging and independent regulation.

**Approach:** 6.78MHz switching frequency in a single regulating rectifier stage independently manages two output voltages: V_OUT2 (stable, low-noise recording) and V_OUT1 (high-bandwidth stimulation).

**Results:**
- **Efficiency:** 92.2% power conversion
- **Output Power:** 131mW
- **Integration:** Compact single-stage design vs. cascaded multi-stage

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only; detailed regulation specs not specified)
