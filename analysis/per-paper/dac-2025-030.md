# SAGA: A Memory-Efficient Accelerator for GANN Construction

**Problem:** Memory bottlenecks in graph-traversal-based Approximate Nearest Neighbor (GANN) construction, especially for dynamic graphs with high-dimensional vertices.

**Key Idea:** Exploit vertex feature similarity in dynamic graphs by clustering vertices from the same starting point and applying mixed-precision quantization on decomposed vertex deltas.

**Hardware:** ASIC accelerator with two-level scheduling and bit-serial mixed-precision execution unit.

**Results:** 9.30× speedup and 7.60× energy savings over NDSearch while maintaining accuracy.

**Theme:** Memory-efficient graph construction through similarity clustering and adaptive quantization.
