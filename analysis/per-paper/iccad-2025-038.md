# STMC: Small-Tile Multiple-Copy Compilation for Reliable Measurement-Based Quantum Computing

**ID:** iccad-2025-038  
**Venue:** ICCAD

## Problem
Measurement-based quantum computing (MBQC) on photonic architectures suffers from imperfect photon control, measurement, and fusion operations; fusion failure and photon loss cause expensive circuit re-execution with significant overhead.

## Motivation
MBQC offers advantages like long qubit decoherence and strong scalability, but high failure rates make it impractical without mitigation techniques.

## Method
STMC breaks quantum circuits into smaller pieces that map onto hardware tiles, then runs multiple copies of each piece simultaneously. When photons are lost or fusion operations fail (when quantum states are combined imperfectly), at least one parallel copy succeeds, avoiding the expensive cost of re-running the entire circuit.

## Key Novelty
Running multiple copies of small circuit pieces in parallel allows STMC to tolerate photon loss and fusion failures without re-running the entire circuit.

## Contributions
1. Fusion graph representation and tile-based partitioning strategy
2. Compact subgraph mapping for resource-constrained tiles
3. Parallel execution of multiple copies with finish-in-order semantics
4. 65.68× execution time speedup at 75% fusion success rate; 36.40× resource state reduction
