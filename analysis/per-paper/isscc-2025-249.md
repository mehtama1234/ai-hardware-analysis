# Spatial-Temporal Beamforming PA for mm-Wave Arrays

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**ID:** isscc-2025-249

## Problem
mm-Wave transmitters face degraded back-off efficiency under high-PAPR constellations (7-9.5dB). Conventional architectures address beamforming and power back-off separately—baseband beamforming increases complexity; RF beamforming suffers mismatch and insertion loss.

## Solution
Spatial-Temporal Beamforming Power Amplifier (ST-BPA) integrates direct-digital amplitude-and-phase beamforming with power back-off operation in unified mm-wave PA. Can be augmented with Doherty, envelope-tracking, or outphasing for further efficiency gains.

## Key Innovation
Direct-digital spatial-temporal PA beamforming unifying amplitude/phase control and power back-off without separate stages.

## Results
- **Frequency:** 24 GHz
- **Performance:** Enhanced back-off efficiency, combined beamforming/PBO

## Hardware Target
ASIC

## Techniques
Circuit design, power efficiency, dataflow
