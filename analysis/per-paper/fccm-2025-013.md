# Analog In-Memory Computing Enhanced FPGA for High-Throughput and Energy-Efficient Acceleration

**Problem:** FPGA routing fabric incurs high data movement overhead because large amounts of data must be shuttled between compute and memory blocks, limiting energy efficiency for AI workloads.

**Method:** The paper enhances FPGAs with in-memory computing macros based on non-volatile RRAM devices, specifically analog Dot Product Engines. Using the Verilog to Routing (VTR) framework, they simulate a novel 40 nm architecture and employ a custom event-driven simulator to evaluate performance.

**Novelty:** Integration of analog RRAM-based Dot Product Engines directly into FPGA fabric to eliminate data movement overhead through in-memory computing.
