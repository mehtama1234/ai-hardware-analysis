# isscc-2025-064

**Title:** 26.2 A Wideband Replicas-Rejection Digital Transmitter Using Joint-Digital-Analog Interpolation and Filtering in 28nm CMOS

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Problem:** Wideband digital transmitters struggle with sampling replicas near signal band (f_LO ± n×f_s) requiring high sampling rates and off-chip RF filters; multi-standard support forces separate LB/HB chains.

**Key Contribution:** Joint digital-analog interpolation/filtering within DPA suppresses replicas without external filters; unified architecture eliminates LB/HB chains.

**Method:** The design combines digital and analog filtering working together to eliminate unwanted signal copies that appear at the output of high-speed transmitters. A digital polyphase filter (multiple parallel signal paths that are individually filtered and then combined) reduces the internal data rate for each channel while keeping the final output speed high. Then analog filtering built directly into the power amplifier circuit (which amplifies the weak signal for broadcast) further removes these unwanted replicas. A single unified architecture handles both low-band and high-band standards without requiring separate circuits for each band, reducing chip area and complexity.

**Hardware:** ASIC

**Techniques:** Circuit design, signal processing, filter design

**Primary Theme:** Joint digital-analog DTX for wideband sub-6 GHz
