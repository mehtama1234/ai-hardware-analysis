# Multi-Objective Multi-Bit Flip-Flop Placement with Pre-Placed Cell Handling

**Venue:** DAC | **ID:** dac-2025-181 | **Type:** Late Breaking Results

## Summary
First comprehensive MBFF placement methodology addressing timing degradation from excessive displacement during clustering and legalization, especially with pre-placed cells. Uses force-based relocation, integrated clustering, and multi-objective optimization. Won 2024 CAD Contest at ICCAD.

## Key Results
- **Achievement:** Won 2024 CAD Contest at ICCAD on Power and Timing Optimization Using Multi-Bit Flip-Flops
- **Approach:** Force model + clustering + legalization + multi-objective candidate selection
- **Targets:** Timing, power, area, and bin utilization optimization

## Technique
Force model relocates flip-flops to reduce timing violations; clustering and legalization process minimizes displacement; multi-objective function selects MBFF candidates while balancing timing, power, area, and utilization.
