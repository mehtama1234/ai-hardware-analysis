# PROFUZZ: Directed Graybox Fuzzing via Module Selection and ATPG-Guided Seed Generation

**Authors:** Raghul Saravanan, Sudipta Paria, Aritra Dasgupta 0002, Swarup Bhunia, Sai Manoj P. D.

**Venue:** ICCAD

## Summary
PROFUZZ integrates ATPG with directed fuzzing for precise hardware verification, improving scalability and coverage through ATPG-guided seed generation.

## Key Contributions
- Integration of ATPG with directed graybox fuzzing for precise seed generation
- Improved scalability supporting large target regions
- 30× greater scalability in terms of handling target sites
- 11.66% higher coverage with 2.76× faster execution vs DirectFuzz
