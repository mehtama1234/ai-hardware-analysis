# ISSCC 2025-204: Ultra-Dense RibbonFET SRAM with PowerVia

## Core Contribution
Intel-18A RibbonFET 6T SRAM achieving record 38.1 Mb/mm^2 density (0.021μm^2 per cell) with improved V_MIN and backside power delivery (PowerVia), advancing memory scaling beyond FinFET limits.

## Technical Approach
- **Bitcell**: 6T SRAM in RibbonFET technology with flexible transistor-width tuning
- **Power Delivery**: PowerVia backside low-resistance metal layers reduce power droop and enable higher speed/lower V_MIN
- **Density Optimization**: Bitcell + peripheral circuit co-design; freed frontside routing via backside power enables compact peripheral logic
- **Assist Circuitry**: Reduced assist requirements; write assist provides 68mV V_MIN improvement vs. FinFET baseline

## Performance Metrics
- **Bitcell Area**: 0.021μm^2 (HDC configuration)
- **Density (HDC)**: 34.3 Mb/mm^2 with write assist; up to 38.1 Mb/mm^2 with larger bit array
- **Area Scaling vs. FinFET**: 0.77x (HCC), 0.88x (HDC)
- **V_MIN Improvement**: 68mV (90th percentile) vs. FinFET-based designs requiring dual assist
- **Technology Node**: Intel 18A (sub-2nm class)

## Baselines
- 7nm FinFET SRAM (larger bitcells, higher V_MIN, requiring more assist)
- Off-chip DRAM (lower density, higher latency)
- Prior RibbonFET designs without PowerVia (higher power)

## Hardware Impact
Critical for next-generation AI accelerators and HPC: enables massive on-chip cache/SRAM (hundreds of MB) at lower power and latency than DRAM. Backside power delivery paradigm allows future nodes to continue density scaling without signal routing congestion.
