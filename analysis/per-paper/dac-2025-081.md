# dac-2025-081: EVA: Generative Engine for Novel Analog Circuit Discovery

**Problem:** Discovering entirely new high-performance analog circuit topologies is underexplored; existing methods reuse known structures.

**Method:** EVA builds novel circuits by using an AI model called a decoder-only transformer that predicts where electronic components should connect, one connection at a time, starting from scratch. First, the model learns general patterns by studying thousands of existing circuit designs (pretraining). Then it's optimized using PPO and DPO—techniques that reward high-performing designs—to generate circuits tailored for specific performance goals.

**Key Novelty:** Transformer-based generative approach creating novel circuits from scratch with targeted optimization via PPO/DPO.

**Hardware Target:** analog, ASIC  
**Technique Category:** circuit-design  
**Workloads:** circuit-synthesis  

**Metrics:** validity, novelty, versatility, training-sample efficiency

**Confidence:** low (abstract only)
