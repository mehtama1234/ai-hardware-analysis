# DiffServe: Efficiently Serving Text-to-Image Diffusion Models with Query-Aware Model Scaling

**Problem:** High-quality diffusion models are 4.6-18x slower than lightweight variants, and query demand fluctuates; static resource provisioning wastes capacity or fails SLOs. Standard quality metrics (CLIP, PickScore) cannot identify "easy" queries for lightweight routing.

**Method:** Model cascade with two innovations: (1) EfficientNet-V2 discriminator trained on binary real/fake classification (ground-truth MSCOCO images as "real", model-generated as "fake") - confidence score routes queries: accepted by lightweight model if confidence > threshold, else deferred to heavyweight. (2) MILP resource allocation maximizes confidence threshold (quality proxy) subject to latency constraints (execution + Little's Law queuing delay) and throughput constraints, solving periodically (~10ms) off critical path. Variables: device counts (x1, x2), batch sizes (b1, b2), threshold t.

**Key novelty:** Real-image-trained EfficientNet discriminator repurposed for diffusion quality routing; MILP co-optimizing confidence threshold, batch sizes, and device allocation under dynamic demand.

**Metrics:**
- Up to 24% FID improvement vs static configurations
- 19-70% lower SLO violation rate vs static provisioning
- Up to 52x lower SLO violations vs Clipper-Heavy
- Up to 20% FID improvement vs Proteus (random routing)
