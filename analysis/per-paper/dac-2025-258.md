# LLMs: A Driving Force in Next Generation Digital Design Automation

**Venue:** DAC | **ID:** dac-2025-258

## Problem
Silicon design automation faces increasing complexity and scale, requiring new approaches to automate diverse design tasks efficiently.

## Key Innovation
Position paper exploring LLMs as transformative agents in silicon design automation, assessing technology readiness and proposing research directions.

## Method
This is a review paper surveying how large language models—AI systems trained on vast amounts of text—can help automate silicon chip design. The authors review existing applications, evaluate which chip design tasks LLMs are ready to tackle, and identify research gaps that must be closed to expand their use.

## Topics Covered
- Current LLM applications in design automation
- Technology readiness assessment
- Proposed applications and research directions
- Transformative potential of generative AI

## Contribution
Systematic exploration of LLM capabilities in chip design, providing roadmap for future research in LLM-driven design automation.

## Limitations
Position/review paper without empirical results; specific quantitative comparisons and benchmarks not provided.
