# TENSORMD: Accelerating Molecular Dynamics with MLIP

## Problem
General-purpose ML frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch) are not optimized for machine-learning interatomic potential (MLIP) workloads, leaving significant performance optimization opportunities on the table.

## Approach
Custom MLIP framework decoupled from existing ML tools to enable HPC-aware optimizations specific to molecular dynamics patterns.

## Key Results
- **1.88x speedup** over 2020 Gordon Bell Prize winner (TensorFlow-based MLIP) on NVIDIA A100
- Scales to **50+ billion atom simulations**
- Demonstrated on diverse supercomputer architectures

## Significance
Demonstrates the value of domain-specific frameworks for HPC+AI workloads. General ML tools sacrifice optimization opportunities by targeting broad use cases; specialized designs can capture 40-88% additional performance by aligning with specific computational patterns of scientific domains.

- Domain-specific acceleration
