# A 0.27-THz Frequency Multiplier Chain Using Harmonic Mixing with Multiplication of × 18 in 65-nm CMOS

**Paper ID:** vlsid-2025-065
**Venue:** VLSID
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Design of high-frequency terahertz multiplier chains requires optimization of harmonic content, rejection of unwanted frequencies, and achieving high output power at extreme frequencies.

## Motivation
THz frequency applications require compact, integrated multiplier chains with high harmonic rejection and efficient conversion gain.

## Method
The design chains frequency multipliers to reach ×18 total multiplication: two triplers (each ×3) and one doubler (×2). The first tripler uses a tail inductor—a coiled circuit element in the power supply—to amplify the 3rd harmonic (a naturally occurring higher-frequency component). The second tripler uses capacitive neutralization (adding capacitors to suppress specific frequencies) to eliminate the fundamental frequency while boosting the 135 GHz output. Buffer circuits between stages are optimized to amplify harmonics before passing the signal forward, and a final push-push doubler (using symmetric transistor pairs) efficiently multiplies by 2 to reach the 0.27 THz target.

## Key Novelty
The design uses two different tripler techniques (inductor-boosted harmonic amplification and capacitive fundamental rejection) with optimized harmonic buffering to efficiently achieve ×18 frequency multiplication at 0.27 THz.

## Contributions
- 8-phase 0.27 THz frequency multiplier chain with ×18 multiplication
- Harmonic rejection ratio > 30 dBc
- Peak conversion gain of 11.2 dB at -14 dBm input
- Saturated output power -0.93 dBm with -4 dBm input
- 134 mW DC power consumption from 1.2V supply
- 43 GHz bandwidth (247-290 GHz)
