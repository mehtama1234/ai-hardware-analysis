# Versatile Rewiring and Concurrent Resynthesis for High-Quality Customized Optimization

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-019

## Problem
Traditional logic synthesis algorithms reach saturation; need for substantial circuit structural improvements beyond standard methods.

## Motivation
High-quality circuit optimization requires exploring beyond conventional synthesis techniques.

## Method
Novel rewiring method using truth tables integrated with concurrent resynthesis framework; partitions circuits into support-limited subcircuits enabling rewiring at scale.

## Key Novelty
Integration of truth-table-based rewiring with concurrent resynthesis partitioning strategy for circuit optimization.

## Contributions
- Novel truth-table-based rewiring method for circuit restructuring
- Concurrent resynthesis framework with support-limited subcircuit partitioning
- Synergistic deployment of multiple synthesis algorithms
- Substantial improvements over strong baselines in node, transistor, and area metrics
