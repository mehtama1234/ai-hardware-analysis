# μEFI: A Microkernel-Style UEFI with Isolation and Transparency

**Problem:** Modern UEFI firmware lacks architectural isolation between drivers and components, creating security and reliability vulnerabilities. Monolithic UEFI design makes it difficult to reason about system behavior and enforce strong isolation guarantees.

**Method:** μEFI restructures UEFI as a microkernel architecture with isolated driver domains and a minimal trusted core. Drivers communicate through capability-based message passing rather than shared memory. Transparency is maintained through monitored cross-domain interactions and auditable firmware operations.

**Novelty:** Application of microkernel isolation principles to UEFI firmware to simultaneously achieve strong isolation boundaries and transparent system behavior.
