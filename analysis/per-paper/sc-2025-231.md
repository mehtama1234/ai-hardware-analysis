# Numerical Properties and Scalability of s-Step Preconditioned Conjugate Gradient Methods

**ID:** sc-2025-231
**Venue:** SC
**Confidence:** low

## Problem

s-step PCG reduces synchronization but has poor numerical properties; basis choice affects stability

## Motivation

Scalability vs accuracy trade-off; need stable s-step methods without sacrificing parallel efficiency

## Approach

Generalized s-step PCG supporting arbitrary basis types; comprehensive comparison of all s-step variants

## Key Novelty

sPCG algorithm with flexible basis selection; theoretical + experimental stability analysis

## Contributions

- Generalization of s-step PCG to arbitrary basis types

- Theoretical stability analysis across basis choices
- Improved speedup vs standard PCG
