# Linearization of Quadrature Digital Power Amplifiers by Neural Network of ULR_LSTM

**Venue:** DATE | **ID:** date-2025-378

## Problem
Digital power amplifiers require complex predistortion; state-of-the-art methods use iterative learning control (ILC) with high computational complexity and extensive training time.

## Motivation
ILC-based digital predistortion is slow to train and compute-intensive; faster alternatives are needed for practical RF/wireless systems.

## Approach
Unsupervised Learning Residual LSTM (ULR_LSTM) for behavioral modeling without ILC + inverse training for DPD derivation + 15-bit transformer-based quadrature DPA in 28nm CMOS with Class-G and IQ-cell-sharing.

## Key Innovation
First unsupervised LSTM approach for digital predistortion, eliminating iterative learning control entirely.

## Results
- Error Vector Magnitude: -40.4dB for 802.11ax 40MHz 64QAM
- No iterative training required

## Target Hardware
ASIC (28nm CMOS RF PA)

## Techniques
Circuit design, approximation (neural DPD)

## Workload
RF/wireless (802.11ax modulation)
