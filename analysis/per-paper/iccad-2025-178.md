# Enhancing Timing Closure via Spatially Embedded Graph Transformer with Low Power/Area Overhead

**Authors:** Joonyoung Seo, Jonghyeon Nam, Howoo Jang, Yoonseok Jung, Seokhyeong Kang

**Venue:** ICCAD

## Summary
SEGT applies graph transformers with spatial embeddings to enable routing-aware timing prediction, enabling proactive timing closure with reduced design overhead.

## Key Contributions
- Spatially embedded graph transformer for accurate timing prediction
- Distance-aware self-attention capturing non-adjacent node interactions
- R2 score improvement: 0.915→0.959 (post-routing delay)
- Reduction in total negative slack with lower power/area overhead
