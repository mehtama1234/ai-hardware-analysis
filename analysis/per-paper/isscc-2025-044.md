# isscc-2025-044: Capacitive Burst-Energy Power Management

**Title:** A 91.25% Peak Power-Conversion-Efficiency Capacitive Power-Management IC Supporting up to 5.68mJ Burst Energy Delivery Using a Single External Capacitor for mm-Scale IoT Applications

**Venue:** ISSCC

## Core Contribution
Capacitive power-management IC achieving 91.25% efficiency delivering up to 5.68mJ burst energy from single external capacitor via stepped-down switched-capacitor converter.

## Key Metrics
- **Efficiency:** 91.25%
- **Burst Energy (22μF MLCC):** 146μJ (35x improvement)
- **Burst Energy (220μF Tantalum):** 5.68mJ (40x improvement)
- **External Capacitor:** Single (vs. multiple on-chip banks)

## Problem
mm-scale IoT batteries have high internal resistance limiting burst power; conventional storage either extracts ~2% (large caps) or provides limited density (on-chip capacitors).

## Method
High-voltage pre-charging of single external capacitor with on-chip step-up/-down switched-capacitor converter.

## Confidence
Low (abstract-only analysis).
