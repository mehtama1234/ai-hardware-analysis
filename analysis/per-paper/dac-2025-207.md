# Guarder: A Stable and Lightweight Reconfigurable RRAM-based PIM Accelerator for DNN IP Protection

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only analysis)

## Problem
RRAM PIM suffers from weight imprecision due to programming stochasticity and cannot effectively protect DNN intellectual property.

## Method
**3T2R cells** enable reliable MACs; **reconfigurable inverter voltages** encode encryption keys in RRAM without reprogramming. **Contrastive training** ensures authorized-vs-unauthorized performance degradation.

## Key Novelty
Co-designed RRAM PIM with in-device key encoding and contrastive training for DNN IP protection without external cryptography overhead.

## Results
- **1.41× area reduction** vs traditional RRAM accelerators
- **2.28× energy reduction**
- Performance gap maintained between authorized/unauthorized chips
