# Guarder: A Stable and Lightweight Reconfigurable RRAM-based PIM Accelerator for DNN IP Protection

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only analysis)

## Problem
RRAM PIM suffers from weight imprecision due to programming stochasticity and cannot effectively protect DNN intellectual property.

## Method
Guarder jointly designs hardware and software to protect neural network models in RRAM (resistive memory). The hardware uses 3T2R cells (3 transistors and 2 resistors each) to reliably perform the multiply-accumulate operations neural networks need, and encodes the encryption key into the memory by tuning inverter voltages to set specific resistance levels. The software applies contrastive training (teaching the model to distinguish strongly between authorized and unauthorized cases) to make the model operate accurately only on authorized chips and poorly on unauthorized ones.

## Key Novelty
Guarder's core idea is encoding encryption keys in 3T2R RRAM cells via tuned inverter voltages, combined with contrastive training to make models work accurately only on authorized hardware.

## Results
- **1.41× area reduction** vs traditional RRAM accelerators
- **2.28× energy reduction**
- Performance gap maintained between authorized/unauthorized chips
