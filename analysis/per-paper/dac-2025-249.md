# dac-2025-249: CXL-INTERPLAY: Unraveling and Characterizing CXL Interference in Modern Computer Systems

## Summary
First real-hardware characterization of CXL interference with memory/storage systems using performance counters and kernel-level reverse reasoning, plus proposed mitigation strategies.

## Key Idea
Systematically measure and analyze CXL performance degradation due to interference with conventional memory/storage on real hardware.

## Contributions
- First real-hardware CXL characterization
- Performance counter reverse-reasoning analysis
- Interference pattern identification
- Mitigation solutions proposed

## Limitations
Limited to specific CXL hardware configuration; generalization not addressed.
