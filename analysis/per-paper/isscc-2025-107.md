# isscc-2025-107: Personalized 35-Keyword KWS with On-Chip Training

**Problem:** Existing KWS systems lack user-specific trainability and support only 10 keywords, resulting in poor accuracy for accented speakers.

**Method:** The system uses a tiny AI model (a convolutional neural network, or CNN) small enough to fit on a single chip. It learns directly from each person's voice on-chip—not on external servers—which enables personalization while saving power. To minimize power consumption to 13.5 microwatts, it uses two techniques: a simplified approach to identifying important sound patterns (called feature extraction) using sine waves and approximate logarithms, and quantization (using lower-precision numbers to reduce power and space needs). This enables accurate recognition of 35 keywords, even with accented speakers, while adapting to each user's unique speech patterns.

**Key Novelty:** On-chip personalized training with sine-based approximate convolution enabling multi-keyword speech spotting across accents.

**Main Results:** 92.2% accuracy on 35 keywords (9 accents), 10.93µW inference, 13.46µW training.

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only analysis)
