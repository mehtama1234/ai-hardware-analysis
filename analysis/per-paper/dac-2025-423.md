# dac-2025-423: Embedding-Level Knowledge Distillation for Transferability

**Problem:** Data-free knowledge distillation methods focus on image-level performance without addressing transferability to downstream tasks.

**Motivation:** DFKD avoids relying on training data, but poor representation transferability limits deployment to new domains.

**Method:** Category-Aware Embedding DFKD operates at embedding level (vs image level) with efficient generator training paradigm to improve representation quality.

**Key Novelty:** Embedding-level distillation prioritizing representation transferability over image-level metrics.

**Contributions:**
- Category-aware embedding formulation for DFKD
- Efficient generator training reducing overhead
- Competitive DFKD performance with improved downstream transferability

**Workloads:** CNN, vision

**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
