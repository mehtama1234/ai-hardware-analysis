# Fast Reference-Less CDR with SAR-Based FA

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**ID:** isscc-2025-250

## Problem
Reference-less CDR circuits for burst-traffic systems have slow frequency acquisition, which is the primary bottleneck for link wake-up latency. Dynamic data-center traffic requires frequent sleep-to-wake transitions with minimal interface delay.

## Solution
Reference-less CDR employs successive-approximation-register (SAR) based frequency band-switching achieving constant 55ns band-search time. Positive-net-current charge pump enables rapid frequency convergence; total frequency acquisition <150ns.

## Key Innovation
SAR-based constant-time frequency-band switching for reference-less CDR with 4× faster FA than prior work.

## Results
- **Band-search time:** 55 ns constant
- **Total FA time:** <150 ns (4× faster)
- **Acquisition speed:** Up to 63.64 Gb/s/µs

ASIC
