# PFASware: Quantifying the Environmental Impact of Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances (PFAS) in Computing Systems

**Venue**: DATE  
**Primary Theme**: PFAS reduction in computing  

## Problem
Computing systems manufacturing uses PFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl forever chemicals) extensively. Need to quantify environmental impact and identify design optimizations to reduce PFAS use.

## Method
Quantify PFAS volume in IC designs across process nodes (7nm, 16nm, 28nm) and lithography methods (EUV vs DUV immersion). Compare PFAS footprint across design choices.

## Key Novelty
Quantifying PFAS environmental footprint in IC design and demonstrating optimization via lithography/node selection.

## Contributions
- Quantified PFAS volume for IC designs at multiple technology nodes
- 20% PFAS reduction achievable with EUV vs DUV at 7nm
- 15% PFAS reduction at 16nm vs 28nm
- Design guidance for PFAS reduction
