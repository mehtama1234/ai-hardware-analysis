# SC-2025-161: LLM-Based HPC Job Scheduling

## Summary
Studies LLM-based reasoning (ReAct framework) for multiobjective HPC job scheduling, comparing against traditional heuristics (FCFS, SJF) and optimization solvers (Google OR-Tools).

## Problem
- Traditional schedulers use static single-objective heuristics
- Cannot simultaneously optimize: makespan, wait time, utilization, fairness
- Lack adaptability to dynamic/bursty workloads
- No interpretable decision-making

## Solution: ReAct-Based Scheduler
Uses LLM with reasoning framework:
1. **Scratchpad memory**: Track scheduling history
2. **Natural-language feedback**: Refine decisions iteratively
3. **Constraint module**: Ensure feasibility and safety
4. **Multi-objective reasoning**: Balance competing goals

## Evaluation Setup
**LLMs tested**:
- OpenAI O4-Mini
- Anthropic Claude 3.7

**Scenarios** (7 real-world patterns):
- Heterogeneous job mix
- Bursty arrivals
- Adversarial cases

**Scale**: 10-100 job instances

## Results
| Aspect | Outcome |
|--------|---------|
| **Multi-objective balance** | Effective |
| **Constraint satisfaction** | Excellent |
| **Interpretability** | Strong (natural language) |
| **Workload adaptation** | Good (no training needed) |
| **Speed** | **Limitation** (overhead) |

## Comparison
- Outperforms FCFS, SJF in multi-objective scenarios
- Comparable or better than OR-Tools (optimization solver)
- Transparent reasoning (human-readable) vs black-box optimization

## Limitations
- Reasoning overhead challenges real-time deployment
- Tested on 10-100 jobs (production clusters: 1000s+)
- Latency not discussed

## Implications
- LLMs promising for complex scheduling decisions
- Interpretability valuable for HPC operators
- Computational efficiency needs improvement for production
