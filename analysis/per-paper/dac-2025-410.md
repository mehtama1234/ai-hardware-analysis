# ABC-FHE: A Resource-Efficient Accelerator for Client-Side Fully Homomorphic Encryption

**Problem:** Client-side FHE operations require 10,000x more computation than plaintext; bootstrappable parameters with large encoding/decryption operations create a computational bottleneck.

**Method:** ABC-FHE organizes computation as a stream (continuous flow through the chip) with a customizable Fourier transformation engine—the mathematical core of encryption—that can switch between NTT (Number-Theoretic Transform) and FFT (Fast Fourier Transform) depending on what's most efficient. Rather than fetching random numbers and transformation helper values from slow off-chip memory, it generates them directly on the chip, which is far faster. The design also schedules operations carefully to speed up CKKS encryption calculations on the client side.

**Key Result:** 1112x speedup (encoding/encryption) vs CPU, 214x vs SOTA; 963x (decoding/decryption) vs CPU, 82x vs SOTA. 28.638 mm² @ 28nm, 5.654W.

**Hardware Target:** ASIC  
**Workloads:** cryptography  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
