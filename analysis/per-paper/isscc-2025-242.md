# Compact Asymmetric Doherty mm-Wave FEM

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**ID:** isscc-2025-242

## Problem
Mm-wave FEMs must achieve high TX efficiency under modulated signals and low RX noise while remaining compact. Conventional Class-AB PAs deliver <10% PAE under high-PAPR OFDM waveforms.

## Solution
Asymmetric Doherty PA architecture provides high TX efficiency through dynamic bias and impedance modulation, integrated with LNA and T/R switch in 0.22mm² footprint for 24-29GHz.

## Key Innovation
Asymmetric Doherty PA achieving high PAE under OFDM while maintaining compact FEM integration.

## Results
- **Area:** 0.22 mm²
- **Frequency:** 24-29 GHz
- **Efficiency:** Improved PAE vs. conventional Class-AB

## Hardware Target
ASIC

## Techniques
Circuit design, power efficiency
