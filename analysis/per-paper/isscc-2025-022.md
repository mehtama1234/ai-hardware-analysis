# isscc-2025-022: High-Speed PAM-4 DSP Transceiver for AI Interconnects

**A 212.5Gb/s DSP-Based PAM-4 Transceiver with 50dB Loss Compensation for Large AI System Interconnects**

## Summary
212.5 Gb/s PAM-4 transceiver in 4nm FinFET achieving 50+ dB channel loss compensation through integrated high-bandwidth analog frontend and DSP-based FFE/MLSD, enabling efficient AI system networking.

## Technical Approach
- **Analog frontend**: High-bandwidth, low-noise design for 2x baud-rate signal handling
- **Equalization**: Extended FFE with floating reflection-cancellation taps
- **Detection**: MLSD receiver DSP for robust data recovery through severe loss
- **Channel**: 2.5e-6 BER through 50+ dB loss

## Key Innovation
Integrated analog-frontend bandwidth enhancement with aggressive DSP equalization enabling practical 212.5 Gb/s links in packaged AI systems with severe loss.

## Contributions
- Extended FFE with floating reflection taps
- MLSD receiver for severe-loss channels
- High-bandwidth analog frontend for PAM-4
- Practical 50+ dB loss demonstration

AI interconnects, HPC systems, data-center networking
