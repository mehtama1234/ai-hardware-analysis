# isscc-2025-228: High-Voltage Power Transmission for Humanoid Robots

**Problem:** Humanoid robots consume most power in distributed limbs/actuators while battery sits in torso. High-current transmission through thick cables causes large I²R losses and adds weight.

**Approach:** Bi-directional dual-path boost-48V-buck hybrid converter stepping voltage up for efficient transmission and down for actuator control.

**Key novelty:** High-voltage transmission architecture for distributed robotic actuators enabling weight reduction.

**Applications:** Light-weight humanoid robot power distribution.

**Tags:** power-delivery, robotics, converter, high-voltage, distributed, efficiency
