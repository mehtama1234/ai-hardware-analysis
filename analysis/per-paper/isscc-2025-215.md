# isscc-2025-215: Integrated Channel Analyzer for Signal Integrity

## Quick Summary
A low-power 4-port integrated channel analyzer (ICA) ASIC in 28nm CMOS achieving DC-to-40GHz signal-integrity measurement using TDR/TDT, consuming only 99.5mW per port.

## Problem
High-speed data links in dense Ethernet switches suffer from:
- Reflection, crosstalk, insertion loss
- Traditional VNAs and sampling oscilloscopes are power-hungry, expensive, limited port count
- Need for low-cost, integrated SI measurement in data-center switches

## Method
The analyzer uses two pulse-based measurement techniques: time-domain reflectometry (sending a pulse down a wire and measuring echoes to detect impedance mismatches) and time-domain transmission (measuring the signal that reaches the far end) to measure signal degradation on high-speed electrical connections. Both techniques are integrated into a single application-specific integrated circuit (ASIC) using 28-nanometer CMOS process technology, allowing four measurement ports to fit on one device. The system covers frequencies from DC (direct current) to 40GHz with a 9.1 picosecond response time, consuming only 99.5 milliwatts per port.

## Key Novelty
A single application-specific integrated circuit (ASIC) with four measurement ports characterizes signal degradation on high-speed data links from DC to 40GHz while consuming only 99.5 milliwatts per port.

## Contributions
- 4-port channel analyzer in 28nm CMOS
- DC-to-40GHz frequency coverage
- 9.1ps rise time (fine temporal resolution)
- 99.5mW/port (vs. power-hungry VNAs)
