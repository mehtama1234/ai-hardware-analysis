# isscc-2025-215: Integrated Channel Analyzer for Signal Integrity

## Quick Summary
A low-power 4-port integrated channel analyzer (ICA) ASIC in 28nm CMOS achieving DC-to-40GHz signal-integrity measurement using TDR/TDT, consuming only 99.5mW per port.

## Problem
High-speed data links in dense Ethernet switches suffer from:
- Reflection, crosstalk, insertion loss
- Traditional VNAs and sampling oscilloscopes are power-hungry, expensive, limited port count
- Need for low-cost, integrated SI measurement in data-center switches

## Method
ASIC-based channel analyzer using time-domain reflectometry (TDR) and time-domain transmission (TDT) techniques. On-chip circuits enable rapid SI measurements across 4 ports without external test equipment, integrated directly in switch/processor.

## Key Novelty
Multi-port integrated ICA ASIC achieving wide bandwidth with ultra-low per-port power consumption.

## Contributions
- 4-port channel analyzer in 28nm CMOS
- DC-to-40GHz frequency coverage
- 9.1ps rise time (fine temporal resolution)
- 99.5mW/port (vs. power-hungry VNAs)
