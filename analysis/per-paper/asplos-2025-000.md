# asplos-2025-000: Has Machine Learning for Systems Reached an Inflection Point?

**Venue:** ASPLOS  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
It is unclear whether ML-for-systems research has reached a tipping point analogous to AlexNet in 2012, where ML transitions from one of many competing approaches to the dominant methodology.

## Motivation
Prior ML-for-systems work has shown isolated wins but has not achieved the comprehensive, field-wide dominance that ML achieved in computer vision and NLP, raising the question of whether the field is now at a structural turning point.

## Method
Position/vision paper surveying ML-for-systems trajectories across sub-domains (scheduling, compilers, storage, networking), comparing adoption curves to known ML inflection points in other fields and evaluating maturation indicators such as production deployments and benchmark standardization.

## Key Novelty
Frames ML-for-systems progress as an inflection-point analysis, drawing a structured parallel to AlexNet's transformation of computer vision.

## Contributions
- Systematic survey of ML-for-systems research trajectories across multiple systems sub-domains.
- Identification of indicators distinguishing pre- and post-inflection-point phases in ML adoption.
- Analysis of remaining gaps (data, benchmarks, deployment pipelines) that may delay a full inflection point.
