# asplos-2025-090 — Generalizing Reuse Patterns for Efficient DNN on Microcontrollers

**Venue:** ASPLOS  
**Confidence:** low (abstract only)  
**Primary Theme:** Generalized computation reuse patterns for DNN inference on microcontrollers

## Problem
DNN inference on microcontrollers is limited by their restricted compute and memory, and existing computation reuse techniques that exploit redundancy in input/activation data are constrained by a narrow definition of reuse patterns, leaving a large portion of potential reuse unexploited.

## Motivation
Microcontrollers are the primary compute substrate for ultra-low-power edge AI, and even small improvements in DNN inference efficiency—through better exploitation of input redundancy—can be decisive for deployability.

## Method
The work introduces generalized reuse, which frames computation reuse in terms of row and column reordering of a matrix view of DNN input or activation maps. By recognizing that different reorderings expose different reuse patterns, the approach dramatically expands the explorable reuse space beyond what prior narrow pattern definitions allow. Two analytical models are developed: one predicts the accuracy impact of a given reuse pattern (approximate computation), and one predicts latency on the target microcontroller. These models enable efficient search over the generalized reuse space to select patterns that best trade off accuracy and speed for a given DNN and hardware.

## Key Novelty
Framing DNN computation reuse as a matrix row/column reordering problem to reveal a vastly larger reuse pattern space, coupled with dual analytical models for accuracy and latency prediction on microcontrollers.

## Contributions
- Concept of generalized reuse patterns via matrix row/column reordering of DNN inputs/activations
- Analytical model for predicting accuracy impact of generalized reuse patterns
- Analytical model for predicting inference latency on microcontrollers under a given reuse pattern
- 1.03-2.2x speedup or 1-8% accuracy improvement over conventional reuse on diverse DNNs and MCUs
