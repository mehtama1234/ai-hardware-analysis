# isscc-2025-213: Ultra-Fast Burst-Mode Clock and Data Recovery

## Quick Summary
A 60Gb/s burst-mode CDR with cross-injection-locking and flash phase detector achieving 0.13ns reconfiguration time, enabling 50% throughput increase in rapidly reconfigurable photonic switch networks.

## Problem
Photonic switches operating at ns timescales demand BM-CDR with sub-ns reconfiguration. Prior injection-locking approaches suffer from poor recovered-clock jitter at higher data rates, limiting throughput.

## Method
Cross-injection-locking technique that suppresses jitter in the recovered clock by using dual-path locking mechanism. Flash phase detector (PD) extracts phase information between data and sampling clock within 2UI window, enabling rapid frequency/phase acquisition in 0.13ns.

## Key Novelty
Cross-injection-locking that reduces jitter vulnerability of injection-locked oscillators while maintaining fast acquisition.

## Contributions
- 60Gb/s NRZ burst-mode CDR
- 0.13ns reconfiguration time (50× improvement)
- Jitter suppression via cross-injection-locking
- Flash phase detector for rapid acquisition
