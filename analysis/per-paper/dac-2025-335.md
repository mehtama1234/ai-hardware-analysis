# TetrisLock: Quantum Circuit Split Compilation with Interlocking Patterns
**Venue:** DAC

## Problem
Untrusted quantum compilers can steal circuit intellectual property (IP) and reverse-engineer proprietary quantum algorithms.

## Motivation
As quantum computing becomes practical, protection against compiler-level IP theft is critical for competitive quantum algorithm developers.

## Method
TetrisLock splits quantum circuits into two interdependent segments with interlocking patterns; original functionality only recoverable by combining both segments; neither segment alone reveals the complete circuit structure; eliminates need for trusted compiler on random circuits.

## Key Novelty
Interlocking split compilation pattern that prevents circuit reconstruction from individual segments.

## Contributions
1. Quantum circuit obfuscation against untrusted compilers
2. Defense against collusion attacks with mismatched qubit counts
3. Minimal circuit depth overhead (<1% accuracy loss)
