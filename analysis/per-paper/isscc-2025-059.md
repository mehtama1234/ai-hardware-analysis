# isscc-2025-059: 6-Phase Hybrid-Multi-Phase DC-DC Converter

**Title:** 21.7 Merging Hybrid and Multi-Phase Topologies: A 6-Phase Triple-Step-Down DC-DC Converter Achieving up to a 60:1 Voltage Conversion Ratio and 868A/cm3 Current Density

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Core Problem:**
Point-of-load converters must deliver >1A at extreme step-down (12V→<1V); single-phase struggles, hybrid scales poorly, conventional multi-phase requires large inductors.

**Method:**
6-phase triple-step-down merges hybrid flying-capacitor benefits with multi-phase inductor scaling; multiple compact inductors replace large single inductors or proliferating flying capacitors.

**Key Innovation:**
Hybrid-multi-phase topology balancing inductive distribution and flying-capacitor efficiency for high current density.

**Metrics:**
- **Phases:** 6
- **VCR:** 60:1 (12V input)
- **Current Density:** 868 A/cm³
- **Output Current:** >1 A

**Architecture:** 6-phase triple-step-down hybrid-multi-phase DC-DC

**Techniques:** Power conversion, circuit design

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)
