# BurstEngine: An Efficient Distributed Framework for Training Transformers on Extremely Long Sequences

**ID:** sc-2025-334  
**Venue:** SC 2025  
**Authors:** Ao Sun, Weilin Zhao, Xu Han, Cheng Yang, Zhiyuan Liu, Chuan Shi, Maosong Sun

## Problem & Motivation

Training LLMs on ultra-long sequences (>1M tokens) faces a fundamental bottleneck: **Tensor Parallelism and Context Parallelism suffer low Model FLOPs Utilization** as sequence length and GPU count increase. Communication overhead dominates computation.

## Solution: BurstEngine

Multi-faceted optimization framework:

### 1. **BurstAttention**
- **Topology-aware ring communication** leveraging network topology for optimal bandwidth usage
- **Fine-grained communication-computation overlap** — overlap allreduce with attention ops
- **Lower communication cost than RingAttention**

### 2. **Memory & Computation Optimizations**
- Sequence-level selective checkpointing (sparse gradient checkpoint)
- Language modeling head fused with loss function
- Workload balancing for various attention masking patterns

## Results

- **1.2x speedup** over SOTA baselines (Context Parallelism, RingAttention)
- **Much lower memory overhead** than baselines
- Scales to 1M+ token sequences efficiently

## Key Insight

By combining topology-aware communication patterns with aggressive overlapping and smart checkpointing, **distributed training efficiency is dramatically improved** without requiring novel hardware.

## Implications

Enables practical training of very long-context transformers on modern GPU clusters, addressing the sequence length scalability wall.
