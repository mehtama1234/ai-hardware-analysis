# Mako: Speculative Distributed Transactions with Geo-Replication

**Problem:** Distributed transactions across geographically replicated systems face high latency from synchronous coordination needed to maintain consistency. Traditional approaches require waiting for acknowledgment from multiple geo-distributed replicas before transaction commitment.

**Method:** The system uses speculative execution where transactions tentatively execute at replica sites before full quorum acknowledgment, reducing round-trip latency. Consistency is maintained through validation and abort protocols that handle incorrect speculation. Different strategies apply to read-only versus update transactions, with coordination mechanisms ensuring correctness.

**Novelty:** Speculative execution of distributed transactions in geo-replicated systems that reduces latency while maintaining strong consistency guarantees.
