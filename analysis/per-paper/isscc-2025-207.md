# ISSCC 2025-207: GaN Watt-Level 220GHz Power Amplifier

## Core Contribution
GaN HEMT solid-state power amplifier (SSPA) operating at 216–226GHz with watt-level output power, multiband impedance correction, and circuit-package co-design for wireless/imaging/radar.

## Technical Approach
- **Device**: GaN HEMT (gallium nitride high-electron-mobility transistor)
- **Frequency Range**: 216–226GHz (10GHz bandwidth, G-band)
- **Output Power**: Watt-level (higher than Si/III-V alternatives at this frequency)
- **Impedance Matching**: Multiband large-signal correction adapting to frequency variation and device nonlinearity
- **Integration**: Circuit-package co-design managing parasitic coupling, thermal distribution, and PCB integration
- **Voltage/Current**: Higher bias than Si CMOS/SiGe, enabling higher power density

## Performance Metrics
- **Frequency**: 216–226GHz (10GHz fractional bandwidth)
- **Output Power**: Watt-level (specific power, PAE not in abstract)
- **Technology**: GaN HEMT

## Baselines
- Si CMOS PAs (low voltage ~1V, limited power, 200–250GHz max)
- Si SiGe PAs (similar voltage/power ceiling)
- III-V GaAs/InP PAs (higher fT but still voltage-constrained)

## Hardware Impact
Enables compact 6G transceivers, high-resolution imaging radar, and integrated terahertz spectroscopy systems. GaN's voltage capability allows practical watt-level power at G-band frequencies, reducing overall transmit architecture complexity.
