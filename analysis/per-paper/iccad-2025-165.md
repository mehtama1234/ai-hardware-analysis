# BARQ: Boundary-Aware Regularized Training for Accurate Inference on Computing-in-Memory Accelerators with Low-Precision A/D Conversion

**Authors:** Tingrui Ren, Bi Wang 0002, Liang Wang, Yuanfu Zhao

**Venue:** ICCAD

## Summary
BARQ addresses the challenge of maintaining inference accuracy in CIM accelerators that employ low-precision ADCs. The technique uses boundary-aware quantization-aware training with ADC bit-width constraints and Euclidean projection-based weight initialization.

## Key Contributions
- ADC bit-width boundary constraints for regulated weight quantization during training
- Euclidean projection-based weight initialization for reduced initial quantization error
- Up to 75% energy reduction and 3.79× hardware efficiency improvement through enhanced sparsity
- Minimal 0.59% accuracy loss on CIFAR-10/100/ImageNet even with 3-bit ADCs
