# An Experimental Demonstration of Neuronal Somatic Behavior Using 2D SnS Memristive Switching Characteristics and its Equivalent Circuit for Spiking Neural Network

**Venue:** VLSID
**Authors:** Anshul Awasthi, Soumi Saha, Parikshit Sahatiya, Surya Shankar Dan

## Problem Statement
This report presents the fabrication and characterization of a 2D SnS memristor, proposing its integrate and fire (I&F) model as a potential hardware implementation of neuronal somatic behavior

## Key Innovation
Novel propose approach

## Method
They fabricated a two-dimensional tin sulfide (SnS) memristor, a device whose electrical resistance changes based on the history of voltages applied to it. The memristor was designed to follow an integrate-and-fire model: it accumulates electrical charge over time until reaching a threshold, then rapidly fires an electrical spike, just like a neuron's cell body (soma) does. Testing showed the memristor's electrical behavior matches experimentally observed neuronal somatic responses. This creates a hardware component that can be used directly in neuromorphic circuits (computing systems that mimic biological neural networks).
