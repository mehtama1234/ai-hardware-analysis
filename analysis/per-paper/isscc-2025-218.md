# isscc-2025-218: High-Efficiency Low-EMI Isolated Converter

## Quick Summary
A 180MHz, 45.3%-peak-efficiency isolated DC-DC converter using Q-downsize Class-D power amplifier with inherent shoot-through blocking, achieving robustness to frequency misalignments and low EMI.

## Problem
Isolated converters suffer from:
- High electromagnetic interference (EMI) from common-mode current across isolation barrier
- Suppressing CM voltage variation requires complex, fully symmetrical topologies
- Dead-time insertion for shoot-through protection adds complexity

## Method
Q-downsize Class-D architecture that:
1. Inherently blocks shoot-through current without dead-time logic
2. Uses fully symmetrical transmitter (TX) topology to minimize CM voltage variation
3. Opposite-direction coil routing cancels magnetic field
4. Achieves 45.3% peak efficiency while tolerating frequency misalignments

## Key Novelty
Q-downsize Class-D with inherent shoot-through blocking, maintaining efficiency across circuit parameter variations.

## Contributions
- 45.3% peak efficiency
- 180MHz switching frequency
- Inherent shoot-through protection
- Robustness to frequency misalignment
- Low EMI via symmetrical design
