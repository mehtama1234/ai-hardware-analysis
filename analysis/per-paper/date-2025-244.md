# Low-Latency Digital Feedback for Stochastic Quantum Calibration Using Cryogenic CMOS

**Venue**: DATE  
**Primary Theme**: Cryogenic ASIC control for quantum gate calibration  

## Problem
Quantum gate calibration requires low-latency feedback between quantum systems and classical control hardware to track drifting parameters; implementing this feedback without on-board compute (FPGA/ASIC) is impossible.

## Method
The team evaluates a stochastic calibration protocol—which adjusts quantum gate parameters based on measurement results—to understand how feedback delays affect performance. They synthesize the required classical control logic and simulate it on 28nm CMOS technology, then extend simulations to 14nm FinFET and cryogenic operating temperatures. These simulations demonstrate the protocol is practical and characterize its resource and performance requirements.

## Key Novelty
Quantum gate calibration can run inside standard computer chips without specialized control hardware, while keeping feedback fast enough to track quantum system drift.

## Contributions
- Analyzed single-shot stochastic calibration protocol performance under timing constraints from experimental quantum systems
- ASIC synthesis at 28nm and 14nm nodes with characterization at both room and cryogenic temperatures
- Shows protocol achievable with low power/resource utilization on commercial process nodes
- Enables rapid tuning and drift control toward fault-tolerant quantum computation
