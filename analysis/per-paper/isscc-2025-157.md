# isscc-2025-157: Ultra-Low-Power Biopotential AFE with C_GND Compensation

**Problem:** Two-electrode biopotential recording (EEG/ECG) systems suffer >100V_PP CMI from 50/60Hz mains coupling via parasitic C_Body and C_GND. Charge-pump CMI rejection limited to ~20V_PP and adds noise; prior CM-averaging ignores C_GND.

**Motivation:** Two-electrode systems attractive for cost/UX; requires high-CMRR without noise or power overhead.

**Method:** The amplifier uses a ground-isolated circuit that explicitly accounts for interference entering through two paths: parasitic coupling to the body and parasitic coupling to the ground. Instead of ignoring the ground-coupling path (as traditional designs do), this circuit lets the interference split between both paths, which allows it to tolerate extremely large interference signals of 133 volts peak-to-peak. The result is excellent interference rejection with a common-mode rejection ratio of 102dB, achieved at ultra-low power consumption of 4.6 microwatts with a noise efficiency factor of 3.3.

**Key Novelty:** High-CMRR AFE with explicit C_GND parasitic compensation for ground-isolated recording.

**Contributions:**
- 102dB CMRR
- 133V_PP CMI tolerance
- 4.6μW power
- 3.3 NEF
