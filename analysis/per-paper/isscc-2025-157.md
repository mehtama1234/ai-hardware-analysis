# isscc-2025-157: Ultra-Low-Power Biopotential AFE with C_GND Compensation

**Problem:** Two-electrode biopotential recording (EEG/ECG) systems suffer >100V_PP CMI from 50/60Hz mains coupling via parasitic C_Body and C_GND. Charge-pump CMI rejection limited to ~20V_PP and adds noise; prior CM-averaging ignores C_GND.

**Motivation:** Two-electrode systems attractive for cost/UX; requires high-CMRR without noise or power overhead.

**Method:** AFE explicitly compensating for parasitic ground capacitance (C_GND) in ground-isolated systems. Maintains high input impedance while rejecting >100V_PP CMI. Ultra-low-power (4.6μW) design achieves 102dB CMRR and 3.3 NEF.

**Key Novelty:** High-CMRR AFE with explicit C_GND parasitic compensation for ground-isolated recording.

**Contributions:**
- 102dB CMRR
- 133V_PP CMI tolerance
- 4.6μW power
- 3.3 NEF

**Hardware Target:** ASIC

**Technique Categories:** circuit-design, analog, low-power

**Metrics:** 102dB CMRR, 133VPP tolerance, 4.6μW, 3.3 NEF

**Baselines:** Charge-pump CMI cancellation, CM-averaging, 3-electrode systems
