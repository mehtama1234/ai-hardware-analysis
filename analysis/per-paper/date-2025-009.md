# date-2025-009: WinAcc: Window-based Acceleration of Neural Networks Using Block Floating Point

## Problem
Deep Neural Networks require efficient computation and energy usage, but frequent floating-point accumulations between vectors create significant overhead. State-of-the-art grouped summation approaches increase register complexity and logic overhead, limiting energy gains.

## Method
WinAcc leverages the observation that DNN data follows a Laplace-like distribution. This enables a customized narrow dynamic-range data format for a low-cost accumulator with narrow shifters and adders, dramatically reducing floating-point overhead.

## Contributions
- 33.95% energy reduction across seven representative DNNs
- 9.5% area reduction while maintaining model performance
- Algorithm and architecture co-design approach

## Results
Compared with state-of-the-art Bucket architecture, WinAcc achieves 33.95% energy reduction and 9.5% area reduction across seven representative DNNs.
