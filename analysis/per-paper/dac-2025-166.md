# Logic Restructuring with Preserved Logic Blocks

**Problem:** Traditional logic optimization decomposes complex cells that should be preserved for technology mapping, harming final PPA.

**Solution:** Transparent-boxing preserves complex cell structures during optimization via wire-based resubstitution while leveraging their internal logic.

**Results:** 4% additional network reduction, 1.8% timing improvement vs. black-boxing.

**Theme:** Logic synthesis with cell-aware optimization.
