# Quantitative Cost Model and Cost Optimization Methods for Multi-Technology-Node Architecture

**ID:** iccad-2025-253

## Problem
Multi-chiplet systems spanning different technology nodes lack accurate cost models. Design optimization for cost across heterogeneous nodes is challenging.

## Motivation
Accurate cost modeling enables cost-optimized design of multi-chiplet systems, improving product competitiveness and manufacturing margins.

## Method
Quantitative cost model for multi-chiplet architectures across different technology nodes. Cost optimization strategies considering packaging, yield, and power. Achieves 18-31% cost savings via optimization.

## Key Novelty
Comprehensive cost model for multi-technology-node chiplet systems with optimization methods.
