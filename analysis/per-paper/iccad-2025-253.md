# Quantitative Cost Model and Cost Optimization Methods for Multi-Technology-Node Architecture

**ID:** iccad-2025-253

## Problem
Multi-chiplet systems spanning different technology nodes lack accurate cost models. Design optimization for cost across heterogeneous nodes is challenging.

## Motivation
Accurate cost modeling enables cost-optimized design of multi-chiplet systems, improving product competitiveness and manufacturing margins.

## Method
The approach builds a detailed cost model for multi-chiplet systems where different chiplets use different manufacturing processes (called technology nodes), accounting for packaging (assembling chips together), yield (manufacturing success rates), and power consumption. It then uses this model to optimize system designs by exploring different arrangements and tradeoffs between these costs. By systematically searching for the best design choices, this optimization achieves 18-31% cost savings compared to baseline configurations.

## Key Novelty
The work models the total cost of multi-chiplet systems using different manufacturing technologies, accounting for packaging, yield, and power, enabling cost-optimized designs.
