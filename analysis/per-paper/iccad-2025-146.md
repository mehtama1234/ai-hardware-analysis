# Simulation Framework and Optimization of Superconducting Transmon-Tunable Coupler-Transmon System for Qudit Gates

**Venue:** ICCAD
**ID:** iccad-2025-146
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Superconducting quantum processors are limited by low qubit counts and high gate errors; extending to qudits offers larger state space but requires optimization for qutrit-based systems.

## Motivation
Optimizing qudit-based quantum processors can improve gate fidelities and move toward fault-tolerant quantum computing.

## Method
An integrated simulation framework predicts cross-Kerr interactions in TTT systems and fidelities of two-qutrit CPhase gates under noise. Control-device co-optimization identifies conditions maximizing target frequency in adiabatic schemes to achieve higher gate fidelity.

## Key Novelty
Simulation framework for predicting and optimizing qutrit gate fidelities in superconducting TTT systems.

## Contributions
- Integrated simulation framework for TTT cross-Kerr interactions
- Noise-aware qutrit CPhase gate fidelity prediction
- Control-device co-optimization methodology
- Identification of conditions for maximum adiabatic target frequency
