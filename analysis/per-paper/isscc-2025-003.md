# isscc-2025-003: Differential Series-Resonance CMOS VCO

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Ultra-low phase noise VCOs for wireless/wireline communications face a trade-off: multicore VCOs achieve low phase noise but incur area, mismatch, and design penalties. Series-resonance VCOs are promising but not yet demonstrated in pure CMOS.

## Approach
Differential **series-resonance CMOS VCO** with novel **pole-convergence technique:**
- Avoids multicore complexity via SR topology
- Differential architecture improves noise performance
- Pure CMOS implementation

## Key Contribution
Pole-convergence enables first differential SR-VCO in pure CMOS, achieving 202.1 dBc/Hz FoMTA at 10MHz offset without multicore overhead.

## Performance
- Phase noise FoM: 202.1 dBc/Hz at 10MHz offset
- Reduced area vs. multicore approaches
- Minimal inter-core mismatch
