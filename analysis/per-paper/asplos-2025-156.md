# asplos-2025-156: Design and Operation of Shared Machine Learning Clusters on Campus

**Venue:** ASPLOS  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Campus GPU clusters must be shared fairly among diverse users, but existing solutions target large industrial settings and don't address academic constraints: limited staffing, heterogeneous hardware, and fair multi-user allocation.

## Motivation
Universities are investing heavily in GPU clusters; ineffective sharing causes poor utilization and inequitable access without a full-stack campus-tailored solution.

## Method
The system stacks four independent layers: automatic hardware configuration and monitoring, fair GPU allocation across users, a user interface for job submission with access controls, and automated incident detection and logging. By automating routine maintenance tasks and scheduling jobs intelligently based on their computational needs, the design keeps the cluster running efficiently with minimal staff. This approach was built specifically for campus environments rather than adapting industrial cluster management systems.

## Key Novelty
A four-layer cluster management system designed specifically for campus environments—with limited staff and mixed hardware—rather than adapted from industrial systems.
