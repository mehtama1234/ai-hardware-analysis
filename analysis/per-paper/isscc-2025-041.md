# isscc-2025-041: PVT-Robust Cascaded RO-DPLL

**Title:** A PVT-Robust 5.5GHz Fractional-N Cascaded RO-Based Digital PLL with Voltage-Domain Feedforward Noise Cancellation

**Venue:** ISSCC

## Core Contribution
Cascaded RO-DPLL achieving 290.8fs rms jitter at 5.5GHz with <7.5% PVT deviation via voltage-domain feedforward noise cancellation.

## Key Metrics
- **Output Frequency:** 5.5GHz
- **Jitter:** 290.8fs rms
- **Fractional Spurs:** -62dBc
- **PVT Deviation:** <7.5%

## Problem
RO-DPLLs suffer from high phase noise and PVT sensitivity; prior PN-suppression techniques require expensive calibration or are energy-inefficient.

## Method
TVC/ADC-based PVT-robust Q-noise quantization combined with voltage-domain feedforward noise cancellation.

Low (abstract-only analysis).
