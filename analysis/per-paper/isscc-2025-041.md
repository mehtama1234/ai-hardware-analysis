# isscc-2025-041: PVT-Robust Cascaded RO-DPLL

**Title:** A PVT-Robust 5.5GHz Fractional-N Cascaded RO-Based Digital PLL with Voltage-Domain Feedforward Noise Cancellation

**Venue:** ISSCC

## Core Contribution
Cascaded RO-DPLL achieving 290.8fs rms jitter at 5.5GHz with <7.5% PVT deviation via voltage-domain feedforward noise cancellation.

## Key Metrics
- **Output Frequency:** 5.5GHz
- **Jitter:** 290.8fs rms
- **Fractional Spurs:** -62dBc
- **PVT Deviation:** <7.5%

## Problem
RO-DPLLs suffer from high phase noise and PVT sensitivity; prior PN-suppression techniques require expensive calibration or are energy-inefficient.

## Method
The design cascades ring oscillators in a fractional-N digital phase-locked loop—a circuit that synchronizes to frequencies by using flexible dividers instead of fixed integer ones. It converts time delays to voltages and digitizes them to suppress quantization noise while remaining reliable across manufacturing processes, voltages, and temperatures. A voltage-domain feedforward noise cancellation technique (measuring and subtracting noise before it accumulates) eliminates the need for complex gain calibration. This achieves 290.8 femtoseconds of jitter at 5.5GHz, with less than 7.5% variation across PVT conditions.
