# Revised Microscaling Format Quantization and Accelerator for LLMs

## Problem
Microscaling (MX) format offers algorithmic benefits as FP32 replacement but lacks practical hardware support; deployment still relies on traditional low-bit quantization, limiting MX adoption at scale.

## Method
Algorithm-hardware co-design with two components: (1) RMFQ introduces Revised MX (RMX) format with novel group direction quantization strategy; (2) RMFA implements RMX-adaptive hardware architecture and encoding scheme optimized for low-bit operations.

## Key Innovation
Revised MX format with group direction strategy and dedicated hardware enables practical 4-bit and 6-bit MX quantization at scale.

## Contributions
- Revised MX format with novel group direction strategy
- RMFQ framework achieving state-of-the-art 4-bit and 6-bit quantization
- RMFA hardware with RMX-adaptive architecture and encoding
- 1.28x speedup and 1.31x energy reduction vs outlier-aware accelerators

## Motivation
MX format lacks practical hardware support; co-design enables deployment benefits.
