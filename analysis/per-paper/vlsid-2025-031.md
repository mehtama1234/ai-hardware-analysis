# VLSID 2025-031: TIPAngle - Traffic Camera Pose Estimation

## Summary
Lightweight Siamese neural network for estimating camera pan/tilt angles from traffic camera frames. Achieves 18.45x speedup and 3x accuracy improvement over ResNet-18, running at 17 Hz on Raspberry Pi.

## Problem
Pan-and-tilt traffic cameras often lack angle sensors. Accurate camera pose estimation is essential for robust cross-camera vehicle tracking when operator changes camera orientation.

## Method
A Siamese neural network (two identical networks that compare image pairs to detect differences) learns to estimate camera pan and tilt angles. The inference runs on Raspberry Pi's CPU at ~17 frames per second through optimization, avoiding the need for external hardware.

## Key Results
- **18.45x speedup** over ResNet-18
- **3x accuracy improvement** in angle prediction
- **57ms per frame** on Raspberry Pi CPU
- **17 Hz throughput** on embedded device
- Real-time capable on CPU-only devices

## Applications
- City-scale traffic monitoring
- Smart traffic light systems
- Vehicle tracking across pan-tilt cameras
- Edge-based video analytics

## Limitations
- Evaluation focused on angle estimation, not full tracking pipeline
- Limited to Raspberry Pi platform
- Single deployment target
- No comparison with other lightweight pose estimation methods

## Hardware Targets
- Embedded edge devices (Raspberry Pi, ARM SoCs)
- Smart city infrastructure
- IoT video cameras
