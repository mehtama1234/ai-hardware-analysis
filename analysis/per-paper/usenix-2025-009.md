# Opening Up Kernel-Bypass TCP Stacks

**Problem:** Kernel-bypass TCP stacks with different design choices for throughput and latency have never been systematically compared across representative workloads, making it difficult to understand performance trade-offs when handling both bulk data transfers over multi-hundred gigabit Ethernet and small request-response messages requiring low latency.

**Method:** The authors benchmarked six kernel-bypass and in-kernel TCP stack implementations against representative workloads spanning bulk data transfers and small request-response messages. They measured throughput and latency characteristics to expose performance trade-offs and generality limitations across different workload patterns.

**Novelty:** First systematic comparative study of kernel-bypass TCP stacks across diverse representative workloads revealing fundamental design trade-offs and lack of generality in existing implementations.
