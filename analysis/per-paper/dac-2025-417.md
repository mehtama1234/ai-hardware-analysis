# AutoPower: Automated Few-Shot Architecture-Level Power Modeling

**Problem:** Traditional power models are inaccurate; ML-based models require extensive training data from known configurations, unrealistic for early-stage design.

**Method:** AutoPower splits a chip's power consumption into independent groups (like the clock, SRAM memory, and logic circuits) and trains a separate machine learning model for each group, based on how that group's power correlates with measurable structural features of the chip design. It then subdivides each power group further into smaller sub-models. This multi-level breakdown lets the models learn accurate power predictions from just a few training examples, rather than requiring extensive data.

**Key Result:** 4.36% MAPE, 0.96 R^2 with only 2 known configurations; 5% lower MAPE than McPAT-Calib.

**Hardware Target:** CPU  
**Workloads:** database  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
