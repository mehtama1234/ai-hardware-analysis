# SC25 Paper Analysis: sc-2025-104

## Million-Atom Ab Initio Electron Dynamics: Discontinuous Galerkin Real-Time Time-Dependent Density Functional Theory

### Summary
This work extends real-time TDDFT from thousands to millions of atoms by using discontinuous Galerkin adaptive local basis, tensor compression, and optimized kernels. Achieving 34.8% peak efficiency on 524,288 Sunway cores, it opens new possibilities for studying ultrafast electron dynamics in large-scale materials and nanophotonic devices.

### Key Problem
- **Scalability Bottleneck**: rt-TDDFT previously limited to thousands of atoms
- **Communication Overhead**: Global communication in traditional approaches scales poorly
- **Computational Cost**: Hybrid functionals require expensive multi-center integral evaluation
- **Application Gap**: Many materials require millions of atoms for accurate simulation

### Technical Approach
1. **Discontinuous Galerkin Basis**: Adaptive local basis reducing global communication
2. **Tensor Compression**: Leverages basis locality to avoid redundant multi-center integral computation
3. **Fused Gemm-Reduce Kernel**: Optimized projection operation with high floating-point efficiency
4. **Hybrid Functional Support**: Enables both local-semi-local and hybrid functionals

### Key Innovations
- **Basis Locality Exploitation**: Reduces inter-process communication through local basis design
- **Tensor Compression**: Eliminates redundant integral evaluation, reducing computation
- **Fused Operations**: Gemm-Reduce kernel achieves several times higher efficiency than standard BLAS

### Contributions
1. Discontinuous Galerkin rt-TDDFT formulation
2. Communication-reduced algorithm design
3. Tensor compression technique
4. High-efficiency fused kernel implementation
5. Million-atom simulation capability
6. Two orders of magnitude scale improvement
7. Support for advanced functionals

### Hardware & Workloads
- **Target Hardware**: New Sunway supercomputer (524,288 CGs)
- **Workloads**: Real-time TDDFT simulations, ultrafast electron dynamics, materials simulation

### Performance Results
- **Scale**: Million-atom systems (vs thousands previously)
- **Peak Efficiency**: 34.8% of theoretical peak on Sunway
- **Kernel Performance**: Several times higher than standard BLAS
- **Improvement Factor**: 100x scale increase in system size

### Applications
- Ultrafast electron dynamics in materials
- Nanophotonic device simulation
- Light-matter interaction studies
- Advanced materials characterization

### Scientific Impact
- Enables study of large-scale materials not possible before
- Supports hybrid functional accuracy for millions of atoms
- Opens new research directions in ultrafast physics
