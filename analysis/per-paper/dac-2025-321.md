# SuperFast: Fast Supernet Training Using Initial Knowledge

## Problem
Once-for-all NAS supernet training requires 72-83+ GPU days, preventing practical multi-target deployment.

## Solution
Two-stage training: pretrain a small subnet, then distribute its knowledge across the supernet. Reverses conventional largest-model warmup; propagates knowledge upward more efficiently.

## Impact
1.4x speedup on ElasticViT, 1.8x on NASViT at baseline accuracy; 4.0pp accuracy gain at 20-50ms latency on mobile.
