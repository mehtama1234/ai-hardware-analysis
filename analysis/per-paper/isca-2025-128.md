# SwitchQNet: Optimizing Distributed Quantum Computing for Quantum Data Centers with Switch Networks

**Venue:** ISCA  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
In quantum data centers (QDCs), quantum processor units (QPUs) in different racks are interconnected via reconfigurable optical switch networks, and cross-rack quantum communications incur both long latency and dynamic switch reconfiguration delays, significantly increasing total circuit execution time and degrading quantum state fidelity.

## Motivation
Near-term distributed quantum computing requires scaling beyond single QPU sizes, but the latency overhead of inter-rack optical switching is a critical bottleneck that compounds decoherence and reduces the effective circuit depth achievable with fault-tolerant quantum error correction.

## Method
SwitchQNet introduces a compiler that co-optimizes quantum communication scheduling at both the program layer (gate decomposition and qubit routing) and the network layer (optical switch reconfiguration scheduling). The compiler models the QDC network topology and switch reconfiguration latencies, then constructs a joint schedule that batches and reorders quantum gate communications to amortize switch reconfiguration overhead across multiple operations. The approach integrates with quantum error correction (QEC) to target fault-tolerant quantum computing (FTQC). The implementation is open-sourced.

## Key Novelty
A cross-layer compiler that jointly optimizes quantum gate scheduling and optical switch reconfiguration in quantum data centers, reducing end-to-end latency 8.02x over prior approaches by amortizing switch setup costs.

## Contributions
- Cross-layer compiler co-optimizing quantum program communication scheduling and optical switch network reconfiguration scheduling in QDCs.
- 8.02x reduction in overall distributed quantum circuit latency compared to prior approaches with small compilation overhead.
- Integration with quantum error correction (QEC) to support fault-tolerant quantum computing in switch-networked QDCs.
- Open-source release of the compiler implementation.
