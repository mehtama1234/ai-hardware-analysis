# asplos-2025-150: Frugal: Efficient and Economic Embedding Model Training with Commodity GPUs

**Venue:** ASPLOS  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Embedding model training on commodity GPUs suffers poor performance from sparse all-to-all communication bottlenecks due to limited interconnect bandwidth and lack of PCIe P2P support.

## Motivation
Commodity GPUs are cost-efficient for embedding models' low compute demands but their communication deficiency makes training impractical without optimization.

## Method
Frugal addresses communication bottlenecks by: (1) spreading embedding tables (lookup tables the model needs during training) across GPUs in a pattern that minimizes lookups between GPUs, (2) compressing or bundling embedding gradients (the training signals sent back across the network) to reduce bandwidth demand, and (3) scheduling work so computation and data transfer overlap, hiding the delay from network waits. Since commodity GPUs lack direct GPU-to-GPU connections, the system routes all communication through the CPU's host memory instead.

## Key Novelty
The system co-designs embedding table partitioning, gradient communication optimization, and compute-communication overlap to fit commodity GPUs' specific communication limitations.

## Contributions
- Communication-aware embedding table partitioning for commodity GPUs
- Gradient communication optimization without PCIe P2P
- Compute-communication overlap scheduling for embedding training
- Cost-effective training on commodity GPU clusters demonstrated
