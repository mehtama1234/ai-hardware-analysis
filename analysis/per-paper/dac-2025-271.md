# dac-2025-271: ADVISOR—HLS Design Space Explorer for Approximate Computing

**Problem:** HLS design space exploration with approximate computing requires expensive full approximation of every variant; prohibitive runtime.

**Motivation:** Approximate computing offers area/power savings but identifying best candidates without evaluating all designs is difficult.

**Method:** ADVISOR uses a fast scoring system called the Approximation Friendliness Index (AFI) to predict which hardware designs will work well with approximation (reducing precision to trade accuracy for speed and power). Instead of fully approximating every design variant—which is computationally expensive—it only applies the full approximation process to designs with high AFI scores. This filtering approach lets the tool explore 68 times faster than exhaustive exploration, because it avoids wasting computation on designs unlikely to benefit from approximation.

**Key Novelty:** AFI metric enables filtering without full approximation of every variant.

**Contributions:**
- AFI static metric for approximation amenability
- 68x faster design exploration
- Equivalent results to exhaustive search

**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
