# dac-2025-271: ADVISOR—HLS Design Space Explorer for Approximate Computing

**Problem:** HLS design space exploration with approximate computing requires expensive full approximation of every variant; prohibitive runtime.

**Motivation:** Approximate computing offers area/power savings but identifying best candidates without evaluating all designs is difficult.

**Method:** Static Approximation Friendliness Index (AFI) scores designs for approximation potential; only high-AFI designs are fully approximated. 68x speedup vs. exhaustive search.

**Key Novelty:** AFI metric enables filtering without full approximation of every variant.

**Contributions:**
- AFI static metric for approximation amenability
- 68x faster design exploration
- Equivalent results to exhaustive search

**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
