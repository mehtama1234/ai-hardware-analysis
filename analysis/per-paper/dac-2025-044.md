# Process-Variation-Aware Design Optimization for Wavelength-Routed Optical NoCs

**Problem:** Microring resonators (MRRs) in wavelength-routed optical NoCs are sensitive to process variation, which shifts transmission spectra and degrades signal quality.

**Key Idea:** Build analytical models of signal power under MRR variation, then jointly optimize MRR radii and signal wavelengths to maximize robustness.

**Approach:** Coupled tuning of geometry and wavelength selection exploits interdependencies; worst-case expected power analysis guides design.

**Results:** 7.51 dB signal transmission power improvement vs. variation-unaware designs.

**Theme:** Robust optical interconnect design through variation-aware joint parameter optimization.
