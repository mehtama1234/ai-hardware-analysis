# isscc-2025-234: Compact All-Digital Fractional Divider with Split-DTC Calibration

**Problem:** DTC-based fractional output dividers suffer PVT variations, gain errors, and INL that degrade jitter. Prior calibration requires external reference clocks or auxiliary PLLs, increasing area and complexity.

**Approach:** All-digital FOD using split-DTC-based background calibration simultaneously correcting gain and INL errors without reference clock or auxiliary PLL.

**Key novelty:** Split-DTC calibration enabling self-referential gain and INL compensation.

**Metrics:** 0.024mm² ultra-compact area, 257fs worst-case jitter, <-77dBc spurious tones.

**Tags:** fractional-divider, dtc, jitter, low-area, clock-generation, calibration
