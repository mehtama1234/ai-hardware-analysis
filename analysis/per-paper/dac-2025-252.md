# dac-2025-252: An Enhanced Data Packing Method for General Matrix Multiplication in Brakerski/Fan-Vercauteren Scheme

## Summary
Polynomial encoding for BFV FHE GEMM with hardware co-design achieving 4.22x speedup on MNIST and 3.99x on CIFAR-10 via FPGA implementation with optimized host-device scheduling.

## Key Idea
Efficient polynomial encoding of GEMM inputs/weights for BFV FHE with specialized hardware scheduling.

## Contributions
- BFV polynomial GEMM encoding
- Variable-size input/weight packing
- FPGA co-design with scheduling optimization
- 4.22x speedup (MNIST), 3.99x (CIFAR-10)

## Limitations
Limited to small datasets; scalability to larger models not shown.
