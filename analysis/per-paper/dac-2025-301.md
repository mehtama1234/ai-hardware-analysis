# EffiCast: Contention-Aware Energy Forecasting for Heterogeneous Processors

**Venue:** DAC 2025  
**Confidence:** abstract-only

## Summary

EffiCast is a sequence-based forecasting methodology for predicting energy efficiency in modern clustered heterogeneous processors under dynamic resource contention. Using LSTM and Transformer models, it achieves 1.82 ms inference latency on real hardware, enabling proactive resource management.

## Key Contributions

- **Sensitivity Analysis**: Comprehensive study of energy efficiency variability across core types, V/f levels, application phases, and contention scenarios
- **Deep Learning Models**: LSTM and Transformer sequences achieving unprecedented forecast accuracy
- **Real Hardware Deployment**: Integration with Intel oneDNN on actual heterogeneous processors with low inference latency
- **Dynamic Optimization**: Framework enabling proactive resource management under variable workloads

## Technical Focus

Addresses the challenge that energy efficiency in heterogeneous systems varies significantly due to contention, making static models ineffective. Uses structured training data to capture multi-dimensional sensitivities.
