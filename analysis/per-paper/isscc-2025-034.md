# isscc-2025-034: Ultra-Low-Power BLE Transceiver

**Title:** A 380μW and -242.8dB FoM Digital-PLL-Based GFSK Modulator with Sub-20μs Settling Frequency Hopping for Bluetooth Low-Energy in 22nm CMOS

**Venue:** ISSCC

## Core Contribution
ULP DPLL-based BLE transceiver supporting GFSK modulation and sub-20μs adaptive frequency hopping at 380μW in 22nm CMOS.

## Key Metrics
- **Power:** 380μW
- **FoM:** -242.8dB
- **Frequency-Hopping Settling:** <20μs
- **Technology:** 22nm CMOS

## Problem
Prior DPLL-based BLE designs struggle with the power/noise/speed trade-off: single-point modulation needs wide PLL bandwidth; two-point needs large look-up tables and slow LMS convergence.

## Method
The design uses a digital phase-locked loop with a digitally controlled oscillator, or DCO—an oscillator whose frequency you adjust with digital signals. By tracking how the DCO's frequency responds to its control inputs (dynamic-tuning-curve tracking), it encodes data via frequency shifts (GFSK modulation) and hops quickly between Bluetooth channels (2.402-2.480GHz) in under 20 microseconds while adapting to manufacturing and operating variations. It cuts power by using fewer switched-capacitor banks—circuits that switch capacitors on and off to tune frequency—which reduces the energy spent on capacitor switching. The result is 380 microwatts of power consumption with -242.8dB efficiency.
