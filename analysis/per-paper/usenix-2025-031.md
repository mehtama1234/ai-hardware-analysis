# DEEPSERVE: Serverless Large Language Model Serving at Scale

**Problem:** Serving large language models at scale in cloud environments faces critical challenges in resource allocation, serving efficiency, and cold start latencies. Traditional approaches cannot rapidly elasticity-scale while maintaining performance.

**Method:** DEEPSERVE abstracts workloads via a request-job-task model and integrates FLOWSERVE, a microkernel-inspired serving engine with NPU-centric execution and SPMD-based parallelism. It employs novel scheduling policies for PD-disaggregated and PD-colocated instances, and applies optimizations including pre-warmed pods, DRAM pre-loading, and NPU-fork to achieve 64-instance scale-up in seconds.

**Novelty:** NPU-fork optimization combined with serverless abstraction enabling sub-second cold-start scaling for LLM serving on disaggregated Ascend NPU clusters.
