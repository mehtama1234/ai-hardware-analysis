# isscc-2025-083: Multi-Mode Fully Integrated Voltage Regulator

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
FIVRs face efficiency tradeoff: high peak-power efficiency vs. poor idle efficiency; soft-switching/DCM reduce ripple but degrade high-load performance.

## Motivation
Modern AI SoCs need localized power delivery with hundreds of Amperes; data centers require both peak and idle efficiency.

## Method
The design combines a power supply (voltage regulator) directly integrated into a 3nm chip that automatically switches its operating mode based on power demand. When the chip draws heavy current, it uses hard-switching—rapidly flipping transistors on and off to transfer power efficiently. At lower power levels, the system automatically switches to soft-switching (transistor transitions with less electrical noise) and then discontinuous conduction mode (where the inductor fully discharges each cycle, reducing noise further). This three-mode approach keeps voltage stable while achieving 86% efficiency when running at light load.

## Key Novelty
The key innovation is automatically switching between three operating modes—hard-switching at high load, soft-switching at medium load, and discontinuous conduction mode at light load—to maintain efficiency across the full power range.

## Contributions
- 86% peak light-load efficiency
- Autonomous mode selection
- Hard-switching for transients
- Soft-switching and DCM for idle power

## Metrics
- Light-load efficiency: 86% peak
- Technology: 3nm FinFET
- Fully integrated design
