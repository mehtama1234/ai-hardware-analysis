# An Ensemble MLP-RF Model for the Prediction of DG-MOSFETs: Addressing Fabrication Process Variations

**ID**: vlsid-2025-055 | **Venue**: VLSID | **Confidence**: low (abstract only)

## Problem Statement
Predicting Double-Gate MOSFET electrical characteristics across process variations requires expensive TCAD simulation. ML-based surrogate models could accelerate device characterization and enable rapid design iteration.

## Motivation
TCAD simulation is computationally intensive (hours to days per device), limiting design exploration. ML surrogate models promise orders-of-magnitude speedup while capturing process variation effects.

## Method
- **Model Architecture**: Ensemble combining MLP and RF regressors
  - MLP: Deep feature extraction capability
  - RF: Process variation tree-based modeling
- **Prediction Targets**: Drain current (Id), gate-source capacitance (Cgs), drain-gate capacitance (Cgd)
- **Classification Task**: Channel length prediction from unknown device characteristics
- **Validation Metrics**: MSE, RMSE, R² (mean squared error, root mean squared error, coefficient of determination)
