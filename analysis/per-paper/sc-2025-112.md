# X-ray Ptychography at the Edge: Towards Real-Time Feedback for High-Speed Nanoimaging

**Venue:** SC | **ID:** sc-2025-112

## Summary
GPU-accelerated iterative reconstruction algorithms deployed at beamline edge for real-time X-ray ptychography feedback, enabling informed experimental decisions during high-speed data acquisition.

## Problem & Motivation
X-ray ptychography is indispensable for nanoscale imaging in materials, electronics, and life sciences. However, recent advances in detector technology dramatically increased data generation rates, making offline reconstruction insufficient. Experiments require real-time feedback to enable adaptive imaging and informed decision-making.

## Key Findings
- Real-time reconstruction capability during continuous high-speed acquisition
- GPU-accelerated efficiency enabling edge deployment
- Support for data-driven experimental adaptation
- Foundation for autonomous experiment execution

## Hardware & Systems
- **Deployment:** Edge GPUs at beamline equipment
- **Target:** X-ray synchrotron facility infrastructure
- **Integration:** Direct attachment to detector systems
- **Architecture:** Real-time streaming reconstruction pipeline

## Techniques
- **GPU-accelerated reconstruction:** Efficient iterative phase retrieval
- **Edge computing deployment:** Minimal latency to experimental control loop
- **Streaming processing:** Real-time output during continuous acquisition
- **Autonomous operation:** Closed-loop feedback enabling automated experiments
