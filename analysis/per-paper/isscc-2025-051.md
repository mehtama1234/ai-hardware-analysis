# isscc-2025-051: Single-Inductor Resonant Ultrasound TX Beamformer

**Title:** A Single-Inductor-Based High-Voltage Transmit Beamformer for Wearable Ultrasound Devices Achieving 88% fCV2 Power Reduction

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Core Problem:**
Wearable ultrasound beamformers for vagus nerve stimulation suffer from fCV² charging losses; multi-inductor resonant designs are incompatible with miniaturization.

**Method:**
Single-inductor architecture that phase-shifts groups of transducer elements, enabling energy recycling across the array while eliminating multiple off-chip inductors and body-diode losses.

**Key Innovation:**
Single-inductor resonant TX beamforming via phase-shifted element groups.

**Metrics:**
- **Power Reduction:** 88% fCV² loss vs. class-D pulser
- **Form Factor:** Single on-chip inductor

**Target:** Wearable ultrasound therapy (epilepsy)

**Techniques:** Circuit design, power optimization

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)
