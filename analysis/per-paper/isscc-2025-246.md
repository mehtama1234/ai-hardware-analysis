# Precision Opamp with On-Chip Heater Trimming

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**ID:** isscc-2025-246

## Problem
MOS-input opamps have high offset (±45µV 3σ) and large offset drift (±330nV/°C 3σ). Dynamic offset cancellation adds complexity; conventional dual-temperature trimming requires expensive test equipment and cannot correct field drift.

## Solution
On-chip heater-based self-trimming performs room-temperature offset and drift correction using thermal gradients, eliminating need for external test equipment and enabling field-deployable calibration.

## Key Innovation
On-chip heater enabling room-temperature offset/drift trim without expensive dual-temperature test equipment.

## Results
- **Offset voltage:** ±5.8 µV (3σ) – 7.8× improvement
- **Offset drift:** ±88 nV/°C (3σ) – 3.75× improvement
- **Temperature range:** -40°C to +125°C
- **Trimming:** Room-temperature self-trim

## Hardware Target
ASIC

## Techniques
Circuit design, power, analog compensation
