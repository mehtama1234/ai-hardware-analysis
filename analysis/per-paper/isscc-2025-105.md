# isscc-2025-105: Compact 3-Stack Doherty Power Amplifier for 5G FR2

**Problem:** Power amplifiers in 5G phased-array systems must deliver high output power while maintaining efficiency during back-off operation.

**Method:** The design uses three power amplifier stages stacked in 22nm FDSOI CMOS (a compact silicon technology) arranged in a Doherty configuration with stacked operational-amplifier-based biasing. In a Doherty topology, the peak and auxiliary amplifier stages dynamically adjust their electrical impedance to maintain high power efficiency across different output power levels. The stacked operational-amplifier biasing further improves efficiency at back-off (reduced power) conditions, which matters for 5G signals that have high peak-to-average power ratios.

**Key Novelty:** Stacked OPA-based bias scheme enabling compact 3-stack Doherty architecture for mm-wave integration.

**Main Results:** >16.5dBm average output power for 5G FR2 band with high PAE at back-off.
