# Asymmetric Predictive Testing for Aging in SRAMs

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only analysis)

## Problem
Symmetric aggressive testing for aging-induced SRAM failures causes excessive over-testing, spuriously marking functional chips as defective.

## Method
Exploits **asymmetric aging** (cells storing zeros fail preferentially on read-1) via differential testing: normal-voltage writes, aggressive read-0, less-aggressive read-1. Includes statistical sampling/simulation for fast convergence.

## Key Novelty
Asymmetric predictive testing reduces over-testing 3-5× by exploiting cell-level aging asymmetry.

## Results
- **3-5× over-testing reduction** for low under-testing limits
