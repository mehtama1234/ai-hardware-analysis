# Design and Technology Co-optimization Utilizing Flip-FET (FFET) Standard Cells

**Problem:** CFET standard cells require excessive tap-cell overhead in FEOL to enable backside routing, preventing efficient exploitation of BEOL backside wiring.

**Method:** Flip-FET flips the lower transistor in a vertically-stacked transistor pair (CFET—complementary field-effect transistor), creating connection points (pins) directly accessible from both the top and bottom metal wiring layers without requiring tap-cells (helper cells needed for routing). To implement this, the optimization process synthesizes multiple Flip-FET cell variants with different pin layouts, optimizes their placement to minimize tap-cells, and rebalances wiring between the top and bottom layers.

**Key Contribution:** Eliminates tap-cell overhead while enabling full backside routing exploitation.

**Results:** Scales chip size beyond CFET with fewer routing failures.
