# isscc-2025-155: WF-Bonding 3D-NAND with High Speed

**Problem:** High-layer-count 3D-NAND faces: dummy-hole removal increases power; pass-voltage disturbance degrades reliability; rising WL capacitance slows program/read; high-speed I/O signal-integrity challenges.

**Motivation:** AI/data workloads need both extreme density and fast I/O; conventional 3D-NAND cannot scale both simultaneously.

**Method:** WF-bonding multi-layer stack with optimized wordline architecture: dummy-hole elimination via high-density cells, disturbance mitigation via efficient WL structuring, signal-integrity-aware 5.6Gb/s/pin I/O design balancing eye-width and eye-height.

**Key Novelty:** WF-bonding 3D-NAND with optimized multi-layer WL architecture enabling simultaneous density, speed, and reliability.

**Contributions:**
- 28Gb/mm2 density
- 1Tb capacity
- 5.6Gb/s/pin I/O
- 3bits/cell TLC with high-layer-count reliability

**Hardware Target:** ASIC

**Technique Categories:** memory-system, packaging, circuit-design

**Workloads:** database

**Metrics:** 28Gb/mm2, 1Tb, 5.6Gb/s/pin, 3b/cell

**Baselines:** Conventional 3D-NAND, prior high-layer stacks

**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
