# (Invited Paper) Overview of 2025 CAD Contest at ICCAD

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-026

## Problem
Electronic design automation research requires engagement between academia and industry with real-world problem statements and benchmarks.

## Motivation
CAD contests foster productive industry-academia collaboration and research productivity in EDA.

## Method
Researchers compete over several months on problems provided by companies that make chip-design tools (EDA software—electronic design automation). Each problem includes industrial benchmarks (actual test cases from real chip projects) and automated evaluators that verify whether solutions work. This gives researchers genuine real-world challenges instead of simplified toy problems.

## Key Novelty
An annual competition (running since 2012) that connects academic researchers with real chip-design problems from industry.

## Contributions
- 247 teams from worldwide participation (record-breaking)
- State-of-the-art problem statements: hardware trojan detection, multibit flip-flop design optimization, incremental placement
- Industrial benchmarks and evaluators
- Industry-academia collaboration fostering
