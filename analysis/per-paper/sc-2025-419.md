# SC-2025-419: DAS-ILU - Distributed Asynchronous Parallel ILU Factorization

## Summary
DAS-ILU improves parallel ILU preconditioning through asynchronous domain decomposition, achieving 2.07x speedup over Chow-Patel and 4.11x over HYPRE by decoupling interior and separator computations.

## Problem
ILU preconditioning is essential for sparse linear solvers but difficult to parallelize due to data dependencies. Existing methods suffer from high cross-processor synchronization overhead and separator-node bottlenecks.

## Method
- **Domain decomposition partitioning**: Separates interior nodes and separator nodes
- **Asynchronous separator updates**: Decouples interior computation from separator processing, reducing synchronization
- **Reduced separator size**: Halves separator node count compared to conventional methods
- **Optimized data exchange**: Minimizes communication overhead
- **Block matrix support**: Extends to block-structured sparse matrices with exact block inversions

## Key Results
- 2.07x faster than Chow-Patel ILU
- 4.11x faster than HYPRE ILU
- Demonstrated on structural mechanics, CFD, and reservoir simulation
- Robust on nonsymmetric and indefinite systems
