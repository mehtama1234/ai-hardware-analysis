# isscc-2025-181: Passive Switched-Capacitor Multimode Amplifier

**Problem**: Harsh-environment sensors (geothermal, up to 200°C) need amplifiers with wide dynamic range (>80dB) and stable temperature behavior; existing designs drift or require complex trimming.

**Solution**: Passive switched-capacitor (SC) multimode amplifier with novel temperature compensation achieving 0.75% logarithmic conformity error from -25 to 200°C.

**Key Results**:
- 0.75% logarithmic conformity error
- >80dB dynamic range maintained across temperature extremes
- Eliminates trimming/compensation overhead

**Hardware**: ASIC  
**Application**: Geothermal & harsh-environment sensor front-end  
**Confidence**: low (abstract only)
