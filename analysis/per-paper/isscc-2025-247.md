# Cascaded PLL with Shared-DSM Quantization Noise Cancellation

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**ID:** isscc-2025-247

## Problem
Fractional-N PLLs suffer from quantization noise and fractional spurs. QEC approaches require intensive calibration with long settling times; multi-PLL architectures introduce mixing spurs; cascaded integer-fractional PLLs leave fractional spurs in-band.

## Solution
Cascaded PLL with two multi-modulus dividers (MMDs) sharing a single delta-sigma modulator (DSM) operates the shared DSM at elevated frequency to push Q-noise out of bandwidth. Dual-MMD structure suppresses fractional spurs in-band via noise subtraction without requiring digital calibration.

## Key Innovation
Shared-DSM dual-MMD cascaded PLL achieving calibration-free quantization noise cancellation.

## Results
- **Jitter:** 96 fs RMS
- **Fractional spur:** -70.6 dBc
- **Settling:** Fast without calibration convergence time

## Hardware Target
ASIC

Circuit design, signal processing
