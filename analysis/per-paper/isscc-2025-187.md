# isscc-2025-187: Quasi-Class-E Colpitts Oscillator for mm-Wave LO Generation

**Problem**: mm-wave communication (5G, satellite) requires ultra-pure local-oscillator signals; existing VCOs achieve only 190–191dB FoM(T), 5–10dB worse than low-frequency counterparts.

**Solution**: Transformer-based two-port resonance in quasi-class-E Colpitts topology enabling high-efficiency fundamental oscillation with integrated phase-noise suppression in 40nm CMOS.

**Key Results**:
- 198.8dBc/Hz FoM(T) (best-in-class)
- -124.9 to -119.8dBc/Hz phase noise @ 10MHz
- 47.3–58.4GHz tuning range
- 5–10dB improvement over prior art

**Hardware**: ASIC  
**Application**: mm-Wave RF signal generation  
**Confidence**: low (abstract only)
