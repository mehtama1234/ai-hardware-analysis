# Discriminate Weight Approximation for Efficient DSP Packing in LLM Accelerators

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Existing DSP packing designs use indiscriminate weight approximation (IWA) to reduce chip area for LLMs, but IWA causes significant accuracy loss and high LUT consumption, making deployments costly and impractical.

## Motivation
DSP packing is essential for reducing ASIC area, but naive weight approximation breaks model accuracy; selective approximation can preserve quality while reducing cost.

## Method
Discriminate Weight Approximation (DWA) selectively approximates weights based on sensitivity analysis, reducing both area and LUT consumption without accuracy degradation. The method identifies which weights can tolerate approximation and which require full precision.

## Key Novelty
Selective weight approximation strategy that discriminates between approximation-tolerant and precision-critical weights for efficient DSP resource packing.

## Contributions
- DWA method achieving 1.5× chip area reduction with imperceptible accuracy loss
- 2.6-4.6× LUT consumption reduction vs. existing IWA methods
- 5× reduction in deployment cost
- Significantly better accuracy than existing IWA approaches
