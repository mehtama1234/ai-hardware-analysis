# 72GS/s Time-Interleaved Pipeline-SAR ADC

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**ID:** isscc-2025-248

## Problem
Time-interleaved ADCs for optical receivers require >100GS/s with high SFDR (>55dB) at wideband (>20GHz) inputs. Massive TI factors degrade SFDR due to bandwidth mismatch and interleaver distortion; high-frequency buffers introduce nonlinearity.

## Solution
72GS/s 9b time-interleaved pipeline-SAR ADC employs linearized wideband input buffers, dual-path bootstrapped switches, and R/R-based open-loop amplifier to minimize calibration. Pipeline-SAR architecture combines pipeline linearity with SAR efficiency at 1.125GS/s per unit, scaled via 64-fold interleaving.

## Key Innovation
Pipeline-SAR TI architecture with linearized buffers and bootstrapped switching achieving >55dB SFDR at 20GHz without calibration.

## Results
- **Sampling rate:** 72 GS/s
- **SFDR @ 20GHz:** 55.3 dB
- **SFDR @ Nyquist (22.5GHz):** 49.3 dB
- **Power:** 393.6 mW
- **Energy:** 53.7 fJ/conversion-step

ASIC
