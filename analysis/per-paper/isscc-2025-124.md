# isscc-2025-124: 132-148GHz CMOS 4TX-4RX FMCW Radar Transceiver Array

**Problem:** D-band (132-148GHz) FMCW radar transceivers are limited by low output power from frequency multipliers and power amplifiers, low on-chip antenna gain, and small array sizes, resulting in insufficient EIRP (<11.6 dBm) for high-resolution imaging and long-range detection.

**Motivation:** D-band spectrum offers large available bandwidth for future high-resolution radars and high-data-rate wireless; compensating for substantial free-space loss requires high EIRP but is constrained by process fMAx and breakdown voltage limitations.

**Method:** The transceiver array implements four transmit and four receive channels with a high-power amplitude-phase harmonic modulated frequency quadrupler, high-power PA using low-loss slow-wave coplanar waveguide (CPW) and LC-ladder-based power-combiner, and a cavity-backed antenna-in-package (AiP) with high gain to achieve 28.7 dBm EIRP.

**Key Novelty:** Cavity-backed antenna-in-package integration combined with LC-ladder power-combiner achieving unprecedented D-band EIRP for multi-channel FMCW radar arrays.

**Contributions:**
- 4TX-4RX CMOS D-band FMCW radar transceiver array
- High-power amplitude-phase harmonic modulated frequency quadrupler
- Low-loss slow-wave CPW and LC-ladder power-combiner for PA efficiency
- Cavity-backed antenna-in-package achieving 28.7 dBm EIRP
