# Intermittent Systems at Small Scale: Execution Model and Design Guidelines

**Authors:** Youngbin Kim, Yoojin Lim  
**Venue:** DAC 2025

## Summary
This paper addresses a critical gap in how intermittent systems (energy-harvesting devices) are modeled and designed. Traditional execution models do not accurately capture the behavior of systems with small energy storage, leading to checkpoint mechanisms that are up to 5.62x less efficient than expected.

## Key Contributions
- New execution model accurately describing small-scale intermittent systems
- Identification of 5.62x power efficiency degradation when using traditional models
- Enhanced static checkpoint techniques (3.04x improvement)
- Enhanced dynamic checkpoint techniques (2.85x improvement)

## Technical Approach
The paper proposes an improved execution model that better reflects the power budgeting and hardware characteristics of small-scale intermittent systems. Design guidelines derived from this model enhance both static and dynamic checkpoint mechanisms.

## Hardware Target
- CPU-based systems with energy harvesting

## Technique Categories
Power management, scheduling
