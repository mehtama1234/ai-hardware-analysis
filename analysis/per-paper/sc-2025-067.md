# MoE-Inference-Bench: Performance Evaluation of Mixture of Expert Large Language and Vision Models

**ID:** sc-2025-067 | **Venue:** SC

## Problem Statement
MoE models have load imbalance and routing overhead at inference; lacks systematic hardware acceleration evaluation

## Motivation
MoE enables scaling LLMs/VLMs efficiently, but inference presents deployment challenges requiring optimization guidance

## Method
Comprehensive benchmark (MoE-Inference-Bench) evaluating batch size, sequence length, FFN dimensions, expert count on H100; tests pruning, fused MoE ops, speculative decoding, quantization, parallelization

## Key Novelty
Systematic MoE inference evaluation across multiple model families (Mixtral, DeepSeek, OLMoE, Qwen); identifies configuration-dependent performance

## Contributions
- Comprehensive MoE inference benchmark
- Evaluation of multiple optimization techniques
- Analysis across diverse hyperparameter configurations
- Deployment insights for MoE models
