# ISSCC-2025-146: High-Efficiency Qi Wireless Charging with Acoustic Noise Reduction

**Problem:**  
High-power wireless charging (Qi MPP >25W) requires robust TRX communication for safety, but ASK modulation introduces acoustic noise in receiver coil.

**Solution:**  
50W Qi system with acoustic noise-reduced ASK modulation and hybrid voltage-/current-mode receiver demodulation achieving 98% efficiency.

**Key Results:**  
- 50W output power
- 98% end-to-end efficiency
- Acoustic noise-reduced communication
- Qi 2.0 compliance

**Key Innovation:**  
Acoustic noise-reduced ASK combined with hybrid demodulation enables high-power Qi 2.0 without coil acoustic artifacts.
