# Nanoelectromechanical Binary Comparator for Edge-Computing Applications

**Venue:** DATE  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Core Idea
Build binary comparators using 4-terminal nanoelectromechanical relays instead of CMOS transistors to achieve extreme temperature/radiation tolerance, zero standby power, and dense device count.

## Problem
Edge computing in harsh environments requires comparators that tolerate high temperature and radiation; CMOS fails in these conditions and wastes standby power.

## Method
- **NEM relay devices:** 4-terminal mechanical relays with ~6 devices per comparator bit (vs 9 CMOS transistors)
- **Fabricated silicon proof-of-concept** on SOI substrate
- **Scalable tree topology** for higher-order comparators

## Key Results
- **6 devices per bit** vs 9 CMOS transistors
- **~47% device reduction** for 64-bit comparators
- **300°C operation** (vs ~85°C CMOS)
- **>1 Mrad radiation hardness**
- **21 fJ/operation** (9x energy reduction vs CMOS)
