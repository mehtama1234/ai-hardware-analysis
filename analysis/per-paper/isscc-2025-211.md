# isscc-2025-211: Ultra-Low-Power Adaptive Crystal Oscillator

## Quick Summary
A 0.36nW, 820µm² 32kHz crystal oscillator for real-time clocks in battery-powered IoT devices, eliminating the need for separate start-up circuits through conduction-angle-adaptive pulse injection.

## Problem
Pulse-injection XOs (PIXOs) achieve sub-nW power but require:
- Separate start-up circuits to initiate oscillation (area overhead)
- Accurate delay generation (area-consuming)
- Robustness against accidental interference

## Method
The crystal oscillator, built in 28nm CMOS, injects tiny pulses of energy at adaptively timed moments in the oscillation cycle (Class-C-like operation, where transistors conduct only during a narrow window). The timing of these conduction-angle pulses adjusts automatically to match the oscillator's state, allowing it to start itself without a separate start-up circuit. This eliminates the need for added control logic while providing robust oscillation that resists interference.

## Key Novelty
By automatically adjusting the conduction angle (the phase when transistors are active in each cycle), the oscillator starts itself without needing a separate start-up circuit.

## Contributions
- 0.36nW power consumption
- 820µm² area (compact)
- Self-starting without additional circuits
- Robust under interference
