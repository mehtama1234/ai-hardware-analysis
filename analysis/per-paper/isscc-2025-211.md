# isscc-2025-211: Ultra-Low-Power Adaptive Crystal Oscillator

## Quick Summary
A 0.36nW, 820µm² 32kHz crystal oscillator for real-time clocks in battery-powered IoT devices, eliminating the need for separate start-up circuits through conduction-angle-adaptive pulse injection.

## Problem
Pulse-injection XOs (PIXOs) achieve sub-nW power but require:
- Separate start-up circuits to initiate oscillation (area overhead)
- Accurate delay generation (area-consuming)
- Robustness against accidental interference

## Method
Conduction-angle-adaptive crystal oscillator using Class-C-like pulse injection in 28nm CMOS. The adaptive mechanism allows the device to self-start without separate start-up logic, while maintaining frequency stability needed for synchronization in IoT networks.

## Key Novelty
Self-starting oscillation capability through conduction-angle adaptation, eliminating separate start-up circuitry complexity.

## Contributions
- 0.36nW power consumption
- 820µm² area (compact)
- Self-starting without additional circuits
- Robust under interference
