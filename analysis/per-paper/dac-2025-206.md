# Re4PUF: A Reliable, Reconfigurable ReRAM-based PUF Resilient to DNN and Side Channel Attacks

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only analysis)

## Problem
ReRAM PUFs suffer from unreliability (read noise, temperature variations) and are vulnerable to DNN modeling and side-channel attacks.

## Method
Re4PUF generates a unique hardware fingerprint using 3 transistors and 2 resistor elements (ReRAM), combined with digital voltage dividers that stabilize readings. When read noise and temperature fluctuations degrade the fingerprint's reliability, instead of permanently rewriting the ReRAM chip, the system adjusts the voltages powering its inverter circuits (the basic logic gates) to compensate. These voltage tweaks can be applied repeatedly at low cost. The adjustable voltages also strengthen security by reducing the predictability that neural-network attacks and side-channel attacks (like power-consumption monitoring) depend on.

## Key Novelty
Instead of rewriting a ReRAM chip to fix an unreliable fingerprint, Re4PUF adjusts the inverter voltages in a 3-transistor, 2-resistor circuit—a simple, cheap reconfiguration that also blocks neural-network and side-channel attacks.

## Results
- **1% BER at 85C** (7.59× improvement vs existing ReRAM PUFs)
- **~50% DNN attack success rate** (robust resistance)
- **<70% side-channel attack success rate**
