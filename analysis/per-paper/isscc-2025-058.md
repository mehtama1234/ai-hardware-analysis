# isscc-2025-058: HuMoniX Sparsity-Exploiting Motion-Generation Processor

**Title:** 23.10 HuMoniX: A 57.3fps 12.8TFLOPS/W Text-to-Motion Processor with Inter-Iteration Output Sparsity and Inter-Frame Joint Similarity

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Core Problem:**
Transformer-based diffusion models for 3D human motion generation suffer from iterative compute overhead and redundant joint computations across denoising steps and frames.

**Method:**
Exploits two sparsity dimensions: (1) inter-iteration output sparsity pruning negligible denoising contributions, (2) inter-frame joint similarity sharing intermediate computations for temporally-correlated joints; hardware dataflow maximizes both patterns.

**Key Innovation:**
Dual-sparsity exploitation in diffusion motion generation: iteration pruning + frame-reuse.

**Metrics:**
- **Throughput:** 57.3 fps (real-time)
- **Efficiency:** 12.8 TFLOPS/W
- **Task:** Text-to-motion 3D generation

**Architecture:** Specialized ASIC with dataflow for inter-iteration/frame sparsity

**Techniques:** Sparsity exploitation, dataflow design, scheduling

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)
