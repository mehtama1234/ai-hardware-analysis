# Universal Checkpointing: A Flexible and Efficient Distributed Checkpointing System for Large-Scale DNN Training with Reconfigurable Parallelism

**Problem:** Existing DNN training systems tightly couple distributed checkpoints to specific model parallelism and hardware configurations, preventing efficient reconfiguration of parallelism strategies during training and adaptation to hardware failures or resource elasticity.

**Method:** When training large neural networks on distributed computers, systems normally save snapshots of training progress (called checkpoints) in a format locked to the current setup—specifically how the model is distributed across devices and what hardware you're using. UCP stores checkpoints in an abstract format that doesn't depend on any particular hardware or parallelization strategy instead. Using pattern-based rules, it automatically remaps the checkpoint data to work with different ways of splitting the model or different hardware configurations, without needing to rebuild the checkpoint from scratch.

**Novelty:** The core idea is storing training checkpoints in a format independent of how you distribute the model across computers or which hardware you use, so you can change your training setup without repeating previous work.
