# Universal Checkpointing: A Flexible and Efficient Distributed Checkpointing System for Large-Scale DNN Training with Reconfigurable Parallelism

**Problem:** Existing DNN training systems tightly couple distributed checkpoints to specific model parallelism and hardware configurations, preventing efficient reconfiguration of parallelism strategies during training and adaptation to hardware failures or resource elasticity.

**Method:** UCP decouples checkpoint structure from parallel training strategies and hardware configurations using an abstract checkpoint representation. A pattern-based reconfiguration pipeline automatically and efficiently maps checkpoint state to various parallelism strategies without full reconstruction.

**Novelty:** Decoupling checkpoint structure from parallelism strategies and hardware configurations to enable flexible reconfiguration during training.
