# ACTINA: Adapting Circuit-Switching Techniques for AI Networking Architectures

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-397
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Static electrical switch networks limit bandwidth allocation flexibility and increase power consumption in large-scale AI systems; dynamic allocation is needed.

## Motivation
Optical Circuit Switching (OCS) enables dynamic reconfiguration and lower power; but its applicability to AI training depends on reconfiguration latency, bandwidth, and topology design.

## Method
Framework for evaluating OCS in AI systems; two in-workload reconfiguration strategies; multi-dimensional all-to-all topology supporting hybrid parallelism.

## Key Novelty
First quantitative framework for OCS in AI networks; demonstrates OCS-enabled multi-dimensional topology achieves 1.75x better perf-power than Fat-Tree/3D-Torus.

## Contributions
1. Framework for trade-off analysis of OCS technologies in AI systems
2. Two in-workload reconfiguration strategies (vs. traditional one-shot approach)
3. OCS-enabled multi-dimensional all-to-all topology for hybrid parallelism
4. Up to 2.3x improvement with low reconfiguration latency (<100 us)

## Hardware Targets

## Technique Categories

## Metrics

## Limitations
Framework-focused; limited prototype implementation; assumes state-of-the-art GPU bandwidth; reconfiguration latency <100 us is optimistic.
