# ACTINA: Adapting Circuit-Switching Techniques for AI Networking Architectures

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-397
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Static electrical switch networks limit bandwidth allocation flexibility and increase power consumption in large-scale AI systems; dynamic allocation is needed.

## Motivation
Optical Circuit Switching (OCS) enables dynamic reconfiguration and lower power; but its applicability to AI training depends on reconfiguration latency, bandwidth, and topology design.

## Method
The framework evaluates optical circuit switching (light-based routing that can be reconfigured dynamically) in AI systems using two strategies that adjust the network during job execution. The system uses a multi-dimensional all-to-all topology (where each component can reach every other through multiple independent paths), enabling hybrid parallelism by allowing different parallelization strategies to work together in the same system.

## Key Novelty
The first quantitative framework for optical circuit switching in AI networks, showing that OCS-enabled multi-dimensional topologies achieve 1.75x better performance-per-watt compared to static topologies like Fat-Tree and 3D-Torus.

## Contributions
1. Framework for trade-off analysis of OCS technologies in AI systems
2. Two in-workload reconfiguration strategies (vs. traditional one-shot approach)
3. OCS-enabled multi-dimensional all-to-all topology for hybrid parallelism
4. Up to 2.3x improvement with low reconfiguration latency (<100 us)
