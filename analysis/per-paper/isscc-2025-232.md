# isscc-2025-232: Heterogeneous STT-MRAM CIM for Bayesian Neural Networks

**Problem:** Noise-exposed image recognition degrades CNN/ViT accuracy. Bayesian neural networks offer superior noise tolerance but require efficient hardware supporting μ-weight (mean) and Δ-weight (deviation) representations.

**Approach:** 22nm heterogeneous STT-MRAM CIM macro combining separate μ-NMC (mean computation) and Δ-IMC (deviation in-memory compute) paths, each optimized for its data type.

**Key novelty:** Dual-path CIM architecture specifically designed for Bayesian weight representation.

**Metrics:** 104.5TOPS/W energy efficiency for noise-tolerant edge-AI inference.

**Tags:** cim, bayesian-nn, noise-tolerant, stt-mram, edge-ai, efficiency
