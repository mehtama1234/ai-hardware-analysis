# isscc-2025-048: Adaptive CM-Tuned Voltage-Biased DCO PLL

**Title:** 34.3 A 4.75GHz Digital PLL with 45.8fs Integrated-Jitter and 257dB FoM Based on a Voltage-Biased Harmonic-Shaping DCO with Adaptive Common-Mode Resonance Tuning

**Venue:** ISSCC

**Core Problem:**
Voltage-biased DCOs offer better noise-power tradeoff than current-biased designs but require complex, PVT-sensitive manual common-mode resonance tuning.

**Method:**
Harmonic-shaping voltage-biased DCO with automatic adaptive CM-resonance tuning via second-harmonic amplitude feedback; wideband CM resonance expansion maintains high impedance across tuning range.

**Key Innovation:**
Automatic foreground/background common-mode resonance tuning eliminating manual circuit complexity.

**Metrics:**
- **Frequency:** 4.75 GHz
- **Integrated Jitter:** 45.8 fs
- **FoM:** 257 dB

**Architecture:** Voltage-biased harmonic-shaping DCO with adaptive CM control

**Techniques:** Circuit design, PLL design, low-noise synthesis

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)
