# date-2025-024: High-Throughput SAT Sampling

## Problem
Traditional Boolean satisfiability (SAT) sampling algorithms operate on CNF directly and are computationally expensive, limiting throughput and unable to effectively leverage GPU parallelism.

## Method
Transforms SAT problems by factoring CNF representations into simplified multilevel, multi-output Boolean functions. Applies gradient-based optimization on the circuit structure, reinterpreting SAT as supervised multi-output regression to enable differentiable, parallel bit-wise operations.

## Contributions
- 33.6x to 523.6x speedup over state-of-the-art heuristic samplers
- GPU-accelerated parallel sampling technique
- Differentiable SAT problem formulation

## Results
Extensive evaluation on 60 benchmark instances demonstrates significant runtime improvements ranging from 33.6x to 523.6x over existing heuristic samplers.
