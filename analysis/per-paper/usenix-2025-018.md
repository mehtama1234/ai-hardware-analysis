# Crash Consistency in Block-Level Caching Systems: An Open CAS Case Study

**Problem:** Block-level caching systems risk losing consistency between cached data and persistent storage during system crashes. Open CAS may exhibit vulnerabilities in maintaining crash consistency guarantees when handling cache operations at the block level.

**Method:** Empirical case study analyzing Open CAS (open-source Content Addressable Storage) to examine crash consistency mechanisms in block-level caching. Likely examines transaction ordering, write-ahead logging, recovery procedures, and identifies consistency gaps through failure scenarios.

**Novelty:** In-depth empirical characterization of crash consistency vulnerabilities and recovery mechanisms in block-level caching systems using production storage software as a case study.
