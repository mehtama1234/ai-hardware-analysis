# isscc-2025-126: RC Oscillator with Path-Delay and Offset Cancellation

**Problem:** Open-loop RC oscillators struggle with frequency stability against process, voltage, and temperature (PVT) variations due to path-delay and comparator offset sensitivity; closed-loop approaches achieve better stability but incur significant area, power, and circuit complexity overhead.

**Motivation:** RC oscillators are critical timers in IoT and edge devices; improving free-running frequency stability without complex compensation networks or switched-resistor calibration is essential for ultra-low-power operation.

**Method:** The reference-replication-based RC oscillator cancels path-delay and comparator-offset effects through integrated compensation circuits, achieving ±9.83ppm/°C temperature coefficient across -40 to 125°C while maintaining 0.4µW/MHz power efficiency without switched resistors or polynomial calibration.

**Key Novelty:** Integrated path-delay and comparator-offset cancellation in open-loop RC oscillators eliminating the complexity-stability tradeoff of prior designs.

**Contributions:**
- Reference-replication-based RC oscillator architecture
- Path-delay and comparator-offset cancellation for improved stability
- 9.83ppm/°C temperature coefficient across wide temperature range
- 0.4µW/MHz power efficiency without complex calibration
