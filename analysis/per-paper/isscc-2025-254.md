# AI-Enabled RFIC Design Space Discovery

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**ID:** isscc-2025-254

## Problem
RF/mmWave IC design relies on intuitive, template-based architectures limiting topology exploration. Circuit-EM co-design requires iterative trial-and-error across transistor sizing, power-combining, matching networks, and layout, extending design time significantly.

## Solution
AI-enabled algorithmic flow combines reinforcement learning for architecture discovery and inverse EM synthesis for passive networks. Automates circuit topology selection, parameter optimization, and layout generation from specifications to fabricated silicon without manual templates or iterative loops.

## Key Innovation
End-to-end AI-enabled RFIC synthesis with active and passive co-optimization from specifications to layout.

## Results
- **34-70GHz broadband PA:** 21.2 dBm P_sat, 26% PAE_max
- **100-120GHz sub-THz PA:** 12.6 dBm P_sat
- **Fabricated results:** First RFIC AI synthesis with measured data

## Hardware Target
ASIC

## Techniques
Compiler, circuit design, ML-aided synthesis
