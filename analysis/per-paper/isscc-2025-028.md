# isscc-2025-028: Bidirectional GaN Load Switch with Dynamic RON Control

**A Dynamic-RON-Diminished Bidirectional GaN Load Switch with Inrush Current Protection and Spike Attenuation**

## Summary
Monolithic bidirectional GaN load switch with dynamic gate-drive control achieving on-resistance reduction while preventing gate overstress, with integrated inrush-current protection for 48V EV power systems.

## Technical Approach
- **Device**: Monolithic bidirectional GaN with common-drain (CD) topology
- **Gate control**: Dynamic selection between gate-to-first and gate-to-second terminal during turn-off
- **Protection**: Inrush-current limiting and spike-attenuation circuits
- **Efficiency**: 38% area reduction vs. discrete back-to-back topologies

## Key Innovation
Dynamic gate-voltage control strategy preventing overstress in monolithic Bi-GaN while enabling efficient bidirectional switching and integrated protection.

## Contributions
- Dynamic RON minimization via gate-control strategy
- Monolithic Bi-GaN CD topology with 38% area saving
- Integrated inrush-current protection
- Spike-attenuation for EMI reduction

## Target Workloads
EV charging, 48V power systems, battery management
