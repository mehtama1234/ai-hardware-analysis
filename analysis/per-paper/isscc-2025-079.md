# isscc-2025-079: Single-Mode High-Efficiency Buck-Boost Converter

**Venue:** ISSCC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Multi-mode hybrid converters suffer from transition glitches/overshoot; cascaded single-mode designs have high ripple and reduced efficiency from discontinuous current delivery.

## Motivation
Li-ion battery devices need efficient 2.8-4.2V to stable 3V conversion with minimal ripple for reliable power rail.

## Method
The design connects a boost bridge (a circuit stage that increases voltage) and a buck bridge (a circuit stage that decreases voltage) in series. A flying capacitor (a capacitor that moves charge around the circuit to balance electrical stress) enables continuous current flow across the full voltage range. By operating in a single fixed configuration rather than switching between multiple operating modes, this eliminates the voltage spikes and output ripple that plague conventional multi-mode converters.

## Key Novelty
A single-mode cascaded buck-boost converter using a flying capacitor to maintain continuous current delivery, which eliminates voltage glitches and ripple by avoiding mode transitions.

## Contributions
- 98.3% peak efficiency
- 7mV output ripple (<0.2% regulation)
- No mode-transition glitches
- Continuous input/output current

## Metrics
- Peak efficiency: 98.3%
- Output ripple: 7 mV
- Input range: 2.8-4.2V
