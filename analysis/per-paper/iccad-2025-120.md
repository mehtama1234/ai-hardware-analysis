# Differentiable Timing-Driven FPGA Placement with Smooth Optimization and ML-Based Delay Calibration

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-120

## Problem
FPGA placement must simultaneously minimize wirelength and optimize timing metrics (TNS/WNS), but traditional placement algorithms struggle with multi-objective optimization and accurate timing estimation.

## Motivation
Placement is critical for timing closure in FPGA design; faster convergence enables more efficient design iterations.

## Method
Differentiable optimization framework with timing preconditioning to smooth the objective landscape, multiplier scheduling scheme for multi-objective balancing, and XGBoost-based machine learning model for net delay prediction to improve timing estimation accuracy.

## Key Novelty
Combining smooth differentiable optimization with ML-based delay calibration for timing-driven FPGA placement.

## Contributions
- Novel timing preconditioning method that smooths the timing objective landscape
- General multiplier scheduling scheme for multi-objective optimization
- XGBoost-based net delay prediction model for improved timing estimation
