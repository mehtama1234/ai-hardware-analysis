# ISSCC-2025-148: Compact Auto-Calibrating OLEDoS Source Driver

**Problem:**  
Microdisplays for AR/VR (4K×4K, >120Hz, 6285 PPI) require compact source-driver ICs with tight area budgets and low display voltage offset for color fidelity.

**Solution:**  
10-bit source-driver IC on OLEDoS backplane with all-channel automatic offset calibration and slew-rate-enhanced amplifier achieving 2273pm²/channel and 1.9mV DVO.

**Key Results:**  
- 2273 pm²/channel area (ultra-compact)
- 1.9 mV display voltage offset
- All-channel automatic calibration
- Support for 6285 PPI, >120Hz refresh

**Key Innovation:**  
First OLEDoS source driver achieving ultra-compact area with per-channel automatic calibration, eliminating external trimming for high-resolution AR/VR displays.
