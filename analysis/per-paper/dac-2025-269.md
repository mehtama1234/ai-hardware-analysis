# Constraint Graph-based PCB Legalization

**Venue:** DAC | **ID:** dac-2025-269

## Problem
Modern high-density PCB designs with dense, heterogeneous, irregular-shaped, and any-oriented components challenge automatic placement legalization, especially on double-sided boards.

## Key Innovation
First constraint graph-based legalization approach with accurate slicing-based component modeling and MILP formulation for complex heterogeneous components.

## Method
1. **Accurate modeling:** Slicing technique (rectangles vs. bounding boxes)
2. **Constraint representation:** Constraint graphs for spatial relationships
3. **Optimization:** MILP formulation to handle:
   - Heterogeneous components
   - Irregular shapes
   - Any orientation
   - High-density designs
   - Double-sided PCBs

## Advantages
- Accurate component representation
- Handles complex constraints
- Expanded solution space via MILP
- Support for any-oriented components

## Hardware Target
PCB Design/Manufacturing

## Techniques
Circuit design, MILP optimization, constraint graph algorithms

## Evaluation
Demonstrated effectiveness and robustness across diverse component scenarios
