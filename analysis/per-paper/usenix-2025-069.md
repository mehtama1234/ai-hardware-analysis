# KVCache Cache in the Wild: Characterizing and Optimizing KVCache Cache at a Large Cloud Provider

**Problem:** Real-world LLM serving systems lack understanding of actual KVCache access patterns, and cache eviction policies are not optimized for production workloads where design decisions are highly workload-dependent.

**Method:** The paper characterizes KVCache workload patterns from production traces at a leading LLM provider, analyzing reuse distributions across single-turn and multi-turn requests and their temporal properties. Based on observations that reuse time and probability are predictable within specific request categories, they propose a workload-aware cache eviction policy that adapts to these patterns.

**Novelty:** First production-based characterization showing KVCache reuses are skewed and single-turn request reuses are equally important as multi-turn requests, contradicting prior synthetic workload assumptions.
