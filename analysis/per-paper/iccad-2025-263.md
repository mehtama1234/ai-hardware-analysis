# Optimal Selection and Placement of Voltage Regulators in 2.5D Heterogeneously Integrated Systems

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**Paper ID:** iccad-2025-263

## Problem Statement
2.5D heterogeneously integrated systems require optimal voltage regulator (VR) placement for efficient power delivery with heterogeneous chiplet loads and nonuniform power distributions.

## Motivation
Manual VR placement becomes intractable as systems scale; automation is essential for meeting power delivery specifications across diverse chiplet configurations.

## Method
The method treats voltage regulator placement as an optimization puzzle: deciding which regulators to use, where to place them, and what type—each choice expressed as a yes/no decision in a mathematical system. To handle complexity, it uses macromodeling—breaking the system into smaller, simplified models instead of simulating every detail. The algorithm then searches for the best combination of number, location, and type of switched-capacitor voltage regulators (devices that stabilize electrical power using capacitors) to install on the silicon interposer connecting the chiplets.

## Key Novelty
The paper automates voltage regulator selection and placement in 2.5D systems with heterogeneous chiplets using binary-choice mathematical optimization and model simplification.

## Contributions

1. Optimal VR selection and placement
2. Macromodel-based MILP formulation
3. Support for power densities 0.8–4.0 W/mm²
