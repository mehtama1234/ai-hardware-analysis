# Optimal Selection and Placement of Voltage Regulators in 2.5D Heterogeneously Integrated Systems

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**Paper ID:** iccad-2025-263

## Problem Statement
2.5D heterogeneously integrated systems require optimal voltage regulator (VR) placement for efficient power delivery with heterogeneous chiplet loads and nonuniform power distributions.

## Motivation
Manual VR placement becomes intractable as systems scale; automation is essential for meeting power delivery specifications across diverse chiplet configurations.

## Method
Formulates VR selection and placement as 0–1 MILP using macromodeling to manage problem size, optimizing for number, location, and type of switched-capacitor voltage regulators (SCVRs) on active silicon interposer.

## Key Novelty
Automated MILP-based optimization for heterogeneous VR placement in 2.5D systems using macromodeling for scalability.

## Contributions

1. Optimal VR selection and placement
2. Macromodel-based MILP formulation
3. Support for power densities 0.8–4.0 W/mm²
