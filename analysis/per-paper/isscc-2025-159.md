# isscc-2025-159: Monolithic GaN Multilevel Supply Modulator

**Problem:** Base-station 5G/6G transmitters need efficient PA power management. AET limited in bandwidth/complexity for high-voltage apps; DET more flexible but lacks efficient multilevel voltage generation.

**Motivation:** Base-station power consumption is major operational cost; monolithic integration reduces area/cost while improving efficiency.

**Method:** Monolithic GaN multilevel supply modulator separating voltage generation (VG, slow/efficient) and tracking (TR, fast) stages. Multilevel architecture reduces ripple/losses vs. binary. 48V/74W for base-station massive-MIMO PA.

**Key Novelty:** Monolithic GaN multilevel modulator separating slow-generation and fast-tracking for DET efficiency.

**Contributions:**
- Monolithic GaN integration
- Adjustable multilevel voltage generation
- 74W/48V PA supply
- Improved DET efficiency

**Hardware Target:** ASIC

**Technique Categories:** power, circuit-design

**Metrics:** 74W, 48V, monolithic GaN, PA supply

**Baselines:** Analog envelope tracking, conventional DET, prior modulators

**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
