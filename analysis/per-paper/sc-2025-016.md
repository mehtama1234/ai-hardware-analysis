# ORBIT-2: Scaling Exascale Vision Foundation Models for Weather and Climate Downscaling

## Overview
Exascale climate foundation model achieving 0.9 km global resolution downscaling via linear-attention Vision Transformer and billion-token sequences on 65,536 GPUs.

## Problem & Motivation
- **Challenge**: Quadratic self-attention complexity in ViTs prevents long-sequence processing; coarse climate models limit regional decision-making
- **Opportunity**: Exascale parallelism enabling hyper-resolution global climate models with generalization across geographies
- **Scale**: 10 billion parameters across 65,536 GPUs, processing 4.2 billion token sequences

## Architecture & Method
**TILES Algorithm (Linear Attention)**
- Tile-wise sequence scaling reducing O(n²) → O(n) self-attention complexity
- Enables billion-token sequence processing on exascale systems
- Critical for high-resolution, geographically diverse climate data

**Residual Slim ViT (Reslim)**
- Lightweight residual learning architecture
- Bayesian regularization for uncertainty quantification
- Efficient prediction balancing model capacity and computational cost

**Exascale Parallelism**
- 65,536 GPUs achieving 4.1 ExaFLOPS sustained throughput
- 74-98% strong scaling efficiency demonstrating near-linear speedup

## Key Contributions
1. **TILES**: Quadratic → linear attention via tile-wise sequence decomposition
2. **Reslim**: Efficient residual ViT with Bayesian uncertainty
3. **Exascale deployment** achieving sustained ExaFLOP throughput
4. **Global hyper-resolution** downscaling (0.9 km) with high accuracy (R² 0.98-0.99)
5. **Billion-token** sequence handling in production inference

## Performance & Results
- **Prediction Accuracy**: R² 0.98-0.99 on 7 km resolution benchmarks
- **Throughput**: 4.1 ExaFLOPS sustained
- **Scaling Efficiency**: 74-98% on 65,536 GPUs
- **Downscaling**: 0.9 km global resolution
- **Sequence Length**: 4.2 billion tokens

## Hardware Implications
- Demonstrates viability of exascale Foundation Models for climate science
- Highlights importance of attention algorithm efficiency for large-scale models
- GPU-centric deployment (A100, H100 class) with massive parallelism
- Strong scaling efficiency validates distributed training methodology

## Limitations
- Extreme resource requirements limit accessibility (65,536 GPUs)
- TILES implementation complexity may hinder adoption
- Limited inference latency / deployment cost analysis
- Bayesian uncertainty calibration quality not evaluated
