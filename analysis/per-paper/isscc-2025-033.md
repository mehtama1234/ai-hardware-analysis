# isscc-2025-033: Ultra-High-DR Audio Amplifier

**Title:** A 121.3dB-DR, 115dB-PSNR, Digital-Input Capacitive-Feedback Class-D Audio Amplifier with Double-Sided Voltage-Boosting (DSVB) Modulation

**Venue:** ISSCC

## Core Contribution
Single-inductor Class-D audio amplifier achieving 121.3dB dynamic range and 115dB PSNR via capacitive-feedback and double-sided voltage-boosting.

## Key Metrics
- **Dynamic Range:** 121.3dB
- **PSNR:** 115dB
- **Efficiency:** 87%
- **Off-Chip Inductors:** 1 (vs. 2 in prior designs)

## Problem
Battery-powered audio requires simultaneous high dynamic range (>120dB), high efficiency, and minimal inductor count; prior single-stage designs sacrificed DR.

## Method
The amplifier combines three techniques in a single power stage: a buck-boost converter (a circuit that can increase or decrease voltage to boost power output), a noise-reduction circuit using capacitor feedback (the chopper-amplifier technique), and just one external inductor. This integrated single-stage design achieves a 121.3dB dynamic range—ability to accurately reproduce very quiet and very loud sounds together—at 87% efficiency, with only 13% power loss. By combining voltage conversion, noise reduction, and power delivery in one stage instead of separating them, the design is both smaller and more efficient than previous approaches that required multiple conversion stages.
