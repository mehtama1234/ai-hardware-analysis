# isscc-2025-114: Xiling—Cryo-CMOS Quantum Gate Controller

**Problem:** Silicon spin qubits require ultra-precise, ultra-low-noise gate biasing at 60mK; room-temperature electronics introduce excessive noise and cabling complexity.

**Method:** Xiling co-integrates (combines on the same chip) two independent ultra-precise voltage generators (18-bit dual-DAC—circuits that convert digital numbers into exact analog voltages) directly with the single-electron transistor at cryogenic temperatures (60mK). Normally, quantum chips require long cables to distant room-temperature electronics (300K), which introduces noise and signal degradation; this design keeps the voltage generators cold and on-chip to eliminate those problems. Special low-power circuits designed for extreme-cold operation deliver 4.6µV precision (18-bit level control) and 4.1nV/√Hz noise floor—the precision required for reliable quantum gate control.

**Key Novelty:** Cryo-CMOS co-integrated DAC achieving 4.6µV precision and 4.1nV/√Hz noise at 60mK for high-fidelity quantum control.

**Main Results:** 18-bit precision, 4.6µV gate control, 4.1nV/√Hz noise, co-integrated with SET.
