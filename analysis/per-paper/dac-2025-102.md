# DANN: Diffractive Acoustic Neural Network for in-sensor computing system target at multi-biomarker diagnosis

**Venue:** DAC 2025  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Implementing diffractive neural networks on integrated acoustic (SAW) systems requires novel training methodologies beyond optical DNN implementations. Hardware constraints of acoustic wave propagation complicate traditional backpropagation.

## Key Insight
Co-training combining Finite Element Analysis (physics simulation) with gradient-descent enables efficient optimization of diffractive patterns for acoustic hardware without expensive full FEA iterations.

## Approach
- Diffractive neural network applied to surface acoustic wave (SAW) systems
- Novel co-training method: FEA simulation + gradient descent optimization
- Analog in-sensor computing architecture eliminates digital processing overhead
- Evaluated on biomarker diagnosis: MDD and prostate cancer detection

## Results
- **MDD diagnosis:** 74.07% accuracy (near clinical levels)
- **Prostate cancer:** 86.0% accuracy
- **Energy efficiency:** 66% savings vs. traditional digital circuits
- Physics-aware training maintains hardware fidelity while enabling efficient convergence

## Hardware Target
- Analog (integrated acoustic wave)

- Near-data processing / in-sensor computing
- Circuit design
