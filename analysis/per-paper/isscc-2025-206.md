# ISSCC 2025-206: Floating-Point CIM with Broadcast Alignment & N2C-MAC

## Core Contribution
Broadcast-alignment FP-CIM macro achieving 62.84 TFLOPS/W (BF16) via embedded lightweight convertor, non-2's-complement MAC exploiting negative-weight sparsity, and dual-bit serial architecture.

## Technical Approach
- **Input Architecture**: Broadcast input + embedded lightweight convertor (vs. peripheral parallel schemes)
- **Alignment**: Adaptive lightweight alignment with dual-bit serial MAC
- **MAC Format**: Non-2's-complement (N2CMAC) exploiting high negative-weight sparsity in neural networks
- **Precision**: Format-mixed BF16/INT8 support
- **CIM Core**: 64kb array in 28nm CMOS

## Performance Metrics
- **BF16 Efficiency**: 62.84 TFLOPS/W
- **INT8 Efficiency**: 90.15 TOPS/W
- **Macro Size**: 64kb (compact for CIM)
- **Technology**: 28nm CMOS

## Baselines
- Prior FP-CIM with peripheral alignment (larger area/power overhead)
- Digital FP32 MAC (5–10x lower energy efficiency)
- Integer-only CIM designs (insufficient accuracy for FP-critical models like transformers)

## Hardware Impact
Addresses precision vs. efficiency tradeoff for transformer edge inference. FP precision maintains accuracy while CIM architecture + N2C-MAC + broadcast optimization keeps energy competitive with INT8 approaches, enabling deployment of FP-precision models on edge accelerators.
