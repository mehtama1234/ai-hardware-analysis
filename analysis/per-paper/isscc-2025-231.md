# isscc-2025-231: 224Gb/s XSR Receiver with Slice-Based CTLE

**Problem:** Extra-short reach (XSR) 224Gb/s receivers require simultaneous CTLE bandwidth doubling and 8x sampling frequency increase while constrained by power and area budgets.

**Approach:** 12nm CMOS receiver using slice-based continuous-time linear equalizer (CTLE) distributing filtering across multiple stages, plus phase-interpolator (PI)-based eight-phase clock generator.

**Key novelty:** Slice-based CTLE architecture enabling efficient bandwidth scaling to 224Gb/s.

**Metrics:** 1.11pJ/bit energy efficiency, 224Gb/s data rate.

**Tags:** receiver, transceiver, high-speed, xsr, ctle, energy-efficient
