# TOGGLE6.0: A 4.8Gbps Next Generation Area and Power Efficient Transceiver for Flash Memory Interface

**Venue:** VLSID 2025  
**Authors:** Hari Vijay Venkatanarayanan, Rustum Prasad Sahu, Maheswara Alamuru, Ergam Reddy Battini, Syed Mohammed Haroon, Saurav Suman, Deepika Mallela, Sanjeeb Kumar Ghosh

## Problem Statement
High-speed NAND flash interfaces (UFS) in mobile devices require 4.8 Gbps data rates while minimizing area and power consumption. Existing transceiver designs exhibit excessive pad capacitance, device stress on drivers, and marginal receiver timing margins, limiting integration density and increasing power overhead.

## Key Contributions
1. **Compact Driver Design**: 42.47% reduction in driver area through thin-oxide device optimization
2. **Reduced Pad Capacitance**: 23% capacitance reduction enabling better signal integrity
3. **Power Efficiency**: 24.65% average power reduction in low-power operation mode
4. **Improved Receiver Margins**: 15% improvement in eye margin enabling faster operation
5. **Compact Receiver**: 10.29% area reduction in receiver circuits
6. **Scalable Architecture**: Multi-tap equalization approach extensible to future higher data rates

## Technical Approach
- **Driver Optimization**: Thin-oxide devices to reduce parasitic pad capacitance while maintaining performance
- **Stress Mitigation**: Elevated ground voltage generator to reduce device stress and hot-carrier degradation in drivers
- **Signal Integrity**: Multi-tap current-based equalization scheme with minimal area overhead
- **Receiver Tuning**: Per-receiver VREF (reference voltage) adjustment to improve timing margins and optimize receiver sensitivity
- **Data Rate**: 4.8 Gbps toggle-mode signaling

## Hardware Target
- **Interface**: NAND Flash Memory / UFS (Universal Flash Storage)
- **Application Domain**: Mobile SoCs, embedded storage controllers
- **Integration**: On-die transceiver for compact mobile devices
- **Signaling**: Toggle-mode (dual-edge clocked)
