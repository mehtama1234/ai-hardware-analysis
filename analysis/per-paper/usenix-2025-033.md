# Obscura: Concealing Recomputation Overhead in Training of Large Language Models with Bubble-filling Pipeline Transformation

**Problem:** Pipeline parallelism in LLM training creates idle periods (bubbles) during gradient computation stages, and gradient checkpointing requires expensive recomputation. These overheads reduce effective hardware utilization and increase training time.

**Method:** When training large language models with pipeline parallelism (splitting computation into stages running in parallel), idle periods called bubbles naturally occur as stages wait for each other. Gradient checkpointing normally requires expensive recomputation that adds to training time, but Obscura schedules this work to run during the bubble periods where hardware would otherwise sit idle. By overlapping recomputation with the waiting time, the extra work becomes hidden and doesn't extend the total training duration.

**Novelty:** Scheduling gradient recomputation to run during the idle waiting periods (bubbles) in pipeline-parallel training.
