# Obscura: Concealing Recomputation Overhead in Training of Large Language Models with Bubble-filling Pipeline Transformation

**Problem:** Pipeline parallelism in LLM training creates idle periods (bubbles) during gradient computation stages, and gradient checkpointing requires expensive recomputation. These overheads reduce effective hardware utilization and increase training time.

**Method:** Obscura applies a pipeline schedule transformation that positions gradient recomputation tasks to execute during the idle bubble periods that occur naturally in pipeline parallelism. By overlapping recomputation with pipeline stages rather than executing sequentially, the recomputation overhead becomes partially hidden within pipeline bubbles, reducing overall critical path length.

**Novelty:** Leveraging pipeline bubble periods as hidden execution slots for recomputation through intelligent schedule transformation.
