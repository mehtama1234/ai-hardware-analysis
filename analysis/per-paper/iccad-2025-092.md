# PPAAS: PVT and Pareto Aware Analog Sizing via Goal-conditioned Reinforcement Learning

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-092

## Problem
Device sizing in analog and mixed-signal circuits requires optimization across diverse performance specifications under process, voltage, and temperature (PVT) variations.

## Motivation
Enable generalized RL-based device sizing that efficiently adapts to wide range of design specifications across PVT corners without excessive training overhead.

## Method
Goal-conditioned RL framework with:
- Pareto-front Dominance Goal Sampling for automatic curriculum learning
- Conservative Hindsight Experience Replay for training stability
- Skip-on-Fail simulation strategy for efficiency reduction

## Key Novelty
First goal-conditioned RL framework enabling efficient policy training for analog device sizing with strong generalization across PVT corners.

## Contributions
- 1.6x improvement in sample efficiency compared to existing sizing methods
- 4.1x improvement in simulation efficiency through Skip-on-Fail strategy
- Pareto-front-based curriculum learning for stable training
- Publicly available code and benchmarks
