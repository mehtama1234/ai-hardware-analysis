# PPAAS: PVT and Pareto Aware Analog Sizing via Goal-conditioned Reinforcement Learning

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-092

## Problem
Device sizing in analog and mixed-signal circuits requires optimization across diverse performance specifications under process, voltage, and temperature (PVT) variations.

## Motivation
Enable generalized RL-based device sizing that efficiently adapts to wide range of design specifications across PVT corners without excessive training overhead.

## Method
The approach uses reinforcement learning—where an algorithm learns by trial and error to reach target goals—to automatically choose component values in analog circuits. It creates an automatic curriculum by sampling training targets from the Pareto frontier (the set of designs where improving one performance metric means sacrificing another), selecting targets based on dominance relationships to prioritize harder problems. During training, it learns from past failed design attempts using Conservative Hindsight Experience Replay (a technique that reuses failed trials carefully to keep training stable), and avoids running expensive simulations that would fail.

## Key Novelty
This is the first goal-conditioned reinforcement learning framework enabling efficient policy training for analog device sizing with strong generalization across PVT corners (the different combinations of manufacturing process, voltage, and temperature variations).

## Contributions
- 1.6x improvement in sample efficiency compared to existing sizing methods
- 4.1x improvement in simulation efficiency through Skip-on-Fail strategy
- Pareto-front-based curriculum learning for stable training
- Publicly available code and benchmarks
