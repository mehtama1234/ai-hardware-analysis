# Graph-Guided Transfer Learning to Boost the Efficiency of System-Level Optimization of Analog/Mixed-Signal Circuits
**Venue:** DAC

## Problem
System-level optimization of analog/mixed-signal circuits requires expensive iterative circuit simulations; transfer learning has limited applicability across dissimilar architectures.

## Motivation
Analog circuit design is a labor-intensive optimization bottleneck; faster design automation through knowledge transfer across circuit families can accelerate development.

## Method
Uses reinforcement learning with graph attention networks (neural networks that analyze circuit connections and identify important patterns) to find electrical trade-offs. A graph similarity detector recognizes when different circuits have structurally similar designs, allowing the system to transfer optimization strategies learned on one circuit to other structurally similar circuits, avoiding expensive repeated simulations.

## Key Novelty
Using graph similarity detection to identify when analog circuits have matching structures, enabling transfer of optimization strategies across different circuit architectures.

## Contributions
1. 11x reduction in simulation count via transfer learning on analog circuits
2. 12.4% improvement in optimization quality vs. from-scratch baseline
3. Generalizable framework applicable to broad range of analog/mixed-signal systems
