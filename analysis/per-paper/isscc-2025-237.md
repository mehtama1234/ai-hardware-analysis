# isscc-2025-237: Full-Datapath CIM for Compound AI with Adaptive Sparsity

**Problem:** Compound AI (multiple specialized models) offers edge deployment but CIM designs assume Gaussian data distribution (breaks with diverse models), perform only MACs (26-72% power in partial-sum movement), exploit rigid model-specific sparsity (incompatible with compound flexibility).

**Approach:** Full-datapath SRAM FP CIM macro integrating partial-sum/residual accumulation and flexible sparsity detection. Adaptive alignment handles arbitrary data distributions; integrated datapath reduces data-movement overhead.

**Key novelty:** Full-datapath CIM with integrated partial-sum accumulation and flexible sparsity patterns, approaching theoretical speedup bound.

**Metrics:** 51.6TFLOPS/W, accuracy loss <-30dB for compound-AI models.

**Tags:** cim, compound-ai, sparsity, llm, full-datapath, edge-deployment
