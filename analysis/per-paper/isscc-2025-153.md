# isscc-2025-153: Dynamic-LLI Wireless Implant Power Transfer

**Problem:** Wireless implant power transfer faces intrinsic PTE–data-rate trade-off; receiver coil size is the miniaturization bottleneck, and frequency-splitting WPDT still requires large coils for critical coupling.

**Motivation:** Minimizing implant invasiveness requires eliminating the coil-size constraint; frequency-splitting helps but not enough without novel coupling enhancement.

**Method:** Enhanced-frequency-splitting WPDT with dynamic link-load isolation (LLI) via switchable receiver tank. LLI reduces effective load resistance seen by the link, lowering critical coupling coefficient proportionally to loaded Q. Dual-frequency flat-region FSK (f_H, f_L) maintains power and data with dynamic LLI enabling sub-cm coils.

**Key Novelty:** Dynamic link-load isolation through switchable receiver LC tank achieving 10× receiver coil reduction.

**Contributions:**
- 60.2% end-to-end efficiency
- 1 Mb/s data rate without trade-off
- 10× coil-size reduction (sub-cm receiver)
- Frequency splitting enhancement

**Hardware Target:** ASIC

**Technique Categories:** power, circuit-design, wireless

**Metrics:** 60.2% efficiency, 1 Mb/s, 10× coil shrinkage

**Baselines:** OOK/FSK/PSK WPDT, conventional frequency-splitting

**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
