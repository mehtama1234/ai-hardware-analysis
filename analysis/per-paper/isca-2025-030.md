# isca-2025-030 — Rethinking Prefetching for Intermittent Computing

**Venue:** ISCA  
**Confidence:** low (abstract only)

## Problem
Conventional hardware prefetchers are energy-wasteful on batteryless energy harvesting systems (EHSs) because they aggressively fetch cache lines that are lost on power failure before any hit. Frequent outages mean prefetched data never accessed before the next power loss represents pure energy overhead.

## Motivation
Batteryless IoT and edge devices powered by energy harvesting experience frequent outages, making energy efficiency the primary design constraint and rendering conventional prefetchers actively harmful.

## Method
IPEX (Intermittence-aware Prefetching EXtension) is an add-on layer that sits on top of existing prefetchers (hardware that tries to fetch data before it's needed) on batteryless energy-harvesting systems, without replacing them. It tracks which prefetched data blocks actually get used before power fails, then suppresses prefetching of blocks that historically go unused before the next outage (reducing 'useless prefetches'). It also monitors harvested energy levels to predict imminent power failures and, at that moment, throttles (reduces) how aggressively it prefetches, targeting only data blocks the processor is likely to use before power loss. This way, harvested energy is redirected from futile prefetches toward making actual progress on the program.

## Key Novelty
IPEX is the first prefetch extension that actively suppresses prefetches based on tracking data usage and predicting power failures, converting energy that would be wasted on cache lines never accessed into forward progress.

## Contributions
1. Characterization of useless prefetches as a dominant energy-waste source in energy harvesting systems.
2. IPEX mechanism integrating with any existing prefetcher to suppress historically useless prefetches.
3. Outage-aware throttling of prefetch degree before predicted power loss.
4. 7.86% avg energy reduction (up to 21.64%) and 8.96% avg performance improvement (up to 23.49%) vs conventional prefetcher.
