# FAB: Fast and Demand-Aware Bandwidth Allocation Method for Wavelength-Routed Optical Networks-on-Chip

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-028

## Problem
Wavelength-routed optical NoCs require optimization of bandwidth allocation via core-to-port mapping and microring resonator radii configuration; existing methods are slow and achieve poor quality solutions for large applications.

## Motivation
Optical interconnects offer high bandwidth and low power for on-chip communication; requires efficient optimization methods.

## Method
Graph-based approach formulating core-to-port mapping and MRR radii configuration as two-stage weighted subgraph matching with strict weight-based strategies and progressive relaxation for infeasible cases.

## Key Novelty
Novel two-stage graph-based approach for fast demand-aware bandwidth allocation in WRONoC.

## Contributions
- Graph-based formulation for WRONoC bandwidth allocation
- Two-stage weighted subgraph matching approach
- Weight-based matching strategies with progressive relaxation
- 87.5% latency reduction with 500,000x speedup vs SOTA
