# Deep Learning-Enabled Supercritical Flame Simulation at Detailed Chemistry and Real-Fluid Accuracy Towards Trillion-Cell Scale

**Venue:** SC
**ID:** sc-2025-126
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Supercritical flame simulations with detailed chemistry and real-fluid transport have been limited to millions of cells due to computational cost.

## Motivation
Next-generation rocket engines and high-energy systems require high-fidelity simulations at unprecedented scale.

## Method
DeepFlame: neural-network-enabled simulator with optimizations in parallel computing, efficiency, and I/O; tuned for Sunway and Fugaku.

## Key Novelty
Trillion-cell flame simulation with deep learning + real-fluid accuracy on leadership supercomputers

## Contributions
1. 618B/154B cell simulations (Sunway/Fugaku)
2. 439/1186 PFlop/s on Sunway FP32/FP16
3. 187/316 PFlop/s on Fugaku FP32/FP16
4. First >100-injector LOX/CH4 engine sim
