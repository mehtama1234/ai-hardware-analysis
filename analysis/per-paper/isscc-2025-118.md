# isscc-2025-118: Codec-Aware 4K Video Super-Resolution ISP

**Problem:** Frame-independent VSR ignores temporal/spatial redundancies in video encoding (I/P/B frames, motion vectors, residuals), causing inefficiency.

**Method:** Instead of treating each frame as a standalone image, this processor uses the structure built into video codecs. Modern video codecs already compute motion vectors (which direction pixels moved) and residuals (the pixel differences they don't need to store) to compress efficiently—this processor feeds those directly into its super-resolution pipeline instead of starting from scratch. It processes multiple frames together through alignment and refinement stages, reusing information across frames and using pre-computed codec data. This approach achieves 210fps performance at 4K resolution with lower energy than conventional super-resolution.

**Key Novelty:** Codec-aware VSR architecture directly exploiting video encoding structure for high-throughput, low-energy super-resolution.

**Main Results:** 210fps 4K Ultra HD VSR, efficient temporal/spatial redundancy exploitation.
