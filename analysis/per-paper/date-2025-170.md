# Neural Circuit Parameter Prediction for Efficient Quantum Data Loading

**Venue**: DATE  
**Primary Theme**: Neural network-predicted quantum circuits  

## Problem
Quantum data loading—encoding classical data into quantum states—is a critical bottleneck in quantum machine learning; existing methods achieve high per-sample accuracy but lack efficiency for large-scale batch loading.

## Method
Proposes Neural Circuit Parameter Prediction using classical deep neural networks to predict parameterized quantum circuit parameters directly from input data; leverages batch inference capability of DNNs; introduces real-valued circuit parameterization and three-phase training strategy for improved efficiency.

## Key Novelty
DNN-based parameter prediction for quantum circuits enabling efficient batch quantum data loading.

## Contributions
- Neural Circuit Parameter Prediction methodology for batch-efficient quantum data loading
- Real-valued parameterization of quantum circuits
- Three-phase training strategy improving training efficiency and accuracy
- 17.31% infidelity improvement and 108x speedup versus existing methods on MNIST
