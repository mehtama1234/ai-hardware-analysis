# isscc-2025-151: Phase-Domain Capacitance-to-Digital Converter

**Problem:** Capacitive displacement sensors suffer high-frequency EMI on floating targets, requiring robust dual-band noise rejection while maintaining 500pF dynamic range.

**Motivation:** Nanomechatronic systems need precise, EMI-immune displacement sensing; capacitive sensors are energy-efficient but vulnerable to environmental interference.

**Method:** Phase-Domain-Delta-Sigma modulator (PD-ΔΣM) with lowpass RC front-end converts capacitance to phase shift, then digitizes with high-resolution modulation. Dual-band filtering: 119dB CMRR @ DC, 97dB @ 100MHz.

**Key Novelty:** Phase-domain ΔΣM architecture eliminating sub-ranging complexity while achieving both high dynamic range and dual-band EMI rejection.

**Contributions:**
- 143dB dynamic range (35.2aF–500pF)
- 119dB DC CMRR + 97dB @ 100MHz
- No sub-ranging required
- <24.7ppm/°C temperature drift

**Hardware Target:** ASIC

**Technique Categories:** circuit-design, analog

**Metrics:** 143dB DR, 119dB CMRR, 97dB EMI rejection

**Baselines:** Traditional capacitive sensors, eddy-current displacement sensors

**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
