# isscc-2025-163: Intrinsically Linear DPD-Less mm-Wave RFDAC

**Problem:** RF digital-to-analog converters suffer mm-wave nonlinearity from code-dependent impedance, parasitics, and LO leakage. Existing solutions (DPD, open-loop, self-calibration) are frequency-dependent, PVT-variant, or power-hungry.

**Motivation:** mm-Wave 5G/6G wireless demands high-linearity, wideband RF signal generation without DPD complexity and power overhead.

**Method:** 21–31GHz RFDAC with invariant output impedance (code-independent unit cells) and scalable adaptive LO-leakage suppression (no feedback loops). Achieves inherent high linearity suitable for high-order QAM without DPD or external calibration.

**Key Novelty:** Intrinsically linear RFDAC with invariant impedance and adaptive LO-leakage suppression.

**Contributions:**
- 21–31GHz broadband DPD-less RFDAC
- Invariant impedance design
- Scalable LO-leakage suppression
- High-linearity QAM without complex baseband

**Hardware Target:** ASIC

**Technique Categories:** circuit-design, analog, rf-design

**Metrics:** 21–31GHz, DPD-less, invariant impedance, scalable LO-leakage

**Baselines:** DPD RFDACs, open-loop/self-calibration schemes, prior mm-wave

**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
