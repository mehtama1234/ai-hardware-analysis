# Microcredentials for Open Hardware and HPC Workforce Development: The Openchip Approach with RISC-V Ecosystem

**ID:** sc-2025-045  
**Venue:** SC  
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
Skills gap in HPC and semiconductor sectors; traditional academic programs cannot keep pace with rapid technological evolution in open hardware

## Motivation
EU Chips Act and national sovereignty initiatives demand workforce development; open platforms like RISC-V require new educational approaches

## Method
The approach breaks workforce training into small, stackable microcredentials (bite-sized certifications) that can be updated quickly as technology evolves, with curricula co-designed by educators and industry partners. It focuses on RISC-V architectures (an open-source chip instruction set) and full-stack HPC system design—meaning students learn both the hardware fundamentals and how to write software that runs efficiently across complete computing systems.

## Key Novelty
The innovation is using microcredentials aligned with TCPP curriculum standards to teach open hardware and HPC at scale, so training can update rapidly and stay current with technology.

## Contributions
1. Proposes microcredential strategy for HPC workforce development
2. Analyzes curricular gaps in open hardware training
3. Provides roadmap for embedding microcredentials in sovereign HPC ecosystems
