# Insights from Optimizing HPL Performance on Exascale Systems: A Comparative Analysis of Panel Factorization

**Venue:** SC
**Paper ID:** sc-2025-003
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
HPL performance on exascale systems is sensitive to many interacting parameters; optimizing panel factorization for heterogeneous GPU-CPU systems is non-trivial

## Motivation
Exascale systems like Frontier are architecturally complex; HPL is primary benchmark for evaluating supercomputing performance; parameter tuning guidance is essential

## Method
Comprehensive empirical HPL analysis on Frontier; detailed modeling and comparative evaluation; two novel PDFACT strategies: dedicated-thread (DT) and GPU-based (GPUPDFACT) using HIP cooperative groups

## Key Novelty
GPU-based panel factorization (GPUPDFACT) outperforming CPU-based PDFACT on Frontier; systematic performance insights and best practices for exascale tuning

## Contributions
