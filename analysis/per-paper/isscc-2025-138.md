# ISSCC-2025-138: UCIe-Compliant Chiplet D2D Link in 2.5D CoWoS

**Problem:**  
Heterogeneous chiplet-based SiPs require high-bandwidth, low-power, low-latency die-to-die links, but proprietary solutions limit interoperability across vendors.

**Solution:**  
UCIe-compliant 4-to-16Gb/s/pin transceiver link in 3nm FinFET within 2.5D CoWoS-S package, proving standardized chiplet interconnects can match custom proprietary efficiency.

**Key Results:**  
- 5.27 Tb/s/mm bandwidth density
- 0.29 pJ/b energy efficiency
- Low BER, low latency

**Key Innovation:**  
First full UCIe standard-compliant D2D implementation in 2.5D packaging, proving standards enable vendor interoperability without sacrificing efficiency.
