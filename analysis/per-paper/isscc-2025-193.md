# isscc-2025-193: V-Band Power Amplifier with Radial Power Combining

**Problem**: 5G/SATCOM at V-band (56–64GHz) require high linear power (>30dBm) with high efficiency (>20% PAE) in voltage-limited CMOS; traditional combining techniques suffer impedance mismatches and routing losses at scale.

**Solution**: Scalable matched-zone-expanding radial power combiner with EM-loss reduction. Radial geometry reduces parasitic routing; matched-zone expansion maintains constant impedance transformation regardless of combining scale; EM-aware layout minimizes losses.

**Key Results**:
- 30.2dBm output power (Psat)
- 23.5% peak PAE (high efficiency)
- 56–64GHz frequency range (V-band)
- Scalable impedance matching
- Reduced combiner losses vs linear topologies

**Hardware**: ASIC (40nm CMOS)  
**Application**: 5G NR FR2-2 / SATCOM  
**Confidence**: low (abstract only)
