# Multi-FPGA Synchronization and Data Communication for Quantum Control and Measurement

**Problem:** Practical quantum computers with thousands of qubits require scalable control systems with nanosecond-scale clock synchronization and low-latency data communication within hundreds of nanoseconds. Most open-source solutions are limited to single-board RFSoC implementations, while commercial multi-FPGA solutions are prohibitively expensive.

**Method:** The system synchronizes multiple FPGAs using a simplified version of Precision Time Protocol (PTP), which keeps their clocks aligned to nanosecond precision. For fast communication between the FPGAs, it uses Aurora protocol, a high-speed serial standard that keeps data transfer delays under hundreds of nanoseconds. These synchronization and communication layers are integrated with QubiC, an open-source quantum control and measurement system, to create a complete, scalable solution for controlling thousands of qubits across multiple FPGA boards.

**Novelty:** It combines a simplified Precision Time Protocol for synchronizing multiple FPGA clocks to nanosecond precision with Aurora protocol for low-latency data communication, enabling a practical multi-FPGA quantum control system.
