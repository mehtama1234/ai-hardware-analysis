# Multi-FPGA Synchronization and Data Communication for Quantum Control and Measurement

**Problem:** Practical quantum computers with thousands of qubits require scalable control systems with nanosecond-scale clock synchronization and low-latency data communication within hundreds of nanoseconds. Most open-source solutions are limited to single-board RFSoC implementations, while commercial multi-FPGA solutions are prohibitively expensive.

**Method:** The design implements minimal Precision Time Protocol (PTP) for clock synchronization across multiple FPGAs and uses Aurora protocol for low-latency data communication for mid-circuit measurement (MCM). The system is integrated with QubiC, an open-source quantum control and measurement system, to provide a complete scalable multi-FPGA solution.

**Novelty:** Implementation of practical multi-FPGA clock synchronization and data communication for quantum control systems using minimal PTP and Aurora protocol.
