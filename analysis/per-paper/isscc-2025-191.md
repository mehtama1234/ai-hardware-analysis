# isscc-2025-191: Ultra-Low-Jitter 13GHz Charge-Pump PLL

**Problem**: RF-sampling systems require ultra-pure clock signals with low jitter and minimal reference spurs; traditional charge-pump PLLs struggle to achieve sub-fs jitter while suppressing spurs.

**Solution**: Dual-path charge-pump PLL with resistive-discharge time-amplifying phase-frequency detector (RD-TAPFD) and series-resonance VCO enabling ultra-low phase noise and spur suppression.

**Key Results**:
- 15.8fsrms integrated jitter (state-of-the-art CPPLL)
- -98.5dBc reference spur (near ideal)
- 13GHz center frequency
- RD-TAPFD improves CP phase noise
- SR-VCO sub-fs jitter contribution

**Hardware**: ASIC  
**Application**: RF-sampling / clock generation  
**Confidence**: low (abstract only)
