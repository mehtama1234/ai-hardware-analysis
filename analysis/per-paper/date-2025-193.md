# FDAIMC: Fully-Differential Analog In-Memory-Computing for MAC in MRAM with Accuracy Calibration

**Venue:** DATE  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Core Idea
Implement STT-MRAM analog in-memory-computing with fully-differential architecture, in-domain calibration, and novel mixed-signal converters to maintain accuracy across process and voltage corners.

## Problem
STT-MRAM's low on/off ratio degrades AIMC performance; process/voltage variation causes unpredictable errors; analog/digital signal incompatibility complicates calibration.

## Method
The circuit uses pairs of magnetic memory cells (STT-MRAM) to perform multiply-accumulate math on data that stays in memory. Since STT-MRAM produces weak signals that become corrupted by manufacturing defects and power supply changes, the design converts voltage readings into timing signals (measuring pulse duration), which are stable and resistant to these variations. The timing is then converted back into the final computation result. The system automatically adjusts its calibration whenever manufacturing differences or power fluctuations occur, keeping computations accurate without requiring a separate analog-to-digital converter.

## Key Results
- **95.44% accuracy** (TT corner)
- **97.8% accuracy** with calibration (FF corner)
- **88.6% accuracy** (SS corner, +30% with calibration)
- **87.2-312.4 TOPS/W** energy efficiency
- **1.64-21.18x area FoM** vs state of the art
