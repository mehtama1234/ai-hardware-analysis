# ChronoTE: Crosstalk-Aware Timing Estimation for Routing Optimization via Edge-Enhanced GNNs

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-082

## Problem
Accurate timing estimation during routing is critical but challenging at advanced nodes due to crosstalk effects. Existing estimation methods are overly pessimistic because they consider coupling capacitance alone without accounting for relative signal arrival times affecting crosstalk-induced delay.

## Motivation
Accurate sign-off quality delay estimation during early global routing can significantly accelerate design closure and reduce chip area by enabling better routing optimization decisions.

## Method
ChronoTE uses a graph neural network—a machine learning model that processes interconnected structures—to estimate how long signals take to travel through chip wiring while accounting for crosstalk (interference between nearby wires). Rather than the traditional pessimistic approach of only considering coupling capacitance, it jointly models the physical wiring layout and timing characteristics, encoding timing-window-aware features (information about when signals arrive) into the connections between nodes. This enables accurate delay prediction early in the design process, before the expensive and time-consuming steps of full routing or parasitic extraction.

## Key Novelty
The key innovation is using a graph neural network enhanced with timing-window-aware features (information about signal arrival times) to predict crosstalk delays accurately during early routing without expensive parasitic extraction.

## Contributions
- Novel edge-enhanced GNN architecture with timing-window-aware feature embedding
- Crosstalk-aware delay estimation considering both capacitance and signal timing relationships
- Sign-off quality predictions achievable in early global routing stage
- Demonstrated area reduction and design closure acceleration on industrial-scale open-source designs
