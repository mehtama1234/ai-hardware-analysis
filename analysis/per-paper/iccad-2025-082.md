# ChronoTE: Crosstalk-Aware Timing Estimation for Routing Optimization via Edge-Enhanced GNNs

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-082

## Problem
Accurate timing estimation during routing is critical but challenging at advanced nodes due to crosstalk effects. Existing estimation methods are overly pessimistic because they consider coupling capacitance alone without accounting for relative signal arrival times affecting crosstalk-induced delay.

## Motivation
Accurate sign-off quality delay estimation during early global routing can significantly accelerate design closure and reduce chip area by enabling better routing optimization decisions.

## Method
ChronoTE is an edge-enhanced graph neural network framework that performs crosstalk-aware net delay estimation by jointly modeling physical topology and timing characteristics. The approach embeds timing-window-aware features into edge representations to enable accurate delay prediction without requiring full routing or parasitic extraction.

## Key Novelty
Edge-enhanced GNN framework incorporating timing-window-aware features for accurate crosstalk-aware delay prediction during early routing stages without full parasitic extraction.

## Contributions
- Novel edge-enhanced GNN architecture with timing-window-aware feature embedding
- Crosstalk-aware delay estimation considering both capacitance and signal timing relationships
- Sign-off quality predictions achievable in early global routing stage
- Demonstrated area reduction and design closure acceleration on industrial-scale open-source designs
