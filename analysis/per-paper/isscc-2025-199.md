# ISSCC 2025-199: Energy-Efficient BJT Temperature Sensor

## Core Contribution
NPN-based temperature sensor using noise-optimized charge-balancing continuous-time ΔΣ modulator achieving record energy efficiency (80fJ.K^2 FoM) with ±0.1°C accuracy in compact 0.05mm^2 area.

## Technical Approach
- **Frontend**: BJT PTAT/CTAT current generation
- **Quantization**: Charge-balancing CTΔΣM (continuous-time delta-sigma modulator)
- **Gain**: Current-assisted amplifier for noise optimization and conversion gain
- **Accuracy**: 1-point trim calibration typical for BJT sensors, achieving ±0.1°C (3σ)

## Performance Metrics
- **Energy Efficiency**: 80fJ.K^2 (2.5x better than prior CTΔΣM BJT sensors)
- **Temperature Range**: -70°C to 125°C
- **Accuracy**: ±0.1°C (3σ)
- **Area**: 0.05mm^2 (1.5x smaller than comparable designs)

## Baselines
- Prior charge-balancing CTΔΣM BJT sensors
- Resistor-based temperature sensors (higher energy consumption historically)
- Conventional discrete-time BJT sensors

## Hardware Impact
Enables on-chip thermal management for high-performance processors with minimal power overhead. Superior energy efficiency makes feasible integration of multiple distributed thermal sensors for fine-grained power/thermal control.
