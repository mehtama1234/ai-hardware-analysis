# Adapting Classic Scheduling Heuristics for Online Execution under Uncertainty

**ID:** sc-2025-060 | **Venue:** SC

## Problem Statement
Task scheduling on heterogeneous distributed systems with incomplete future knowledge

## Motivation
Real-world systems require online scheduling decisions without full task graph knowledge; classical offline schedulers (HEFT, CPoP) assume complete information unavailable at runtime

## Method
Modular parametric online scheduler adapting classical list-scheduling heuristics with dynamic decision-making based on evolving execution state

## Key Novelty
Extends HEFT/CPoP scheduling strategies to online setting with adaptive behavior; achieves near-offline performance (3-5% overhead vs 10% for naive baseline)

## Contributions
- Online scheduling framework bridging classical offline and online approaches
- Dynamic scheduling variants for different estimation methods and CCR settings
- Comprehensive evaluation on scientific workflow benchmarks
