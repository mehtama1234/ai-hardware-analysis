# Few-shot Learning on AMS Circuits and Its Application to Parasitic Capacitance Prediction

**Venue:** DAC | **Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem & Motivation
AMS circuit design needs rapid parasitic extraction for timing/power closure; deep learning requires large labeled datasets that are scarce in IC design.

## Core Method
CircuitGPS: heterogeneous graph representation of AMS circuits (component nodes, coupling-capacitance links) with hybrid Transformer for subgraph embeddings. Pre-trains on link prediction (coupling existence?), fine-tunes on edge regression (coupling magnitude). Small-hop sampling + low-cost positional encoding.

## Key Contribution
Few-shot graph learning on heterogeneous circuit representations enabling zero-shot transfer to diverse AMS designs.

## Results
- ≥20% coupling existence accuracy gain
- ≥0.067 MAE reduction on capacitance estimation
- Zero-shot scalability to diverse AMS designs
