# isscc-2025-128: 4.6GHz Low-Jitter PLL with Crystal Oscillator Co-Design

**Problem:** Phase-locked loop (PLL) jitter and power optimization historically neglects the full frequency-generation chain including crystal oscillator (XO) driver, reference buffer, and PLL itself, resulting in suboptimal overall system jitter figure-of-merit.

**Motivation:** State-of-the-art PLLs achieve sub-50fs integrated jitter with few mW power; however, co-optimization of PLL and XO subsystems can further reduce jitter and power by addressing the driver and reference buffer contributions.

**Method:** The PLL-XO co-design employs a self-aligned pulse-injection driver that optimizes the interaction between crystal oscillator and PLL, achieving 4.6GHz operation with 63.3fs integrated jitter while including XO power and noise in a unified -255.2dB figure-of-merit.

**Key Novelty:** Self-aligned pulse-injection driver enabling co-design optimization of PLL and crystal oscillator for improved jitter figure-of-merit including full system power and noise.

**Contributions:**
- PLL-XO co-design approach optimizing full frequency-generation chain
- Self-aligned pulse-injection driver architecture
- 4.6GHz operation with 63.3fs integrated jitter
- -255.2dB FoMJ including XO power and noise
