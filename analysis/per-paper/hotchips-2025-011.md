# High Density Si-IPD Technologies as Enabler for High-Performance and Low-Power Consumption Processor Chips

**Venue:** HOTCHIPS  
**Confidence:** high (abstract provided)

## Problem
Power delivery networks in high-performance processors require large on-chip capacitance with minimal area footprint; traditional discrete capacitors consume significant board space. Si-IPD (Silicon Integrated Passive Devices) with high-density silicon capacitors integrate voltage regulation directly on-chip.

## Motivation
Power delivery efficiency and density directly impact processor performance and thermal behavior; on-chip integration of passive elements reduces parasitic inductance and improves voltage delivery quality.

## Method
High-density Si-IPD technology integrates silicon capacitors and inductors directly into processor chips using specialized fabrication techniques, achieving higher capacitance density than traditional approaches and reducing parasitic resistance and inductance in the power delivery path.

## Key Novelty
High-density integrated silicon passive devices enabling efficient on-chip power delivery with minimal area overhead.

## Contributions
- High-density silicon capacitor technology for on-chip integration
- Integrated passive device architecture reducing PDN parasitic impedance
- Improved voltage regulation efficiency and transient response
- Demonstrated application to high-performance processor design
