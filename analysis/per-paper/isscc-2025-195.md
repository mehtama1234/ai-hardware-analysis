# ISSCC 2025-195: High-Speed 8b Pipelined ADC with Concurrent Residue Transfer

## Core Contribution
Novel 8b 10GS/s 2-channel time-interleaved pipelined ADC that parallelizes residue transfer and quantization, eliminating the residue amplifier bottleneck and achieving 22fJ/conv-step energy efficiency.

## Technical Approach
- **Key Innovation**: Concurrent residue transfer and quantization instead of serialized pipelined stages
- **Inter-stage Gain**: Simple source follower (linear, wideband, low-power) replaces high-speed residue amplifier
- **Power Management**: Automatic common-mode power-gating without switching overhead
- **Single-channel**: 5GS/s (fastest 8b pipelined to date)
- **2-channel TI**: 10GS/s aggregate with balanced current distribution

## Performance Metrics
- **Speed**: 5GS/s single-channel, 10GS/s 2-channel time-interleaved
- **Energy Efficiency**: 22fJ/conv-step FoM
- **Linearity**: Maintains output swing and linearity vs. residue-amplifier schemes
- **Resolution**: 8-bit

## Baselines
- Prior pipelined ADCs with residue amplifiers (3GS/s limit)
- SAR ADCs with time-interleaving (lower speed, calibration overhead)
- Flash ADCs (6b resolution limit)

## Hardware Impact
Enables high-speed wideband signal acquisition for 5G/6G communications, radar, and imaging systems without exponential power and area scaling of time-interleaved SAR or flash approaches.
