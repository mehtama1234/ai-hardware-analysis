# dac-2025-029: AttenPIM - Dual-mode PIM for Attention

**Title:** AttenPIM: Accelerating LLM Attention with Dual-mode GEMV in Processing-in-Memory

**Venue:** DAC 2025

**Core Issue:** PIM architectures apply uniform modes to distinct score/context GEMV patterns; dynamic matrix dimensions cause inefficiency.

**Key Contribution:** Dual-mode PIM with distinct execution for score ($Q \times K_t$) and context ($S \times V$); 1.13-5.26x speedup, 17-49% energy reduction.

**Hardware Target:** PIM (processing-in-memory)

**Workload:** LLM inference, attention, transformer

**Metrics:**
- Speedup: 1.13-5.26x
- Energy: 17-49% reduction

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)
