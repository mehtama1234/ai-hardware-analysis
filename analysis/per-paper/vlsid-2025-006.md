# Analysis and Design Considerations for MASH of Noise Shaped SAR ADCs

**Venue:** VLSID
**Authors:** R. Arundeepakvel, Ankesh Jain

## Problem Statement
This brief analyses the residue amplifier non-idealities and design considerations for noise shaping (NS) successive approximation register (SAR) based multi stage noise shaping (MASH) analog to digital converter (ADC) architecture

## Key Innovation
Novel circuit/architecture design

## Method
The paper analyzes a multi-stage analog-to-digital converter (ADC) where each stage uses a successive approximation register (SAR) converter with noise shaping—pushing unwanted quantization noise to higher frequencies for the next stage to handle. The residue (leftover signal) from each stage is amplified and fed to the next stage in sequence. The paper examines how real-world imperfections in this residue amplifier degrade performance and identifies the critical design decisions needed to build these converters.
