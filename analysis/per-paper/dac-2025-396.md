# dac-2025-396: CHORD: Composable Hybrid Optical Reconfigurable Diffractive Framework For Optical Neural Network

**Problem:** Diffractive optical neural networks (DONNs) lack reconfigurability due to fixed post-fabrication optical structures, limiting dynamic AI workload adaptation.

**Method:** CHORD combines fixed optical hardware (built into the chip and unchangeable) with adjustable electronic controls. During training, the system learns the best control settings for each task, optimizing them across the entire system. This allows one physical chip to handle many different AI problems. It pairs the speed advantage of optical computing — which uses light to naturally parallelize operations — with the flexibility of electronically adjustable settings.

**Key Novelty:** First composable hybrid optical/photonic DONN enabling exponentially expanded functional space through full-system differentiable learning.

**Contributions:**
- Composable hybrid optical/photonic architecture
- Full-system learnable repurposing
- Exponentially larger functional space
- 74x faster, 194x lower energy than digital
