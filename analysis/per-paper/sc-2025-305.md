# SC-2025-305: Reproducibility Report for SC25 Paper X-MoE: Enabling Scalable Training for Emerging Mixture-of-Experts Architectures on HPC Platforms

## Summary
This is a reproducibility report from the SC25 Reproducibility Initiative Committee evaluating the artifact associated with the X-MoE paper on scalable mixture-of-experts training on HPC platforms. The report details artifact evaluation findings, including code quality, documentation, and reproducibility of published results.

## Key Contributions
1. **Independent artifact evaluation** of X-MoE implementation
2. **Reproducibility assessment** of X-MoE's claims on HPC platforms
3. **Artifact quality documentation** for future use

## Technical Scope
- Evaluation of X-MoE paper's artifact (code, data, experimental setup)
- Validation of scalable MoE training on HPC systems
- Assessment of reproducibility and replicability

## Hardware & Systems
- HPC systems used in original evaluation
- GPU-accelerated training clusters

## Evaluation Details
- **Committee**: SC25 Reproducibility Committee
- **Evaluation criteria**: Artifact description and evaluation appendix standards
- **Focus**: Validation of X-MoE's scalability claims on HPC platforms

## Limitations & Scope
- Report is assessment-focused rather than methodological contribution
- Specific evaluation findings depend on X-MoE paper contents
- This paper is a meta-evaluation rather than original research contribution
