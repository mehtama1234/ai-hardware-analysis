# Intelligent Sampling of Extreme-Scale Turbulence Datasets for Accurate and Efficient Spatiotemporal Model Training

**ID:** sc-2025-049  
**Venue:** SC  
**Confidence:** high

## Problem
Training on massive datasets is energy-intensive and slow; Moore's law end means efficiency gains must come from data strategy

## Motivation
Extreme-scale DNS turbulence data requires new approaches; post-Dennard scaling era demands efficiency rethinking

## Method
The SICKLE framework uses maximum entropy sampling, a technique that selects the most diverse and informative data points from the dataset—instead of random selection, it prioritizes examples that represent the full range of system conditions. The approach is evaluated against two alternatives: purely random sampling and phase-space sampling (selecting from specific regions of possible states). By intelligently curating which data the model trains on, this method reduces both the dataset size needed and the computational energy required.

## Key Novelty
Maximum entropy sampling intelligently selects the most representative training data, reducing both dataset size and computational energy costs, as validated on the Frontier supercomputer.

## Contributions
1. Develops SICKLE sparse curation framework
2. Introduces MaxEnt sampling for turbulence datasets
3. Demonstrates energy reductions up to 38x
