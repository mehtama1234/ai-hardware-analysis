# Intelligent Sampling of Extreme-Scale Turbulence Datasets for Accurate and Efficient Spatiotemporal Model Training

**ID:** sc-2025-049  
**Venue:** SC  
**Confidence:** high

## Problem
Training on massive datasets is energy-intensive and slow; Moore's law end means efficiency gains must come from data strategy

## Motivation
Extreme-scale DNS turbulence data requires new approaches; post-Dennard scaling era demands efficiency rethinking

## Method
SICKLE framework with MaxEnt (maximum entropy) sampling; compares MaxEnt vs. random and phase-space sampling

## Key Novelty
MaxEnt-based intelligent curation for model training; energy-aware benchmarking on Frontier supercomputer

## Contributions
1. Develops SICKLE sparse curation framework
2. Introduces MaxEnt sampling for turbulence datasets
3. Demonstrates energy reductions up to 38x

## Hardware Targets
Frontier supercomputer

## Technique Categories
Machine Learning, Data Curation, Energy Efficiency

## Workloads
Spatiotemporal model training, DNS turbulence simulation

## Metrics
- energy_reduction: up to 38x
- accuracy_improvement: demonstrated

## Baselines
- Random sampling
- Phase-space sampling

## Limitations
Specific to turbulence datasets; generalization to other domains unclear
