# Towards Fast Automatic Design of Silicon Dangling Bond Logic

**Venue**: DATE  
**Primary Theme**: automated SiDB design  

## Problem
Automating design of Silicon Dangling Bond (SiDB) logic implementations is inefficient due to massive search space exploration. SiDB logic realizes functions through quantum dot arrangements but determining optimal arrangements computationally expensive.

## Method
When automatically searching for the best quantum-dot circuit arrangement, this method identifies regions that can't possibly work and eliminates them during the search. By cutting away these impossible paths before exploring them fully, it avoids wasted computation. This continuous pruning of impossible designs dramatically speeds up finding the optimal arrangement.

## Key Novelty
By identifying and removing impossible designs during the search, the method achieves a 33-times speedup in automated SiDB logic design.

## Contributions
- Comprehensive search space pruning strategies for SiDB design
- 33x runtime reduction in design automation
- Drastic search space reduction enabling scalable synthesis
