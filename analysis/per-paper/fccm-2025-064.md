# An Efficient FPGA-Based Hardware Accelerator of Fully Quantized Mamba-2

**Problem:** Mamba-2 inference exhibits element-wise operations that underutilize GPU tensor cores, reducing efficiency. Research on full-quantization strategies for Mamba-2 is lacking.

**Method:** The paper proposes Hfqmamba2, a hybrid-precision full-quantization strategy balancing performance and resource usage. A reconfigurable FPGA architecture is designed with a segmented lookup table for efficient RMSNorm inverse square root approximation and an intra-layer computation pipeline for SSM operations, implemented and evaluated on Alveo U50.

**Novelty:** Reconfigurable FPGA architecture with segmented quantization strategy and lookup tables for efficient Mamba-2 acceleration, addressing both quantization and hardware dataflow inefficiencies.
