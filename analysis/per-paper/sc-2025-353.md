# Orchestrating Quantum-HPC Workflows with Distributed Quantum Circuit Cutting

**Paper ID:** sc-2025-353
**Venue:** SC
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Quantum computers are limited by qubit counts, preventing execution of large-scale quantum algorithms

## Motivation
Circuit cutting enables larger quantum computations by decomposing circuits, but needs HPC integration for scalability

## Method
Qdislib breaks large quantum computations into smaller pieces that individual quantum computers can execute. It maps the circuit using graphs (networks of nodes representing each operation), then uses two techniques to split it: wire cutting breaks the quantum connections between qubits, and gate cutting replaces complex multi-qubit operations with simpler single-qubit operations plus classical post-processing. These pieces are distributed across multiple quantum processors, with classical computing coordinating results between them. The library integrates with quantum programming frameworks Qiskit and Qibo.

## Key Novelty
Qdislib enables large quantum circuits to run on current quantum computers by splitting them across multiple quantum processors while automatically coordinating the classical computation needed between them.
