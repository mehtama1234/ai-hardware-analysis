# Orchestrating Quantum-HPC Workflows with Distributed Quantum Circuit Cutting

**Paper ID:** sc-2025-353
**Venue:** SC
**Confidence:** low

## Problem Statement
Quantum computers are limited by qubit counts, preventing execution of large-scale quantum algorithms

## Motivation
Circuit cutting enables larger quantum computations by decomposing circuits, but needs HPC integration for scalability

## Method
Qdislib: distributed quantum circuit cutting library with graph-based circuit representation, wire/gate cutting, support for Qiskit/Qibo

## Key Novelty
Distributed quantum circuit cutting library seamlessly integrated with hybrid quantum-classical HPC systems

## Contributions
- Qdislib distributed quantum circuit library
- Support for wire and gate cutting techniques
- Multi-library compatibility (Qiskit, Qibo)
- Heterogeneous execution across CPU/GPU/QPU
