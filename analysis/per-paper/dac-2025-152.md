# GLOVA: Global and Local Variation-Aware Analog Circuit Design with Risk-Sensitive Reinforcement Learning

**Authors:** Dongjun Kim, Junwoo Park, Chaehyeon Shin, Jaeheon Jung, Kyungho Shin, Seungheon Baek, Sanghyuk Heo, Woongrae Kim  
**Venue:** DAC 2025

## Summary
GLOVA automates analog circuit design by using risk-sensitive reinforcement learning to optimize circuit sizing while accounting for process, voltage, temperature (PVT) variations and random manufacturing mismatches. The approach achieves 80.5x improvement in sample efficiency and 76x speedup compared to prior variation-aware sizing methods.

## Key Contributions
- Risk-sensitive RL framework for analog circuit design with PVT robustness
- Ensemble-based critic ensuring sample-efficient learning
- Mu-sigma evaluation reducing simulation cost for failed design identification
- Industrial-level validation supporting corner and Monte Carlo simulations

## Technical Approach
GLOVA leverages risk-sensitive reinforcement learning to optimize circuit parameters while explicitly accounting for PVT variations. An ensemble-based critic improves learning efficiency. The framework incorporates mu-sigma evaluation and simulation reordering to reduce the cost of identifying designs that fail robustness requirements.
