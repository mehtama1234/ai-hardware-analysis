# Principle-based Dataflow Optimization for Communication Lower Bound in Operator-Fused Tensor Accelerator

## Summary
Principle-based approach to optimize tensor accelerator dataflow with communication lower bounds and profitable operator fusion identification.

## Key Results
- **63.6% data movement saving** vs TPUv4i
- **1.33x speedup** vs TPUv4i (62.4% vs Gemmini)
- **Buffer and bandwidth** not increased

## Core Innovation
First principles-based characterization of memory communication bounds for fused tensor operators; identifies all profitable fusions.

## Technique
Derives optimal tiling, scheduling, mapping for tensor operators; constructs FuseCU architecture supporting identified profitable fusions.
