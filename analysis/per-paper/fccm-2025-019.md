# ITERA-LLM: Boosting Sub-8-Bit Large Language Model Inference via Iterative Tensor Decomposition

**Problem:** Deploying LLMs on resource-constrained platforms with sub-8-bit quantization causes significant accuracy degradation. Standard quantization-only methods cannot maintain model accuracy when precision falls below 8 bits.

**Method:** ITERA-LLM combines sub-8-bit quantization with SVD-based iterative low-rank tensor decomposition to compensate for quantization error and reduce computational complexity. A hardware-aware Design Space Exploration (DSE) process optimizes the configuration across accuracy, latency, and resource utilization for the specific target LLM.

**Novelty:** Software-hardware co-design integrating iterative tensor decomposition with sub-8-bit quantization, guided by hardware-aware DSE to achieve high compression ratios without accuracy loss.
