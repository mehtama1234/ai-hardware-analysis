# Hardware Sentinel: Protecting Software Applications from Hardware Silent Data Corruptions

**Venue:** ASPLOS 2025  
**ID:** asplos-2025-041  
**Confidence:** low (abstract only)

## Problem
Silent Data Corruptions (SDCs) caused by silicon defects propagate undetected through large-scale data center applications, causing unpredictable failures (segfaults, crashes, log anomalies) that are difficult to attribute to hardware without systematic cross-layer analysis.

## Motivation
As chips scale in transistor density and fleets grow to millions of nodes, silicon defect rates increase, making reliable SDC detection critical to service-level objectives in production cloud infrastructure.

## Method
Hardware Sentinel correlates software-layer failure indicators—segmentation faults, core dumps, application crashes, and structured logs—with CPU hardware identities across a fleet, building statistical models that link failure patterns to defective hardware. Validated over 6 years of failure data from 13 workloads across 27 regions, cross-referencing with Fleetscanner and Ripple hardware tests.

## Key Novelty
A production-validated, software-failure-driven SDC detection framework that identifies defective CPUs from application-layer signals without dedicated hardware test circuits.

## Contributions
- Hardware Sentinel framework correlating software failure indicators to SDC-inducing CPU defects.
- 1.74x coverage over Fleetscanner, 1.92x over Ripple hardware testing.
- Identified hundreds of defective CPUs across 7 generations from multiple manufacturers.
- Characterization of top kernel exceptions correlated with SDC failures across 13 workloads and 27 regions.
