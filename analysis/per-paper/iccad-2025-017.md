# CarbonClarity: Understanding and Addressing Uncertainty in Embodied Carbon for Sustainable Computing

**Venue:** ICCAD  
**ID:** iccad-2025-017

## Problem
Deterministic embodied carbon models fail to account for spatial and temporal variability in semiconductor supply chain, limiting carbon-aware design decisions.

## Motivation
Embodied carbon is a significant contributor to computing's carbon footprint; uncertainty quantification enables informed design choices.

## Method
Probabilistic framework models embodied carbon distributions across energy-per-area, gas-per-area, yield, and carbon intensity by technology node; case studies evaluate chiplet vs monolithic architectures.

## Key Novelty
First probabilistic embodied carbon model capturing supply chain uncertainties across multiple technology nodes.

## Contributions
- Probabilistic framework for embodied carbon with uncertainty distributions
- Quantification of embodied carbon variance across technology nodes
- Evaluation of chiplet vs monolithic design carbon impact and uncertainty
- Device provisioning methodology under carbon budget constraints
