# DReX: Accurate and Scalable Dense Retrieval Acceleration via Algorithmic-Hardware Codesign

**Venue:** ISCA  
**ID:** isca-2025-091  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Approximate nearest-neighbor search (ANNS) used in RAG dense retrieval is dataset-specific and inherently low-recall, causing irrelevant or incomplete context to degrade LLM generation quality. Exact nearest-neighbor search is too compute- and bandwidth-intensive for conventional processors.

## Motivation
As RAG systems grow to handle trillion-token datastores, retrieval latency, accuracy, and energy directly bottleneck time-to-first-token and generation quality for LLM applications.

## Method
DReX combines two hardware tiers: in-DRAM logic performs early filtering by comparing query vectors against embedding centroids to eliminate distant candidates without moving full vectors off-chip, and an outside-DRAM near-memory accelerator then executes exact k-NN search on the filtered candidate set. The in-DRAM filter exploits processing-in-memory (PIM) capabilities to compute approximate distance bounds, bypassing DRAM-to-processor transfers for the bulk of vectors. This two-stage pipeline minimizes off-chip data movement while guaranteeing exact retrieval on the survivors.

## Key Novelty
Using in-DRAM logic for coarse vector filtering combined with a near-memory accelerator for exact search, eliminating the recall-versus-speed tradeoff of ANNS without bulk off-chip data movement.

## Contributions
1. Dataset-agnostic dense retrieval scheme that avoids ANNS approximation errors entirely.
2. In-DRAM early-filter mechanism that uses PIM logic to discard far-away embeddings before DRAM-to-processor transfer.
3. Near-memory accelerator for exact k-NN search on the filtered candidate set.
4. End-to-end RAG evaluation showing 6.2-7x reduction in time-to-first-token vs. state-of-the-art ANNS.

## Metrics
- **Speedup:** 6.2-7x reduction in time-to-first-token vs. state-of-the-art ANNS mechanism
- **Accuracy:** exact nearest-neighbor (no recall degradation)

## Baselines
- State-of-the-art ANNS-based retrieval mechanism

## Limitations
Recall and latency benefits depend on the filtering selectivity of the in-DRAM stage, which may vary with embedding distribution; detailed area/power numbers and comparison to GPU baselines are not stated in the abstract.
