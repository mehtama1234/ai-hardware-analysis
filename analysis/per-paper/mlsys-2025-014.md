# N:M Sparse DNN Kernels for RISC-V MCUs via Custom ISA Extension

**Venue:** MLSys 2025 | **ID:** mlsys-2025-014 | **Confidence:** high

## Problem
N:M semi-structured sparsity cannot be efficiently exploited on RISC-V MCUs because standard ISAs lack the hardware primitives to accelerate structured sparse operations.

## Method
xDecimate custom RISC-V ISA instruction performs hardware-accelerated N:M sparse MAC. Decimate Im2col reformulates convolution for xDecimate. MATCH compiler generates xDecimate-aware kernels. Synthesized at 22nm on Vega PULP SoC (basis for commercial GAP9). Supports 1:4, 1:8, 1:16 sparsity on ResNet-18 and ViT.

## Novelty
First RISC-V ISA extension for N:M semi-structured sparsity enabling 1:4 to 1:16 patterns at only 5% area overhead on a commercial-grade SoC.

## Metrics
- 3.21x speedup on ResNet-18 at 1:16 sparsity
- 1.81x speedup on ViT at 1:16 sparsity
- 5% area overhead (22nm)
