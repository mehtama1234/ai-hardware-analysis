# Synthesis of CFET Cell Library Leveraging Backside Metal Routing

**Venue:** DAC  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only analysis)

## Problem
CFET stacking reduces routing tracks and increases complexity; current cell synthesis cannot efficiently use backside metal resources or handle transistor folding.

## Method
Uses **Euler paths** to estimate lower bounds on CPP, **dynamic programming** for FMRT calculation, and **SMT** for final placement and routing. Enables backside metal utilization in CFET cell synthesis.

## Key Novelty
First cell synthesis to leverage backside metal routing resources at standard cell stage with efficient transistor folding.

## Results
- **1% CPP reduction**
- **45% M2 track reduction**
- **19% runtime reduction**
