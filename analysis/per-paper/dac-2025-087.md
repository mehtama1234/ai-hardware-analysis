# dac-2025-087: KLiNQ: Qubit Readout via Compressed Neural Networks

**Problem:** Qubit readout is slow/error-prone; current NN methods require large models and lack support for mid-circuit measurements.

**Method:** KLiNQ shrinks neural networks by 99% using knowledge distillation—teaching a small network to mimic a large one—while keeping 91% accuracy in identifying quantum states. Each qubit runs its own compact neural network on a Xilinx UltraScale+ FPGA (reprogrammable hardware), achieving independent 32-nanosecond readouts. This enables fast, simultaneous measurements across all qubits, supporting mid-circuit measurements critical for quantum error correction.

**Key Novelty:** 99% NN compression maintaining accuracy enabling rapid independent qubit readouts for mid-circuit measurements.

**Hardware Target:** FPGA  
**Technique Category:** quantization, circuit-design  
**Workloads:** quantum-computing  

**Metrics:**
- Accuracy: 91% discrimination
- Model reduction: 99%
- Latency: 32 ns readout

**Confidence:** low (abstract only)
