# MARC: Motion-Aware Rate Control for Mobile E-commerce Cloud Rendering

**Problem:** Cloud rendering systems for mobile e-commerce must balance video quality, bandwidth, and latency. Static bitrate control fails to adapt to varying motion levels in video streams, wasting bandwidth on low-motion scenes or degrading quality during high-motion content.

**Method:** MARC detects motion intensity in video frames using optical flow or frame differencing, adjusts encoding bitrate and resolution dynamically based on motion levels, and optimizes transmission scheduling to prioritize high-motion regions. The system feeds motion metadata to the cloud encoder to allocate more bits to complex scenes.

**Novelty:** Closed-loop motion-aware rate adaptation that uses perceptual motion cues to guide bitrate allocation in cloud rendering systems.
