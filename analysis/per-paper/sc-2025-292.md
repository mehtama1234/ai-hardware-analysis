# GreenMix: Energy-Efficient Serverless Computing via Randomized Sketching on Asymmetric Multi-Cores

**Venue:** SC 2025  
**Authors:** Rohan Basu Roy, Tirthak Patel, Baolin Li, Siddharth Samsi, Vijay Gadepally, Devesh Tiwari

## Problem Statement
Modern processors (ARM big.LITTLE, Intel Alder Lake) feature asymmetric cores with different energy-performance characteristics. Serverless computing platforms ignore this heterogeneity, missing opportunities to reduce energy consumption and keep-alive costs while maintaining QoS.

## Technical Approach
**Randomized Sketching for Serverless:**
- Models function assignment to core types as an optimization problem
- Randomized sketching algorithm efficiently explores core type assignment space
- Jointly optimizes: energy efficiency, keep-alive costs, QoS targets
- Approximation guarantee: within 10% of optimal solution

**Key Insight:**
- Big cores: High performance for latency-sensitive functions
- Little cores: Energy efficiency for throughput-oriented or batch functions
- Intelligent allocation balances these competing objectives

## Evaluation Platforms
- **ARM big.LITTLE:** 4 big cores, 4 little cores
- **Intel Alder Lake:** Performance cores (P-cores) and efficiency cores (E-cores)

## Results
- Outperforms competing serverless schedulers in energy and keep-alive costs
- Maintains QoS targets during optimization
- 10% approximation guarantee to optimal solution

## Key Benefits
1. **Energy Reduction:** Leverages core heterogeneity for efficiency
2. **Cost Reduction:** Lower keep-alive overhead (major serverless expense)
3. **QoS Compliance:** Maintains latency/throughput requirements
4. **Portability:** Works across asymmetric multi-core architectures

## Limitations & Future Work
- Approximation gap (10%) to optimality may limit gains on small systems
- Scalability to massive datacenters (10k+ functions) not evaluated
- Real production workload traces not used (only synthetic)
- Keep-alive cost model assumptions and derivation not detailed
- Interaction with other serverless features (autoscaling) not explored
