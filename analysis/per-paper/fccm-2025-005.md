# Ph.D. Project Hardware-Aware Neural Networks

**Problem:** Deploying overparameterized neural networks on resource-constrained hardware like FPGAs requires new approaches that transcend traditional precision and overparameterization paradigms to reduce inference costs.

**Method:** The research proposes hardware-aware neural network design methodologies—PolyLUT, NeuraLUT, and NeuraLUT-Assemble—that leverage Boolean lookup tables to enhance network expressivity and efficiency. These approaches strategically integrate diverse data representations and arithmetic operations at the FPGA circuit level.

**Novelty:** Using Boolean lookup tables (LUTs) as the fundamental building block for hardware-aware neural networks that integrate multiple levels of precision and expressivity for FPGA deployment.
