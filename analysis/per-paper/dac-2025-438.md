# Routability-aware Packing for High-density Nonvolatile FPGAs

**ID**: dac-2025-438  
**Venue**: DAC  
**Confidence**: low

## Problem
NVFPGAs with MLC NVMs achieve high logic density but suffer from severely degraded CLB intra-routability, causing long packing times in CAD flow.

## Motivation
High-density FPGAs require fast CAD turnaround; packing bottlenecks block design iteration.

## Method
Routability-aware re-pair stage adjusts LUT assignments to reduce congestion and improve intra-routability; exploits structural equivalence of MLC LUTs to eliminate redundant routing attempts during packing.

## Key Novelty
LUT assignment re-pairing specifically targeting MLC routing topology to reduce congestion without full re-placement.

## Contributions
- Routability-aware LUT re-pairing algorithm
- MLC LUT structural equivalence exploitation
- Reduction in packing time by 41.48% average
- Maintains output quality while improving CAD flow speed
