# dac-2025-241: Measurement-based uncomputation of quantum circuits for modular arithmetic

## Summary
Formalizes measurement-based uncomputation (MBU) for single-qubit registers to reduce Toffoli counts and depths in modular arithmetic circuits by 10-25%, with potential applications to cryptanalysis.

## Key Idea
Use measurement outcomes to enable probabilistic uncomputation, reducing gate overhead in quantum modular arithmetic operations.

## Contributions
- 10-15% Toffoli reduction (architecture 1)
- ~25% reduction (alternative architecture)
- Extensible to modular multiplication/exponentiation
- Relevant to quantum RSA attacks

## Limitations
Single-qubit registers only; practical scalability not addressed.

## Confidence
**low** (abstract-only)
