# hpca-2025-120: From Optimal to Practical: Efficient Micro-op Cache Replacement Policies for Data Center Applications

**Venue:** HPCA  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Data center applications suffer frequent micro-op (uop) cache misses because no effective uop-cache-specific replacement policy exists; the uop cache has fundamentally different cost characteristics from conventional caches (variable and disproportional miss costs, partial hits), making standard policies like LRU and even Belady's algorithm suboptimal.

## Motivation
The CPU frontend is a major energy and performance bottleneck for data center applications, and improving uop cache utilization is critical—but without a theoretical near-optimal bound for uop caches, it is impossible to know how much improvement is achievable or how to design toward it.

## Method
FLACK is introduced as a near-optimal offline replacement policy that accounts for uop cache-specific properties: variable miss costs (due to disproportional re-fetch penalties) and partial hits (when only some micro-ops of an instruction group are cached). FLACK substantially outperforms Belady's algorithm, establishing a new uop-cache-specific optimality bound. FURBYS is then designed as a practical online policy that mimics FLACK via profile-guided methods, using three components: (1) whole-execution hit/miss profiles per static instruction, (2) detection of locally/transiently hot data, and (3) selective ignorance of entries with profiled low hit rates during replacement decisions.

## Key Novelty
FLACK—a near-optimal offline uop cache replacement algorithm that accounts for variable miss costs and partial hits, surpassing Belady's algorithm and providing a new theoretical bound for practical policy design.

## Contributions
- FLACK near-optimal offline policy for uop caches, showing 4.46% greater miss reduction than Belady's algorithm
- Establishment of a new theoretical upper bound for uop cache replacement policy improvement
- FURBYS practical profile-guided policy achieving 14.34% average miss reduction vs. LRU (1.84x over state-of-the-art)
- 3.10% performance-per-watt improvement; FURBYS equivalent to LRU on 1.5x uop cache size (up to 2x)

## Limitations
