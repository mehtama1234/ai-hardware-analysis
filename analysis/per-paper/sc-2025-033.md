# sc-2025-033: Evaluation of Test-Time Compute Constraints on Safety and Skill Large Reasoning Models

## Summary
Study evaluating the impact of compute constraints (reasoning length and quantization) on the safety performance of reasoning language models. Proposes length-controlled policy optimization (LCPO) and quantization strategies to optimize efficiency-safety trade-offs.

## Problem
- Reasoning models (o1, o3-style) improve performance with longer chain-of-thought (CoT)
- Longer reasoning = higher inference cost
- Practical deployment requires compute constraints
- Unclear how safety performance degrades under resource limitations

## Method
### Approach 1: Length Constraint via LCPO
- Fine-tune reasoning models using Length-Controlled Policy Optimization (RL-based)
- Enforce user-defined maximum CoT length
- Maintain reasoning quality within budget

### Approach 2: Quantization
- Apply quantization (lower precision) to model
- Maximize CoT generation within compute budget
- Trade precision for compute efficiency

## Key Innovations
1. **LCPO method**: RL-based approach to enforce reasoning length constraints while maintaining performance
2. **Efficiency-safety analysis**: Systematic evaluation of safety trade-offs under resource constraints
3. **Dual-strategy evaluation**: Comparing length control vs. quantization approaches

## Hardware Targets
- GPUs (inference servers)
- Quantized hardware (reduced precision accelerators)

## Results (from abstract)
- Framework for evaluating compute constraints on reasoning safety
- Two complementary constraint strategies (length vs. quantization)
- Trade-offs between computational efficiency and safety quantified

## Technique Category

- Reinforcement learning (policy optimization)
- Model quantization and compression
- Safety and alignment evaluation
- Test-time compute scaling
