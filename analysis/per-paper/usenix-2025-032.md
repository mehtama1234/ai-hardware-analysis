# SwCC: Software-Programmable and Per-Packet Congestion Control in RDMA Engine

**Problem:** RDMA networks suffer from congestion that degrades throughput and latency, but existing congestion control mechanisms are hardcoded in hardware and lack the flexibility to adapt policies dynamically or apply fine-grained per-packet control decisions. Current approaches operate at flow granularity, limiting optimization potential.

**Method:** SwCC implements a programmable layer in RDMA engines that allows runtime deployment of custom congestion control algorithms operating at per-packet granularity. The system decouples policy from hardware, enabling algorithms to inspect packet metadata and dynamically adjust forwarding, marking, or throttling decisions without modifying underlying silicon.

**Novelty:** Per-packet, software-programmable congestion control policies directly in RDMA engines rather than fixed hardware mechanisms or host-based approaches.
