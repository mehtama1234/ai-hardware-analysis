# isscc-2025-255: Ultra-Low-Power Spoken Language Understanding SoC

**Title:** An 8.62μW 75dB-DR SoC End-to-End Spoken-Language-Understanding SoC With Channel-Level AGC and Temporal-Sparsity-Aware Streaming-Mode RNN

**Venue:** ISSCC 2025

**Problem:** Voice-controlled IoT/wearable nodes need always-on audio classification for continuous speech with wide dynamic range (>60dB), but conventional ADC+DSP systems waste >50% power in analog front-end; prior analog feature extractors were either too power-hungry or couldn't handle multi-class, variable-amplitude inputs without off-chip classifiers.

**Key Innovation:** Streaming-mode RNN exploiting temporal sparsity + integrated channel-level AGC for end-to-end SLU.

**Approach:** Single SoC integrating analog front-end (channel-level AGC), feature extraction, and RNN inference; temporal sparsity in speech reduces RNN computation.

**Results:**
- **Power:** 8.62μW total
- **Dynamic Range:** 75dB, handling >60dB speech variation
- **Capability:** End-to-end continuous speech understanding (not single-word KWS)

**Confidence:** Low (abstract-only; no full-text accuracy/latency metrics)

**Tags:** ultra-low-power, speech-processing, streaming-rnn, dynamic-range, iot-edge
