# Telos: A Dataflow Accelerator for Sparse Triangular Solver of Partial Differential Equations

**ID:** isca-2025-044 | **Venue:** ISCA | **Confidence:** low (abstract only)

## Problem
Sparse triangular solve (SpTRSV) dominates runtime in preconditioned iterative solvers for PDE-derived linear systems due to loop-carried dependencies that severely limit parallelism. Prior accelerators cannot simultaneously achieve high parallelism and good data locality for structured sparsity from stencil-based PDE discretizations.

## Motivation
SpTRSV is the critical bottleneck in scientific PDE solvers, and structured stencil-based sparsity patterns offer exploitable regularity that generic sparse solvers ignore.

## Method
Telos is a dataflow accelerator that exploits structured (stencil-pattern) sparsity in SpTRSV. A plane-parallel pipelining technique maps dependent computations across processing elements (PEs) while preserving data locality within each plane. A cross-plane communication aggregation technique reorganizes inter-plane data transfers into systolic flows, enabling pipelined resolution of loop-carried dependencies with minimal stall overhead. Computation is overlapped with memory accesses to hide latency.

## Key Novelty
Plane-parallel pipelining that resolves SpTRSV loop-carried dependencies in a systolic, locality-preserving manner by exploiting the regular stencil structure of PDE-derived sparsity.

## Contributions
1. Identification and exploitation of structured (stencil-pattern) sparsity to extract pipeline parallelism in SpTRSV.
2. Plane-parallel pipelining technique that maps dependent computations onto PEs while preserving data locality.
3. Cross-plane communication aggregation to streamline data transfers into a systolic execution pattern.
4. Demonstrated speedups of 61x over CPU, 8x over GPU, and 11x over state-of-the-art accelerator.
