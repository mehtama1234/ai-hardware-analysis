# Comparison-Free Bit-Stream Generation for Cost-Efficient Unary Computing

**Problem:** Comparator-based binary-to-unary converters have high area and power cost, scaling poorly with precision.

**Method:** Comparison-free unary generation mechanism avoiding comparators; introduces serial and parallel (exact/approximate) variants.

**Key Contribution:** Eliminates comparator overhead in unary conversion while maintaining conversion accuracy.

**Results:** Reduced area, power, and area-delay product vs state-of-the-art across four use cases.
