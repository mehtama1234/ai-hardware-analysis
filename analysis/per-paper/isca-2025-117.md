# Bishop: Sparsified Bundling Spiking Transformers on Heterogeneous Cores with Error-constrained Pruning

**Venue:** ISCA  
**Confidence:** low (abstract only)

## Problem
Spiking transformers combine attention mechanisms with spike-based computation, but their large size and high spatiotemporal volume of computation make efficient hardware acceleration difficult due to variable activation sparsity and the inefficiency of treating attention's binary spike operations with conventional accelerator designs.

## Motivation
Spiking neural networks are promising for ultra-low-power edge deployment, but the lack of a dedicated accelerator for spiking transformers leaves their spatiotemporal sparsity and data-reuse opportunities unexploited.

## Method
Bishop introduces the Token-Time Bundle (TTB) container that groups spike data across tokens and time steps for efficient packing. A heterogeneous accelerator routes high-density TTBs to a dense core array and low-density TTBs to a sparse core array via a stratifier. BSA (Bundle Sparsity-Aware) training improves structured TTB-level firing sparsity. ECP (Error-Constrained TTB Pruning) trims spiking query/key/value activations with a provable error bound before and after attention map computation. A reconfigurable TTB spiking attention core executes attention as AND-and-Accumulate operations on binary spikes.

## Key Novelty
The Token-Time Bundle abstraction that jointly packs spatial and temporal spike data to enable simultaneous dense/sparse core routing and weight reuse across both dimensions.

## Contributions
1. Token-Time Bundle (TTB) abstraction for efficient spatiotemporal spike data representation and reuse
2. Heterogeneous accelerator architecture with stratifier routing and dedicated dense/sparse core arrays
3. BSA training pipeline that enhances structured TTB-level firing sparsity during model training
4. Error-Constrained TTB Pruning (ECP) for spiking attention with formal error bounds
