# A Partitioning-Based CAD Flow for Interposer-Based Multi-Die FPGAs

**Problem:** Multi-die interposer-based FPGA architectures face limited inter-die connectivity resources and increased interposer delays compared to intra-die routing, which directly impact routability, routed wirelength, and maximum clock frequency.

**Method:** The approach presents FPGAPart, a timing-driven netlist partitioner tailored for FPGA designs, integrated with VTR 7.0. It leverages VTR's pre-packing solutions as clustering hints and uses the FP-Growth algorithm to detect frequently occurring patterns across timing paths for enhanced clustering. Neighborhood influences-based cutting planes are introduced into the core ILP solver to achieve significant runtime speedup with minimal solution quality degradation.

**Novelty:** FPGAPart is the first open-source timing-driven netlist partitioner designed specifically for FPGA architectures, incorporating neighborhood influences-based cutting planes for 38× faster ILP optimization with <1% quality loss.
