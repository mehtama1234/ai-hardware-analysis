# date-2025-000: A High-Performance and Flexible Accelerator for Dynamic Graph Convolutional Networks

## Problem
Dynamic Graph Convolutional Networks (DGCNs) require simultaneous processing of spatial information (GCN module) and temporal information (RNN module) with distinct computational patterns. Existing hardware platforms struggle to achieve both high performance and energy efficiency across these heterogeneous module types.

## Method
HiFlex implements multiple homogeneous processing elements (PEs) for core computations, combined with a dynamically configurable interconnection fabric. The fabric supports point-to-point and multicast communication patterns. A dynamic control policy partitions and allocates hardware resources based on each layer's computational requirements.

## Contributions
- 38% reduction in execution time vs. ES-DGCN, ReaDy, and RACE
- 42% energy consumption decrease
- Dynamic resource allocation and partitioning strategy for heterogeneous DGCN modules

## Results
Evaluated on real-world dynamic graphs with average improvements of 38% execution time and 42% energy savings compared to state-of-the-art approaches.
