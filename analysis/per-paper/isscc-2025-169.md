# isscc-2025-169: Semiconductors in Software-Defined Vehicles

**Title:** The Crucial Role of Semiconductors in the Software-Defined Vehicle

**Problem:** Automotive industry transformation toward emission compliance, connected intelligence, and sustainability requires new semiconductor capabilities.

**Approach:** Position paper on semiconductor roadmap for software-defined vehicle (SDV) platforms with central compute and over-the-air updates.

**Key Result:** Framework connecting chip architecture to automotive system transformation.

**Hardware:** SoC, ASIC (automotive platforms).

**Tags:** automotive, software-defined-vehicle, sdv, emissions, connectivity, semiconductors
