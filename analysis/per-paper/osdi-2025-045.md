# Decouple and Decompose: Scaling Resource Allocation with DeDe

**Problem:** Resource allocation optimization in cloud systems has grown beyond the capacity of commercial solvers as problem scale increases. Existing approaches either specialize in narrow domains or impose workload-specific assumptions that limit generalizability.

**Method:** DeDe identifies that most real-world resource allocation problems have inherently separable structure, optimizing aggregate utility under separate constraints. It decouples entangled resource and demand constraints, decomposing the optimization into alternating per-resource and per-demand subproblems that can be solved efficiently and in parallel. The framework is implemented as a Python package with a familiar modeling interface.

**Novelty:** Exploiting the inherent separability of resource and demand constraints to decompose large-scale resource allocation problems into parallelizable subproblems.
