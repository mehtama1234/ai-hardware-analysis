# Optimus: Accelerating Large-Scale Multi-Modal LLM Training by Bubble Exploitation

**Problem:** Training multimodal LLMs is inefficient due to substantial GPU bubbles caused by heterogeneous modality models and complex data dependencies in 3D parallelism. The scheduling of encoder and LLM computations leads to idle GPU time.

**Method:** Optimus schedules encoder computation within LLM bubbles by searching separate parallel plans for encoder and LLM components and applying a bubble scheduling algorithm. The system decomposes encoder layer computation into kernels, analyzes bubble patterns in 3D parallelism, and optimizes sub-millisecond level scheduling while preserving data dependencies.

**Novelty:** The key insight that encoder computation can be scheduled within LLM bubbles, coupled with practical algorithms that enable this scheduling across all GPUs without breaking data dependencies in the MLLM architecture.
