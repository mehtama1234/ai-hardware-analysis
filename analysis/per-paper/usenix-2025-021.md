# Optimus: Accelerating Large-Scale Multi-Modal LLM Training by Bubble Exploitation

**Problem:** Training multimodal LLMs is inefficient due to substantial GPU bubbles caused by heterogeneous modality models and complex data dependencies in 3D parallelism. The scheduling of encoder and LLM computations leads to idle GPU time.

**Method:** Optimus works by finding idle GPU time that occurs when different parts of a multimodal LLM (processing both images and text) finish computing at different times. It creates separate execution plans for the image encoder and language model, then uses a scheduling algorithm to fit encoder work into GPU idle gaps. The system breaks encoder operations into small units (kernels), identifies idle moments in the parallel GPU setup (called 3D parallelism), and schedules encoder tasks into those gaps at sub-millisecond precision while maintaining correct data flow between components.

**Novelty:** Because multimodal LLMs have idle GPU time while the language model runs, you can fill that time by scheduling encoder work, and this works across all GPUs without disrupting data flow.
