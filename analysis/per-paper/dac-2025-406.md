# FeKAN: Efficient Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks Accelerator

**Problem:** KANs require dynamic B-spline interpolation operations, but existing MLP-optimized accelerators use vector-matrix multiplication, wasting hardware on this specialized workload.

**Method:** FeFET-based LUT/CAM codebook approach with CSC encoding and grouped-pipeline optimization to convert B-spline interpolation into static lookups.

**Key Result:** 150.68K-fold speedup over CPU, 4664x over A6000 GPU; 11196x energy efficiency gain.

**Hardware Target:** ASIC, CIM  
**Workloads:** LLM-inference, transformer  
**Confidence:** low (abstract-only)
