# Layer-Specific Hardware Pooling Designs for CNN Accelerators

**Paper ID:** vlsid-2025-068
**Venue:** VLSID
**Confidence:** low

## Problem
CNN accelerator design typically uses uniform pooling (e.g., max pooling only), but different layers may benefit from different pooling strategies.

## Motivation
Optimization of hardware efficiency and model accuracy by tailoring pooling strategy to individual layers; explore combinatorial approaches beyond single pooling method.

## Method
Design-space exploration of combinatorial pooling techniques: combinations of Max pooling with deviation-based methods (AAD, BWAD, AMD) across different CNN layers. Evaluated on AlexNet, UNet, VGG-16. FPGA synthesis on Xilinx Zedboard; ASIC synthesis in Cadence 45nm.

## Key Novelty
Layer-specific pooling optimization; first systematic exploration of combinatorial pooling in CNN accelerators; demonstrates that mixed pooling strategies outperform uniform approaches.

## Contributions
- Layer-specific pooling design methodology
- Combinatorial pooling optimization framework
- Improved model accuracy with hardware gains
- For AlexNet with pattern '110' (BWAD-BWAD-Max): 4.27% footprint saving vs Max, 14.77% vs AAD, 11.87% vs BWAD, 14.64% vs AMD
- 6.67% accuracy increase over SOTA
