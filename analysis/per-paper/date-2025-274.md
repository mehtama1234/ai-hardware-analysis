# Late Breaking Results: Improving Deep SNNs with Gradient Clipping and Noise Exploitation in Neuromorphic Devices

**Venue**: DATE  
**Primary Theme**: Noise-aware SNN training for neuromorphic hardware  

## Problem
Deep SNNs trained with soft resets are computationally expensive and unsuitable for neuromorphic hardware; hard reset-based SNNs suffer performance degradation without gradient clipping.

## Method
Proposes gradient clipping technique for hard reset-based SNN training; explores exploitation of device noise to enhance learning performance; evaluates across multiple datasets and models.

## Key Novelty
Device noise can be leveraged to enhance SNN learning performance in hard reset regime, contrary to treating noise as pure interference.

## Contributions
- Gradient clipping technique improving training performance in hard reset SNNs
- Demonstration that device noise enhances rather than degrades learning
- Bridges algorithmic-hardware gap for practical neuromorphic computing
