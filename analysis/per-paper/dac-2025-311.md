# Local-GS: Edge Accelerator for Gaussian Splatting Training

**Venue:** DAC 2025  
**Confidence:** abstract-only

## Summary

Local-GS is a specialized accelerator for 3D Gaussian Splatting training on edge devices. Uses order-independent rendering to eliminate depth-wise dependencies and exploits splat locality for parallel scheduling, achieving 26.9-53x speedup vs. Jetson NX and 3 orders of magnitude efficiency improvement.

## Key Contributions

- **Order-Independent Rendering**: Breaks depth-wise dependencies enabling parallel splat processing
- **Locality-Aware Scheduling**: Pixel intersection test unit schedules workload based on Gaussian spatial locality
- **Unified Cores**: Training and rendering cores execute in parallel with tight coupling for efficiency
- **Significant Performance**: 26.9-53x speedup, 3 orders of magnitude energy efficiency vs. edge GPU baseline

## Technical Focus

Addresses data dependency and hardware utilization bottlenecks in edge-based 3D reconstruction. Exploits observation that Gaussians with high spatial overlap can be processed in parallel if rendering is order-independent.
