# Behavior-Centric Analog Circuit Optimization with Technology Portability

**Venue:** DAC | **ID:** dac-2025-194

## Summary
Decouples analog circuit representation from technology nodes via transistor electrical properties rather than sizes. Uses RBF-KAN surrogate modeling to approximate nonlinear behavior efficiently, enabling technology-agnostic optimization and 3.5×-6.2× design porting acceleration.

## Key Results
- **Design Porting:** 3.5×-6.2× acceleration across technology nodes
- **Large Signal FOM:** 1.76×-2.64× improvement
- **Small Signal FOM:** 1.73×-2.48× improvement
- **Surrogate Data Efficiency:** Requires only few hundred simulations vs. blackbox

## Technique
Represents circuit behavior via transistor electrical properties instead of device sizes; maps electrical properties to sizes per technology; RBF-KAN approximates nonlinear behavior accurately with minimal simulations.
