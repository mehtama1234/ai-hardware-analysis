# Scaling Laws of Graph Neural Networks for Atomistic Materials Modeling

**Venue:** DAC | **ID:** dac-2025-190

## Summary
Explores scaling laws for billion-parameter GNNs on terabyte-scale datasets for atomistic materials modeling. Develops foundational GNN and adapts LLM-inspired training techniques to GNN architectures, bridging the gap between GNN and LLM scale.

## Key Results
- **Model Scale:** Billion-parameter GNN (vs. prior millions)
- **Dataset Scale:** Terabyte-scale training
- **Approach:** LLM-inspired training techniques applied to GNN

## Technique
Develops billion-parameter foundational GNN; uses LLM techniques for efficient large-scale data and model management; explores three scaling dimensions: architecture size, dataset volume, and training method applicability.
