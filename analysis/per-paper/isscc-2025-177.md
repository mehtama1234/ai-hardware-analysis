# isscc-2025-177: Ultra-Low-Voltage Fractional-N Sampling PLL

**Title:** 19.8 A 0.65V-VDD 10.4-to-11.8GHz Fractional-N Sampling PLL Achieving 73.8fsrms Jitter, -271.5dB FoMN, and -61 dBc in-Band Fractional Spur in 40nm CMOS

**Problem:** Sub-0.7V PLLs face voltage-headroom constraints degrading fractional-N performance, nonlinearity, and noise folding.

**Approach:** Hybrid cascaded DPD (HC-DPD) + coarse DTC + fine R-FDAC + negative-level-inverter SPD for robust LV operation.

**Key Result:** 73.8fsrms jitter, -271.5dB FoMN, -61dBc fractional spur at 0.65V (10.4-11.8GHz).

**Hardware:** ASIC (PLL).

**Tags:** pll, fractional-n, cryo-cmos, low-voltage, sampling-pll, jitter
