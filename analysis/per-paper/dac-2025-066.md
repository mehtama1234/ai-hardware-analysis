# DuQTTA: Dual Quantized Tensor-Train Adaptation with Decoupling Magnitude-Direction for Efficient Fine-Tuning of LLMs

**Venue:** DAC | **Confidence:** Low (abstract-only)

## Problem
Parameter-efficient fine-tuning methods for LLMs struggle to balance accuracy and efficiency at scale, particularly when targeting deployment on resource-constrained edge devices with severe memory and compute limitations.

## Motivation
LLM deployment on edge devices requires extreme compression while maintaining task-specific performance, necessitating beyond-PEFT innovations combining multiple compression and optimization techniques.

## Method
DuQTTA combines Tensor-Train decomposition with dual-stage quantization and adaptive optimization strategy with decoupled magnitude-direction updates to minimize model size, achieving up to 65x compression on LLaMA2-7B while improving over LoRA through architectural co-design for edge deployment.

## Key Novelty
Dual-stage quantization of Tensor-Train components with decoupled magnitude-direction updates for extreme LLM compression without full-parameter fine-tuning.

## Contributions
- DuQTTA method combining Tensor-Train decomposition and dual-stage quantization for LLM compression
- Adaptive optimization strategy with decoupled update mechanism for improved performance
- Up to 65x compression rate on LLaMA2-7B compared to full model
- Outperforms LoRA by 4.44% (LLaMA2-7B), 3.14% (LLaMA3-8B), 0.97% (LLaMA2-13B)

## Metrics
- Compression: 65x rate vs LLaMA2-7B
- Accuracy: 4.44% improvement on LLaMA2-7B over LoRA, 3.14% on LLaMA3-8B, 0.97% on LLaMA2-13B

## Baselines
- LoRA, Full-parameter fine-tuning, Other PEFT methods

## Limitations
Evaluation limited to QA/instruction-following without exploring full deployment pipeline.
