# asplos-2025-105: FSMoE: A Flexible and Scalable Training System for Sparse Mixture-of-Experts Models

**Venue:** ASPLOS  
**Confidence:** high (full text read)

## Problem
Existing MoE training systems (DeepSpeed-MoE, Tutel) support only limited routing functions and use fixed or heuristic pipeline degrees, failing to overlap inter-node AlltoAll dispatch/combine with intra-node ESP-AllGather/ESP-ReduceScatter communications. They also do not co-design Gradient-AllReduce scheduling with MoE-layer communication, leaving AlltoAll overhead at 30-60% of total iteration time.

## Motivation
AlltoAll communication in MoE layers accounts for 30-60% of total training time across measured GPT-2 and Mixtral workloads, and the combination of DP+MP+EP+ESP parallelisms creates complex overlapping opportunities that existing systems miss, causing sustained communication bottlenecks.

## Method
FSMoE divides each mixture-of-experts layer into six customizable pieces (Gate, Order, I-Order, Dispatch, Combine, Expert) with a two-phase profiler measuring matrix operations (GEMM) and network communications. It overlaps three streams simultaneously—each node sending data to all others (AlltoAll), coordinating data within each node (ESP-AllGather/ReduceScatter), and expert computation—by identifying the bottleneck and selecting the optimal batch-size per scenario through optimization (SLSQP), while also splitting synchronization (Gradient-AllReduce) into chunks that fit idle computation gaps, refined by greedy search then evolutionary optimization.

## Key Novelty
FSMoE is the first mixture-of-experts system to overlap both between-node communication (AlltoAll) and within-node communication (ESP-AllGather/ReduceScatter) with expert computation by adapting batch-sizes per scenario, while also partitioning gradient synchronization (Gradient-AllReduce) work into idle pipeline gaps.

## Contributions
- A modular MoE abstraction with six sub-modules, four pre-built gating functions (GShard, Sigmoid, X-MoE, SoftMoE), and hook-based non-invasive extensibility.
- A three-stream scheduling algorithm overlapping intra-node and inter-node communications with computation, with SLSQP-solved optimal pipeline degrees independently per forward and backward phase.
- An adaptive gradient partitioning method that hides Gradient-AllReduce within overlappable windows across MoE and dense layers using a two-step greedy + differential evolution optimizer.
- 1.18x-1.22x speedup over Tutel+PipeMoE on 1458 configured MoE layer cases; 1.19x-3.01x speedup over DeepSpeed-MoE on GPT-2/Mixtral models on up to 48-GPU clusters.

## Metrics
- Speedup vs Tutel+PipeMoE: 1.18x-1.22x on 1458 configured MoE layers
- Speedup vs DeepSpeed-MoE: 1.19x-3.01x on GPT-2 and Mixtral real-world models
- Gating function speedup: up to 1.42x vs DeepSpeed-MoE on individual gating benchmarks
- Performance model quality: R^2 >= 0.9999 for all collective types

## Baselines
- DeepSpeed-MoE
- Tutel (with PipeMoE)
- Tutel-Improved (PipeMoE + Gradient-AllReduce overlap with dense ops)
- PipeMoE+Lina

## Limitations
Evaluated on clusters up to 48 GPUs with commodity RTX A6000 and RTX 2080Ti GPUs connected via InfiniBand; scaling behavior on larger production clusters (e.g., 1000+ GPUs with H100/NVLink) is not characterized.
