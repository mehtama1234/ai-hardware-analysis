# RapidPnR: Accelerating the Physical Design for FPGAs via Design-Level Parallelism

**Problem:** Physical design (placement and routing) has become a performance bottleneck for large-scale FPGA designs, with runtimes extending design iteration cycles and impacting debugging and optimization efficiency.

**Method:** The approach automatically divides the circuit design into smaller, independent pieces. Each piece then goes through placement and routing (deciding where components sit on the chip and how they connect) in parallel—all pieces processed at the same time instead of waiting for each one to finish. Once each piece is placed and routed, the results merge back into one complete FPGA design. The entire process runs automatically without any manual work needed.

**Novelty:** The core idea is automatically dividing the circuit into pieces so that placement and routing can happen in parallel instead of sequentially.
