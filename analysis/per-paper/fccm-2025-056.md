# RapidPnR: Accelerating the Physical Design for FPGAs via Design-Level Parallelism

**Problem:** Physical design (placement and routing) has become a performance bottleneck for large-scale FPGA designs, with runtimes extending design iteration cycles and impacting debugging and optimization efficiency.

**Method:** The approach automatically partitions synthesized netlists into smaller design pieces, executes parallel physical design (placement and routing) on each piece independently, then merges the results into a complete design. The fully-automated flow requires no manual intervention and is evaluated on real circuit benchmarks.

**Novelty:** Automatic design-level parallelization of the physical design flow through netlist partitioning and parallel place-and-route of partitions.
