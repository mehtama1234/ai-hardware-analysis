# New Time-Domain Preconditioners for HB Jacobian of RF Circuits

## Problem
Harmonic balance Jacobian solving for RF circuits requires preconditioners that balance linear solver efficiency with Newton iteration reduction, especially for nonlinear circuits.

## Solution
Novel time-domain preconditioners addressing both efficiency and convergence; extension to distributed device circuits; matrix norm metric for a priori preconditioner selection.

## Impact
Improved convergence efficiency and solver cost for strongly nonlinear RF circuit simulation.
