# dac-2025-077: UniCoS: Unified Neural and Accelerator Co-Search for CNNs and ViTs

**Problem:** Current co-search methods suffer from lengthy training times and inadequate hardware design space exploration.

**Method:** UniCoS uses a training-free accuracy proxy—a method that predicts how well a network will perform in seconds without actually training it—to quickly evaluate candidate CNN and ViT architectures. It combines this with a clustering-based algorithm that explores heterogeneous hardware dataflows (different patterns for how computations can be organized in accelerators). Together, these enable rapid joint searching: finding the best combination of network architecture and accelerator design at the same time.

**Key Novelty:** Training-free accuracy proxy enabling second-scale evaluation, unified CNN/ViT co-search with clustering-based dataflow exploration.

**Hardware Target:** ASIC, GPU  
**Technique Category:** compiler, dataflow, scheduling  
**Workloads:** CNN, transformer, vision  

**Metrics:**
- Search speedup: 48x (3 hours)
- EDP improvement: 3.54x
- Accuracy gain: 1.76% on ImageNet

**Confidence:** low (abstract only)
