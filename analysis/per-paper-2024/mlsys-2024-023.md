# L-GreCo: Layerwise-adaptive Gradient Compression For Efficient Data-parallel Deep Learning

**Problem:** In data-parallel distributed deep learning, gradient communication during backpropagation creates a communication bottleneck. Uniform compression strategies fail to exploit layer-specific gradient distributions, where deeper and shallower layers have different sparsity and magnitude patterns.

**Method:** L-GreCo applies layerwise-adaptive compression that selects per-layer compression ratios based on gradient statistics and layer characteristics. It likely monitors gradient magnitudes and variances per layer, then dynamically assigns higher compression to layers with smaller gradient contributions while preserving precision in sensitive layers, combined with selective gradient dropping or quantization.

**Novelty:** Adaptive per-layer compression ratios that exploit layer-specific gradient distributions rather than applying uniform compression across all layers.
