# FedTrans: Efficient Federated Learning via Multi-Model Transformation

**Problem:** Federated learning systems face efficiency challenges due to communication overhead and heterogeneous device capabilities when coordinating distributed training across multiple clients. Model adaptation and transformation strategies are needed to match diverse hardware and network conditions.

**Method:** FedTrans applies multi-model transformation techniques to adapt and restructure models during federated training for improved efficiency. The approach transforms models to account for heterogeneous client capabilities, likely reducing communication overhead through model adaptation, compression, or selective layer transformations across federated participants.

**Novelty:** Using multi-model transformation as a core mechanism to optimize federated learning efficiency across heterogeneous distributed devices.
