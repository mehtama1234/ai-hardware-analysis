# FedTrans: Efficient Federated Learning via Multi-Model Transformation

**Problem:** Federated learning systems face efficiency challenges due to communication overhead and heterogeneous device capabilities when coordinating distributed training across multiple clients. Model adaptation and transformation strategies are needed to match diverse hardware and network conditions.

**Method:** FedTrans optimizes distributed neural network training across devices with varying capabilities—weak devices like phones and powerful servers mixed together—by transforming models to suit each device. It uses techniques like compression to make models smaller, or selective layer transformations to update only the most important parts of the network. This reduces the amount of data that must be communicated when devices sync their training progress, addressing the main performance bottleneck in federated learning systems.

**Novelty:** FedTrans uses model transformation—reshaping neural networks to match each device's capabilities—as the core strategy to make federated learning efficient.
