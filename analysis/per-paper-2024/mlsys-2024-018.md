# JIT-Q: Just-in-time Quantization with Processing-In-Memory for Efficient ML Training

**Problem:** ML training incurs substantial memory bandwidth overhead and compute inefficiency. Applying quantization during training requires efficient mechanisms to reduce precision without sacrificing model accuracy or requiring excessive data movement.

**Method:** JIT-Q applies quantization dynamically during training while leveraging processing-in-memory (PIM) to execute quantized operations directly within memory hierarchy. By co-locating quantization logic with PIM primitives, the approach minimizes data movement and computes on reduced-precision representations in-place.

**Novelty:** Combining just-in-time quantization with processing-in-memory to perform quantized training operations in-situ within memory, eliminating bandwidth-intensive data movement.
