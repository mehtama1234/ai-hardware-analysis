# Torch2Chip: An End-to-end Customizable Deep Neural Network Compression and Deployment Toolkit for Prototype Hardware Accelerator Design

**Problem:** Deploying neural networks to custom hardware accelerators requires integrated optimization, compression, and compilation steps that are not unified. Practitioners lack end-to-end tooling for compressing models and mapping them to prototype accelerator specifications.

**Method:** Torch2Chip provides an end-to-end pipeline that ingests PyTorch models and applies customizable compression techniques including quantization and sparsity optimization. The toolkit compiles compressed models to target-specific hardware descriptions and automates the mapping of neural network operations to custom accelerator resources.

**Novelty:** An integrated, customizable toolkit bridging PyTorch and custom hardware accelerator design with built-in neural network compression and hardware-aware deployment automation.
