# HeteroSwitch: Characterizing and Taming System-Induced Data Heterogeneity in Federated Learning

**Problem:** Federated learning systems experience data heterogeneity induced by system-level factors such as device heterogeneity, network variability, and distributed resource availability. This system-induced heterogeneity degrades convergence speed and model accuracy across heterogeneous clients.

**Method:** HeteroSwitch performs empirical characterization of system factors that induce data heterogeneity and applies adaptive mechanisms to dynamically respond to detected heterogeneity patterns. The system switches aggregation and training strategies based on measured system-induced heterogeneity, optimizing convergence for heterogeneous federated environments.

**Novelty:** Explicitly isolating and addressing system-induced data heterogeneity as a distinct problem from natural data skew in federated learning.
