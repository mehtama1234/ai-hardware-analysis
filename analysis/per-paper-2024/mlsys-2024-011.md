# Accurate Low-Degree Polynomial Approximation of Non-Polynomial Operators for Fast Private Inference in Homomorphic Encryption

**Problem:** Non-polynomial operators such as activation functions cannot be directly computed in homomorphic encryption schemes, forcing private inference to rely on expensive polynomial approximations that degrade both speed and accuracy.

**Method:** The paper proposes techniques for constructing low-degree polynomial approximations of non-polynomial operators (ReLU, sigmoid, etc.) that can be efficiently evaluated within homomorphic encryption. The approach optimizes the balance between polynomial degree and approximation error, enabling rapid evaluation of neural network layers on encrypted data.

**Novelty:** A method to achieve accurate low-degree polynomial approximations of non-polynomial operators, significantly reducing the computational overhead of private inference in homomorphic encryption.
