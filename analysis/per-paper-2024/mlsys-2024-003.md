# Schrodinger's FP Training Neural Networks with Dynamic Floating-Point Containers

**Problem:** Neural network training requires substantial computational resources and memory due to fixed high-precision floating-point requirements throughout training. Training efficiency is bottlenecked by the cost of maintaining uniform precision across all operations.

**Method:** The paper proposes a dynamic floating-point container system that adapts precision levels during training execution. Containers employ a superposition-inspired approach to transition or maintain multiple precision states, adjusting floating-point precision based on training phases and computational requirements. This allows flexible precision handling that optimizes the efficiency-accuracy tradeoff dynamically rather than using static precision throughout training.

**Novelty:** Dynamic floating-point containers that adaptively adjust precision during training execution based on computational requirements and training phases.
