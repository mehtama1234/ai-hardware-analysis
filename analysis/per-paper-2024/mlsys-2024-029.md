# DiffusionPipe: Training Large Diffusion Models with Efficient Pipelines

**Problem:** Training large-scale diffusion models incurs prohibitive memory and computational costs due to sequential denoising across many timesteps and the need to maintain large intermediate activations. Pipeline parallelism becomes inefficient for diffusion models because of their strong sequential dependencies and dynamic timestep-based computation patterns.

**Method:** DiffusionPipe applies optimized pipeline parallelism specifically tailored to diffusion model training by addressing timestep dependencies and denoising dynamics. The approach likely incorporates diffusion-aware stage partitioning, adaptive scheduling to minimize pipeline bubbles, and communication-computation overlap strategies that account for the sequential nature of the denoising process across GPUs.

**Novelty:** Diffusion-specific pipeline parallelism that explicitly accounts for timestep-dependent computation and denoising stages rather than applying generic pipeline strategies designed for other models.
