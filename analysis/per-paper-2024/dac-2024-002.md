# Low-Power Inference Engine with Quantization-Aware Circuit Design

**Problem:** Power consumption limits inference deployment on edge devices. Standard neural networks require high-precision arithmetic, increasing circuit power overhead and silicon area.

**Method:** The paper co-designs quantization-aware training with circuit implementation to reduce arithmetic precision requirements. Quantized models use fewer bits for computation, enabling specialized low-power arithmetic units in the circuit. The inference engine implements ResNet-50 with optimized dataflow tailored to reduced-precision operations.

**Novelty:** Co-design of quantization-aware training with specialized circuit arithmetic units to minimize power consumption in edge inference.

**Theme:** Low-power inference through quantization-aware circuit co-design
