# AWQ: Activation-aware Weight Quantization for On-Device LLM Compression and Acceleration

**Problem:** Large language models are too large and compute-intensive for efficient on-device inference. Existing quantization methods do not account for activation patterns, leading to suboptimal compression-accuracy tradeoffs.

**Method:** AWQ performs weight quantization guided by activation-aware analysis, examining actual activation patterns during inference to determine per-channel quantization sensitivity. Weights corresponding to high-magnitude activation channels are quantized less aggressively while low-activation channels use lower precision, enabling sub-bit quantization while preserving model accuracy. The method adapts quantization granularity dynamically based on observed activation statistics rather than applying uniform quantization.

**Novelty:** Using actual activation patterns to guide per-channel weight quantization sensitivity rather than relying on weight statistics or uniform quantization schemes.
