# Lancet: Accelerating Mixture-of-Experts Training via Whole Graph Computation-Communication Overlapping

**Problem:** Mixture-of-Experts training incurs frequent communication between experts and parameter servers, creating synchronization bottlenecks where computation and communication happen sequentially. This sequential execution limits training throughput and scalability.

**Method:** Lancet overlaps computation and communication across the entire MoE computation graph by using pipelining and scheduling to identify independent computation that can proceed while communication is in flight. The system coordinates overlapping at whole-graph level to hide communication latency behind useful work.

**Novelty:** Whole-graph computation-communication overlapping strategy specifically targeting MoE training patterns rather than layer-by-layer or point-wise overlapping.
