# VQPy: An Object-Oriented Approach to Modern Video Analytics

**Problem:** Video analytics systems struggle with modularity and composability when building complex processing pipelines. Imperative approaches lack abstraction mechanisms for reusable, optimizable video operations.

**Method:** VQPy lets you build video analytics pipelines by composing reusable components—like combining video filters—instead of writing everything from scratch. You describe what you want analyzed (such as 'detect objects in each frame') using a query language (a declarative way to specify what operations you need) rather than writing detailed code. A compiler then optimizes by combining related operations into fewer steps, ordering them efficiently, and running them on fast hardware accelerators like GPUs.

**Novelty:** VQPy applies object-oriented design to video analytics, letting you combine reusable components that are automatically optimized by the system.
