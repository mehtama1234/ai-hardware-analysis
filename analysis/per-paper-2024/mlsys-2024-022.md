# Accelerating ReLU for MPC-Based Private Inference with a Communication-Efficient Sign Estimation

**Problem:** ReLU activation functions are expensive to compute in Multi-Party Computation (MPC) based private inference systems due to their non-linear nature and high communication overhead between parties. Standard MPC protocols for ReLU require many rounds of secure computation.

**Method:** The approach uses a communication-efficient sign estimation technique to accelerate ReLU computation in MPC. By optimizing the protocol for computing the sign function (which determines whether ReLU outputs the input or zero), the method reduces communication rounds between parties. This likely combines approximation or novel cryptographic protocols to minimize message passing overhead.

**Novelty:** Communication-efficient sign estimation protocol that reduces message rounds for ReLU computation in MPC-based private inference.
