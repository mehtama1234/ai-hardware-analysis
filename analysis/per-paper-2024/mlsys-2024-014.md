# QMoE: Sub-1-Bit Compression of Trillion Parameter Models

**Problem:** Trillion-parameter models require prohibitive storage and memory bandwidth for inference. Achieving sub-1-bit quantization while maintaining accuracy is challenging, especially for Mixture of Experts architectures.

**Method:** Quantizes model weights to sub-1-bit precision using techniques specialized for Mixture of Experts architectures. Leverages sparse expert activation patterns and applies per-expert quantization parameters to maintain accuracy at extreme compression ratios.

**Novelty:** First to achieve practical sub-1-bit quantization on trillion-parameter models by exploiting MoE sparsity patterns.
