# BBS: Bi-Directional Bit-Level Sparsity for Deep Learning Acceleration

**Problem:** Existing accelerators exploit sparsity at coarse granularities (structured, vector-level) and typically in only one dimension (weights or activations), missing fine-grained optimization opportunities. Bit-level sparsity remains largely unexploited, especially in bidirectional fashion.

**Method:** BBS implements bidirectional bit-level sparsity detection and exploitation in hardware, identifying zero bits in both weight and activation streams and routing computations only to non-zero positions. The architecture uses efficient masking and skipping mechanisms with dedicated bit-level granularity support and control logic to minimize overhead while maximizing the benefits of sparse bit patterns.

**Novelty:** First to exploit bit-level sparsity simultaneously and bidirectionally across both weights and activations in a hardware accelerator design.
