# Fine-Tuning Language Models Using Formal Methods Feedback: A Use Case in Autonomous Systems

**Problem:** Language models deployed in autonomous systems may violate formal safety specifications or exhibit behaviors inconsistent with required properties. Fine-tuning mechanisms that incorporate formal verification feedback are needed to align model outputs with formal requirements.

**Method:** The approach uses formal verification (checking whether outputs satisfy strict logical safety specifications through techniques like model checking or theorem proving) to evaluate the model's behavior. When violations are detected, the verification produces training signals. The model then learns from them by adjusting its parameters (through gradient-based optimization) to minimize error signals derived from specification violations—teaching it to consistently follow the formal safety requirements.

**Novelty:** The key innovation is integrating formal verification feedback directly into the fine-tuning process, so the model learns to produce outputs that satisfy formal safety specifications.
