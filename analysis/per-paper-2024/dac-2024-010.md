# Supply-Chain Security in Hardware Design for AI Systems

**Problem:** Supply-chain attacks can introduce unauthorized hardware modifications to AI accelerators that evade detection, compromising system integrity and security at manufacturing or design stages.

**Method:** The framework catches supply-chain attacks—unauthorized hardware modifications—using formal verification and lightweight runtime monitoring together. Formal verification mathematically proves the hardware design is correct against threat models that describe potential attacks, catching hidden vulnerabilities before manufacturing. Lightweight runtime monitoring watches the actual running hardware to detect any unauthorized modifications introduced during manufacturing or deployment, maintaining minimal performance overhead through selective instrumentation of only the most critical hardware components.

**Novelty:** Combining formal verification of hardware designs with lightweight runtime monitoring to detect supply-chain attacks in AI systems while preserving performance.
