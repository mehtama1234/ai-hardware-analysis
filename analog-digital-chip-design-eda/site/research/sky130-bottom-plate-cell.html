Sky130 Bottom-Plate Cell

This page publishes the isolated one-bit bottom-plate transistor-cell experiment. The generated measurements are appended below. A timeout is recorded as a circuit or simulation failure, not treated as a missing pass.

The fixture was rerun with explicit top and bottom leakage paths, Gear integration, tighter voltage/charge tolerances, and deterministic non-uic startup. It still timed out in all 4/4 cases. The failure therefore persists after a solver-initialization check and remains a cell/control-topology issue, not evidence of a working bottom-plate switch.
