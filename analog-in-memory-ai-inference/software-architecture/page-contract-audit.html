Analog AI Page Contract Audit

This audit checks whether the visible HTML and markdown review surface now reads as one system. A page or spec is aligned only when it names its object, constraint, design move, evidence, allowed claim, refused claim, and next handoff.

Audit Rule

The writing goal is not more pages. The goal is a reader who can move from model graph to analog/digital placement, from placement to lab evidence, from lab evidence to backend import, and from backend import to claim readiness without guessing the next step.

Contract ItemMeaning
ObjectThe concrete thing under review: workload, graph, placement, simulator result, RTL trace, OpenLane report, board trace, power trace, or claim.
ConstraintThe reason the object may fail: converter cost, tile size, noise, drift, memory movement, fallback, timing, thermal, unsupported operation, or missing provenance.
Design moveThe action the system takes: keep analog, keep digital, split, bit-slice, retry, fall back, calibrate, measure, import, or block.
EvidenceThe named file, endpoint, report, trace, or measurement that supports the move.
Claim boundaryThe exact sentence allowed now and the stronger sentence that remains blocked.
Next handoffThe page, script, endpoint, or lab artifact that consumes this page's output.
